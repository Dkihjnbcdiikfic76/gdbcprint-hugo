About Bincai Color Printing

January 1, 1

Company Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. was founded in 2003 in Guangzhou, now headquartered in Shunde Daliang, Foshan — the manufacturing heart of China’s Pearl River Delta. We are a full-chain printing and packaging enterprise integrating design, development, raw material processing, printing, laminating, die-cutting, mounting, box gluing, and logistics — all under one roof.

Factory & Capacity

  • Factory Area: 10,000+ sqm (with new 8,000 sqm smart factory opened 2025)
  • Employees: 80+ skilled workers
  • Daily Output: 1.7 million color boxes + 200,000+ gift boxes
  • Annual Output: 10+ million boxes exported globally

Our Products

We specialize in premium paper packaging:

  • Color Corrugated Boxes — Custom printed, die-cut, for shipping and retail
  • Cosmetic Paper Boxes — Premium folding cartons for beauty and skincare
  • Gift Boxes — Rigid boxes with magnetic closure, ribbon, velvet inserts

  • Paper Bags — Custom printed shopping and gift bags
