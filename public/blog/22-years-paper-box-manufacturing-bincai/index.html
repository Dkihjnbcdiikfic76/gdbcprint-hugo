22 Years of Paper Box Manufacturing — The Bincai Story

May 20, 2026

Founded in 2003 in Guangzhou, Guangdong Bincai Color Printing has grown from a small print shop to a 10,000 sqm full-chain packaging enterprise. In 2025, we opened a new 8,000 sqm smart factory in Shunde Daliang, Foshan.

Today, with KBA 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, fully automatic production lines, and 80+ skilled workers, we produce over 1.7 million boxes daily for clients across 50+ countries.

Our ISO 9001 and FSC certifications demonstrate our commitment to quality and sustainability. Whether you need 500 custom gift boxes or 1 million folding cartons, Bincai delivers.
