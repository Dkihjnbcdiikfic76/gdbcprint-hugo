From Design to Delivery: How a Custom Paper Box Is Made at Bincai

June 10, 2026

Whether you’re launching a new cosmetic line or rebranding a premium spirit, understanding how your packaging is made helps you communicate better with your manufacturer — and get better results. Here’s exactly how a custom paper box moves through Bincai’s 10,000 sqm factory.

Step 1: Design & Structural Engineering

Every box starts with your requirements. Our in-house structural engineers convert your concept into a production-ready die line:

  • CAD-based structural design — precise dimensions, material thickness calculations, and closure mechanics
  • Material selection — SBS (solid bleached sulfate), CCNB (clay-coated news back), kraft board, or corrugated; 1mm–3mm thickness for rigid boxes, 300–400gsm for folding cartons
  • Prototyping — physical samples cut on our Kongsberg digital cutting table before committing to steel dies

Step 2: Prepress & Plate Making

Once the design is approved, our prepress team prepares Kodak CTP (computer-to-plate) output:

  • Color separation and trapping for multi-color jobs
  • Proofing on calibrated Epson proof printers for color accuracy sign-off
  • Offset plates exposed at 2400 dpi for razor-sharp text and smooth gradients

Step 3: Offset Printing — KBA 1050 + Heidelberg 7+1 UV

This is where Bincai’s dual-press strategy shines:

PressBest ForMax Speed
KBA Rapida 1050 4-colorHigh-volume CMYK jobs, folding cartons, corrugated15,000 sheets/hour
Heidelberg Speedmaster 7+1 UVPremium rigid boxes with spot colors, gold/silver, UV coating12,000 sheets/hour

The Heidelberg’s UV curing dries ink instantly — no smudging, no waiting, and a crystal-clear gloss that makes logos pop off the shelf.

Step 4: Surface Finishing

Post-print, sheets go through our finishing department:

  • Hot foil stamping — gold, silver, holographic, or matte foil applied with heated brass dies
  • Embossing / Debossing — raised or recessed textures for logos and patterns
  • Spot UV coating — selective high-gloss varnish on logos or design elements
  • Matte or gloss lamination — full-sheet film lamination for durability and tactile feel

Step 5: Die-Cutting & Creasing

Custom steel-rule dies cut and crease each sheet:

  • Bobst die-cutters handle formats up to 1050×750mm
  • Creasing ensures clean folds without cracking on coated stock
  • Stripping stations remove waste automatically

Step 6: Gluing & Assembly

Folding cartons go through high-speed folder-gluers. Rigid boxes are hand-assembled by our skilled team:

  • Automatic folder-gluer — 30,000 cartons per hour for straight-line and auto-lock-bottom styles
  • Rigid box assembly line — manual wrapping, magnetic closure insertion, ribbon attachment, and velvet/flocking interior lining
  • Each workstation includes inline QC checks

Step 7: Quality Inspection

With ISO 9001 governing every step, final inspection uses AQL (Acceptable Quality Level) sampling:

  • Print registration and color consistency checks
  • Structural integrity — box compression, lid fit, magnet strength
  • Surface quality — no scratches, glue marks, or foil misalignment

Step 8: Packing & Global Shipping

  • Flat-packed for folding cartons; assembled in poly-bags for rigid boxes
  • Standard export cartons with moisture barrier
  • FCL (full container load) and LCL shipping from Guangzhou/Shenzhen ports
  • Door-to-door delivery to North America, Europe, Middle East, and Asia-Pacific

Why This Process Matters for Your Brand

At 1.7 million boxes per day and 22 years of continuous operation, Bincai has refined each step into a repeatable, quality-controlled system. Whether you order 5,000 boxes or 500,000, the same ISO 9001 discipline applies — because your brand deserves packaging that matches the product inside.
