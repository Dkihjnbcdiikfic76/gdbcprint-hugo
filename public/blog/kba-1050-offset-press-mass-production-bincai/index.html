KBA 1050 4-Color Press: The Backbone of Bincai's 1.7M Box Daily Output

June 11, 2026

Behind every paper box shipped from Guangdong Bincai Color Printing is a manufacturing engine that runs with German precision. At the heart of our 10,000 sqm factory in Dongguan’s Pearl River Delta manufacturing hub sits the KBA 1050 4-color offset press — a workhorse that anchors our daily output of 1.7 million boxes.

Why KBA 1050?

The Koenig & Bauer (KBA) Rapida 105 is a large-format sheetfed offset press built for high-volume commercial printing. At Bincai, this press handles:

  • Format size up to 740 × 1,050 mm — accommodating large folding carton layouts and rigid box wraps with minimal waste
  • 4-color CMYK in a single pass — consistent, sharp color registration at speeds exceeding 15,000 sheets per hour
  • Heavy stock capability — handles paperboard from 200 gsm lightweight cartons to 600 gsm rigid box covers without compromise
  • Quick-change plate system — enables fast job turnover between production batches, keeping lead times short

The Two-Press Strategy: KBA + Heidelberg

Bincai operates a dual-press production line that no single-technology shop can match:

This combination means a single order can run through the KBA for base printing, then through the Heidelberg for premium spot UV or foil-ready coating — all under one roof, with no outsourcing delays.

Factory Scale That Delivers

Our 10,000 sqm facility in Dongguan is purpose-built for paper packaging at scale:

  • 22 years of continuous operation since 2004
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system
  • FSC® certified (Forest Stewardship Council) — chain of custody for sustainable sourcing
  • 1.7 million boxes daily at full capacity across all production lines
  • In-house die-cutting, gluing, foil stamping, embossing, and window patching — no subcontracting, no delays

What This Means for Your Order

When you place an order with Bincai, the KBA 1050 ensures:

  1. Consistent color — Every sheet matches the first. Pantone precision across the full print run.
  2. Short lead times — High-speed production plus in-house finishing means orders ship faster.
  3. Scalable volumes — From 1,000 sample units to 500,000+ mass production, the same quality standard applies.
  4. Competitive pricing — Efficiency at scale translates directly to better unit economics for bulk orders.
