Custom Rigid Box with Window Display Perfume Packaging

June 10, 2026

Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s custom rigid box with die-cut window display for perfume packaging merges shelf impact with luxury protection. A precision die-cut window showcases the bottle through optional PET or PLA film, while the rigid board construction, magnetic closure, and velvet/foam interior cradle the product securely. Manufactured in our ISO 9001:2015 certified 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) — KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, 22 years of expertise, 1.7M daily output.

Applications

  • Luxury Perfume & Fragrance: Branded perfume gift sets, limited edition releases, travel-size collections
  • High-End Cosmetics: Skincare serums, premium facial oils, luxury cream jars
  • Candles & Home Fragrance: Premium scented candles, reed diffusers, room sprays
  • Beverage & Spirits: Miniature spirit bottles, craft gin/vodka, collector’s edition packaging
  • Jewelry & Accessories: Fragrance + jewelry combo gift sets, bridal party gifts
  • Corporate Gifts: Executive fragrance gifts, VIP client appreciation packaging

Customization Options

  • Box Style: Two-piece rigid with magnetic flap, book-style opening, drawer with window, hinged lid
  • Window Shape: Rectangle, circle, oval, heart, custom die-cut silhouette matching bottle shape
  • Window Material: Clear PET (standard), biodegradable PLA film, or open cutout
  • Sizes: Custom-fit to any bottle dimensions — 50×50×80mm to 250×250×350mm
  • Materials: 157gsm art paper wrap over 1.5–3.0mm greyboard, specialty papers (linen, leatherette, pearl)
  • Interior: Velvet flocking (black, white, red, navy), satin lining, die-cut foam insert, molded pulp
  • Printing: CMYK offset + Pantone spot on Heidelberg 7+1 UV, full coverage or pattern
  • Finishes: Gold/silver/rose gold foil stamping, embossing/debossing, spot UV, soft-touch matte lamination
  • Closure: Hidden magnet, ribbon tie, magnetic flap, or friction fit
