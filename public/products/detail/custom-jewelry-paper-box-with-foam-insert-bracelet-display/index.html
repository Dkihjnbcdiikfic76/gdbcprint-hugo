Custom Jewelry Paper Box with Foam Insert Bracelet Display

June 10, 2026

Product Overview

Showcase bracelets, bangles, and fine jewelry with this premium custom jewelry box featuring a precision-cut foam insert. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing — an ISO 9001 & FSC certified factory with 22 years of packaging expertise and daily output exceeding 1.5 million boxes. Each box is hand-finished by our 80+ skilled craftspeople in Foshan, Guangdong.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Press: Heidelberg 7+1 UV / KBA 1050 4-color offset
  • Construction: Rigid greyboard 1200–1600gsm, wrapped in 157gsm art paper
  • Insert: Custom die-cut EVA foam, velvet flocking, or satin-lined cavities
  • Daily output: 200K+ rigid gift boxes per day
  • Board tolerance: ±0.3mm precision die-cut with Bobst flatbed
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody

Customization Options

  • Sizing: 80 × 80 × 30mm (compact) to 350 × 250 × 80mm (multi-piece)
  • Printing: CMYK offset, Pantone spot colors, full bleed coverage
  • Finishes: Matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch coating, aqueous varnish
  • Embellishments: Gold/silver/rose gold foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV
  • Insert types: Die-cut foam, velvet-flocked tray, satin pillows, card slots
  • Closure: Magnetic flap, ribbon tie, lift-off lid, book-style hinge

Applications

Bracelets, bangles, necklaces, rings, earring sets, watches, cufflinks, luxury accessories, bridal jewelry, corporate gift sets, and premium retail display packaging.
