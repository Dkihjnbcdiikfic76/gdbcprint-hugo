Product Overview

Bincai’s hexagonal wine bottle gift box combines architectural geometry with premium presentation. The six-sided rigid construction creates a commanding shelf presence, while the satin ribbon bow closure and gold foil exterior deliver luxury unboxing. Die-cut foam cradle insert secures standard 750ml bottles. Ideal for wineries, distilleries, corporate gifting, and luxury retail. Manufactured with KBA 1050 4-color offset printing in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility. 22 years of premium packaging expertise.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Press: KBA 1050 4-color offset — precise registration for geometric patterns
  • UV Enhancement: Heidelberg 7+1 UV press — selective spot UV on brand elements
  • Board: 1.5mm greyboard core, 157gsm art paper wrap with matte lamination
  • Interior: Die-cut high-density EVA foam cradle, black velvet flocking overlay
  • Closure: Satin ribbon bow (15mm width, 12 color options) with elastic retention
  • Exterior Finish: Brushed gold foil base + custom embossed logo on lid
  • Shape: Hexagonal (6-sided), flat-pack shippable pre-assembly
  • Daily Output: 220,000+ rigid boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody
