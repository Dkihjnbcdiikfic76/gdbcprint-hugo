Custom Rigid Gift Box with Ribbon Gold Foil Embossing

June 10, 2026

Product Overview

A premium custom rigid gift box featuring a satin ribbon closure and elegant gold foil embossed logo — the signature luxury packaging solution for high-end brands. Handcrafted in our dedicated rigid box production line at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., each box combines 2.5mm greyboard rigidity with premium art paper wrapping and precision foil stamping. The ribbon adds a tactile unboxing experience while the embossed gold foil conveys brand prestige.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Box construction: 2.5mm greyboard core, wrapped in 157gsm art paper
  • Ribbon: High-density satin ribbon, color-matched to brand palette
  • Foil stamping: Heidelberg cylinder press with heated brass die, gold/silver/copper/rose gold
  • Embossing: Multi-level blind embossing up to 1.5mm depth on 2.5mm board
  • Daily rigid box output: 220,000+ gift boxes
  • Lamination: Soft-touch matte, high-gloss, or anti-scratch
  • Interior lining: Velvet flocking, satin fabric, or custom-printed paper

Customization Options

  • Size: From 50mm × 50mm × 30mm to 500mm × 400mm × 150mm
  • Shape: Rectangular, square, hexagonal, round, custom die-cut contours
  • Closure: Magnetic flap, ribbon tie, lid-and-base, book-style hinge, drawer slide
  • Foil colors: Gold (standard), silver, copper, rose gold, holographic, matte gold
  • Embossing types: Blind emboss, foil+emboss combo, multi-level sculpted emboss
  • Interior: Custom die-cut EVA foam, velvet flocking, satin padded, cardboard partitions
  • Surface: Matte lamination + spot UV, soft-touch velvet coating, linen-textured paper

Applications

Designed for luxury brands packaging: premium cosmetics, fragrances, jewelry sets, watches, corporate gifts, wedding favors, limited-edition collectibles, fine teas, premium spirits, and luxury confectionery. The ribbon+foil combination is especially popular for gift-season limited editions and brand anniversary packaging.

Production Quality & Workflow

Every rigid box passes through 15 quality control checkpoints across our dedicated production line:

  1. Raw Material Inspection — Board caliper (±0.1mm), paper GSM verification, ribbon tensile strength test
  2. Die-Cutting QC — Edge precision ±0.5mm, crease depth calibration, waste stripping integrity
  3. Board Assembly — Greyboard lamination bond strength (≥2.5 N/cm² peel test), corner squareness (90° ±0.5°)
  4. Wrapping & Gluing — Surface bubble detection, seam overlap ≥12mm, adhesive coverage ≥95%
  5. Magnet Insertion — Polarity verification, pull-force test (≥3N for standard flap), recess depth consistency
  6. Foil Stamping — Heated brass die alignment (±0.3mm), foil adhesion rub-test (50 cycles minimum), color consistency under D65 lighting
  7. Embossing Registration — Multi-level depth measurement (laser profilometer), emboss-to-print register (±0.2mm)
  8. Ribbon Installation — Thread tension test (1.5-2.5N), bow symmetry check, color match against Pantone swatch
  9. Lid-Base Fit — Gap tolerance ±1mm, hinge smoothness (opens/closes without resistance), flat-close verification
  10. Print Colorimetry — X-Rite spectrophotometer measurement against brand Pantone references, ΔE ≤ 2.0
  11. Interior Fit — Insert alignment, foam/EVA density verification, flocking adhesion test
  12. Drop Test — 1.2m free-fall drop onto concrete (6 faces, 3 edges, 1 corner), box must protect contents
  13. Environmental — 48-hour humidity chamber (85% RH, 40°C), 24-hour cold cycle (-20°C), adhesive and board integrity verified
  14. Final Visual — Inspection under D65 (daylight), TL84 (store), and A (incandescent) lighting conditions
  15. Pack-Out Audit — Polybag seal, barcode scan, carton label verification, pallet stacking test

Production timeline: Dieline approval (24h) → Digital proof (48h) → Sample production (3-5 days) → Customer approval → Mass production (10-15 days) → QC & shipping.
