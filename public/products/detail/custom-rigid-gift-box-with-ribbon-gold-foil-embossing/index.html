Custom Rigid Gift Box with Ribbon Gold Foil Embossing

June 10, 2026

Product Overview

A premium custom rigid gift box featuring a satin ribbon closure and elegant gold foil embossed logo — the signature luxury packaging solution for high-end brands. Handcrafted in our dedicated rigid box production line at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., each box combines 2.5mm greyboard rigidity with premium art paper wrapping and precision foil stamping. The ribbon adds a tactile unboxing experience while the embossed gold foil conveys brand prestige.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Box construction: 2.5mm greyboard core, wrapped in 157gsm art paper
  • Ribbon: High-density satin ribbon, color-matched to brand palette
  • Foil stamping: Heidelberg cylinder press with heated brass die, gold/silver/copper/rose gold
  • Embossing: Multi-level blind embossing up to 1.5mm depth on 2.5mm board
  • Daily rigid box output: 200,000+ gift boxes
  • Lamination: Soft-touch matte, high-gloss, or anti-scratch
  • Interior lining: Velvet flocking, satin fabric, or custom-printed paper

Customization Options

  • Size: From 50mm × 50mm × 30mm to 500mm × 400mm × 150mm
  • Shape: Rectangular, square, hexagonal, round, custom die-cut contours
  • Closure: Magnetic flap, ribbon tie, lid-and-base, book-style hinge, drawer slide
  • Foil colors: Gold (standard), silver, copper, rose gold, holographic, matte gold
  • Embossing types: Blind emboss, foil+emboss combo, multi-level sculpted emboss
  • Interior: Custom die-cut EVA foam, velvet flocking, satin padded, cardboard partitions
  • Surface: Matte lamination + spot UV, soft-touch velvet coating, linen-textured paper

Applications

Designed for luxury brands packaging: premium cosmetics, fragrances, jewelry sets, watches, corporate gifts, wedding favors, limited-edition collectibles, fine teas, premium spirits, and luxury confectionery. The ribbon+foil combination is especially popular for gift-season limited editions and brand anniversary packaging.

Production Quality

Every rigid box passes through 12 quality control checkpoints: board thickness verification, adhesive bond strength test, foil adhesion rub-test, ribbon tension consistency, lid alignment (±1mm), colorimetry check against brand Pantone, and final appearance inspection under D65 standard lighting.
