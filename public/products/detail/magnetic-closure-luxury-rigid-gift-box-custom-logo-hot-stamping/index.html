Magnetic Closure Luxury Rigid Gift Box Custom Logo Hot Stamping

June 10, 2026

Luxury magnetic closure rigid gift box with custom logo hot foil stamping — crafted at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain paper box manufacturer with 22 years of rigid box expertise (founded 2003). Our 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) in Foshan operates KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, producing 1.7 million boxes daily. ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified. Every magnetic closure box features precision-aligned N52-grade neodymium magnets for a satisfying snap-shut action.

Customization Options

  • Box Style: Book-style magnetic flap, two-piece lid-and-base, clamshell, drawer-slide, hinged flip-top
  • Exterior Wrapping: 128–200gsm art paper (matte, gloss, soft-touch velvet, linen-textured, pearlescent, metallic); PU leatherette for ultra-luxury feel
  • Foil Stamping: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper, holographic, matte gold — single or multi-level with blind emboss/deboss combination
  • Finishes: Matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch coating, spot UV, aqueous varnish, textured embossing
  • Magnets: N52 neodymium (6–12mm diameter), single/dual/perimeter strip, concealed between board layers
  • Interior: Velvet flocking (12+ colors), satin lining, custom-printed paper, die-cut EVA foam tray, ribbon pull-tab
  • Edge Details: Gold/silver gilding, colored edge painting, ribbon wrap-around tie
  • Size: 80×80×30mm mini to 450×350×120mm large format

Production Specifications

  • Press: KBA Rapida 105 4-color at 15,000 sheets/hour + Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV
  • Board: Rigid greyboard 1200–1600gsm with double-wrapped construction
  • Foil Stamping: Bobst SP 102 BMA with ±0.15mm registration, multi-zone temperature control
  • Assembly: Fully automatic rigid box line — corner taping, wrapping lamination, magnet insertion
  • Daily Capacity: 220,000+ rigid magnetic closure boxes
  • Lead Time: 10–15 working days new tooling; 7–10 days repeat orders
