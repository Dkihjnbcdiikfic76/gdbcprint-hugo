Corrugated Gift Box with Custom Print Embossing Spot UV

June 10, 2026

Product Overview

This premium corrugated gift box combines the structural strength of corrugated board with luxury finishing techniques — custom CMYK offset printing, embossed branding, and spot UV highlight. Manufactured by Bincai Color Printing (founded 2003, Foshan, Guangdong), this box delivers retail-shelf presentation inside a shipping-grade structure. Our 10,000 sqm factory + 8,000 sqm smart factory (2025) produces 1.7 million boxes daily, with 80+ skilled employees and a full in-house finishing line (KBA 1050, Heidelberg 7+1 UV, Bobst die-cutters, automated gluing).

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Press: KBA 1050 4-color offset for liner printing; Heidelberg 7+1 UV for premium finishes
  • Flute Options: E-flute (1.2mm, fine print), B-flute (2.5mm, crush-resistant), BC-flute (6mm, heavy-duty)
  • Liner Grades: 157gsm/200gsm/250gsm coated art paper, 175gsm kraft, 200gsm white-top
  • Embossing: Copper die, up to 1.5mm relief depth, registered to print ±0.2mm
  • Spot UV: High-gloss selective coating, 80-100 micron thickness, ΔE ≤ 2.0 color fidelity
  • Die-Cutting: Bobst flatbed, precision ±0.3mm, complex window and handle cuts
  • Daily Output: 1.7M color boxes + 220,000+ gift boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
