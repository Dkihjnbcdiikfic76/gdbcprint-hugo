Eco-Friendly Kraft Paper Mailer Box with Paper Tape 100 Recyclable

June 11, 2026

Eco-friendly kraft paper mailer box with paper tape closure — 100% recyclable, completely plastic-free — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) with KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses. Every component from the FSC-certified kraft board to the paper-based tear strip and soy-ink printing is biodegradable and curbside recyclable. Daily output 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • E-commerce subscription box shipping
  • Direct-to-consumer (DTC) brand packaging
  • Eco-conscious cosmetics and skincare mailers
  • Sustainable food and snack delivery boxes
  • Book and media mailers (corrugated kraft variant)
  • Apparel and fashion accessory shipping boxes

Customization Options

  • 100% plastic-free construction: paper tape instead of plastic tape, paper tear strip
  • FSC-certified kraft paper in natural brown, bleached white, or black kraft
  • Soy-based ink printing: single-color stamp logo or full CMYK offset
  • Custom embossing/debossing for brand logo on natural kraft surface
  • Paper tear strip with easy-open perforation — no scissors needed, fully recyclable
  • Self-locking bottom (crash-lock) or tuck-top closure designs
  • Interior printing for branded unboxing experience
  • Die-cut handle or cotton rope handle (compostable) for retail-ready presentation
  • Custom sizes from 150×100×30mm to 500×400×150mm
  • Window patch: PLA (compostable bioplastic) or fully paper-based lattice window

Production Specifications

  • FSC Mix-certified kraft paperboard: 250gsm–450gsm for mailer durability
  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press for high-volume kraft printing
  • Soy-based ink (EN 13432 compliant) for full biodegradability
  • Water-based adhesive for box gluing — zero VOC emissions
  • Paper tape: reinforced kraft gummed tape, water-activated, plastic-free
  • Automatic folder-gluer line with inline quality inspection
  • ISO 9001:2015 quality management at every production stage
  • Corrugated kraft option available: B-flute, E-flute for extra crush protection
