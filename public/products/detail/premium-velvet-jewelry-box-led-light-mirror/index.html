Product Overview

The premium velvet jewelry box with integrated LED lighting transforms jewelry presentation from static display to illuminated showcase. When the recipient lifts the magnetic-closure lid, a soft warm-white LED strip activates automatically, bathing the jewelry in gallery-quality light — while the interior lid mirror provides a convenient viewing angle. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. — ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified, 22 years of luxury packaging — each box combines 1200gsm rigid greyboard construction with premium electrostatic velvet flocking (1.5mm fiber length) and a concealed LED module powered by two CR2032 coin cells (included, 200+ hours runtime).

Design Features

  • Auto-Activating LED Light: Magnetic reed switch embedded in box rim — LED strip illuminates automatically when lid opens, turns off when closed. Warm white 3000K, 8-LED strip for even illumination.
  • Interior Lid Mirror: 2mm beveled glass mirror mounted inside lid — recipient can see jewelry in the box without additional tools. Anti-tarnish backing for jewelry-safe environment.
  • Premium Velvet Flocking: 1.5mm nylon velvet fibers electrostatically applied at 45kV — dense, uniform coverage in 20+ colors with anti-tarnish treatment for silver and gold jewelry.
  • Magnetic Closure: Two N35 neodymium magnets (8mm × 2mm) embedded in the lid rim and base wall — satisfying magnetic snap, no visible hardware.
  • Jewelry-Specific Inserts: Die-cut foam inserts with ring slots (up to 3 rings), necklace channels, bracelet cushions, and earring card slots — all velvet-covered.

Applications

  • Engagement Rings: The LED illumination makes diamonds and gemstones sparkle dramatically — a proposal presentation that matches the moment.
  • Necklace & Pendant Sets: Necklace hangs on velvet-covered hook with pendant displayed against contrasting velvet background under LED light.
  • Bracelet & Bangle Display: Curved velvet cushion holds bracelets securely — LED light accents metal finishes.
  • Earring Presentation: Stud earrings mounted on velvet card, drop earrings displayed on slotted insert — illuminated for maximum brilliance.
  • Wedding Jewelry: Bridal jewelry sets for the wedding day — box doubles as a keepsake display on the vanity.
