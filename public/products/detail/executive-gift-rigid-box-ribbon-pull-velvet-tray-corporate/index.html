Product Overview

Guangdong Bincai’s executive rigid gift box is designed for premium corporate gifting — award ceremonies, VIP client presentations, executive onboarding kits, and luxury brand retail. The signature satin ribbon pull-tab opens a perfectly tensioned hinged lid to reveal a removable velvet-flocked tray that elegantly presents pen and notebook sets, watch and cufflink pairings, or custom curated corporate gift collections. 3mm CNC-wrapped greyboard construction with precision mitered corners. 22 years of manufacturing. ISO 9001:2015 & FSC certified from our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Press: KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset — 15,000 sheets/hour, Heidelberg ColorControl
  • Finishing: Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV with inter-deck UV curing, single-pass CMYK+spot+coating
  • Foil Stamping: Bobst SP 102 BMA hot foil — gold, silver, copper, rose gold, Pantone metallics — ±0.15mm registration
  • Construction: 3mm greyboard (1500–2000gsm), CNC corner-wrapped, mitered ±0.2mm angle tolerance
  • Surface: Leatherette PU wrap (12 colors), linen-textured paper, or soft-touch matte lamination
  • Tray: Removable 1.5mm greyboard tray with electrostatic velvet flocking, CNC-milled product cavities
  • Magnets: 6× N52 neodymium hidden magnets for authoritative snap-close
  • Ribbon: Double-face satin 12mm pull-tab, 16 color options, bar-tacked for 15kg+ pull strength
  • Facility: 18,000 sqm (10,000 + 8,000 smart factory 2025), dedicated rigid box lines
  • Daily Output: 220,000+ rigid boxes

Quality Assurance

7-stage QC: (1) greyboard caliper ±5%, (2) warp test ≤1mm corner lift, (3) CMYK Delta E ≤2.5, (4) foil registration ±0.15mm, (5) magnetic closure alignment ±0.3mm gap, (6) ribbon pull strength ≥15kg, (7) ISTA 1A drop test on assembled box with simulated product weight.
