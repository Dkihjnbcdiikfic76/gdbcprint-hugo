Product Overview

The Custom Rigid Trunk-Style Gift Box combines old-world craftsmanship aesthetics with modern precision manufacturing. Produced by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. — operating an 18,000 sqm facility (10,000 sqm main factory + 8,000 sqm smart factory commissioned 2025) with 22 years of paper packaging expertise — this trunk box features real metal corner protectors, a stitched leather carrying handle, and a telescopic lid that evokes vintage travel trunks. It transforms any product into an heirloom unboxing experience.

The box body is built with 2.5mm greyboard wrapped in 157gsm art paper, printed on our Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 7+1 UV press or KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset press — achieving photographic color fidelity with inline UV curing for scratch resistance. Metal corners are riveted through the board for structural integrity, not merely glued, and the leather handle is attached with reinforced rivets rated for weights up to 8kg. Interior lining options include velvet flocking (black, red, cream, custom PMS), satin, faux suede, or die-cut EVA foam inserts sized precisely to cradle your product.

Key Specs at a Glance: ISO 9001:2015 & FSC certified | 1.7M boxes daily output | Heidelberg 7+1 UV / KBA 1050 offset | 2.5mm rigid greyboard | Metal corners + leather handle | MOQ 500 | 10–14 day lead time | Custom insert included

Applications

Trunk-style rigid boxes serve as keepsake packaging that consumers retain long after purchase. Ideal for:

  • Luxury Spirits & Wine: Single-bottle or double-bottle configurations with die-cut foam nests, satin ribbon lid stays, and embossed distillery logos
  • Premium Watch & Jewelry Sets: Multi-slot velvet interiors for watch collections, necklace + earring sets, or bridal jewelry presentations
  • Corporate Executive Gifts: Custom-embossed company logos on the lid, interior compartments for pen sets, business card holders, and USB drives
  • Limited Edition & Collector’s Items: Numbered editions, anniversary releases, commemorative packaging with hot-foil stamping and debossing
  • High-End Confectionery: Multi-tier chocolate or macaron presentations for luxury hotels, resorts, and duty-free retail

Customization Options

Every element of the trunk box is configurable to match your brand identity:

  • Print & Decoration: Full CMYK offset litho, Pantone spot colors, hot foil stamping (gold, silver, rose gold, holographic), embossing/debossing, spot UV varnish, matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch coating
  • Corner Hardware: Antique brass, polished gold, chrome silver, matte black, rose gold — in rounded or squared profiles
  • Handle Material: Full-grain leather, PU leather, braided rope, metal chain, fabric strap — custom color matching available
  • Interior: Velvet flocking (any PMS), satin, suede, EVA foam (laser-cut to product profile), cardstock partitions, ribbon pull-tabs
  • Closure: Telescopic friction lid, magnetic flip-top, buckle-and-strap, metal latch with lock hasp

Production Specifications

  • Board: 2.0–3.0mm greyboard (recycled content 85%+) wrapped in 157gsm C2S art paper or 128gsm kraft
  • Printing: Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 7+1 UV (max sheet 750×1050mm) or KBA Rapida 105 4-color
  • Surface: Aqueous coating (standard), UV gloss/matte varnish, soft-touch lamination, anti-scuff laminate
  • Assembly: Machine-assisted corner gluing, automatic inner tray insertion, manual hardware riveting and handle attachment
  • Quality Control: AQL 2.5 (major), AQL 1.5 (critical) — each unit inspected for corner alignment (±0.5mm), hinge operation, print registration, and leather attachment strength
