Executive-grade portfolio-style magnetic closure presentation box — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a Diamond Member on Made-in-China.com since 2015, Audited Supplier with 22 years of experience. Inspired by premium document folios, this box features a book-style magnetic flap that opens to reveal a thoughtfully organized interior: a clear PET document sleeve on the inner lid for brochures, certificates, or welcome letters; an elastic pen loop for branded writing instruments; and a custom die-cut foam tray for the primary gift item. The invisible neodymium magnetic closure provides a satisfying, secure snap. Ideal for corporate award ceremonies, luxury brand press kits, VIP client gifts, and executive onboarding packages. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across 18,000 sqm factory.

  • Luxury brand press and media kit packaging
  • VIP client welcome and onboarding gift boxes
  • Executive retreat and conference attendee kits
  • Real estate and property development sales presentation
  • Automotive key handover ceremony packaging
  • University and institutional certificate presentation

Customization Options

  • Cover: art paper 157gsm wrapped with matte/gloss lamination; premium leatherette or linen texture; or real leather for ultra-luxury
  • Closure: invisible neodymium magnets (2–4 pieces per flap); optional ribbon tie or metal clasp
  • Document sleeve: clear PET 0.3mm–0.5mm welded pocket; die-cut diagonal or straight opening
  • Pen loop: elastic webbing 10mm–15mm width in black, white, or brand color; optional leather strap with snap
  • Foam tray: die-cut EVA foam with velvet flocking in custom cavity shapes; optional satin-covered card tray
  • Printing: CMYK offset with spot PMS colors; full-wrap exterior and interior panel printing
  • Foil stamping: gold, silver, rose gold, copper debossed foil on cover logo and interior branding
  • Embossing: multi-level 3D embossing on cover for tactile brand experience
  • Accessories: optional business card slot, USB drive cavity, or key-ring hook
  • Size: A4 portfolio (320×240×40mm) to large format (450×350×60mm)
