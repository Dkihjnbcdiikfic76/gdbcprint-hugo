Magnetic Closure Single-Door Gift Box with Ribbon Pull Premium

June 11, 2026

Product Overview

The single-door magnetic closure gift box with ribbon pull elevates the unboxing experience to a ritual. A gentle tug on the satin ribbon releases the concealed neodymium magnets with a satisfying resistance — then the lid swings open like a fine book cover, revealing the velvet-flocked interior. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. — an ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certified factory with 22 years of rigid box expertise (founded 2003) — each box is hand-assembled by our 80+ skilled craftspeople using precision CNC-wrapped greyboard (1200-1600gsm), art paper (157gsm), and 3M-grade neodymium magnets rated for 10,000+ open/close cycles. Our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility (10,000+8,000 smart factory) with the 2025 8,000 sqm smart factory expansion produces 220,000+ rigid gift boxes daily.

Design Features

  • Single-Door Architecture: The lid hinges along one edge and opens like a book cover — more dramatic than a two-piece lift-off lid, and less complex (lower cost) than a double-door design.
  • Concealed Neodymium Magnets: Four N35-grade magnets (8mm × 3mm) embedded in the board thickness — invisible from the exterior, delivering 1.2 kg holding force per pair. Rated for 10,000+ open/close cycles without loss of magnetism.
  • Satin Ribbon Pull Tab: 25mm double-face satin ribbon in matching or contrasting color — hand-sewn through the board with reinforced stitching, tested to 15 kg pull strength.
  • Velvet Flocking Interior: Electrostatic flocking process applies 0.5-1.0mm nylon fibers to a PU adhesive base — creates a non-abrasive, light-absorbing surface that cradles products and eliminates micro-scratches.
  • Precision Wrapped Edges: 4-sided CNC edge wrapping with 157gsm art paper — zero exposed greyboard, sharp 90° corners with ±0.3mm tolerance.

Applications

  • Luxury Apparel: Silk scarves, ties, gloves, and accessories presented in a box that matches their refinement.
  • Premium Corporate Gifts: Executive welcome kits, milestone awards, and VIP client gifts with debossed/foil-stamped company logo.
  • Watch & Jewelry: Single timepiece or jewelry piece display with custom flocked insert tray.
  • Stationery Sets: Fountain pens, leather journal sets, and desk accessories — the book-like opening feels natural for writing instruments.
  • Gourmet Food: Artisan chocolate collections, premium tea sets, and limited-edition confectionery.

Production Specifications

  • Greyboard Preparation: 1200-1600gsm sheets CNC-cut to ±0.2mm, groove-scored for hinge flexibility
  • Wrapping: 157gsm art paper precision-wrapped on semi-automatic casing-in machines with PVA adhesive
  • Magnet Embedding: N35 neodymium magnets set into CNC-routed cavities, secured with hot-melt adhesive
  • Flocking: 40kV electrostatic flocking booth — 0.5-1.0mm nylon fibers applied at 100g/m² density
  • Assembly: Manual hand-assembly by 80+ skilled craftspeople, each box inspected for magnet alignment, edge sharpness, and ribbon stitching integrity
  • QC: Magnetic pull force test (>1.0 kg), 200-cycle open/close endurance test, ribbon pull test (>10 kg), rub resistance ASTM D5264
  • Factory: 18,000 sqm (10,000+8,000 smart factory), 8 production lines, 220,000+ rigid boxes daily
  • Lead Time: 7-10 days sample, 18-22 days mass production
