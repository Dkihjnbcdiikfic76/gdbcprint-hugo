Custom Printed Corrugated Mailer Box Self-Locking E-commerce

June 10, 2026

Product Overview

A custom printed corrugated mailer box with a self-locking crash-lock bottom — designed for e-commerce brands that need branded unboxing experiences without tape or assembly complexity. Manufactured at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., this mailer pops open and locks into shape in under 2 seconds, slashing fulfillment center packing time. Printed with water-based flexo inks on single-wall corrugated in B, C, or E flute for the ideal balance of protection, print quality, and shipping weight.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Flute: B-flute (3mm, crush resistant), C-flute (4mm, heavy items), E-flute (1.5mm, fine print)
  • Liner: Kraft (brown), white top (high-quality print), test liner (economy), FSC-certified options
  • Printing: KBA flexographic press — up to 4 colors, water-based inks
  • Self-locking bottom: Die-cut crash-lock mechanism, snaps into place, holds up to 5kg
  • Daily corrugated output: 500,000 boxes across dedicated corrugated line
  • Wall construction: Single-wall (standard), double-wall (heavy duty)
  • Closure: Self-locking + optional tear strip for easy customer opening

Customization Options

  • Size: From 150mm × 100mm × 50mm to 600mm × 400mm × 300mm
  • Flute: B (everyday shipping), C (fragile/heavy items), E (luxury/retail presentation), BC double-wall (maximum protection)
  • Print: 1-4 color flexo, inside print (branded interior surprise), outside only, or both sides
  • Liner: Kraft (natural brown), white top (vibrant colors), mottled (speckled), coated white (offset-quality)
  • Features: Tear strip, perforated return label flap, die-cut handle, ventilation holes
  • Inserts: Corrugated dividers, die-cut foam, molded pulp trays

Applications

Engineered for: subscription box fulfillment, DTC e-commerce shipping, apparel delivery, beauty product kits, meal kit packaging, electronics shipping, book mailers, and Amazon FBA-compliant branded packaging. The self-locking bottom eliminates tape and speeds up packing station throughput.

Production Quality

In-line QC includes: flute crush test (ECT per ASTM D5639), bursting strength (Mullen test), glue bond integrity, print registration check, die-cut accuracy (±1mm), and flat crush resistance. All boxes tested for transit simulation if requested.
