Custom Sleeve-Style Folding Carton for Premium Chocolate Confectionery

June 11, 2026

Custom sleeve-style folding carton for premium chocolate and confectionery packaging — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). The sleeve-style design wraps around a tray or inner box and slides open to reveal the product, delivering a luxury unboxing experience perfect for artisan chocolate bars, truffle collections, and premium confectionery sets. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across 10,000 sqm factory floor. Daily output 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • Artisan chocolate bar and tablet packaging
  • Premium truffle and praline gift boxes
  • Luxury confectionery and candy gift sets
  • Seasonal holiday chocolate collections
  • Wedding favor and celebration chocolate boxes

Customization Options

  • Sleeve-style sliding design with integrated inner tray
  • Hot foil stamping in gold, silver, copper, or custom metallic
  • Soft-touch matte lamination for premium tactile feel
  • Spot UV on logo, cocoa pod pattern, or brand motif
  • Die-cut window in sleeve for product preview
  • Embossing and debossing for textured brand identity
  • Food-grade inks and water-based coatings (FDA-compliant)
  • Custom sizes from 80×80×15mm single-bar to 300×200×50mm multi-piece sets

Production Specifications

  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press for high-speed CMYK printing
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV press for specialty coatings and instant curing
  • Fully automatic die-cutting and folder-gluer lines
  • Board: 250gsm–400gsm SBS (Solid Bleached Sulfate) for food-grade purity
  • MOQ: 1000 units per design; lead time: 12–18 working days
