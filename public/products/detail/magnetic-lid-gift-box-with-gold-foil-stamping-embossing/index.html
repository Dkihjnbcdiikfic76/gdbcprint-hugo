Magnetic Lid Gift Box with Gold Foil Stamping and Embossing

June 10, 2026

Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s magnetic lid gift box combines gold foil stamping with deep embossing to create tactile brand presence. The raised embossed logo or pattern catches light and invites touch, while gold foil adds warmth and luxury. Rigid 2.5mm board construction with magnetic lid closure and custom satin or velvet interior. Manufactured in our ISO 9001:2015 certified 10,000 sqm factory — 22 years, Heidelberg 7+1 UV + KBA 1050 presses, 1.7M daily output.

Applications

  • Luxury Corporate Gifts: Executive year-end gifts, VIP client appreciation, milestone celebration
  • Premium Cosmetics: Skincare sets, fragrance gift collections, limited edition launches
  • Jewelry & Accessories: Necklace, bracelet, cufflink presentation boxes
  • Watches: Single and multi-watch presentation with custom pillow inserts
  • Tech & Gadgets: Premium earphones, smart accessories, limited edition tech products
  • Wine & Spirits: Collector’s bottle packaging, tasting set boxes, vintage presentation
  • Wedding & Events: Luxury favor boxes, bridal party gifts, table number holders

Customization Options

  • Box Style: Two-piece magnetic lid, book-style with magnetic flap, drawer with magnetic catch
  • Sizes: Fully custom — 80×80×40mm to 450×350×180mm
  • Exterior: Art paper (matte/gloss), leatherette, linen, velvet paper, pearlescent, holographic
  • Foil Colors: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper, holographic, matte gold, brushed metal
  • Embossing: Single-level, multi-level sculpted, blind emboss (no foil), registered emboss
  • Interior: Velvet flocking, satin lining, suede, die-cut foam/EVA, cardboard tray with ribbon
  • Printing: CMYK offset + Pantone spot, full flood color, pattern, gradient
  • Magnetic Lid: Hidden neodymium magnets, full-perimeter magnetic strip, or corner magnets
