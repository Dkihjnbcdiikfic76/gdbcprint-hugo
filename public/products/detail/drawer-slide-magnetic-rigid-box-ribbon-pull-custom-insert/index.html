Drawer Slide Magnetic Rigid Box Ribbon Pull Custom Insert

June 10, 2026

Luxury drawer-slide magnetic rigid box with satin ribbon pull-tab and custom die-cut foam insert — the premium unboxing format for high-value products. The smooth sliding action, magnetic lock, and ribbon pull create an elegant reveal experience. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.

Applications

  • Luxury Watches: Watch presentation with custom-fit watch pillow; branded drawer compartments for straps and accessories
  • Fine Jewelry: Necklace, bracelet, and ring sets in layered trays; pull-out reveals multi-level organization
  • Premium Electronics: Earphones, smart accessories, portable tech; foam insert molded to device contours
  • Executive Pens & Writing Instruments: Single or multi-pen presentation; branded interior with ink cartridge storage
  • Luxury Spirits & Cigars: Single-bottle presentation with accessory drawer; cigar humidor-style presentation box
  • Branded Merchandise: High-end promotional kits, influencer gift boxes, VIP customer appreciation packages
  • Limited-Edition Collectibles: Sneakers, designer toys (art toys), fashion accessories, commemorative coins/medals

Customization Options

  • Drawer Configuration: Single drawer (slide-out from sleeve), double drawer (slide from both ends), stacked multi-drawer (2–4 levels), drawer with flip-lid top compartment
  • Slide Mechanism: Finger-jointed board rails for smooth friction fit; optional metal ball-bearing rails for heavy contents (500g+)
  • Magnetic Lock: N52 magnets at drawer rear engage at full insertion for satisfying “click” stop; prevents accidental opening
  • Ribbon Pull: 15mm/25mm satin ribbon in 20+ colors; leather-look pull tab, metal ring pull, fabric loop, or custom die-cut pull
  • Outer Wrap: 128–200gsm art paper (matte, gloss, soft-touch, linen, pearlescent, metallic); faux leather paper, textured woodgrain, carbon fiber
  • Printing: CMYK + PMS spot colors, hot foil stamping (gold/silver/rose gold/holographic), embossing/debossing, spot UV, full-surface glitter UV
  • Interior Insert: Die-cut EVA/PU foam (any cavity configuration), velvet-flocked foam, molded pulp (eco option), layered fabric-covered trays
  • Size: From 150×80×40mm (pen/pocket item) to 400×300×120mm (multi-watch / large gift set)

Production Specifications

  • Drawer Construction: Precision-scored greyboard with finger-jointed corners; reinforced rail edges for smooth sliding; 5,000+ open/close cycle tested
  • Sleeve Construction: 4-sided wrap-around sleeve with precision glue seam; ±0.5mm dimensional tolerance for consistent slide fit
  • Magnet Integration: Drawer-end and sleeve-interior magnet placement; 2–6 magnets per unit; pull force 0.5–3.0 kgf per pair
  • Foam Insert: CNC knife-cut EVA/PU foam with ±0.2mm cavity tolerance; firm density (45–70 Shore A) for product grip; anti-yellowing formulation for white/light foams
  • Ribbon Attachment: Reinforced heat-sealed ribbon tab inserted and glued between board layers; pull-strength tested to 5 kgf
  • KBA 1050: Outer wrap and sleeve printing at 15,000 sheets/hour
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV: Multi-color + UV coating in one pass
  • Lead Time: 15–20 working days (complex assembly); 7–10 days for repeat orders
  • Daily Capacity: 20,000–35,000 drawer-slide boxes

Quality Details

  • ISO 9001:2015 — full QC documentation at every production stage
  • FSC Chain-of-Custody certified
  • Slide Fit: 100% functional test — drawer must slide smoothly without excessive resistance or looseness
  • Magnet Engagement: 100% magnetic lock verification; audible/kinetic “click” feedback on every unit
  • Drop Test: ISTA 1A protocol — drawer must remain closed; contents protected
  • Ribbon Pull Strength: Destructive pull test on sampling basis — minimum 5 kgf before failure
  • Print Alignment: ±0.1mm foil-to-print registration; D50 light booth final inspection
  • Free pre-production sample with full decoration — 7–10 working days
