Luxury watch and jewelry rigid paper box with magnetic flap closure — the definitive premium packaging for timepiece and fine jewelry brands. Manufactured at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a 22-year specialist in rigid luxury boxes (founded 2003). Our 10,000 sqm factory in Foshan operates KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses with Bobst foil stamping, producing 220,000+ rigid boxes daily. ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified. Every box features precision N52 magnetic flap closure, plush velvet or suede interior, and custom watch/jewelry cushion inserts.

Applications

  • Luxury watch packaging for retail and e-commerce
  • Wristwatch display boxes for boutiques and trade shows
  • Jewelry set packaging (necklace + earrings + bracelet + ring)
  • High-end corporate gift watch sets
  • Limited-edition collector watch presentation
  • Premium smartwatch and wearable tech packaging
  • Fine jewelry brand countertop display
  • Wedding party gift packaging (groomsmen watches, bridesmaid jewelry)

Customization Options

  • Exterior Wrapping: 128–200gsm art paper (matte, gloss, soft-touch, linen-textured, pearlescent, metallic), PU leatherette, genuine wood veneer laminate
  • Foil Stamping: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper, matte gold, holographic — single or multi-level with blind emboss/deboss
  • Magnetic Flap: N52 neodymium magnets (6–12mm), hidden between board layers; single, dual, or full-perimeter magnetic frame
  • Interior Lining: Velvet flocking (12+ colors), microfiber suede, satin, custom-printed art paper
  • Watch Cushion: Velvet-covered foam pillow, die-cut EVA with flocking, removable watch holder, snap-in bracelet channel
  • Jewelry Insert: Multi-compartment die-cut foam tray — individual cavities for rings, earrings, necklaces, bracelets
  • Additional Features: LED light module (battery, activates on open), mirror inside lid, printed care/warranty card slot
  • Edge Finishing: Gold/silver gilding, colored edge painting, ribbon pull-tab
  • Size: Single watch (120×90×70mm) to multi-piece set (350×280×100mm)

Production Specifications

  • Presses: KBA Rapida 105 4-color (15,000 sheets/hr) + Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV
  • Foil Stamping: Bobst SP 102 BMA — ±0.15mm registration, multi-zone temperature control, holographic foil capability
  • Rigid Board: Greyboard 1200–1600gsm, double-wrapped, corner-taped for structural integrity
  • Magnet Integration: Machine-pressed N52 neodymium magnets; polarity sensor verification on-line; pull-force 0.8–2.5 kgF
  • Velvet Flocking: Electrostatic flocking — 0.8–1.5mm polyester fiber pile; adhesive cured at 55–65°C
  • Assembly: Fully automatic rigid box line — wrapping, flocking, magnet insertion, hinge assembly, cushion placement
  • Daily Capacity: 220,000+ rigid boxes total; 30,000–50,000 dedicated watch/jewelry box capacity
  • Lead Time: 10–15 working days new tooling; 7–10 days repeat orders

Quality Assurance

  • ISO 9001:2015 — documented quality checkpoints throughout production
  • FSC Chain-of-Custody — sustainable paper and board, certificate available per order
  • Magnet Alignment: 100% inspection — flap closes flush ≤0.3mm gap on all sides
  • Hinge Test: 5,000 open/close cycles — zero looseness, cracking, or delamination
  • Foil Durability: Sutherland rub test — 50 double rubs, 4-lb weight, zero visible wear
  • Velvet Adhesion: Accelerated aging (72h at 57°C / 95% RH) + 500-cycle rub test — zero peeling
  • Cushion Fit: ±0.5mm cavity tolerance for watch and jewelry insert precision
  • Odor Testing: VOC compliance per GB/T — safe for enclosed display cases and direct handling
  • Free pre-production sample with full decoration and your watch/jewelry dimensions — 5–7 working days
