Magnetic Closure Rigid Box with Ribbon Pull for Luxury Stationery Sets

June 11, 2026

Sophisticated magnetic closure rigid box with satin ribbon pull tab — thoughtfully designed for luxury stationery sets, premium desk accessories, and executive corporate gifts. Crafted at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003), our 18,000 sqm Foshan factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) operates KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, producing 1.7 million boxes daily. ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified. The integrated ribbon pull-tab provides effortless lid opening while N52 neodymium magnets deliver a refined closure.

Applications

  • Luxury writing paper and stationery gift sets
  • Premium desk accessory and organizer packaging
  • Executive pen, business card holder, and notebook combo sets
  • High-end office supply and corporate welcome kits
  • Wedding and event stationery suites
  • Bespoke personalized stationery packaging

Customization Options

  • Box Style: Book-style flap, two-piece lid-and-base, drawer-slide, clamshell with ribbon hinge
  • Exterior Wrapping: 128–200gsm art paper (matte, gloss, soft-touch velvet, linen-textured, pearlescent, metallic)
  • Foil Stamping: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper — multi-level emboss/deboss combination
  • Finishes: Matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch coating, spot UV, aqueous varnish
  • Ribbon: 15–25mm double-face satin in 20+ colors (Pantone matched), grosgrain or velvet ribbon options, bow-tie closure variant
  • Magnets: N52 neodymium (6–10mm), dual-perimeter strip for 300×250mm+ sizes
  • Interior: Die-cut EVA foam compartments, flocked tray inserts, custom-printed paper dividers, elastic pen loops, certificate/document pocket
  • Size: 200×150×40mm to 350×280×60mm multi-compartment sets
  • Accessories: Removable drawer trays, magnetic accessory compartments, ribbon placeholders

Production Specifications

  • Press: KBA Rapida 105 4-color at 15,000 sheets/hour + Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV
  • Board: Rigid greyboard 1200–1600gsm with precision double-wrapped construction
  • Foil Stamping: Bobst SP 102 BMA with ±0.15mm registration, multi-zone temperature control
  • Ribbon Integration: Heat-sealed between board and wrapping paper — zero adhesive bleed
  • Assembly: Automatic rigid box line — corner taping, wrapping, magnet insertion, ribbon placement
  • Daily Capacity: 220,000+ rigid magnetic closure boxes
  • Lead Time: 10–15 working days new tooling; 7–10 days repeat orders

Quality Assurance

  • Ribbon Pull Test: 5kgF sustained load — zero detachment or fraying
  • Magnet Alignment: 100% inspection — lid closes flush ≤0.3mm gap
  • Hinge Durability: 5,000 open/close cycles — zero looseness or delamination
  • Foil Adhesion: Sutherland rub test — 50 double rubs with 4-lb weight, no wear
  • Ribbon Colorfastness: Xenon arc test 40 hours — ≤4.0 ΔE color shift
  • ISO 9001:2015 documented quality gates at every stage
  • FSC Chain-of-Custody certified sustainable materials
  • Free pre-production samples in 5–7 working days

Factory

  • 22 years of manufacturing since 2003 in Foshan, Guangdong, China
  • 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) (commissioned 2025)
  • 80+ skilled employees across design, prepress, printing, and finishing
  • KBA 1050 4-color + Heidelberg 7+1 UV + Bobst foil stamping + automatic rigid box line
  • Daily capacity: 1.7 million color boxes + 220,000+ rigid gift boxes
