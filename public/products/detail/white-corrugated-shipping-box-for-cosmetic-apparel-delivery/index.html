White Corrugated Shipping Box for Cosmetic Apparel Delivery

June 10, 2026

Premium white corrugated shipping box engineered for cosmetic and apparel e-commerce delivery — by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003), a 22-year full-chain packaging manufacturer. 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) with KBA 1050 4-color offset and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, integrated corrugated production line. Daily output 1.7 million boxes. ISO 9001 & FSC certified. Custom sizes, branded printing, and ISTA-tested structural integrity.

Applications

  • Cosmetic and skincare product e-commerce shipping
  • Apparel, fashion, and clothing brand mailer packaging
  • Beauty subscription box monthly delivery packaging
  • Fragrance and perfume bottle protective shipping
  • Luxury fashion accessory (scarves, ties, belts) mailer boxes
  • Direct-to-consumer (DTC) brand branded outbound packaging
  • Boutique retail restocking and wholesale order fulfillment
  • Influencer PR kit and media sample mailing boxes

Customization Options

  • Flute Profile: B-flute (3mm, crush resistant for heavy items), C-flute (4mm, all-purpose), E-flute (1.5mm, premium print surface), or double-wall BC-flute (7mm, heavy-duty)
  • Board Grade: Kraft liner (brown), white top kraft liner, white duplex (coated print surface), or full bleached white throughout
  • Printing: Flexographic (1–4 color) for cost-effective branding; offset litho-laminated (full CMYK + Pantone) for premium print quality
  • Structure: Self-locking crash-lock bottom, tuck-top with dust flaps, roll-end tuck-top (RETT), or telescoping two-piece design
  • Closure: Integrated tear strip for tool-free opening; adhesive strip with release liner; perforated zip-open panel
  • Coatings: Aqueous coating, UV spot varnish, matte/gloss lamination, or soft-touch coating for premium unboxing
  • Add-Ons: Die-cut handle, ventilation holes, tamper-evident seals, QR code printing, inside printing (brand messaging or return instructions)
  • Size Range: Compact 150×100×50mm (single cosmetics item) to 600×400×400mm (bulk apparel order)

Production Specifications

  • KBA Rapida 105 4-color: Offset litho-laminated printing at 15,000 sheets/hour for premium white-top graphics
  • Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV: Specialty coatings, metallic inks, and instant curing
  • Corrugator: In-line single-face and double-backer production for B/C/E-flute board; FSC-certified paper rolls
  • Die-Cutting: Bobst SPO flatbed die-cutter — ±0.5mm registration on corrugated; custom cutting dies
  • Folder-Gluer: Bobst Masterfold automatic — crash-lock, 4/6-corner, and auto-bottom configurations
  • Tear Strip: Hot-melt applied reinforced tear tape — 3mm or 6mm width; positioned at specified distance from edge
  • Lead Time: 7–12 working days for standard configurations; 12–18 days with offset litho-lamination
  • Daily Capacity: 100,000–300,000 corrugated mailers per day

Quality & Testing

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified — quality gates at flute formation, lamination, die-cutting, gluing, and final inspection
  • FSC Chain-of-Custody — certified sustainable fiber throughout the supply chain
  • Edge Crush Test (ECT): 32–44 lb/in (B-flute single-wall) per TAPPI T-811 for stacking strength
  • Bursting Strength: 200–275 PSI (single-wall) per TAPPI T-810 for puncture resistance
  • Box Compression Test (BCT): Tested per ISTA 1A/3A — adequate for 6-high warehouse stacking
  • Drop Test: ISTA 1A — 10-drop sequence from 760mm (30") with no structural failure or product damage
  • Tear Strip: Consistent tear initiation and propagation — tested at 100% on-line
  • Print Registration: ±0.5mm on flexo; ±0.2mm on offset litho-laminated
  • Moisture Content: 6.5–8.5% per TAPPI T-412 for dimensional stability in shipping
  • Free pre-production sample — 5–7 working day turnaround

FAQ

Q: What is the minimum order quantity for custom white corrugated shipping boxes?

A: Standard MOQ is 500–1000 pcs. For B/C-flute single-wall boxes with flexographic printing, we accept 500 pcs trial orders. Offset litho-laminated boxes (premium white-top with CMYK) start at 1000 pcs. We offer free pre-production samples (5–7 working days, freight collect).

Q: Can you print my brand logo on white corrugated mailer boxes?

A: Yes, with flexographic (1–4 color, cost-effective for simple logos) or offset litho-lamination (full CMYK + Pantone for photographic-quality branding). Our KBA 1050 4-color offset press runs at 15,000 sheets/hour on white-top kraft liner. B-flute and E-flute both support branded printing; C-flute single-wall also available. MOQ for branded boxes: 500 pcs.

Q: Are these corrugated boxes ISTA-tested for e-commerce shipping?

A: Yes, all our corrugated mailers undergo ISTA 1A (10-drop sequence from 760mm) and ISTA 3A (parcel delivery simulation) testing. We perform Edge Crush Test (32–44 lb/in for single-wall), Bursting Strength (200–275 PSI), and Box Compression Test. Self-locking bottom structures and tear strip closures are 100% functionally tested on-line.

Q: What is the lead time for custom corrugated shipping boxes?

A: Standard configurations (flexo-printed, B/C-flute single-wall): 7–12 working days. Offset litho-laminated configurations (white-top with CMYK): 12–18 working days. Repeat orders with existing tooling: 5–7 working days. We ship worldwide via DHL/FedEx/UPS (express) or sea freight (economy) from our 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) in Foshan, Guangdong.
