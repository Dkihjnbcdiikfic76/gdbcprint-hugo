Custom Printed Corrugated Mailer Box E-Commerce Branded

Custom printed corrugated mailer box for e-commerce branding — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). 10,000 sqm factory with KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses. Daily output 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • E-commerce and DTC brand shipping
  • Subscription box services (beauty, snack, lifestyle)
  • Retail product mailers and direct-to-consumer fulfillment
  • Promotional and limited-edition kit packaging
  • Small electronics and accessory shipping
  • Apparel, cosmetics, and gift mailers

Customization Options

  • Self-locking crash-lock bottom for fast assembly — no tape needed
  • Integrated tear strip for easy consumer unboxing experience
  • Full CMYK + Pantone offset printing on white top kraft liner
  • Inside printing for branded unboxing moments
  • Die-cut handles, thumb holes, or window cutouts
  • Single-wall (B/C-flute) or double-wall (BC-flute) construction
  • Custom sizes from small jewelry mailers to large apparel boxes

Production Specifications

  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press with precise registration
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV press for high-impact brand graphics
  • Corrugator with B-flute, C-flute, E-flute, and BC-flute options
  • Auto die-cutting with custom steel rule dies
  • Flexo folder-gluer for high-volume standard mailers
  • White top kraft liner for superior print quality on brown board
  • Water-based and soy-based ink options for eco-conscious brands
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system

Quality Details

  • Edge crush test (ECT) and burst strength (Mullen) verified per order
  • Consistent glue bonding on crash-lock and auto-bottom constructions
  • Flat shipping — mailers store and ship knocked-down, assemble in seconds

  • 100% dimensional check against approved dieline before production
  • MOQ 500–1000 units, scalable to container-load volumes
  • Free pre-production samples with 5–7 working day turnaround
