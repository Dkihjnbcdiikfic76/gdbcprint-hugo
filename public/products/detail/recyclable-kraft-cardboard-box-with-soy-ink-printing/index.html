Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s recyclable kraft cardboard box with soy ink printing represents the purest expression of sustainable packaging. Every component — from the FSC certified kraft paperboard and recycled greyboard core to the soy-based inks and water-based adhesives — is chosen for end-of-life recyclability and biodegradability. Zero plastic. Zero solvent emissions. Full compostability in industrial facilities. Manufactured in our ISO 9001:2015 certified 10,000 sqm factory backed by 22 years of expertise.

Applications

  • Organic & Natural Brands: Skincare, supplements, essential oils — packaging that matches brand values
  • Food & Beverage: Artisan chocolate, specialty coffee, tea, baked goods with food-safe soy inks
  • Eco-conscious E-commerce: Sustainable DTC shipping boxes with full brand printing
  • Corporate Sustainability Kits: Employee welcome kits, ESG report packaging, green initiative giveaways
  • Farmers Market & Artisan Goods: Honey, jam, candles, handmade soaps, craft goods
  • Subscription Boxes: Zero-waste beauty boxes, organic snack subscriptions, eco-living kits
  • Wedding & Event: Rustic kraft aesthetic for eco-weddings, green conferences, sustainability summits

Customization Options

  • Styles: Tuck-top, mailer, drawer box, pillow box, gable box, sleeve box, two-piece rigid
  • Sizes: Fully custom — from 50×50×20mm (jewelry) to 400×300×150mm (gift sets)
  • Materials: 100% recycled kraft (post-consumer), FSC virgin kraft, grass paper, bagasse (sugarcane fiber) board
  • Board Thickness: 1.0mm to 3.0mm greyboard or solid kraft board
  • Printing: Soy-based CMYK offset on Heidelberg 7+1 UV press; Pantone spot colors available
  • Finishes: Blind embossing/debossing (no foil — maintains recyclability), aqueous matte coating, registered emboss
  • Inserts: Die-cut kraft card inserts, molded pulp trays (compostable), corrugated kraft dividers
  • Closure: Natural cotton ribbon tie, paper twine, kraft paper sticker seal, interlocking tuck

Quality Assurance

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified — documented QC at every production stage
  • Incoming material: FSC chain-of-custody verification, basis weight, moisture content (6–8% target)
  • Print quality: spectrophotometer color density per 500 sheets, registration tolerance ±0.15mm
  • Structural: box compression test (BCT), drop test (ISTA 1A), glue bond strength
  • Final inspection: AQL 2.5 Level II, 100% functional test on closures and fit
  • Environmental claims verification: third-party recyclability and compostability certification available
  • Lab testing partnerships: SGS, Intertek, TÜV Rheinland

Sustainability Credentials

  • FSC Mix Credit: Full chain-of-custody — on-pack FSC logo available
  • Soy Inks: USDA BioPreferred, biodegradable, heavy-metal-free, lower VOC than petroleum inks
  • Recycled Core: Greyboard with ≥85% post-consumer recycled content
  • Water-Based Adhesives: Zero VOC emissions, repulpable in standard recycling streams
  • Plastic-Free: No lamination film, no synthetic coatings, no plastic windows
  • Industrial Compostable: EN 13432 certified upon request (full board + ink decomposition within 90 days)
  • Carbon Footprint: Approximately 40% lower than conventional plastic-laminated rigid box equivalent
