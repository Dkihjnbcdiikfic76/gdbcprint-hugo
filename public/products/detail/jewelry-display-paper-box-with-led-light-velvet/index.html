Premium jewelry display paper box with integrated LED light and velvet interior — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of manufacturing excellence (founded 2003) and Diamond Member on Made-in-China.com since 2015. The integrated LED lighting transforms a standard jewelry box into an illuminated display case, dramatically enhancing product presentation at retail counters, trade shows, and e-commerce unboxing moments. Produced across 18,000 sqm of factory space (10,000 + 8,000 sqm smart factory, 2025 expansion) on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 7+1 UV offset presses. Daily output: 1.7 million color boxes + 220,000+ rigid gift boxes. ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, and REACH compliant.

Applications

  • Necklace & Pendant Display: Premium presentation boxes with necklace hooks, bust inserts, or flat display trays with LED highlighting the pendant section
  • Ring Boxes: Single and multi-ring display boxes with LED spotlight on each ring slot; cushion or foam ring holders with flocking
  • Bracelet & Bangle Packaging: Long-format display boxes with bracelet bar inserts and perimeter LED lighting for 360° presentation
  • Earring Display: Stud earring card holders with LED backlight; drop earring hanging trays with top-mounted LED
  • Watch Presentation: Luxury watch boxes with LED-lit watch pillow and compartment for additional straps
  • Jewelry Sets: Multi-compartment boxes for necklace + earring + ring sets with individually lit sections
  • Retail Counter Display: Freestanding or stackable display boxes designed for jewelry store counters and luxury boutique presentation

Customization Options

  • LED Lighting System: Battery-powered (CR2032/AAA lithium) LED module with 20,000+ hour lifespan; touch-activated (press top or lid) or magnetic reed switch auto-on when opened; warm white (3000K), cool white (6000K), or RGB color options; single-point spotlight, perimeter strip, or full-base illumination
  • Interior Material: Velvet flocking (black, navy, burgundy, grey, cream), satin-lined cavities with die-cut foam inserts, suede-like microfiber lining, or molded EVA foam with flocked surface; custom-fit cutouts for specific jewelry shapes
  • Exterior Finish: Full-color CMYK offset printing with Pantone matching; hot foil stamping (gold, silver, rose gold); embossing and debossing for brand logos; matte or gloss lamination; soft-touch velvet coating; wrapped art paper (157–250gsm)
  • Structure: Two-piece lid-and-base, book-style hinge, magnetic flap closure, or drawer-style; window cutout with clear PET or UV-resistant acrylic for visible product display; custom dimensions from 70×70×30mm (ring box) to 300×200×60mm (necklace set)
