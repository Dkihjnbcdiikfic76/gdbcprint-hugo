Magnetic Gift Box for Premium Electronics — Custom Rigid Packaging

June 11, 2026

Product Overview

Bincai’s magnetic rigid gift box for premium electronics is designed for brands that demand unboxing excellence. The precision-cut EVA foam insert cradles headphones, smartwatches, earbuds, or accessories with zero movement during transit. A soft-touch matte finish and strong magnetic closure deliver the tactile premium experience consumers expect. Manufactured in our Foshan smart factory with Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset printing.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Press: Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset — vibrant solids, sharp text on dark backgrounds
  • Board: 2.0mm greyboard core, 157gsm art paper wrap
  • Magnetic Closure: Dual N35 neodymium magnets, concealed beneath wrap layer
  • Insert: CNC-cut EVA foam (black/white/grey), precision-fit cavities
  • Soft-Touch Finish: Aqueous soft-touch coating, fingerprint-resistant
  • Daily Output: 220,000+ gift boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody
