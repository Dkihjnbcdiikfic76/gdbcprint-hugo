Book Style Rigid Gift Box with Magnetic Flap Custom Print

June 10, 2026

|

Premium book style rigid gift box with magnetic flap custom print — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). 10,000 sqm factory with KBA and Heidelberg presses. Daily output 1.5 million boxes.

Applications

Book-style rigid boxes open like a hardcover book, creating an elegant unboxing experience that enhances brand perception. Ideal for:

  • Luxury Cosmetics & Fragrances: Perfume sets, skincare collections, makeup palettes
  • Premium Electronics: Smartphones, watches, headphones, accessories
  • Corporate Gifts: Executive notebooks, pen sets, award presentations
  • Jewelry & Watches: High-end timepieces, fine jewelry collections, cufflinks
  • Wine & Spirits: Premium bottle presentation boxes with internal inserts
  • Confectionery: Luxury chocolate collections, artisanal gift sets

Customization Options

  • Materials: 1200-1800 gsm greyboard core wrapped in 157-250 gsm art paper, kraft paper, specialty textured paper, or leatherette
  • Printing: Full-color CMYK offset on KBA 1050 4-color press; up to 7 colors + UV on Heidelberg 7+1 press
  • Finishing: Gold/silver/copper hot foil stamping, blind embossing/debossing, spot UV varnish, matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch coating
  • Closure: Hidden magnetic flap, ribbon pull-tab, metal snap buttons, or elastic band
  • Interior: Custom die-cut foam inserts, velvet/satin lining, vacuum-formed trays, ribbon pulls, card slots
  • Size: Fully custom dimensions — from compact jewelry boxes to large format gift boxes up to 450×350mm

Production Specifications

  • Press: KBA 1050 4-color offset + Heidelberg Speedmaster 7+1 UV
  • Die-Cutting: Automatic Bobst-style die-cutting with ±0.5mm precision
  • Gluing: Automatic rigid box forming lines with inline corner taping
  • Assembly: Hand-finishing station for premium detailing (ribbon threading, insert placement)
  • Daily Capacity: 200,000+ rigid gift boxes per day
  • Lead Time: 10–15 working days for standard orders; 7–10 days for repeat orders
  • MOQ: 500–1000 units; trial orders and small batches welcome

Quality Assurance

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system — every batch traceable
  • FSC Chain of Custody certified — responsible forest sourcing
  • Incoming Inspection: Paper weight, thickness, moisture content verified on arrival
  • Inline QC: Color density checks every 500 sheets, glue strength testing every hour
  • Final Inspection: AQL 2.5 sampling standard; dimensional tolerance ±1.5mm; color delta E < 2.0
  • Drop Testing: ISTA 1A simulated drop tests for structural integrity
  • Free pre-production sample with full finishing (freight collect); mass production only after sample approval
