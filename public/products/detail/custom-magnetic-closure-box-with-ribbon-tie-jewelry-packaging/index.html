Custom Magnetic Closure Box with Ribbon Tie Jewelry Packaging

June 10, 2026

Product Overview

An elegant magnetic closure box with a satin ribbon tie — the signature packaging solution for high-end jewelry brands. The concealed N52-grade neodymium magnets deliver a satisfying snap-shut action while the ribbon tie adds a tactile unboxing experience. Manufactured at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s 18,000 sqm facility with Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses and 22 years of rigid box expertise.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Press: Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset — crisp whites, deep blacks, vibrant Pantone
  • Board: Rigid greyboard 1200–1600gsm, double-wrapped construction
  • Magnets: N52-grade neodymium, 6–10mm diameter, concealed between board layers
  • Ribbon: 15–25mm satin or grosgrain, pre-tied bow or wrap-around closure
  • Daily output: 200K+ rigid boxes, including magnetic closure variants
  • Q/C: 100% magnet alignment check, closure force test, drop test

Customization Options

  • Sizing: 100 × 80 × 40mm to 400 × 300 × 100mm
  • Exterior: CMYK offset, full-coverage Pantone, textured paper, leatherette wrap
  • Finishes: Soft-touch, matte/gloss lamination, spot UV, aqueous varnish
  • Foil stamping: Gold, silver, copper, rose gold, holographic — single or multi-level
  • Interior: Velvet flocking, satin lining, custom-printed paper, die-cut EVA foam tray
  • Closure style: Single magnet, double magnet strip, full-perimeter magnetic frame
  • Ribbon options: Color-matched, contrast, embossed logo, wax-seal charm

Applications

Fine jewelry, engagement rings, luxury watches, bracelets, earring sets, bridal party favors, high-end cosmetics, premium corporate gifts, and boutique retail packaging.
