Hexagonal Rigid Paper Box with Custom Foam Insert Luxury

June 10, 2026

Description

The hexagonal rigid paper box with custom foam insert stands apart from standard rectangular packaging — the distinctive six-sided geometry creates instant shelf differentiation and a premium unboxing experience that customers remember. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003), on KBA 1050 4-color offset presses and Heidelberg 7+1 UV press in our 10,000 sqm ISO 9001:2015 & FSC certified factory. Daily output 1.7 million boxes.

The hexagonal shape requires precision die-cutting and case-making expertise — our specialized hexagonal wrapping and assembly lines ensure consistent geometry across production runs, with tight tolerances on all six panels. The custom EVA or PU foam insert is precision die-cut to cradle your product securely, preventing movement during transit while showcasing it elegantly upon opening. Available with magnetic closure, ribbon pull, or friction-fit lid options.

Features

  • Distinctive Hexagonal Shape — Six-sided geometry differentiates your product on retail shelves and in unboxing content; ideal for brands seeking unique packaging identity
  • Rigid 2mm Greyboard Construction — Dense 800–1600 gsm greyboard wrapped in art paper, specialty stock, or fabric for luxury weight and feel
  • Custom Die-Cut Foam Insert — Precision-cut EVA or PU foam tailored to your product dimensions; available in black, white, charcoal, or custom colors with velvet flocking
  • KBA 1050 4-Color Offset Printing — High-speed CMYK with Pantone spot color matching for vibrant, brand-accurate reproduction on hexagonal panels
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV Coating — Soft-touch, gloss, or matte UV finishes with inline curing for premium tactility and scratch resistance
  • Magnetic Closure Option — Hidden neodymium magnets for a clean exterior profile and satisfying snap-close action
  • Hot Foil Stamping — Gold, silver, rose gold, or holographic foil on lid, base, or individual panels
  • Embossing & Debossing — Dimensional textures for logos, patterns, and decorative elements across hexagonal surfaces
  • Sustainable Materials — FSC-certified greyboard and art paper; fully recyclable; soy-based inks available
  • Low MOQ — 500–1000 unit minimum with trial orders accepted; free pre-production samples

Customization Options

  • Dimensions — Custom hexagonal sizes; common ranges from 80mm to 300mm across flats
  • Material — Art paper (128–200 gsm), specialty metallic/pearlescent paper, linen, or fabric-wrapped
  • Finishing — Matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch coating, spot UV, aqueous varnish
  • Foil Colors — Gold, silver, rose gold, copper, holographic, matte gold, matte silver
  • Closure Type — Magnetic lid, ribbon tie, friction-fit, or hinged with clasp
  • Foam Insert — EVA (density 38–60 shore), PU foam, velvet-flocked foam; single or multi-cavity
  • Interior Lining — Velvet, satin, suede, or flocked paper in any Pantone-matched color
  • Printing — CMYK offset with up to 4 Pantone spot colors; full-wrap or panel-specific design

Production Specifications

  • Presses — KBA 1050 4-color offset (CMYK) + Heidelberg 7+1 UV (inline spot coatings)
  • Board — 800–1600 gsm greyboard core; 128–200 gsm art paper wrap
  • Foam — EVA foam 38–60 shore hardness; die-cut to ±0.5mm tolerance
  • Hexagonal Wrapping — Specialized case-making jigs for consistent 120° internal angles and tight seam alignment
  • Assembly — Semi-automatic hexagonal case-making and foam insertion lines
  • QC — 100% inspection on panel alignment, foam fit, magnet function, and print registration
  • Certifications — ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain of Custody
