FAQ

Q: Do the seeds in the hang tag actually germinate — and what’s the shelf life?

A: Yes. We use non-GMO wildflower seed mixes (typically 5–7 species including poppy, daisy, and cornflower) embedded in handmade recycled cotton paper. Germination rate is tested per ISO 11269-2 at ≥85% before production. The seed-embedded tags have a shelf life of 18–24 months when stored at 15–25°C and <60% RH. Each tag includes soy-ink printed planting instructions: soak in water for 4 hours, place on moist soil, cover lightly, keep in sunlight. For custom seed blends (herb gardens, tree seeds, regional wildflowers), we source from certified suppliers and provide germination certificates with every order.

Q: What happens if the PLA window is accidentally disposed of in regular trash — will it biodegrade?

A: PLA (polylactic acid) made from corn starch requires industrial composting conditions (58°C ± 2°C, >60% humidity, active microbial environment) to fully degrade — typically 90–180 days per EN 13432. In a landfill, PLA degrades much slower (3–5 years estimate) and may not fully break down in anaerobic conditions. For maximum environmental benefit, we recommend the die-cut open window option for products that don’t require a sealed window, or adding a note on the box directing consumers to industrial composting facilities. The PLA-free version is our bestseller for brands that prioritize end-of-life simplicity.

Q: Can I order a small batch of seed tags with our company’s custom seed mix?

A: Yes. For custom seed blends, minimum order is 5,000 tags (typically added to a 1,000-box order with 5 tags per box for multi-item gift sets). Lead time is 15–20 days including seed sourcing, germination testing, and paper production. For standard wildflower mix with your custom tag shape and branding, MOQ is 500 boxes with one tag per box, lead time 10–15 days. We provide a seed viability test report and germination photos with every custom order. Single-tag sampling (with your artwork) is free — 5–7 working days.

Q: How does the kraft eco box compare to a laminated rigid box on cost and minimums?

A: Kraft eco boxes are typically 30–50% lower cost than laminated rigid boxes because they use fewer materials (no greyboard, no lamination, no magnet) and simpler production (single-pass print + die-cut vs. multi-step rigid box assembly). MOQ starts at 500 units vs. rigid boxes at 1,000+, and tooling costs are lower (single steel-rule die vs. multi-cavity rigid box tooling). The trade-off: kraft boxes have a softer, more organic aesthetic — ideal for natural/artisan brands — while rigid boxes project luxury and heft. Many clients use both: rigid for flagship products, kraft for seasonal and promotional lines.
