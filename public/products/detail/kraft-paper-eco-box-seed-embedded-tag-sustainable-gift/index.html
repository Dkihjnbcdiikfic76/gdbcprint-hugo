Earth-conscious kraft paper eco gift box with a plantable seed-embedded hang tag — packaging that gives back to the planet after unboxing. Produced at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003), our Foshan factory spans 10,000 sqm with KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, delivering 1.7 million boxes daily. ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified. Printed with soy-based inks on FSC-certified kraft paperboard. The detachable hang tag is embedded with wildflower seeds — customers plant it and grow flowers, transforming packaging waste into blooming gardens.

Applications

  • Sustainable brand and eco-conscious retail gift packaging
  • Organic skincare, natural cosmetics, and wellness product boxes
  • Artisan food, organic tea, and specialty coffee gift packaging
  • Wedding favor and eco-event gift box packaging
  • Zero-waste store and refillery product packaging
  • Corporate sustainability initiative and green marketing kits

Customization Options

  • Box Style: Tuck-top, auto-lock bottom, drawer-slide, two-piece lid-and-base, pillow box, gable box with cotton handle
  • Paper: 250–400gsm FSC-certified kraft (natural brown, bleached white, or custom-dyed earth tones)
  • Printing: Soy-based CMYK offset (matte, low-VOC); single-color eco stamp with minimalist aesthetic
  • Seed Tag: Custom shape die-cut (circle, leaf, heart, brand logo silhouette); embedded seed mix (wildflower, herb, tree, or custom blend); printed with soy ink planting instructions
  • Finishes: Uncoated natural matte, aqueous matte varnish, blind emboss/deboss logo
  • Window: PLA biodegradable film window (compostable) or die-cut open window
  • Handles: Cotton rope (natural/colored), jute twine, kraft paper twist handle
  • Size: 80×80×40mm to 350×250×100mm
  • Additional Eco Features: 100% recycled kraft option, water-activated paper tape closure, paper ribbon belly band
