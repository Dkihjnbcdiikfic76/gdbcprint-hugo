Magnetic Closure Box with Clear Sleeve Window Premium Gift Packaging

June 11, 2026

Premium magnetic closure rigid box with a transparent PET sleeve window — crafted by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of manufacturing excellence. The clear sleeve allows the product inside to be showcased without removing it from the box, making it ideal for premium gift sets, cosmetics, and corporate giveaways. The magnetic flap closure provides a satisfying snap-close mechanism while the sleeve adds an extra layer of protection during transit. Produced on KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset presses across 10,000 sqm of factory space. Daily output capacity of 1.5 million boxes.

Applications

  • Premium cosmetics and skincare gift sets
  • Corporate holiday gift packaging
  • Luxury confectionery and chocolate presentation
  • Wedding favor and event giveaway boxes
  • Electronics accessories retail packaging

Customization Options

  • Sleeve material: Clear PET, frosted PET, or tinted PVC
  • Surface finishing: Matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch coating, spot UV
  • Printing: CMYK offset, PMS spot colors, gold/silver foil stamping
  • Interior: Velvet flocking, satin lining, custom foam inserts
  • Magnetic closure: Single or double magnet configuration, neodymium grade N35-N52

Production Specifications

  • Base material: 1200–1800 gsm greyboard, wrapped in 157 gsm art paper
  • Window sleeve: 0.3–0.5 mm transparent PET, die-cut to specification
  • Printing: Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset — up to 7 colors + spot UV in one pass
  • Finishing: Laminating machine, foil stamping (gold/silver/holographic), embossing/debossing
  • Assembly: Automatic rigid box forming line with magnet insertion
  • Quality control: 100% visual inspection, magnet strength testing, sleeve fit verification

Why Bincai

22 years of experience (since 2003). ISO 9001 certified quality management and FSC certified sustainable sourcing. Full in-house production from prepress to finishing — no outsourcing, consistent quality. Competitive MOQ starting at 500 units with 15-day lead time for standard orders.
