FAQ

Q: Will the foam tray leave marks or impressions on the candle surface during long-term storage?

A: No — our foam trays are made from non-reactive, acid-free EVA or PU foam that will not transfer color, texture, or chemical residue to candle wax, glass jars, or labels. For soft natural wax candles (soy, coconut, beeswax), we recommend PU foam at 30kg/m³ density — soft enough to cushion without compression marks. We can also add a tissue or satin liner between the foam cavity and candle for absolute contact-free presentation.

Q: How does the magnetic closure hold up during shipping — won’t it pop open?

A: Our N48 neodymium magnets provide 1.2-1.8kg of holding force per magnet pair — a 4-magnet configuration delivers over 5kg total closure force, more than sufficient to keep the lid firmly closed during FedEx/UPS/DHL transit. We conduct 1.2m drop testing with filled candle simulators and ISTA 3A vibration testing as standard. For international container shipping, we add a clear shrink band or removable paper seal as a secondary closure backup.

Q: Can the foam tray be designed to hold different candle sizes in different compartments?

A: Yes — this is one of the most common configurations for gift sets. The CNC router can cut different cavity profiles in different positions on the same tray. For example: a 3-wick jar candle (100mm diameter) in the center, two votive candles (50mm diameter) on each side, and a matchbox slot. We accept a dimension table or CAD file showing each candle’s position and profile, and we return a tray layout proof for approval before cutting.

Q: What are the lead time and MOQ considerations for custom satin and foam combinations?

A: Standard lead time is 15-20 days for existing materials and tooling. Custom-dyed satin interior adds 5-7 days. New foam tray CNC tooling adds 3-5 days (we route from CAD directly — no physical die required). MOQ is 500 units for standard configurations, 1,000 for custom satin color or custom foam layout. Pre-production samples with your exact candle dimensions ship within 5 days for fit verification before full production.
