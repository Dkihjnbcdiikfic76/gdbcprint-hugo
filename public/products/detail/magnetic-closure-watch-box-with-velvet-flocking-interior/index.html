Magnetic Closure Watch Box with Velvet Flocking Interior

June 10, 2026

Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s magnetic closure watch box with velvet flocking interior delivers luxury unboxing for premium timepieces. The 2.5mm rigid board construction, magnetic closure, and plush velvet-flocked interior cradle watches securely while the exterior wrap — available in matte, leatherette, or pearlescent finishes — offers full brand customization. Manufactured in our ISO 9001:2015 certified 10,000 sqm factory with 22 years of expertise and daily output of 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • Luxury Watch Brands: Timepiece presentation, retail display, collector’s edition packaging
  • Corporate Gift Watches: Branded employee recognition, milestone anniversary gifts
  • Watch Retailers: In-store display, gift-with-purchase packaging, e-commerce shipping
  • Smartwatch Packaging: Premium unboxing for high-end smartwatches and accessories
  • Jewelry & Watch Combos: Multi-compartment boxes for watch + bracelet/cufflink sets
  • Limited Editions: Special release packaging with serialized numbering and certificates

Customization Options

  • Box Style: Book-style magnetic flip, two-piece rigid with magnetic flap, drawer-style, hinged lid
  • Sizes: Custom-fit — single watch (100×80×60mm) to multi-watch (350×250×80mm)
  • Exterior Materials: Matte art paper, soft-touch velvet paper, leatherette (PU), linen texture, pearlescent
  • Interior: Velvet flocking (black, navy, grey, burgundy, cream), suede microfiber, satin lining
  • Watch Pillow: Satin-covered foam, velvet-flocked, or leatherette — removable for strap watches
  • Printing: CMYK offset + Pantone spot on Heidelberg 7+1 UV, full coverage
  • Finishes: Gold/silver/rose gold foil stamping, embossing/debossing, spot UV, edge gilding
  • Add-ons: Branded sleeve, authentication card slot, multi-compartment, drawer for accessories
