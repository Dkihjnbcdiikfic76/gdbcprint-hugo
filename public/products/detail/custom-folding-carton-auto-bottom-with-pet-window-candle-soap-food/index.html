FAQ

Q: What is the minimum order quantity for custom folding cartons?

A: Standard MOQ is 1000 pcs. For cartons with hot foil stamping, embossing, or multi-color spot UV, MOQ is 2000 pcs. Free pre-production sample — 7–10 working day turnaround, freight collect.

Q: How quickly does the auto-bottom erect during assembly?

A: Each carton pops open and locks in under 2 seconds — simply squeeze the sides and the bottom automatically locks. No manual folding, no tape, no glue required. This design is ideal for high-volume production lines and contract packagers.

Q: Can the carton be food-safe for direct food contact?

A: Yes. We offer food-grade SBS (solid bleached sulfate) board that complies with FDA 21 CFR for direct food contact. For EU markets, migration testing per EU 1935/2004 is available. The PET window is also food-grade. For products requiring a barrier, we can add a PE or PLA coating.

Q: Do you offer sustainable or eco-friendly material options?

A: Yes. We offer FSC-certified board, kraft-back board with 100% recycled content, and PLA (compostable) windows instead of PET. Aqueous coating can replace plastic lamination for fully plastic-free packaging. Soy-based inks are standard for all our offset printing.
