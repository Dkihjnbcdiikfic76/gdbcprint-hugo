Magnetic Closure Lipstick Gift Box

June 11, 2026

Product Overview

Designed for luxury cosmetic brands, the Bincai Magnetic Closure Lipstick Gift Box delivers a premium unboxing experience in a slim, elegant form factor. The soft-touch matte finish combined with magnetic snap closure creates a tactile, high-end feel that elevates brand perception. Manufactured on Heidelberg 7+1 UV and KBA 1050 presses with ±0.15mm print registration tolerance.

Features

  • Slim Profile: 25×25×90mm standard, perfect for single lipstick or rollerball presentation
  • Magnetic Closure: Neodymium strip magnets with 5,000-cycle durability rating
  • Soft-Touch Finish: Velvet matte lamination with optional spot UV on logo
  • Foil Options: Gold, silver, rose gold, holographic — applied via Bobst flatbed hot stamping
  • Interior Options: Die-cut foam insert, satin ribbon pull, or cardboard partition
  • Custom Dimensions: Available from 20×20×70mm to 50×50×120mm

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Press: Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset and KBA 1050 4-color — ±0.15mm print registration for brand logo precision
  • Board: 2.0mm rigid greyboard core wrapped in 157gsm art paper
  • Magnetic Closure: Neodymium strip magnets embedded along full length, 5,000-cycle durability rating
  • Soft-Touch Finish: Aqueous soft-touch coating over matte lamination, fingerprint-resistant
  • Foil Stamping: Bobst flatbed hot stamping press, gold/silver/rose gold/holographic foil options
  • Interior Insert: CNC die-cut EVA foam (30–45 Shore A hardness), velvet-flocked tray, or satin ribbon pull
  • Daily Output: 220,000+ gift boxes + 1.7M color boxes
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, REACH-compliant materials
