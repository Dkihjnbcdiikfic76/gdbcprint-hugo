FAQ

Q: What leatherette textures and colors are available? A: We offer smooth, pebbled (Saffiano-style), and crocodile-embossed textures. Color range includes classic black, ivory white, navy blue, burgundy red, blush pink, emerald green, and chocolate brown. Custom Pantone-matched leatherette colors are available for orders of 3000+ units. All materials are phthalate-free and REACH-compliant.

Q: Can the ring slot fit different ring sizes? A: Yes. We die-cut ring slots to your specified dimensions — typically 17–22mm width for standard rings (US size 4–10). We recommend providing a sample ring for perfect fit verification. Slots can be horizontal or angled presentation, with velvet-covered foam inserts that gently grip the ring band.

Q: Is the velvet interior safe for fine jewelry and precious metals? A: Yes. Our velvet flocking is pH-neutral and free from sulfur compounds that could tarnish silver. For premium jewelry (gold, platinum, diamond), we offer anti-tarnish treated velvet and microfiber suede lining options. All interior materials undergo tarnish-resistance testing.

Q: What’s the minimum order quantity for custom leatherette jewelry boxes? A: Our standard MOQ is 500 units with stock leatherette colors. Custom Pantone-matched leatherette requires 3000+ units. We accept trial orders and provide free pre-production samples (freight collect) with 5–7 day turnaround. Rush production available for 1000+ unit orders.
