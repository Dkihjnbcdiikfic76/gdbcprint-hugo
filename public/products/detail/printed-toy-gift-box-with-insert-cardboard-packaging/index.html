Product Overview

The Bincai Printed Toy Gift Box with Insert delivers everything a toy brand needs in one cost-effective folding carton: vibrant shelf appeal, secure product protection, and an unboxing experience that delights children and parents alike. The die-cut cardboard insert cradles the toy — whether it’s an action figure, a board game, or a puzzle set — preventing shifting during transit while presenting the product at its best angle when the lid is opened. Printed in full CMYK on Heidelberg Speedmaster 7+1 UV and KBA Rapida 105 4-color presses, your artwork comes to life with crisp detail that makes toys jump off the shelf.

Manufactured at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s 18,000 sqm dual-factory complex in Foshan, every toy box benefits from 22 years of folding carton specialization, ISO 9001:2015 quality systems, and FSC chain-of-custody certification — so you can market your toys with confidence. With a daily output of 1.7 million color boxes and 220,000+ gift boxes, we handle volumes from small-batch trial runs to million-unit global rollouts.

FAQ

Q: Is the printed toy gift box safe for children’s products? What safety standards do you meet? A: Yes. Our toy boxes are manufactured with child safety as a priority. All materials comply with EN 71-3 (heavy metals migration limits for toy packaging) and ASTM F963 toy safety standards. Corners are die-cut with a minimum 3mm radius to eliminate sharp edges, and we use non-toxic, low-VOC inks and adhesives. FSC certification ensures paperboard comes from responsibly managed forests — a growing requirement for toy brands selling into EU and North American markets. Third-party lab testing certificates (SGS, Intertek, TÜV) are available on request.

Q: Can the insert be designed to hold multiple toy pieces or accessories? A: Absolutely. Our in-house structural design team creates multi-cavity die-cut inserts that organize action figure accessories, game tokens, puzzle pieces, or STEM kit components into dedicated compartments. We can design friction-fit slots, fold-up dividers, or nested tray levels within a single insert. For action figures, we commonly design the insert to lock the figure at the waist and ankles while holding accessories (weapons, alternate hands, stands) in adjacent cutouts. CAD drawings and 3D renderings are provided for approval before tooling.

Q: What is your MOQ for toy boxes, and can I start with a small test order? A: MOQ starts at 500 units for standard tuck-top or STE cartons with a single-color insert. For boxes with window film, hot foil stamping, or complex multi-cavity inserts, MOQ is 1,000 units to cover tooling costs. We welcome trial orders — free pre-production samples (freight collect) let you evaluate fit, finish, and print quality before committing to a full production run. Many toy brands start with a 500–1000 piece trial for a Kickstarter campaign or trade show launch, then scale to mass production.

Q: Are your printing inks and materials safe if the toy box is handled by young children? A: Yes. We use soy-based or low-VOC offset inks that meet EN 71-3 and CONEG (heavy metals) regulations. Our aqueous and BOPP lamination films are phthalate-free and non-toxic. For brands with the strictest requirements — such as infant/toddler toys (0–3 years) — we can produce with food-grade inks and water-based coatings only, eliminating solvent-based components entirely. Full material safety data sheets (MSDS) are provided with every order, and we recommend third-party testing for your specific market’s regulatory requirements.
