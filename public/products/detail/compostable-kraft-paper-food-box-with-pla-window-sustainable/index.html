FAQ

Q: Are these kraft food boxes with PLA windows fully compostable in home composting systems?

A: Our PLA-windowed kraft boxes are certified to EN 13432 and ASTM D6400 standards for industrial composting facilities, where controlled temperature (58°C+), humidity, and microbial conditions ensure complete biodegradation within 90–180 days. While the kraft board component will break down in well-managed home compost piles, the PLA window requires the higher sustained temperatures of an industrial facility to fully hydrolyze and biodegrade — we recommend disposal through municipal organics collection where available. For brands requiring home-compostable certification (e.g., OK Compost HOME via TÜV Austria), we offer alternative window materials including cellulose-based films — please contact our team to discuss your specific certification requirements.

Q: What is the maximum temperature these boxes can withstand for hot food applications?

A: The FSC-certified kraft paperboard and PLA window combination is designed to handle food temperatures up to 85°C (185°F) without structural deformation or window clouding, making these boxes suitable for freshly prepared hot foods including burgers, fried chicken, stir-fries, and steamed items. For applications exceeding 85°C, we recommend our anti-fog coated PLA window option (available in 80μm gauge) and a brief cooling period of 2–3 minutes before lid closure to prevent condensation buildup inside the window cavity. The aqueous moisture-barrier interior coating remains stable across the full 0°C to 85°C range and will not transfer chemicals to food contents at any temperature within the certified range.

Q: Can I order these compostable boxes with a custom printed logo and brand colors?

A: Absolutely — every order is manufactured to your exact branding specifications using our KBA 1050 CMYK offset press for full-color graphics or Pantone spot-color matching for precise corporate identity reproduction. You can print on the exterior kraft surface, the interior panel (visible when opened), or both, with soy-based inks that maintain compostability certification. We also offer hot foil stamping in gold, silver, rose gold, copper, and custom foil colors for metallic logo accents, plus embossing and debossing for tactile brand elements. Our prepress team provides free artwork consultation including dieline templates, bleed setup, and color-separation guidance — simply send your AI/PDF/PSD files and we’ll return a digital proof within 24 hours.

Q: What is the minimum order quantity for custom-sized compostable PLA window boxes, and how long does sampling take?

A: The standard MOQ for custom-sized and custom-printed compostable kraft boxes with PLA windows is 500–1000 units depending on box dimensions and print complexity — trial quantities as low as 300 units can be accommodated for simple single-color designs. Sampling follows a two-stage process: first, we produce a white (unprinted) structural sample in your exact dimensions and material specification within 3–5 working days so you can verify fit, closure, and board weight; once approved, we produce a pre-production printed sample with your artwork within an additional 5–7 working days. Both sample stages are free of charge — you pay only the courier freight (typically DHL/FedEx, 3–5 days to major global markets). Full production lead time after sample approval is 7–15 working days.
