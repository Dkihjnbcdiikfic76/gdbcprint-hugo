Double layer jewelry gift box with pull-out drawer for rings, bracelets, and earring storage — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). 10,000 sqm factory with KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses. The innovative two-tier design features an upper lid compartment for necklaces and a smooth-gliding lower drawer for rings and bracelets, each lined with plush velvet. Daily output 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • Jewelry retail display and gift packaging
  • Wedding party favor and bridesmaid gift boxes
  • Online jewelry brand premium unboxing experience
  • Ring and bracelet set presentation boxes
  • Earring and pendant storage/display boxes
  • Travel jewelry case with compact drawer design

Customization Options

  • Upper lid with die-cut velvet cavities for necklace + earrings
  • Lower pull-out drawer with ring roll inserts (4–12 ring slots)
  • Velvet lining colors: black, navy, burgundy, ivory, grey, custom Pantone
  • Magnetic snap closure on drawer for secure storage
  • Hot foil stamping: gold, silver, rose gold, holographic on lid exterior
  • Embossed logo with raised tactile effect on lid or drawer front
  • Satin ribbon pull for drawer — color-matched to velvet interior
  • Mirror insert inside lid for try-on convenience
  • LED lighting strip activated when lid opens (battery compartment)
  • Custom sizes: compact 120×80×40mm to large 250×180×60mm
  • Exterior finishes: matte lamination, soft-touch, leatherette texture, or linen wrap

Production Specifications

  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press for exterior cover printing
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV press for metallic inks and spot coatings
  • Greyboard core: 1200gsm–2000gsm for premium weight and drawer durability
  • Velvet lining: 100% polyester flocked velvet, 0.8–1.2mm pile height
  • Precision drawer mechanism: ±0.3mm tolerance for smooth glide
  • Fully automatic rigid box assembly with drawer integration
  • ISO 9001:2015 quality management at every production stage
  • FSC-certified paper sourcing for sustainable luxury packaging
