Double-Deck Velvet Earring Stud Display Box — Magnetic Lid with 24-Pair Tray

June 11, 2026

Product Overview

Bincai’s double-deck velvet earring display box is engineered for jewelry retailers who need high-density stud and earring presentation. The hinged top deck holds 12 pairs of stud earrings on elastic straps over a velvet bed, while the lower compartment stores an additional 12 pairs or larger drop earrings. A polished mirror insert on the inside lid lets customers try earrings at the counter. 22 years of precision box manufacturing.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Press: KBA 1050 4-color offset — precise registration for branded exteriors
  • Board: 2.0mm greyboard core for structural rigidity
  • Interior: 2mm velvet flocking with elastic stud retention straps
  • Tray: Removable EVA foam tray with 12 pre-cut stud slots
  • Mirror: 1.5mm polished glass mirror, full lid coverage
  • Closure: Magnetic flap with reinforced hinge
  • Daily Output: 220,000+ gift boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody
