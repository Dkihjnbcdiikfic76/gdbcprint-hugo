Product Overview

Bincai’s corrugated book wrap mailer is engineered for e-commerce fulfillment of books, tablets, documents, and flat products. The self-sealing adhesive strip eliminates tape, while the tear-strip opening provides a frustration-free customer unboxing experience. Adjustable depth accommodates varying product thicknesses. Manufactured on fully automatic corrugated lines in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Flute: B-flute (2.5mm thickness) — optimal crush resistance for flat products
  • Liner: 125gsm kraft outside, 125gsm kraft inside
  • Production Line: Fully automatic corrugator with inline flexo printing
  • Adhesive: Pressure-sensitive hot-melt self-seal strip, -20°C to 60°C range
  • Tear Strip: Integrated tear tape for easy opening
  • Daily Output: 1.7 million color boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody

FAQ

Q: What is the minimum order quantity for corrugated book wrap mailers? A: The standard MOQ is 1,000 units. For custom tooling or specialty flute grades, we recommend 5,000+ units to optimize per-unit costs. Bincai’s fully automatic corrugated lines (18,000 sqm facility in Foshan, Guangdong) can handle both small-batch prototyping and mass production at 1.7M boxes/day.

Q: How does the self-sealing adhesive perform in cold or hot conditions? A: Bincai uses pressure-sensitive hot-melt adhesive rated for -20°C to 60°C. The adhesive is tested to ≥3.0 N/cm peel strength at 23°C (TAPPI T-810). For extreme climates, we offer cold-chain adhesive with -40°C rating.

Q: Can you print my brand logo on the mailer? A: Yes. We offer flexo printing (1-2 colors) inline during corrugation for cost-effective branding, or full-color offset litho labels for high-end presentation. Bincai’s KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses produce sharp graphics at CMYK + Pantone spot accuracy (ΔE ≤ 2.0).

Q: Is this mailer compliant with Amazon FBA and ISTA standards? A: Yes. All Bincai corrugated mailers are designed to meet Amazon FBA dimensional tier requirements and pass ISTA 1A drop test protocols (10-drop sequence). We also offer ISTA 3A pre-certification testing for high-volume fulfillment programs.
