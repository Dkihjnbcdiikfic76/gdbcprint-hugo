Product Overview

Bincai’s corrugated book wrap mailer is engineered for e-commerce fulfillment of books, tablets, documents, and flat products. The self-sealing adhesive strip eliminates tape, while the tear-strip opening provides a frustration-free customer unboxing experience. Adjustable depth accommodates varying product thicknesses. Manufactured on fully automatic corrugated lines in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Flute: B-flute (2.5mm thickness) — optimal crush resistance for flat products
  • Liner: 125gsm kraft outside, 125gsm kraft inside
  • Production Line: Fully automatic corrugator with inline flexo printing
  • Adhesive: Pressure-sensitive hot-melt self-seal strip, -20°C to 60°C range
  • Tear Strip: Integrated tear tape for easy opening
  • Daily Output: 1.7 million color boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody
