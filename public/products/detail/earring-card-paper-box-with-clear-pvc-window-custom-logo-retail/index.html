Earring Card Paper Box with Clear PVC Window Custom Logo Retail

June 11, 2026

Premium earring card paper box with clear PVC window and custom logo printing — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). Our 18,000 sqm production facility (10,000 sqm primary plant + 8,000 sqm auxiliary workshop) houses KBA 1050 4-color offset and Heidelberg Speedmaster 7+1 UV presses, delivering a daily output of 1.7 million boxes. ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain of Custody certified, we produce card-style earring packaging that combines product visibility with brand presentation — the clear PVC window showcases earrings while the printed card surface communicates your brand identity at retail point of sale.

Applications

Earring card paper boxes are the go-to packaging format for retail jewelry displays, combining a rigid or semi-rigid card backing with a die-cut transparent window. Ideal for:

  • Earring Retail Display: Peg-hook compatible hanging cards for jewelry stores, boutiques, and department store counters
  • E-Commerce Packaging: Lightweight, flat-profile boxes that minimize shipping costs while protecting earrings during transit
  • Trade Shows & Exhibitions: Branded earring cards that create a cohesive, professional booth presentation
  • Subscription Boxes: Compact card boxes that fit neatly inside monthly jewelry subscription kits
  • Wholesale Distribution: Bulk earring packaging for manufacturers shipping to multiple retail outlets
  • Promotional Giveaways: Cost-effective branded packaging for event freebies, influencer gifts, and brand collaborations
  • Craft & Handmade Markets: Professional presentation for artisan jewelry makers selling at craft fairs and online marketplaces

Customization Options

  • Materials: 300–400 gsm FBB (Folding Box Board), SBS (Solid Bleached Sulphate), or kraft paper card stock; PVC or PET windows in 0.2–0.5mm thickness with crystal clarity or matte frosted finish
  • Printing: Full-color CMYK offset on KBA 1050 4-color press; PMS spot colors; 7-color + UV on Heidelberg Speedmaster 7+1 for premium vibrant output
  • Finishing: Matte or gloss lamination for durability and shelf appeal, soft-touch velvet coating, gold/silver/copper hot foil stamping, blind embossing/debossing, spot UV varnish for contrast highlights
  • Window: Die-cut PVC or PET window — shape can be rectangular, oval, heart, or custom contour; window positioning at top, center, or full-card; optional anti-scratch coating
  • Size: Common retail sizes range from 70×110mm (stud earring pairs) to 150×210mm (hoop/drop earrings); fully custom dimensions available
  • Closure & Display: Tuck-top folding, self-locking base, hang tab with euro-slot for peg-hook display, or backer card with adhesive strip
  • Interior: Optional foam pad, velvet flocking, or satin ribbon slot to secure earring posts and backs

FAQ

Q: Can the PVC window be custom shaped to match my earring design? A: Absolutely. Our automatic die-cutting lines can produce windows in any shape — rectangular, oval, circular, heart-shaped, or a custom contour that follows your earring profile. We create a custom cutting die (included at no extra charge for orders above 5,000 units), and the PVC/PET film is precision-patched with ±0.5mm tolerance.

Q: What’s the difference between PVC and PET window material? A: PVC offers excellent clarity at a lower cost and is the most common choice for earring card boxes. PET (polyester) provides superior clarity, better scratch resistance, and is a more environmentally friendly option — fully recyclable and accepted in most municipal recycling streams. PET costs approximately 15–20% more than PVC. Both are available in 0.2–0.5mm thicknesses.

Q: Can I order a small batch to test the market before committing to a large order? A: Yes. While our standard MOQ is 500 units, we welcome trial orders of 100–300 units for market testing. Unit pricing is higher at these volumes due to setup costs, but it’s an excellent way to validate packaging design before scaling. We also provide free pre-production samples with full finishing so you can evaluate quality before any commitment.

Q: How do hanging earring card boxes hold up during shipping and retail handling? A: Our card boxes are engineered for durability. 300–400 gsm paperboard with matte or gloss lamination resists bending and moisture. The euro-slot hang tab is reinforced and tested to support 500g+ without tearing. For e-commerce shipments, we recommend adding a corrugated mailer or padded envelope — the flat profile of card boxes stacks efficiently and resists crushing better than rigid boxes.
