Custom Jewelry Gift Box with Velvet Insert Necklace Packaging

June 10, 2026

Custom jewelry gift box with velvet insert for necklace, bracelet, ring, and earring packaging — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). Designed to showcase fine jewelry with elegance and protection. The plush velvet or satin interior with die-cut cavities holds each piece securely, while the rigid box structure protects against impact during shipping and retail handling. Produced on KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across 18,000 sqm (10,000+8,000 smart factory) of factory space. Daily output over 220,000+ gift boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion).

Customization Options

  • Magnetic flap closure or lift-off lid for elegant opening
  • Velvet, satin, suede, or leatherette interior lining
  • Die-cut foam or velvet insert with precision cavities for each piece
  • Hot foil stamping in gold, silver, rose gold for brand logos
  • Embossing and debossing for subtle dimensional branding
  • Spot UV or full gloss on matte black/white exterior
  • Ribbon pull tab or satin ribbon closure
  • Custom sizes from 63×63×25mm (single ring) to 300×200×50mm (full set)

Production Specifications

  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press for high-speed precision printing
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV press for specialty coatings and instant curing
  • Fully automatic rigid box production line with corner wrapping
  • Greyboard thickness: 800gsm–2000gsm for luxury weight and protection
  • Surface paper: 128gsm–200gsm art paper with soft-touch or matte lamination
  • Interior: Italian velvet, microfiber suede, or satin in custom colors
  • ISO 9001:2015 quality management throughout production
  • FSC-certified paper sourcing for sustainable luxury packaging
