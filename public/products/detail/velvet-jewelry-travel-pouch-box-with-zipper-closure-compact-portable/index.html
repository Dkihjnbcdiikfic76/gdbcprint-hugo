Product Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s velvet jewelry travel pouch box is engineered for the modern jewelry brand that values both protection and portability. The compact zipper-closure design combines plush velvet flocking on the exterior with a smooth satin interior, reinforced by a rigid greyboard frame that prevents crushing during travel. Ideal for retail display, e-commerce fulfillment, and duty-free gift packaging — manufactured on our KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses in an ISO 9001 & FSC certified 18,000 sqm facility with 22 years of expertise.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Exterior: Velvet flocking (0.5–1.5mm pile height) on 1200–1600gsm greyboard substrate — soft-touch luxury feel with crush resistance
  • Interior Lining: 100% polyester satin in custom PMS colors, anti-tarnish treated for silver jewelry compatibility
  • Core Construction: Rigid greyboard frame (2.0mm thickness) prevents deformation during transit
  • Zipper: YKK #3 or #5 nylon coil zipper in gold/silver/black/custom finish — tested to 10,000 open/close cycles
  • Press: Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset for exterior printing; interior pad printing for logo on satin
  • Binding: Heat-sealed edges with reinforced stitching on zipper attachment points
  • Daily Output: 220,000+ jewelry boxes (part of Bincai’s 1.7M total daily capacity)
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, SGS

Design Features

  • Crush-Proof Frame: 2.0mm greyboard core withstands compression in luggage and handbags
  • Anti-Tarnish Interior: Satin lining treated with anti-corrosion agent — safe for silver, gold, platinum
  • Zipper Security: Full-length YKK zipper with fabric pull tab, won’t scratch jewelry surfaces
  • Compact Silhouette: Available from 80×80×25mm (ring size) to 200×150×40mm (necklace size)
  • Custom Debossing: Blind deboss or foil-stamped logo on velvet exterior without disrupting pile texture
  • Divider Options: Removable satin-covered partition, elastic retention straps for rings/earrings

Applications

  • Retail Jewelry: In-store display packaging that doubles as carry-home pouch — no secondary bag needed
  • E-Commerce: Crush-proof design eliminates damage returns, fits standard poly mailers for low-cost shipping
  • Duty-Free & Travel Retail: Compact footprint maximizes shelf density, zipper allows customer inspection without damage
  • Bridal & Wedding: Bridesmaid gift packaging, ring bearer presentation, wedding favor pouches
  • Watch & Accessory: Small-parts organization for watch straps, cufflinks, tie pins, brooches
