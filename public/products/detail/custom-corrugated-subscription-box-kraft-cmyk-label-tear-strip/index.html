Custom Corrugated Subscription Box — Kraft Body + Full-Color CMYK Label with Tear Strip

June 11, 2026

Product Overview

Bincai’s custom corrugated subscription box combines the cost-efficiency of kraft corrugated board with a full-color CMYK offset litho label for vibrant branding. The self-locking crash-lock bottom assembles in under 3 seconds, and the tear-strip opening creates a premium unboxing moment. Ideal for beauty boxes, snack subscriptions, apparel bundles, and book-of-the-month fulfillment. 22 years of packaging manufacturing.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Flute: B-flute (2.5mm) — optimal crush resistance for mixed-product boxes
  • Body: 125gsm kraft liner, natural brown finish
  • Label: 157gsm art paper, full-color CMYK offset litho, matte lamination
  • Label Size: Full top panel coverage or partial (customer-specified)
  • Bottom: Crash-lock self-locking with reinforced tabs
  • Opening: Integrated tear strip with pull-tab
  • Daily Output: 1.7 million color boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody
