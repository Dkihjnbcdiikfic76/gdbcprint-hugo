Product Overview

A sophisticated rectangular rigid gift box with an integrated magnetic flap closure and precision spot UV detailing — the go-to choice for brands that want understated elegance with a tactile surprise. Manufactured at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., this box combines 2.5mm greyboard rigidity with Heidelberg 7+1 UV press spot coating for visual depth that catches light on retail shelves. The magnetic flap closes with a satisfying snap, eliminating the need for ribbons while maintaining a clean, modern aesthetic.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Box construction: 2.5mm greyboard core, 157gsm art paper wrap, magnetic flap mechanism
  • Spot UV: Heidelberg 7+1 UV press — precision registration ±0.1mm on logo, patterns, or full-surface flood + spot
  • Magnets: Neodymium disc magnets (8mm × 1mm), hidden between board layers, 1.2kg hold force per pair
  • Lamination: Soft-touch matte base with high-gloss spot UV contrast
  • Daily rigid box output: 220,000+ gift boxes across dedicated production line
  • Interior: Custom die-cut EVA foam, velvet flocking, or satin-lined cavity

Customization Options

  • Size: From 80mm × 80mm × 30mm to 450mm × 350mm × 120mm
  • Shape: Rectangular, square, book-style with spine, landscape orientation
  • Spot UV pattern: Logo highlight, geometric texture, all-over pattern, or selective accent
  • Foil stamping: Combine with gold/silver/rose gold foil on logo for premium contrast
  • Matte finish: Anti-fingerprint soft-touch coating available
  • Interior: Single cavity, multi-cavity EVA foam tray, ribbon pull-tab for easy product removal
  • Color: Full CMYK + Pantone spot color matching on KBA 1050 4-color press

Applications

Ideal for: premium skincare sets, luxury candles, boutique chocolates, jewelry presentation, corporate welcome kits, tech accessory unboxing, wedding favors, and limited-edition product launches. The magnetic flap + spot UV combination signals modern luxury without ostentation.

Production Quality

Every box passes 14 quality checkpoints: board caliper verification, magnet adhesion pull test (minimum 100N), spot UV registration alignment (±0.1mm), flap alignment tolerance (±0.5mm), rub resistance test, colorimetry to Pantone standard, and final D65 lighting inspection. Magnetic closure tested to 10,000 open/close cycles without degradation.
