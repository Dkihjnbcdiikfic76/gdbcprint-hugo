Product Overview

Our custom magnetic closure fountain pen display box wraps premium greyboard in elegant faux leather, creating a sophisticated presentation befitting luxury writing instruments. The book-style lid opens on a hidden magnetic closure to reveal a plush suede-lined interior with a precision die-cut pen tray — cradling each fountain pen, rollerball, or ballpoint in its own sculpted recess. The faux leather exterior is available in classic black, rich cognac brown, deep navy, or custom colors with optional debossing and hot foil stamping for brand logos. Manufactured in our 10,000 sqm ISO 9001-certified facility with Heidelberg 7+1 UV printing for any interior branding, each box reflects Bincai’s 22 years of premium packaging expertise.

Design Features

  • Faux Leather Exterior Wrap: Premium polyurethane leather bonded to rigid greyboard with precise corner folding for seamless edges
  • Suede Interior Lining: Soft microfiber suede in complementary colors providing a non-abrasive cradle for fine writing instruments
  • Hidden Magnetic Closure: Industrial-grade neodymium magnets ensure the lid closes flush and stays securely shut
  • Die-Cut Pen Tray: Custom-sculpted recesses match exact pen dimensions — single pen, dual pen, or collector set configurations
  • Debossed Branding: Logo, monogram, or decorative pattern debossed into the faux leather surface
  • Hot Foil Stamping: Gold or silver foil on spine, lid, or interior for premium branding
  • Satin Ribbon Hinge: Reinforced satin ribbon hinge prevents lid over-extension while adding elegance

Applications

  • Luxury fountain pen and rollerball presentation
  • Corporate executive gift sets and award presentations
  • Limited edition writing instrument collector packaging
  • Retail point-of-sale display for pen boutiques
  • Personalized pen gifting for graduations and milestones
  • Branded promotional pen sets for corporate events

Customization Options

  • Faux leather colors: classic black, cognac brown, navy, burgundy, charcoal grey, or custom PMS
  • Single pen, dual pen, triple pen, or 6-pen collector set configurations
  • Interior lining: microfiber suede, velvet flocking, or satin in custom colors
  • Debossing depth and pattern fully customizable
  • Gold, silver, rose gold, or copper hot foil stamping on exterior and interior
  • Custom foil-stamped logo on the satin ribbon pull
  • Pen tray material: flocked plastic, suede-wrapped cardboard, or molded EVA foam
  • Gift sleeve or belly band with complementary branding
