Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s magnetic closure watch box with suede interior delivers understated luxury for discerning watch brands. The premium suede microfiber lining — softer and more durable than flocking — cradles timepieces without risk of micro-scratches. Rigid 2.5mm board, magnetic closure, removable watch pillow, and full custom logo options. Manufactured in our ISO 9001:2015 certified 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) — 22 years, Heidelberg 7+1 UV + KBA 1050 presses, 1.7M daily output.

Applications

  • Luxury Watch Packaging: High-end timepiece presentation and storage
  • Watch Retail Display: Countertop and window display for boutiques
  • Corporate Gifting: Branded executive watch gifts with custom logo
  • E-Commerce Fulfillment: Premium unboxing for online watch sales
  • Collector’s Edition: Limited run watch packaging with serialized numbering
  • Travel Storage: Compact single-watch travel case
  • Watch Servicing: Return packaging after repair or service

Customization Options

  • Box Style: Book-style magnetic flip, two-piece rigid, drawer-style, hinged lid
  • Sizes: Single watch (110×90×70mm), duo (220×100×70mm), multi-watch (350×200×80mm)
  • Exterior: Matte art paper, leatherette (PU), linen, velvet paper, pearlescent, carbon fiber texture
  • Suede Interior: Black, navy, grey, tan, burgundy, cream — 0.8mm microfiber suede
  • Watch Pillow: Suede-covered, velvet, or leatherette — removable, fits 38–46mm case
  • Logo Application: Gold/silver foil stamping, embossing/debossing, UV print, laser engraving
  • Finishes: Spot UV, soft-touch matte lamination, edge painting, ribbon pull
  • Add-ons: Branded outer sleeve, authentication card holder, multi-compartment, accessory drawer
