Velvet Jewelry Gift Box with Magnetic Closure & Gold Foil for Ring Packaging

Applications

  • Luxury ring and earring packaging for jewellery brands

Customization Options

  • Velvet-lined interior in 12+ color options (black, navy, burgundy, cream, etc.)

  • Embossing and debossing for tactile brand elevation
