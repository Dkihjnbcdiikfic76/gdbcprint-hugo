Product Overview

The Magnetic Closure Tri-Fold Wallet Box is the definitive packaging solution for gift cards, vouchers, certificates, and premium loyalty cards — transforming a flat piece of plastic or paper into a memorable gift moment. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (22 years of experience, 18,000 sqm ISO 9001 & FSC certified facility), this box uses precision-placed neodymium magnets embedded between the board layers to achieve a satisfying, silent magnetic snap that signals quality before the recipient sees the contents.

The tri-fold construction opens like a luxury wallet — the cover panel lifts to reveal a velvet or satin-lined interior with a die-cut slot, ribbon cradle, or corner tucks that hold gift cards, certificates, or vouchers securely in position. When closed, the magnetic flap aligns flush with zero gap — a testament to our ±0.3mm die-cutting and assembly tolerances. Full-color printing on our Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 7+1 UV or KBA Rapida 105 4-color press ensures vibrant brand colors with inline UV curing for immediate scuff resistance.

Applications

The tri-fold magnetic format elevates the perceived value of gift cards and certificates — turning a transactional item into an experience:

  • Retail Gift Cards: Department stores, fashion brands, electronics retailers — the magnetic snap and soft-touch finish communicate premium brand positioning
  • Loyalty & Membership Cards: VIP tier cards, annual membership certificates, employee recognition — velvet interior signals exclusivity
  • Corporate Gift Certificates: Holiday bonuses, service awards, client appreciation — custom foil-stamped company logo on the cover
  • Wedding & Event Vouchers: “Redeem this for…” experience gifts, honeymoon fund cards, charity donation certificates
  • Hotel & Spa Gift Vouchers: Spa treatment certificates, resort credit cards, anniversary packages — the box becomes a keepsake

Customization Options

  • Print & Decoration: Full CMYK offset, Pantone spot colors, hot foil stamping (gold, silver, rose gold, matte gold), embossing/debossing, spot UV, soft-touch lamination
  • Magnet Configuration: Two-disc neodymium magnets (8mm or 10mm diameter, N35 grade), bar magnets for extended closure area, or magnetic sheet for full-edge contact
  • Interior Lining: Velvet flocking (black, red, navy, cream, custom PMS), satin, faux suede, textured paper, or printed pattern liner
  • Card Retention: Die-cut slot (credit-card-sized, 85.6×54mm), elastic ribbon band, corner tucks, magnetic flap, or clear PET sleeve
  • Additional Features: Ribbon pull-tab, thumb-notch for easy opening, multi-panel fold (4-panel, 6-panel), integrated envelope for cash or letter, QR code panel for digital activation

Production Specifications

  • Board: 1.5–2.0mm greyboard (recycled content 85%+) wrapped in 157gsm C2S art paper, double-sided lining
  • Magnet: N35 neodymium disc magnets (8–10mm diameter, 1.5mm thickness), 3M 300LSE adhesive-backed, buried in board layers with 0.3mm paper over-wrap — invisible when closed
  • Printing: Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 7+1 UV (max sheet 750×1050mm) or KBA Rapida 105 4-color
  • Surface: Soft-touch matte lamination (1.2–1.5 mil), gloss UV spot varnish, embossing/debossing (single or multi-level)
  • Assembly: Machine-wrapped panels with automatic magnet inlay station, manual magnet polarity verification (100% check), hand-folded and final assembly
  • Quality Control: AQL 2.5 (major), AQL 1.5 (critical) — magnetic closure force (4–6N per pair), panel alignment (±0.3mm), print registration, interior fitment for standard card dimensions
