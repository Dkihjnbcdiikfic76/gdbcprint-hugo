Product Overview

Reverse tuck folding carton for cosmetic and skincare packaging — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). The reverse tuck structure (tuck flaps on opposite sides) provides a clean, professional opening experience ideal for premium beauty products. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across an 18,000 sqm integrated campus in Foshan, Guangdong. Daily output 1.7 million color boxes + 220,000+ gift boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion). ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certified. Designed for cosmetic brands requiring shelf-ready retail packaging with luxury finishes — soft-touch lamination, foil stamping, spot UV, and embossing.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Structure: Reverse tuck end (RTE) — top tucks back, bottom tucks forward; also available: straight tuck (STE), auto-lock bottom, crash-lock
  • Press: Heidelberg 7+1 UV for spot coatings, metallic inks, and Pantone matching; KBA 1050 4-color for high-volume CMYK production
  • Die-Cutting: Bobst flatbed with ±0.15mm print-to-cut registration; window patching for product visibility
  • Board Grades: 300–400gsm SBS (solid bleached sulphate), 350gsm CCNB (clay-coated newsback), 18–24pt coated unbleached kraft
  • Finishes: Soft-touch matte lamination (velvet tactile), gloss lamination, spot UV, aqueous coating, hot foil stamping (gold/silver/rose gold/holographic), embossing/debossing
  • Food-Safe Inks: Low-migration UV and conventional offset inks for direct food contact (when specified)
  • Daily Output: 1.7 million color boxes + 220,000+ gift boxes (post-2025 expansion)
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, GMI packaging color standards
