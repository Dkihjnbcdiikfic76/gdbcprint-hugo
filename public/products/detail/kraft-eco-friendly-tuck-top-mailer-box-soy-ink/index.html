Kraft Eco-Friendly Tuck-Top Mailer Box with Soy Ink Print

June 11, 2026

Product Overview

This eco-friendly kraft tuck-top mailer box by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing delivers sustainable packaging without compromising on print quality or structural integrity. Made from 100% recycled kraft board (350gsm–400gsm) with FSC Chain-of-Custody certification, it features soy-based ink printing for a fully biodegradable packaging solution. The tuck-top closure eliminates the need for tapes or adhesives, simplifying both assembly and recycling. With 22 years of manufacturing expertise and daily output of 1.7 million boxes from our 18,000 sqm facility, Bincai serves e-commerce brands, subscription box companies, and retailers seeking verified sustainable packaging.

Features

  • 100% Recycled Kraft: Post-consumer recycled content, fully biodegradable and curbside recyclable
  • Soy-Based Ink: Low-VOC, food-safe formulation; prints solid colors on kraft with Heidelberg 7+1 UV
  • FSC Certified: Chain-of-Custody certification ensures responsible forest sourcing
  • Tuck-Top Closure: Self-locking design — no tapes, glues, or additional closures needed
  • Board Grades: 350gsm, 380gsm, and 400gsm kraft board; lighter for inserts, heavier for structural boxes
  • Print Quality: 1-2 color flexo or CMYK offset on kraft surface
  • Size Range: 100×80×50mm to 450×350×200mm
  • Custom Options: Die-cut windows, tear strips, custom inserts, interior printing
  • Industry Applications: E-commerce shipping, subscription boxes, organic food, cosmetics, apparel
  • Material Certifications: FSC, REACH, RoHS compliant

Factory

Bincai’s 18,000 sqm factory in Shunde, Foshan integrates sustainable manufacturing practices across all production lines. Our KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses run soy-based inks with inline spectrophotometry for consistent color on kraft substrates. Die-cutting via Bobst flatbed ensures clean edges and precise fold lines. An 80+ person skilled workforce and ISO 9001:2015 quality system maintain consistent output across orders large and small.
