Custom Magnetic Closure Wine Bottle Gift Box

June 11, 2026

Product Overview

This custom magnetic closure wine bottle gift box is engineered specifically for 750ml standard wine and spirits bottles. The precision-cut EVA foam insert cradles the bottle securely, while the N35-grade magnetic closure provides a premium unboxing experience. Produced by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing — manufacturing premium packaging since 2003 — each box uses 2.5mm greyboard for structural integrity and 200gsm art paper wrap for a refined finish. Our 18,000 sqm facility with daily output of 1.7 million boxes ensures consistent quality at any order volume. Ideal for wineries, spirits brands, corporate gifting, and premium retail.

Features

  • Bottle-Specific Design: Fits standard 750ml Bordeaux, Burgundy, and spirits bottles; custom sizes available
  • Magnetic Closure: 6 × N35 neodymium magnets for secure closure on vertically stored bottles
  • Foam Insert: CNC-cut EVA foam in black or white, available as full-bottle holder or side-cradle
  • Two-Piece Construction: Separate lid and base for elegant presentation
  • Material Options: 2.5mm greyboard standard; 3.0mm available for extra-heavy bottles
  • Exterior Finish: Matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch, leatherette wrapping
  • Foil Stamping: Gold, silver, rose gold, or copper on lid top and spine
  • Ribbon Closure: Optional satin ribbon tie as secondary closure mechanism
  • Print: Heidelberg 7+1 UV for crisp foil registration and vibrant CMYK
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody

Factory

Located in Shunde, Foshan, Guangdong, Bincai’s 18,000 sqm integrated factory houses KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses alongside Bobst flatbed die-cutters and automated magnetic box assembly lines. Our 80+ skilled workers and 15 quality testing instruments ensure every box passes material inspection, magnet pull-force testing (≥2.5 N/cm² per magnet), drop testing per ISTA 1A, and AQL statistical sampling. 3-5 day pre-production samples available.
