Magnetic Closure Gift Box with Removable Insert Tray

June 10, 2026

Product Overview

A premium magnetic closure gift box featuring a removable insert tray — the ultimate packaging solution for multi-product gift sets, sampler collections, and curated brand experiences. The removable tray allows customers to lift out the entire product array in one elegant motion, revealing often a second layer beneath. Manufactured at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. using Heidelberg 7+1 UV press printing and precision hand assembly, each box delivers the kind of unboxing experience that drives social media sharing and repeat purchases.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Box construction: 2.5mm greyboard core, 157gsm art paper wrap, magnetic closure with hidden neodymium magnets
  • Removable tray: Die-cut 2.5mm greyboard with velvet flocking, satin lining, or custom-printed paper wrap
  • Tray cavities: Precision die-cut — square, rectangular, circular, or custom contour; depth from 10mm to 80mm
  • Printing: Heidelberg 7+1 UV press — CMYK + spot color + UV coating in single pass
  • Magnets: 4 hidden neodymium magnets (10mm × 1.5mm), 1.5kg total hold force
  • Daily output: 220,000+ rigid boxes

Customization Options

  • Size: Box from 150mm × 150mm × 50mm to 450mm × 350mm × 120mm; tray fits inside with 2mm clearance
  • Tray layers: Single tray, double stacked trays, tray + base compartment
  • Cavities: 1-12 cavities per tray; customizable shape for each product type
  • Tray lining: Velvet flocking (black/white/custom), satin fabric, suede, or printed paper
  • Box finish: Matte lamination + spot UV, soft-touch coating, linen texture, leatherette paper
  • Foil stamping: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper — on lid exterior and/or tray surface
  • Ribbon: Optional satin ribbon pull-tab for easy tray removal

Applications

Designed for: luxury skincare gift sets, fragrance discovery sets, premium tea collections, corporate gift boxes, wedding party favors, jewelry multi-piece sets, artisan chocolate assortments, and limited-edition brand collaborations. The removable tray makes the packaging reusable, adding long-term brand presence in the customer’s home.

Production Quality

Every box undergoes 16 quality checkpoints: lid-to-base alignment (±0.5mm), magnet recess depth verification, tray fit tolerance (±1mm), cavity dimensional accuracy, flocking adhesion test, foil rub resistance, colorimetry to Pantone, and full assembly function test (5 open/close cycles minimum).

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the advantage of a removable insert tray in a gift box? A: The removable tray allows customers to lift out the entire product array in one elegant motion, often revealing a second layer beneath. This creates a memorable unboxing experience that drives social media sharing and makes the packaging reusable — extending brand presence in the customer’s home. Ideal for multi-product gift sets, sampler collections, and curated brand experiences.

Q: What tray lining options are available? A: Bincai offers velvet flocking (most popular for jewelry), satin fabric (luxury cosmetics), suede-effect paper (premium watches), and custom-printed paper wrap (branded interiors). All lining materials are available in black, white, cream, navy, and custom Pantone-matched colors. The tray is precision die-cut to your product dimensions with ±1mm tolerance.

Q: How many product cavities can the removable tray hold? A: 1 to 12 cavities per tray, with customizable shapes — square, rectangular, circular, or custom contour. We also offer double-stacked trays and tray-plus-base-compartment configurations for larger gift sets. Cavity depth ranges from 10mm to 80mm depending on product type.
