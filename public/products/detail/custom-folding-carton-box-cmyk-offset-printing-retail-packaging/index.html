Customization Options

  • Multiple carton styles: straight tuck end, reverse tuck end, auto-bottom, seal end
  • Window patching with PVC, PET, or biodegradable film for product visibility
  • Hang tab for retail pegboard display
  • Hot foil stamping with metallic, holographic, or matte foils
  • Embossing and debossing for logo and pattern enhancement

Production Specifications

  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press — high-speed, consistent color
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV press for specialty coatings and metallics
  • Auto die-cutting with precision steel rule dies (tolerance ±0.3mm)
  • High-speed folder-gluer lines for efficient carton assembly
  • Paper stock: 200gsm–450gsm coated art paper, C1S/C2S, kraft, specialty
  • Food-safe and low-migration inks available for food contact packaging
  • FSC Chain of Custody certified — sustainable forestry sourcing
