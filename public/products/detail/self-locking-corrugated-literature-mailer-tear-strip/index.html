Self-Locking Corrugated Literature Mailer with Tear Strip

June 12, 2026

Product Overview

The self-locking corrugated literature mailer eliminates the most frustrating part of e-commerce fulfillment — the tape gun. Snap-lock bottom and tuck-lock top closures require zero tape, zero adhesive, zero tools. A perforated tear strip on the top panel lets recipients open the mailer cleanly in one pull. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. — ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified, 22 years of packaging manufacturing — each mailer is precision die-cut from E-flute or B-flute corrugated board for crush resistance during transit. Our 18,000 sqm factory runs Bobst flatbed die-cutters producing 500,000+ mailers daily.

Design Features

  • Zero-Tape Assembly: Self-locking bottom (push-to-lock interlocking flaps) and tuck-lock top closure — no tape gun, no adhesive, no staples. Reduces fulfillment labor by 70%.
  • Perforated Tear Strip: Full-width perforation across the top panel — recipient pulls the tear strip and the mailer opens cleanly without scissors or tearing the box.
  • Crush-Resistant Corrugated: E-flute (1.2mm) for books/documents up to 1.5 kg or B-flute (2.5mm) for heavier items up to 5 kg — fluting provides air-cushion impact protection.
  • Flat-Ship Design: Ships flat in stacks of 100 — 8× more mailers per pallet vs. pre-assembled. Quick pop-up assembly at the fulfillment station.
  • Optional Return Strip: Second tear strip on the bottom panel for easy returns processing — reuse the same mailer for exchanges.

Applications

  • Book Fulfillment: Hardcover and paperback books — the E-flute mailer is the publishing industry standard for single-book shipments.
  • Catalog Mailers: Annual catalogs, lookbooks, and brand books mailed to customers and prospects.
  • Document Shipping: Contracts, certificates, legal documents — B-flute crush protection with professional presentation.
  • Art Print Mailers: Limited-edition prints, posters, and photographs — flat mailer with rigid board insert option.
  • Subscription Boxes: Monthly book clubs, document-of-the-month, and curated paper goods subscriptions.

Production Specifications

  • Die-Cutting: Bobst flatbed die-cutters — crease-to-cut registration ±0.3mm, tear strip perforation 3mm cut / 1mm tie at 6 teeth/cm
  • Board: E-flute (1.2mm caliper), B-flute (2.5mm), EB-flute (3.7mm) — single-wall or double-wall construction
