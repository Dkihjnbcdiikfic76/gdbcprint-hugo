Product Overview

The octagonal (8-sided) rigid gift box breaks free from the rectangular box paradigm with a geometric form that commands attention before it’s even opened. Hand-tied with a generous satin ribbon bow and secured by concealed neodymium magnets, this box transforms gift-giving into theater. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. — an ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certified factory with 22 years of luxury packaging expertise — each octagonal box requires specialized die-cutting, angle-calibrated wrapping, and precision magnet placement that only experienced artisans can execute. Our 10,000 sqm Foshan factory (plus 8,000 sqm smart factory expansion, 2025) produces 220,000+ rigid boxes daily on Heidelberg 7+1 UV and KBA 1050 presses.

Design Features

  • Octagonal Geometry: Eight equal sides create a symmetrical form that stands out on retail shelves and in social media unboxing content — 3× more Instagram engagement than rectangular boxes according to client analytics.
  • Hand-Tied Satin Ribbon: 25mm double-face satin ribbon in your choice of color — tied in a classic bow by our finishing artisans, not machine-applied. Each bow is hand-fluffed for consistent presentation.
  • Magnetic Closure: Two concealed N35 neodymium magnets (10mm × 3mm) embedded in the lid rim align with steel strike plates in the base — invisible from the exterior, satisfying magnetic snap on close.
  • Angle-Precision Wrapping: Each 135° octagonal corner requires CNC-scored greyboard and art paper wrapping with ±0.5° angle tolerance — a skill that takes 3+ months of training to master.
  • Optional Viewing Window: Die-cut lid window with PET film for product visibility without opening — ideal for perfume bottles, candles, and confectionery.

Applications

  • Premium Perfume & Fragrance: The octagonal form echoes classic perfume bottle stoppers — a natural design language for luxury fragrance brands.
  • Wedding Favors: Mini octagonal boxes as table favors filled with chocolates, Jordan almonds, or small keepsakes — custom-printed with couple’s names and wedding date in gold foil.
  • Jewelry Presentation: Engagement rings, bracelets, and fine jewelry presented in a box as special as its contents.
  • Limited Edition Collectibles: Numbered edition packaging for watches, figurines, and artist collaborations.
  • Luxury Confectionery: Premium chocolate assortments, macaron sets, and artisan candy collections.

Production Specifications

  • Die-Cutting: Custom octagonal die with 8-steel-rule blades, CNC-machined to ±0.1mm — Bobst Novacut 106-E (9,000 sheets/hr)
  • Greyboard: 1200-1600gsm greyboard, groove-scored at 135° on CNC grooving machine with depth tolerance ±0.05mm
  • Wrapping: Hand-wrapping on semi-automatic casing-in machines — 8 corners individually burnished for sharp definition
  • Magnet Placement: CNC-routed magnet cavities at 4 lid-rim positions, N35 magnets set with hot-melt adhesive
  • Ribbon Application: Hand-tied bow with consistent 3-loop presentation, ribbon ends heat-sealed to prevent fraying
  • QC Protocol: Angle measurement (±0.5° per corner), magnet pull force (>1.0 kg), ribbon pull strength (>8 kg), rub resistance
  • Daily Output: 220,000+ rigid boxes across all production lines
  • Lead Time: 10-12 days sample (complex geometry), 20-25 days mass production
