Recyclable Kraft Paper Gift Box with Magnetic Closure Eco-Luxury Packaging

June 11, 2026

Recyclable brown kraft paper rigid gift box with magnetic closure — designed by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. for brands that want luxurious presentation without compromising on sustainability. Made from 100% recycled greyboard and wrapped in natural kraft paper, the entire box is fully recyclable in standard paper waste streams. Printed with soy-based inks that are low-VOC and biodegradable, while the magnetic closure delivers the premium unboxing experience customers expect. The natural kraft aesthetic with subtle branding creates an authentic, artisanal feel that resonates with eco-conscious consumers. Produced across 10,000 sqm of factory space on Heidelberg 7+1 UV and KBA 1050 presses. Daily output capacity of 1.5 million boxes.

Applications

  • Sustainable fashion and apparel brands
  • Organic skincare and natural cosmetics
  • Artisanal food and beverage gift sets
  • Eco-friendly corporate gifts and event favors
  • Zero-waste subscription box packaging

Customization Options

  • Kraft shade: Natural brown, light kraft, dark kraft, or white kraft
  • Printing: Single-color soy ink, multi-color CMYK, or branded stamp
  • Finishing: Matte lamination (water-based), embossing/debossing (no foil)
  • Interior: Recycled cardboard insert, shredded kraft filler, or unbleached cotton lining
  • Closure: Single or dual neodymium magnets (N35-N52)
  • Add-ons: Jute twine wrap, seed paper tag, or branded kraft belly band

Production Specifications

  • Base material: 100% recycled 1200–1600 gsm greyboard, FSC certified
  • Wrapping: 128–200 gsm natural kraft paper (unbleached)
  • Ink: Soy-based offset ink — low VOC, biodegradable, food-safe option available
  • Printing: Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset with extended gamut for natural substrates
  • Adhesive: Water-based eco-adhesive, solvent-free
  • Assembly: Automatic rigid box line with clean magnet housing
  • Quality control: Recyclability verification (PTS method), magnet strength testing, kraft paper color consistency, soy ink adhesion test
