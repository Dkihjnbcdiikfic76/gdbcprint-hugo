Product Overview

Professional 6-bottle wine shipper box engineered for direct-to-consumer (DTC) wine fulfillment, winery club shipments, and e-commerce wine retail. The double-wall BC-flute corrugated construction (ECT-44, burst strength 275 psi) provides crush resistance through the parcel carrier network, while the custom molded pulp insert cradles each bottle individually — preventing glass-to-glass contact and absorbing transit vibration. Die-cut integrated handholds make carrier handling effortless, and the self-locking bottom eliminates tape for faster packing line throughput. ISTA 6-FedEx-A tested and certified for single-parcel wine shipment. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003) — 22 years of structural packaging expertise.

Applications

  • Winery DTC club shipments and tasting room fulfillment
  • Online wine retail and marketplace shipping (Vivino, Wine.com, brand websites)
  • Holiday and corporate wine gift shipping
  • Wine sample distribution to distributors, importers, and sommeliers
  • Wedding and event wine favor packaging
  • Cellar-to-consumer auction and collectible wine transport

Customization Options

  • Box Dimensions: Standard 6-bottle (340×240×320mm); customizable to 1, 2, 3, 4, or 12-bottle configurations
  • Liner Options: Kraft (standard), white top, or coated white for high-end branding
  • Printing: Full CMYK flexo printing on exterior — up to 3 colors, halftone up to 85 LPI; single-pass post-print option on Heidelberg for premium graphics
  • Pulp Insert: Standard molded pulp (recycled paper); upgrade to EPS foam for extreme temperature protection or die-cut EVA foam for luxury presentation
  • Closure: Self-locking bottom (standard), tuck-top, or RSC with tape strip
  • Branding: Printed logo, QR code for wine info, varietal checklist, or tasting notes on exterior panels
  • Handholds: Standard die-cut handholds; optional perforated tear-away for consumer-friendly opening
