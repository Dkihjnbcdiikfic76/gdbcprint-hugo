Die-Cut Folding Carton with Window Display Cosmetic Box

June 10, 2026

Custom die-cut folding carton with transparent window display for cosmetics, skincare, and beauty product packaging — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). The precision die-cut window with PET or PVC film lets consumers see the product inside before purchase, driving shelf appeal and retail conversion. KBA 1050 4-color offset press and Heidelberg 7+1 UV press deliver vibrant printing across our 10,000 sqm ISO 9001 & FSC certified factory. Daily output capacity of 1.7 million boxes with fully automatic folder-gluer lines.

Applications

  • Skincare product packaging: serums, creams, moisturizers, face masks
  • Cosmetics and makeup packaging: lipstick, eyeshadow palettes, foundation, compact powder
  • Fragrance and perfume secondary packaging
  • Hair care and personal care product boxes
  • Beauty gift sets and holiday cosmetic collections
  • Nail polish and nail art kit packaging
  • Travel-size and sample-size cosmetic packaging

Customization Options

  • Precision die-cut window with crystal-clear PET (rigid) or PVC (flexible) film
  • Custom window shapes: round, oval, rectangle, heart, star, or brand logo silhouette
  • Folding carton structure: reverse tuck, straight tuck, auto-lock bottom, or crash-lock base
  • CMYK offset printing with Pantone spot color matching for exact brand identity
  • Hot foil stamping in gold, silver, holographic, or custom metallic on logo and accents
  • Embossing and debossing for tactile 3D branding on carton panels
  • Soft-touch matte lamination for premium tactile feel — popular in luxury cosmetics
  • Spot UV gloss varnish to highlight logos, patterns, or product names
  • Inner trays and inserts: die-cut cardboard platforms, foam cutouts, or velour pads
  • Custom sizes from 30×30×30mm (lipstick box) to 300×200×100mm (gift set box)
  • Food-grade inks and coatings available for products with direct contact

Production Specifications

  • Presses: KBA 1050 4-color (15,000 sheets/hour) + Heidelberg 7+1 UV with instant curing
  • Board: 250-400gsm FBB (Folding Box Board), SBS (Solid Bleached Sulfate), or CCNB
  • Window film: 0.2-0.5mm PET or PVC, ultrasonically welded or glued to carton interior
  • Surface finishes: gloss/matte lamination, soft-touch, Spot UV, aqueous coating, hot foil
  • Die-cutting: laser-cut steel rule die with ±0.3mm tolerance for precision window alignment
  • Folder-gluer: fully automatic high-speed lines with window inspection video systems
  • Quality control: ISO 9001 documented; 100% visual inspection on cosmetic packaging orders
  • Lead time: 12-18 days for new custom folding cartons; 5-8 days for reprints
  • MOQ: 500 units per SKU; pre-production digital proofs with 3-day turnaround
