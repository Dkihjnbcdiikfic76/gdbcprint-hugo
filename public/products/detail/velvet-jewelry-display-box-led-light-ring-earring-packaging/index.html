Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s velvet jewelry display box with integrated LED light transforms jewelry presentation. As the magnetic flip lid opens, a soft LED illuminates the velvet interior, making rings and earrings sparkle with gallery-quality lighting. Premium velvet flocking, gold foil branding, and rigid construction create a luxury unboxing experience. Manufactured in our ISO 9001:2015 certified 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) — 22 years, Heidelberg 7+1 UV + KBA 1050 presses, 1.7M daily output.

  • Bridal & Engagement: Ring presentation boxes, bridal party jewelry gifts
  • Luxury Branding: Branded jewelry packaging for designer collections

Customization Options

  • Box Style: Magnetic flip lid, book-style, two-piece rigid, drawer, sliding lid
  • Sizes: Single ring (50×50×35mm), earring pair (80×60×35mm), multi-piece (200×150×50mm)
  • Exterior: Velvet paper (black, navy, burgundy, emerald, cream, pink), leatherette, linen
  • Interior: Velvet flocking, suede microfiber, satin — color-matched or contrast
  • Inserts: Die-cut foam for rings, earring card slots, necklace hooks, bracelet channels
