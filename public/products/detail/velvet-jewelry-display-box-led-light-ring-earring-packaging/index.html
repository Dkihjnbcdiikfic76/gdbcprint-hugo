Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s velvet jewelry display box with integrated LED light transforms jewelry presentation. As the magnetic flip lid opens, a soft LED illuminates the velvet interior, making rings and earrings sparkle with gallery-quality lighting. Premium velvet flocking, gold foil branding, and rigid construction create a luxury unboxing experience. Manufactured in our ISO 9001:2015 certified 10,000 sqm factory — 22 years, Heidelberg 7+1 UV + KBA 1050 presses, 1.7M daily output.

Applications

  • Jewelry Retail: In-store display, countertop presentation, window showcase
  • E-Commerce Packaging: Premium unboxing for online jewelry sales and Instagram-worthy packaging
  • Bridal & Engagement: Ring presentation boxes, bridal party jewelry gifts
  • Luxury Branding: Branded jewelry packaging for designer collections
  • Trade Shows: Eye-catching illuminated display for jewelry exhibitions
  • Gift Packaging: Birthday, anniversary, Valentine’s, Mother’s Day jewelry gifts
  • Custom Jewelers: Independent jewelry designer packaging with logo

Customization Options

  • Box Style: Magnetic flip lid, book-style, two-piece rigid, drawer, sliding lid
  • Sizes: Single ring (50×50×35mm), earring pair (80×60×35mm), multi-piece (200×150×50mm)
  • Exterior: Velvet paper (black, navy, burgundy, emerald, cream, pink), leatherette, linen
  • Interior: Velvet flocking, suede microfiber, satin — color-matched or contrast
  • Inserts: Die-cut foam for rings, earring card slots, necklace hooks, bracelet channels
  • LED: Warm white, cool white, or RGB — battery-powered (CR2032/AAA), USB rechargeable, or induction
  • LED Activation: Magnetic reed switch (auto-on when opened), touch sensor, or manual switch
  • Printing: CMYK offset + Pantone spot, gold/silver/rose gold foil stamping, embossing
  • Add-ons: Branded sleeve, ribbon pull, authentication card, care instructions pocket
