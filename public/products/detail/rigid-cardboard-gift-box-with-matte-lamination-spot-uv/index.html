Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s rigid cardboard gift box with matte lamination and spot UV creates a tactile luxury experience through contrast: the velvety soft-touch matte surface meets precisely registered high-gloss spot UV accents. Magnetic closure, custom foam/satin interior, and rigid 2.0–3.0mm board construction convey premium quality. Manufactured in our ISO 9001:2015 certified 18,000 sqm facility (10,000+8,000 smart factory) with Heidelberg 7+1 UV and KBA 1050 presses — 22 years, daily output 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • Luxury Corporate Gifts: Executive gift sets, award ceremony packaging, VIP appreciation
  • Premium Cosmetics: Skincare gift sets, fragrance collections, makeup palettes
  • Electronics & Tech: Premium earphones, smartwatches, accessories, gadget gift sets
  • Watches & Jewelry: Timepiece presentation, bracelet/necklace gift boxes
  • Wine & Spirits: Single bottle presentation, collector’s edition packaging
  • Limited Editions: Product launches, holiday collections, collaboration drops
  • Wedding & Event: Bridal party gifts, luxury favor boxes, table centerpieces

Customization Options

  • Style: Two-piece rigid, magnetic flip-lid, book-style, drawer, double-door
  • Sizes: Custom — 100×100×50mm to 400×300×150mm
  • Wrap Paper: Matte art paper (157gsm), soft-touch velvet paper, leatherette, linen texture, pearlescent
  • Lamination: Full matte with selective spot UV, soft-touch, or anti-scratch matte
  • Spot UV: High-gloss registered to logo, pattern, or full flood with matte negative space
  • Interior: Velvet flocking, satin lining, die-cut foam/EVA insert, cardboard tray, silk ribbon pull
  • Printing: CMYK offset + Pantone spot, Heidelberg 7+1 UV, full coverage or pattern
  • Finishes: Gold/silver/copper/rose gold foil stamping, blind embossing, debossing, registered emboss
  • Magnetic Closure: Hidden neodymium magnets, ribbon tie, or magnetic flap
