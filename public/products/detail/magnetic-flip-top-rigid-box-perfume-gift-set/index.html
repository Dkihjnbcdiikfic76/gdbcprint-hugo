Applications

  • Luxury perfume and eau de parfum gift sets
  • Fragrance discovery set packaging (4-8 miniature bottles)
  • Premium cologne and aftershave gift boxes
  • Branded perfume launch event packaging
  • High-end department store fragrance presentation

Customization Options

  • Flip-top lid with dual magnetic closure for secure seal
  • Full velvet or satin interior lining in custom Pantone colors
  • Die-cut foam or velvet cavities for individual bottle placement
  • Hot gold, silver, or rose gold foil stamping on lid and spine
  • Embossed/debossed brand logo on exterior
  • Satin pull ribbon for elegant lid lifting
  • Spot UV and matte lamination contrast finishing
  • Custom sizes for single bottle to multi-bottle gift sets
  • Window cutout with PET film for bottle preview

Production Specifications

  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press with inline aqueous coating
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV press for premium spot UV accents
  • Fully automatic rigid box production line
  • Greyboard core: 1200gsm–2500gsm for flip-top structural strength
  • Surface wrapping: 157gsm art paper, specialty textured stock
  • Interior: velvet flocking or satin fabric in custom colors
  • Magnets: neodymium N35 grade, 8mm–12mm diameter, 2-4 per box

Production Quality & Testing

  • Heidelberg Prinect Inpress closed-loop color management
  • ΔE ≤ 2.0 color accuracy across production
  • Magnet pull-force testing at 3.5–5.5N per magnet pair
  • Flip-top hinge cycle testing: 500+ open/close without degradation
  • Velvet rub-fastness testing to ISO 105-X12
  • 100% visual inspection under D50 standard lighting
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management
  • FSC-certified paperboard from sustainable sources
