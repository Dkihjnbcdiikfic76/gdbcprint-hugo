Jewelry Ring Display Box LED Spotlight Velvet Flocking Interior

June 11, 2026

Captivating jewelry ring display box with integrated LED spotlight and luxurious velvet flocking interior — designed to make every ring sparkle at first sight. Manufactured at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003), our 18,000 sqm Foshan factory runs KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses with Bobst foil stamping and automatic rigid box production lines, producing 220,000+ rigid gift boxes daily alongside 1.7 million color boxes. ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certified. The warm LED spotlight activates automatically upon opening via a magnetic reed switch, illuminating the ring from an optimal 45° angle for maximum diamond brilliance and gemstone fire. Every LED module undergoes 100% illumination testing and 10,000-cycle switch endurance verification.

  • Engagement & Wedding: Proposal ring presentation, wedding band exchange boxes, bridal party gift packaging — creates the unforgettable “big moment” reveal
  • Luxury Jewelry Retail: In-store display packaging for diamond rings, gemstone rings, and fine jewelry — elevates countertop presentation and justifies premium pricing
  • E-Commerce Unboxing: Direct-to-consumer fine jewelry shipments — transforms the unboxing moment into shareable social media content that builds brand equity
  • Jewelry Designer & Artisan: Independent jewelry designers and bespoke jewelers — professional packaging that matches the craftsmanship of the piece inside
  • Certification & Appraisal: GIA/IGI diamond certificate presentation boxes — secure, impressive packaging for certified gemstones
  • Anniversary & Milestone Gifts: 25th anniversary bands, eternity rings, milestone celebration gifts — packaging that marks the occasion
  • Trade Show & Exhibition: Jewelry trade fair display boxes — eye-catching LED lighting draws visitor attention from across the aisle
  • Corporate & Recognition: Service award rings, championship rings, corporate milestone gifts — elegant packaging for prestigious recognition jewelry

Production Specifications

  • Press: KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset at 15,000 sheets/hour for wrapping paper + Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV for premium registered graphics, spot UV, and specialty coatings
  • Board: Rigid greyboard 1200-1600gsm — double-wrapped construction with precision 90° corner formation, board thickness selected by box size for proportional aesthetics and structural integrity
  • Velvet Flocking: Electrostatic flocking process — 1.0-1.5mm nylon fiber length, ≥95% surface coverage density, adhesive curing at 80°C for permanent bond
  • Foil Stamping: Bobst SP 102 BMA automatic foil stamping press — brass die creation from customer artwork, ±0.15mm multi-color foil registration, 3,000 sheets/hour throughput
  • Magnetic Closure: N52-grade neodymium disc magnets (6-10mm diameter, 1-2mm thickness) — recessed-mounted between board layers, invisible from exterior, holding force 0.8-2.5 kg per magnet pair
  • LED Assembly: Pre-wired LED module with magnetic reed switch — inserted during interior assembly between velvet layer and greyboard, battery compartment with screw-secured or magnetic access panel
  • Assembly Line: Automatic rigid box line for board wrapping and corner formation + manual LED integration and interior flocking station + final QC inspection station
  • Daily Capacity: 220,000+ rigid gift boxes, of which 30,000+ are LED-equipped jewelry boxes
  • Lead Time: 12-18 working days for new tooling (die creation + sample approval) / 8-12 working days for repeat orders with existing tooling

Quality Assurance

  • LED Function: 100% illumination test — brightness ≥85% rated lumens, sustained for ≥200 hours continuous operation
  • Switch Reliability: 10,000 open/close activation cycles — zero switch failure, zero intermittent activation, zero delay >0.3 seconds
  • Battery Life Verification: 200-hour minimum tested runtime on CR2032 at 20°C ambient — accelerated aging test equivalent to 2 years shelf life
  • Ring Mount Stability: 30° forward tilt + 30° lateral tilt test — ring stays securely in mount with zero displacement or slippage
  • Velvet Adhesion: Cross-hatch tape test (ISO 2409) — zero fiber shedding, zero adhesive failure at flocking-substrate interface
  • Magnet Alignment: 100% visual inspection — lid closes flush to ≤0.3mm gap, magnetic attraction engages smoothly without lid slam
  • Foil Adhesion: Sutherland rub test — 50 double rubs with 4-lb weight, zero visible wear, zero color transfer
  • Corner Integrity: Drop test from 1 meter onto concrete — corners maintain 90° angle within ±1° tolerance, no board delamination
  • Color Consistency: Delta E ≤1.5 across production batches for velvet flocking and wrapping paper — verified by X-Rite spectrophotometer
  • ISO 9001:2015 quality gates at raw material receiving, board wrapping, flocking application, LED integration, and final inspection
