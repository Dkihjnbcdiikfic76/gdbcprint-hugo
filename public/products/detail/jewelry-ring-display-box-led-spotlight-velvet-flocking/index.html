Jewelry Ring Display Box LED Spotlight Velvet Flocking Interior

June 11, 2026

Captivating jewelry ring display box with integrated LED spotlight and luxurious velvet flocking interior — designed to make every ring sparkle at first sight. Manufactured at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003), our 10,000 sqm Foshan factory runs KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, producing 1.7 million boxes daily. ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified. The warm LED spotlight activates automatically upon opening, illuminating the ring from an optimal 45° angle for maximum brilliance.

Applications

  • Premium engagement ring and wedding band presentation
  • Luxury jewelry boutique retail display packaging
  • High-end ring brand unboxing experience packaging
  • Diamond and gemstone ring certification presentation
  • Anniversary and milestone ring gift packaging
  • Jewelry trade show and exhibition display boxes

Customization Options

  • Box Style: Book-style magnetic flap, two-piece lid-and-base, clamshell, pedestal lift-top
  • Exterior Wrapping: 128–200gsm art paper (matte, gloss, soft-touch velvet, linen-textured, pearlescent); PU leatherette
  • Foil Stamping: Gold, silver, rose gold, holographic — multi-level emboss/deboss on lid and spine
  • Finishes: Matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch coating, spot UV, textured embossing, edge gilding
  • Ring Mount: Vertical slit, horizontal groove, pedestal cone, or custom silicone ring holder
  • LED Lighting: Warm white (3000K), cool white (6000K), or RGB color options; touch-switch or lid-activated magnetic reed switch; 200+ hours battery life (CR2032 replaceable)
  • Interior Velvet: Electrostatic flocking in 12+ colors (black, ivory, navy, burgundy, blush pink); optional satin or suede lining
  • Size: 60×60×40mm single-ring to 200×150×50mm multi-ring display
  • Accessories: Certificate/document pocket, LED brightness dimmer, USB-C rechargeable variant

Production Specifications

  • Press: KBA Rapida 105 4-color at 15,000 sheets/hour + Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV
  • Board: Rigid greyboard 1200–1600gsm with precision double-wrapped construction
  • Velvet Flocking: Electrostatic flocking at 1.0–1.5mm fiber length, ≥95% coverage density
  • Foil Stamping: Bobst SP 102 BMA with ±0.15mm registration
  • LED Assembly: Pre-wired LED module inserted during interior assembly; each unit tested for illumination and switch function
  • Assembly: Automatic rigid box line + manual interior LED integration station
  • Daily Capacity: 200,000+ jewelry boxes
  • Lead Time: 12–18 working days new tooling; 8–12 days repeat orders

Quality Assurance

  • LED Function: 100% illumination test — brightness ≥85% rated lumens for ≥200 hours
  • Switch Reliability: 10,000 open/close activation cycles — zero switch failure
  • Ring Mount Stability: 30° tilt test — ring stays securely in mount, zero slippage
  • Velvet Adhesion: Cross-hatch tape test — zero fiber shedding
  • Magnet Alignment: 100% inspection — lid closes flush ≤0.3mm gap
  • Foil Adhesion: Sutherland rub test — 50 double rubs with 4-lb weight, no wear
  • ISO 9001:2015 quality gates at every production stage
  • FSC Chain-of-Custody certified sustainable materials
  • Free pre-production samples in 5–7 working days
