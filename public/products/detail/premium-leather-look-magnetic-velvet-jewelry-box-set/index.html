Premium Leather-Look Magnetic Velvet Jewelry Box Set

June 11, 2026

Product Overview

This premium jewelry box set from Guangdong Bincai Color Printing combines a sophisticated leather-look exterior with plush velvet interior for luxury jewelry presentation. The 3-piece nesting design includes a large necklace box, medium bracelet box, and small ring box — ideal for jewelry brands, retailers, and special occasion gifting. Each box features N35-grade magnetic closure and is wrapped in PU leather-look paper with gold foil logo stamping. With 22 years of manufacturing excellence since 2003, Bincai produces 220,000+ gift boxes daily from our 18,000 sqm integrated facility.

Features

  • Nesting Set Design: Large (180×120×50mm), Medium (120×80×40mm), Small (60×60×35mm)
  • Leather-Look Exterior: PU-coated art paper available in black, navy, burgundy, cream, and custom colors
  • Interior Velvet: Full flocking in matching or contrasting color — black, white, beige, grey
  • Magnetic Closure: 2 × N35 magnets per box for gentle, silent closure
  • Foil Stamping: Gold, silver, or rose gold foil on lid top with ≤0.1mm registration tolerance
  • Insert Options: EVA foam die-cut for specific jewelry shapes, satin pillows, display cards
  • Print Method: Heidelberg 7+1 UV for foil registration precision
  • Custom Shapes: Heart, hexagonal, octagonal, and book-style formats available
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody
  • Sample Service: 3-5 day pre-production samples with digital proofs

Factory

Bincai operates an 18,000 sqm facility in the Pearl River Delta’s manufacturing hub — Shunde, Foshan, Guangdong. Our production lines feature KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, Bobst flatbed die-cutters, and specialized jewelry box assembly stations with velvet flocking booths. Quality control includes 15-point inspection for jewelry boxes: material inspection, flocking density check, magnet pull-force (≥2.0 N/cm²), foil registration (±0.1mm), lid alignment (gap ≤1mm), drop test per ISTA 1A, and AQL sampling per ISO 2859-1. 80+ skilled workers, 15 testing instruments, free samples in 3-5 days.
