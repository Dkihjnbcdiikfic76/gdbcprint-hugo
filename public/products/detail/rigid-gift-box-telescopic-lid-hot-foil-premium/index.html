Custom Rigid Gift Box with Telescopic Lid Hot Foil Stamping

June 11, 2026

Custom rigid gift box with telescopic lid and hot foil stamping — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). The telescopic lid design features a two-piece construction where the lid extends partially over the base, creating an elegant reveal effect favored by premium cosmetic, jewelry, and corporate gift brands. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across 10,000 sqm factory floor. Daily output 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • Luxury cosmetic and skincare gift sets
  • Premium jewelry and watch presentation boxes
  • Corporate gift and executive thank-you packaging
  • High-end confectionery and chocolate gift boxes
  • Perfume and fragrance collection presentation

Customization Options

  • Telescopic lid depth: 30mm to 120mm for dramatic reveal or subtle overlay
  • Hot foil stamping in gold, silver, rose gold, copper, or holographic foil
  • Matte or gloss lamination with soft-touch velvet finish option
  • Embossing and debossing for dimensional brand logos
  • Spot UV on logo or pattern for high-gloss contrast on matte background
  • Magnetic closure insert for secure lid retention
  • Custom foam, velvet, or satin interior inserts with die-cut cavities
  • Board thickness: 1200gsm–2000gsm greyboard with 128gsm–157gsm art paper wrap

Production Specifications

  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press for high-speed precision printing
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV press for specialty coatings and instant curing
  • Fully automatic rigid box production line with corner taping
  • Hot foil stamping: Bobst BMA foil stampers with temperature-precision control
  • Board caliper: 1.5mm–3.0mm finished wall thickness
  • MOQ: 500 units per design; lead time: 15–20 working days
