Magnetic Closure Gift Box for Premium Chocolate and Confectionery

June 10, 2026

Custom magnetic closure gift box for premium chocolate and confectionery — produced by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a 22-year packaging specialist (founded 2003) operating KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses on a 10,000 sqm factory floor. Designed for chocolatiers, patisseries, and gourmet food brands, this magnetic lid box features food-safe interior materials, a custom PET tray insert for individual chocolate cavities, and elegant gold foil branding. Daily output 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • Handcrafted artisan chocolate gift sets (truffles, pralines, bonbons)
  • Seasonal holiday chocolate collections (Christmas, Valentine’s Day, Lunar New Year)
  • Macaron and French patisserie presentation boxes
  • Gourmet cookie and biscuit gift packaging
  • Premium tea and confectionery pairing gift sets
  • Wedding favor and celebration confectionery boxes
  • Corporate gourmet food gift programs

Customization Options

  • Magnetic flip-cover lid with rigid board construction
  • Food-safe PET, PVC, or cardboard insert trays with precision-cut cavities
  • Gold, silver, or copper hot foil stamping for brand decoration
  • CMYK offset printing with Pantone spot color matching
  • Soft-touch matte or high-gloss lamination finish
  • Embossing and debossing for tactile branding
  • Spot UV for contrast effects on matte backgrounds
  • Die-cut window with clear PET film for product visibility
  • Ribbon tie closure for dual security + decorative accent
  • Biodegradable and FSC-certified board options
  • Custom sizes for 4-piece, 8-piece, 12-piece, 16-piece assortments
