Magnetic Closure Gift Box for Premium Chocolate and Confectionery

June 10, 2026

Custom magnetic closure gift box for premium chocolate and confectionery — produced by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a 22-year packaging specialist (founded 2003) operating KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across 18,000 sqm of factory floor (10,000+8,000 smart factory). Designed for chocolatiers, patisseries, and gourmet food brands, this magnetic lid box features food-safe interior materials, a custom PET tray insert with precision-cut cavities for individual chocolates, and elegant gold foil branding on the exterior. Each box is a statement of luxury — transforming premium confections into unforgettable gifting experiences. Daily output of 1.7 million boxes ensures reliable supply for seasonal peaks and brand rollouts.

Applications

This magnetic closure confectionery box serves the premium food gifting sector:

  • Artisan chocolate gift sets — truffles, pralines, ganaches, and bonbons in custom cavity layouts
  • Seasonal holiday collections — Christmas, Valentine’s Day, Lunar New Year, Easter, and Mid-Autumn Festival editions
  • Macaron and French patisserie presentation — delicate pastries require rigid-box protection with soft-touch interiors
  • Gourmet cookie and biscuit gift packaging — butter cookies, shortbread, and luxury snacks for corporate gifting
  • Premium tea and confectionery pairing sets — dual-compartment inserts for tea sachets + chocolates
  • Wedding favor and celebration boxes — personalized with couple names, dates, and foil-stamped motifs
  • Corporate gourmet food gift programs — branded with company logo, ideal for client appreciation and employee rewards

Customization Options

Every element of this magnetic closure box is customizable to your brand identity and product requirements:

  • Magnetic flip-cover lid with 2.0mm–2.5mm rigid greyboard construction — satisfying magnetic snap closure
  • Food-safe PET, PVC, or cardboard insert trays with precision-cut cavities to cradle each chocolate or confection
  • Gold, silver, or copper hot foil stamping — Heidelberg cylinder press with ±0.1mm registration for crisp metallic branding
  • CMYK offset printing with Pantone spot color matching — KBA 1050 delivers 15,000 sheets/hour at 200 LPI resolution
  • Surface finishes: soft-touch matte (velvet feel), high-gloss lamination, or combination finishes
  • Embossing and debossing — raised or recessed logo/text for tactile luxury that consumers love to touch
  • Spot UV on matte backgrounds — dramatic contrast that highlights logos, patterns, or product photography
  • Die-cut window with clear PET film for product visibility — show the product without opening the box
  • Ribbon tie closure for dual security + decorative accent — satin, grosgrain, or custom-printed ribbon
  • Biodegradable and FSC-certified board options — sustainable luxury for eco-conscious chocolate brands
  • Custom sizes for 4-piece, 6-piece, 8-piece, 12-piece, 16-piece, and 24-piece assortments

Production Workflow

  1. Prepress & Design — artwork review, color separation, and CTP plate making with digital proof for color approval
  2. Board Laminating — greyboard laminated with selected art paper on high-speed sheet laminator
  3. Offset Printing — KBA 1050 CMYK printing at 15,000 sheets/hour with inline spectrophotometer color monitoring
  4. Finishing — lamination (gloss/matte/soft-touch), then hot foil stamping and embossing on Heidelberg cylinder press
  5. UV Specialty — Heidelberg 7+1 UV for spot UV accents, white underprint, and high-gloss effects
  6. Die-Cutting — flatbed die-cutter shapes the box blank with cavity holes for tray insertion
  7. Tray Forming — PET/PVC trays are thermoformed or CNC-cut, then inserted and bonded
  8. Magnet Insertion — neodymium magnets embedded into board layers with precise polarity alignment
  9. Assembly & Folding — box is folded, magnet closure tested, and flat-packed for efficient shipping
  10. QC Inspection — 100% visual check: foil registration, magnet strength, tray fit, surface finish uniformity

Food Safety & Quality Assurance

All food-contact materials — PET trays, interior paper liners, adhesives — comply with FDA 21 CFR and EU Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004 for food-contact articles. Paperboard is sourced from FSC-certified mills with full chain-of-custody traceability. Inline QC checks at every stage verify cavity precision (±0.5mm), magnetic closure strength (minimum 3N pull force), hot foil registration (±0.1mm), and surface finish consistency. Optional features include inner wax paper or greaseproof lining for direct food contact, tamper-evident sealing, and desiccant packet compartments for humidity-sensitive confections.
