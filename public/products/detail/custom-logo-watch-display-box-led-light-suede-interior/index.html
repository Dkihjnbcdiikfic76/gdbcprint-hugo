Custom Logo Watch Display Box with LED Light and Suede Interior

June 10, 2026

Luxury custom logo watch display box with integrated LED light and premium suede interior — engineered to elevate the unboxing moment for discerning watch enthusiasts. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003), our 18,000 sqm Foshan factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) runs KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, producing 1.7 million boxes daily. ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified. The magnetic reed switch activates warm LED illumination the moment the lid opens — no buttons, no cables, just an instant premium presentation.

Features

  • Integrated LED light strip with magnetic reed switch — activates automatically on open, deactivates on close
  • Premium suede or electrostatic velvet flocking interior lining in 8+ colors
  • Removable watch pillow with adjustable cushion for any band size (16–24cm wrist)
  • N52-grade neodymium magnets for satisfying, secure magnetic closure
  • Custom logo embossing, debossing, or multi-level gold/silver foil stamping
  • High-gloss piano lacquer finish or matte soft-touch textured exterior
  • ISO 9001:2015 & FSC certified quality management — full chain-of-custody
  • MOQ 300 units, trial orders accepted, scalable to 50,000+ unit production runs
  • Free pre-production samples (freight collect) within 5–7 working days

Applications

  • Luxury watch brand retail packaging and boutique display
  • Corporate gift and executive appreciation watch sets
  • Watch collector display cases and home storage solutions
  • E-commerce premium watch shipping with protective foam insert
  • Watchmaker and jeweler presentation cases
  • Trade show, exhibition, and pop-up store product display
  • Wedding party groomsmen gift packaging
  • Limited edition and commemorative watch packaging

Customization Options

  • Configuration: Single, double, triple, or 6-watch collector slot configurations
  • LED Color: Warm white 3000K (classic), cool white 6000K (contemporary), or RGB programmable with remote
  • LED Power: Replaceable CR2032 battery (200+ hours), AAA battery pack, or USB-C rechargeable module
  • Exterior: PU leatherette (black, brown, cognac, navy), linen-textured paper wrap, velvet-covered MDF, or handcrafted wood veneer (walnut, oak, rosewood)
  • Exterior Finish: High-gloss piano lacquer, matte soft-touch, satin aqueous varnish, or natural wood oil/wax
  • Foil Stamping: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper, holographic — flat or multi-level emboss/deboss on lid exterior and spine
  • Interior Lining: Suede (black, grey, beige, navy, brown, burgundy), electrostatic velvet flocking, satin jacquard
  • Watch Pillow: Suede-wrapped foam, velvet-covered, or leather-trimmed; removable with snap or Velcro attachment
  • Additional Compartments: Drawer for straps/tools, document/certificate pocket, accessory tray for cufflinks
  • Size: 100×100×80mm single to 350×250×100mm multi-watch collector
  • Personalization: Individual name plaque engraving, numbered limited edition plates, QR code authenticity labels

Production Specifications

  • Press: KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset at 15,000 sheets/hour + Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV for premium finishes
  • Board: Rigid greyboard 1200–1600gsm or 9–12mm MDF — double-wrapped construction with precise ±0.3mm edge alignment
  • Foil Stamping: Bobst SP 102 BMA with ±0.15mm registration, multi-zone temperature control for consistent foil transfer
  • Piano Finish: Multi-coat UV-cured lacquer system — 3 base coats + 2 top coats, polished between layers for mirror gloss
  • Suede Application: Water-based adhesive bonding with heat-press curing at 60°C — zero peeling at corners and edges
  • LED Assembly: Pre-wired LED strip module inserted and tested during interior assembly; magnetic reed switch soldered and stress-relieved
  • Assembly: Automatic rigid box line for body construction + manual interior/LED integration station
  • Daily Capacity: 200,000+ jewelry and watch boxes
  • Lead Time: 12–18 working days new tooling; 8–12 days repeat orders

Quality Assurance

  • LED Function: 100% illumination test — brightness ≥85% rated lumens sustained for ≥200 hours continuous operation
  • Switch Reliability: 10,000 open/close activation cycles — zero reed switch failure, zero intermittent flicker
  • Watch Pillow Fit: Compression/recovery test — pillow returns to ≥95% original shape after 72 hours under 500g load
  • Suede Bond: Cross-hatch tape test per ASTM D3359 — 5B rating, zero fiber pull-out
  • Magnet Alignment: 100% inspection — lid closes flush, ≤0.3mm gap on all four corners
  • Piano Finish: 60° gloss meter — ≥90 GU; cross-hatch adhesion test — ≤5% coating removal
  • Foil Adhesion: Sutherland rub test — 50 double rubs with 4-lb weight, zero visible wear
  • Drop Test: ISTA 1A — 10 drops from 760mm on all faces/corners, no structural failure
