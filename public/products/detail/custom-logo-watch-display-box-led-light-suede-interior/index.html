Custom Logo Watch Display Box with LED Light and Suede Interior

June 10, 2026

Luxury custom logo watch display box with integrated LED light and premium suede interior — engineered to elevate the unboxing moment for discerning watch enthusiasts. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003), our 10,000 sqm Foshan factory runs KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, producing 1.7 million boxes daily. ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified. The magnetic reed switch activates warm LED illumination the moment the lid opens — no buttons, no cables, just an instant premium presentation.

  • Configuration: Single, double, triple, or 6-watch collector slot configurations
  • LED Color: Warm white 3000K (classic), cool white 6000K (contemporary), or RGB programmable with remote
  • LED Power: Replaceable CR2032 battery (200+ hours), AAA battery pack, or USB-C rechargeable module
  • Exterior: PU leatherette (black, brown, cognac, navy), linen-textured paper wrap, velvet-covered MDF, or handcrafted wood veneer (walnut, oak, rosewood)
  • Exterior Finish: High-gloss piano lacquer, matte soft-touch, satin aqueous varnish, or natural wood oil/wax
  • Foil Stamping: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper, holographic — flat or multi-level emboss/deboss on lid exterior and spine
  • Interior Lining: Suede (black, grey, beige, navy, brown, burgundy), electrostatic velvet flocking, satin jacquard
  • Watch Pillow: Suede-wrapped foam, velvet-covered, or leather-trimmed; removable with snap or Velcro attachment
  • Additional Compartments: Drawer for straps/tools, document/certificate pocket, accessory tray for cufflinks
  • Size: 100×100×80mm single to 350×250×100mm multi-watch collector
  • Personalization: Individual name plaque engraving, numbered limited edition plates, QR code authenticity labels

Production Specifications

  • Press: KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset at 15,000 sheets/hour + Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV for premium finishes
  • Board: Rigid greyboard 1200–1600gsm or 9–12mm MDF — double-wrapped construction with precise ±0.3mm edge alignment
  • Foil Stamping: Bobst SP 102 BMA with ±0.15mm registration, multi-zone temperature control for consistent foil transfer
  • Piano Finish: Multi-coat UV-cured lacquer system — 3 base coats + 2 top coats, polished between layers for mirror gloss
  • Suede Application: Water-based adhesive bonding with heat-press curing at 60°C — zero peeling at corners and edges
  • LED Assembly: Pre-wired LED strip module inserted and tested during interior assembly; magnetic reed switch soldered and stress-relieved
  • Assembly: Automatic rigid box line for body construction + manual interior/LED integration station
  • Daily Capacity: 200,000+ jewelry and watch boxes
  • Lead Time: 12–18 working days new tooling; 8–12 days repeat orders
