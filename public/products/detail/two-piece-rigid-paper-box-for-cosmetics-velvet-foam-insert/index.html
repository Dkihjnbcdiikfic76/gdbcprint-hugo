Two-Piece Rigid Paper Box for Cosmetics Velvet Foam Insert

June 10, 2026

Premium two-piece rigid paper box with velvet lining and custom die-cut foam insert for luxury cosmetics packaging — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. With 22 years of manufacturing experience, ISO 9001 & FSC certifications, and a 10,000 sqm factory operating KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, we produce rigid set-up boxes that combine structural integrity with luxury finishes for the world’s leading cosmetics brands.

Applications

Two-piece rigid boxes with foam inserts provide the ultimate protection and presentation for high-value cosmetic products:

  • Luxury Skincare: Serum bottles, cream jars, face oil sets, anti-aging treatment kits, travel-size collections
  • Fragrance & Perfume: Eau de parfum bottles, perfume gift sets, solid perfume compacts, scented candle trios
  • Color Cosmetics: Eyeshadow palettes, lipstick sets, foundation compacts, powder cases, brush collections
  • Beauty Gift Sets: Holiday limited editions, Mother’s Day/Valentine’s Day collections, brand launch kits
  • Professional Salon: Hair care sets, nail art kits, professional makeup artist palettes
  • Men’s Grooming: Shaving kits, beard oil sets, cologne gift collections
  • CBD & Wellness: Tincture sets, wellness oil collections, aromatherapy roller sets

Customization Options

  • Box Structure: Classic two-piece (lid + base), shoulder-neck (telescope with inner neck), book-style with magnetic flap, drawer slide box, hexagonal or octagonal shapes
  • Outer Wrapping: 128–200gsm art paper in matte, gloss, soft-touch velvet, linen-textured, pearlescent, or metallic finish; specialty papers (Kraft, uncoated, recycled) available
  • Printing: Full CMYK + 7 PMS spot colors, gold/silver/rose gold foil stamping, embossed logo and patterns, spot UV (gloss + matte), glitter UV, holographic cold foil
  • Interior Lining: Velvet flocking (polyester, 0.8–1.5mm pile), premium suede (microfiber), satin (polyester or silk blend), brushed nylon, custom-printed paper lining
  • Foam Insert: Die-cut EVA foam, PU foam, or flocked foam in any cavity configuration — single cavity, multi-compartment, layered trays; foam color matching to brand palette
  • Custom Colors: 12+ standard velvet colors (black, navy, burgundy, cream, grey, champagne, royal blue, emerald, blush pink, chocolate, white, charcoal) plus custom Pantone-matching
  • Add-Ons: Satin ribbon pull-tab, magnetic closure, gold edge gilding, foil-stamped interior logo, printed insert card, outer sleeve/obi band
  • Size: Fully custom — from 40×40×30mm for compacts to 400×300×120mm for multi-product gift sets

Production Specifications

  • Rigid Box Line: Fully automatic rigid box production — greyboard cutting, corner taping, wrapping, and assembly in a continuous line
  • Greyboard: 1200–1800gsm density options; single-ply for standard boxes, double-ply laminated for extra structural strength
  • Corner Wrapping: Precision 4-corner + all-edge wrapping with 90° sharp corners; rounded corners available (R3–R20 radius)
  • Foam Insert Die-Cutting: CNC knife cutting ±0.2mm tolerance for precise cavity fit; kiss-cut foam layers for depth control
  • Velvet Application: Hot-melt or water-based adhesive with temperature-controlled lamination; wrinkle-free guarantee on all visible surfaces
  • KBA 1050: Outer wrap printing at 15,000 sheets/hour, up to 1050×740mm sheet size
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV: 7 colors + UV coating in one pass for elaborate outer wrap designs
  • Lead Time: 10–15 working days; 5–7 days for repeat orders with existing tooling
  • Daily Capacity: 50,000–80,000 rigid set-up boxes per day

Quality Details

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified — full production traceability and documented QC checkpoints at each stage
  • FSC Chain-of-Custody certified — sustainable paper and board sourcing throughout the supply chain
  • Structural Integrity: 100% drop test sampling — 76cm free-fall (6 faces, 3 edges, 1 corner) per ISTA 1A
  • Velvet Adhesion: Accelerated aging test (72h at 57°C / 95% RH) — no peeling, bubbling, or delamination
  • Foam Insert Fit: Cavity tolerance ±0.5mm; 100% fit-check with actual product sample before mass production
  • Print Registration: ±0.1mm foil-to-print alignment; D50 5000K light booth inspection
  • Odor Testing: Residual solvent and adhesive odor test per GB/T — safe for food-adjacent and direct skin-contact cosmetics
  • Free pre-production sample with full decoration — 5–7 working day turnaround
