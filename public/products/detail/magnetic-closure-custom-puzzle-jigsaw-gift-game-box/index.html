Premium magnetic closure puzzle gift box for jigsaw puzzles, board games, and collectible toys — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a Diamond Member on Made-in-China.com since 2015. This book-style magnetic flap box features full-color CMYK exterior printing with game board artwork or custom brand graphics, a precision die-cut EVA foam insert with puzzle piece cavities, and a satin ribbon pull for elegant opening. The rigid greyboard construction protects puzzle pieces during retail display and storage while the magnetic closure keeps contents secure. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across our 18,000 sqm factory.

Applications

  • Jigsaw puzzle gift sets (500/1000/2000 piece puzzles)
  • Family board game packaging
  • Educational and STEM toy gift boxes
  • Collectible card game (CCG) storage boxes
  • Children’s puzzle and activity kit packaging
  • Custom photo puzzle memorial and wedding gift boxes
  • Escape room game kit packaging
  • 3D wooden puzzle model kit boxes

Customization Options

  • Printing: full-color CMYK offset, 1-4 PMS spot colors, or premium UV printing
  • Exterior finish: matte lamination, glossy lamination, soft-touch velvet, or textured linen
  • Hot foil stamping: gold, silver, copper, holographic, or custom metallic foil
  • Embossing/debossing: raised title lettering, blind debossed pattern, textured game board feel
  • Foam insert: EVA (ethylene-vinyl acetate) in custom density and color, custom-cut cavities
  • Ribbon: satin, grosgrain, cotton, or branded ribbon with woven logo
  • Box size: custom dimensions for any puzzle format — square, rectangular, round, or custom shape
  • Additional features: clear PET display window, magnetic easel stand, drawer compartments

Production Specifications

  • Presses: KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset + Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV
  • Die-cutting: Bobst Autoplaten die-cutter — precise cavity and box structure cutting
  • Foam fabrication: In-house CNC routing for EVA/PU foam inserts with ±0.5mm tolerance
  • Gluing: Automatic folder-gluer with magnetic pocket insertion station
  • QC: 3-stage inspection — incoming material, inline production, final AQL 2.5 sampling
  • Daily output: 1.7 million boxes across all production lines
