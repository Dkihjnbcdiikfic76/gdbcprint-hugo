Premium double wall heavy duty corrugated shipping box in custom sizes — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. With 22 years of experience, ISO 9001 & FSC certifications, and a 10,000 sqm factory producing 1.5 million boxes daily, we deliver industrial-grade packaging that protects your products through the toughest supply chains.

Applications

Double wall corrugated boxes provide maximum crush resistance for heavy, fragile, or high-value shipments. Ideal for:

  • E-Commerce Fulfillment: Heavy product shipping, multi-item orders, subscription boxes requiring extra protection
  • Electronics & Appliances: TVs, monitors, computer equipment, kitchen appliances, sensitive electronics
  • Automotive Parts: Engine components, body panels, filters, aftermarket parts distribution
  • Industrial Equipment: Machinery components, tools, hardware, factory-to-distributor shipments
  • Furniture: Flat-pack furniture, home décor, lighting fixtures, fragile decorative items
  • Food & Beverage Logistics: Bulk food ingredients, bottled beverages, catering supplies
  • Pharmaceuticals & Medical: Medical device shipping, laboratory equipment, temperature-sensitive products

Customization Options

  • Flute Construction: BC-flute (6–7mm, standard heavy duty), AB-flute (8–9mm, extra heavy), or custom flute combinations for specific compression needs
  • Board Grades: 200# to 350# test (bursting strength); ECT 32 to ECT 71 (edge crush); weather-resistant and water-resistant board available
  • Printing: Flexo printing (1–3 colors), high-graphic preprint with litho-laminated top sheet for retail-ready appearance; full-color digital for short runs
  • Closure: Regular slotted container (RSC), half-slotted (HSC), full telescope (FTD), self-locking bottom, tear-strip opening, perforated return panels
  • Features: Die-cut handles, ventilation holes, divider inserts, foam cushioning, anti-static coating for electronics
  • Size: Fully custom — from small 200×150×100mm mailers to large 1200×800×600mm industrial containers

Production Specifications

  • Corrugator: In-house single-facer and double-backer for A, B, C, E, F, and combined flutes
  • Die-Cutting: Rotary and flatbed die-cutting for precise slotting and scoring
  • Flexo Printing: 3-color in-line flexo with automatic stacker; high-graphic preprint via litho lamination
  • Gluing/Stitching: Automatic folder-gluer with gluing, taping, and wire stitching options
  • Daily Capacity: 800,000 sqm of corrugated board output per day
  • Lead Time: 10–15 working days for custom sizes; 5–7 days for standard sizes with printing

Quality Assurance

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified — material traceability from paper roll to finished box
  • FSC Chain of Custody — responsible sourcing throughout the supply chain
  • Board Testing: Edge crush test (ECT) per TAPPI T-839, burst strength (Mullen) per TAPPI T-810, flat crush (FCT) per ISO 3035
  • Box Compression: BCT (box compression test) per ISO 12048 — we certify weight capacity before production
  • Environmental Conditioning: Pre- and post-conditioning at 23°C / 50% RH per TAPPI T-402
  • Drop Testing: ISTA 3A and ISTA 1A simulated drop and vibration testing for e-commerce certification
  • Free pre-production sample for structural approval before full production
