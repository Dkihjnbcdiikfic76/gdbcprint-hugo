Corrugated insulated meal kit shipper for frozen food subscription boxes by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. — a Diamond Member on Made-in-China.com with 22 years of manufacturing excellence (founded 2003). Double-wall BC or EB flute corrugated construction with integrated thermal insulation liner keeps contents frozen for 24–48 hours during transit. Gel pack compartments and tear-strip easy-open design for a premium unboxing experience. Produced on our BHS 2.5m Corrugator line and KBA 1050 4-color Flexo press across our 18,000 sqm facility (10,000+8,000 smart factory, 2025 expansion). ISO 9001:2015 and FSC® certified.

  • Cold chain pharmaceutical and medical shipping
  • Perishable food e-commerce (meat, seafood, dairy)

Quality Control Gates

  1. Paper Roll Inspection — Burst strength (≥200 PSI), edge crush test (ECT ≥32 lbs/in), moisture content
  2. Corrugator Monitoring — Flute height, bond strength, caliper inline on BHS digital control
  3. Print Registration — Flexo registration within ±1mm, color density to Pantone standards
  4. Die-Cutting — Score depth and slot accuracy, ±0.5mm tolerance
  5. Insulation Assembly — Liner adhesion, gel pack fit, tear-strip perforation test
  6. Cold Chain Validation — ISTA 7E thermal profiling, 24–48 hour temperature hold test
  7. Final QC — AQL 2.5 Level II, drop test (1.2m, 6 faces), compression test
