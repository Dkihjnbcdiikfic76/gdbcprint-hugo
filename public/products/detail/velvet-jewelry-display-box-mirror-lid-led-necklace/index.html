Velvet Jewelry Display Box with Mirror Lid and LED Strip for Necklace Packaging

June 11, 2026

Luxurious velvet jewelry display box with an integrated mirror lid and LED strip lighting — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. The mirror insert inside the lid allows customers to view themselves wearing the jewelry piece instantly upon opening, while the soft LED strip enhances presentation and highlights the brilliance of gemstones, gold, and silver. The velvet-flocked interior with a custom necklace insert keeps the jewelry secure and tangle-free. Powered by a discreet battery compartment, the LED activates automatically when the lid is opened via a magnetic reed switch. Produced on KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset presses across 10,000 sqm of factory space. Daily output capacity of 1.5 million boxes.

Applications

  • Necklace and pendant retail packaging
  • Luxury jewelry brand presentation boxes
  • Bridal and wedding jewelry gift sets
  • Trade show and exhibition jewelry display
  • High-end e-commerce jewelry shipping

Customization Options

  • Exterior material: Velvet (black, navy, burgundy, emerald), PU leather, or linen-textured paper
  • Interior: Custom die-cut velvet/Foam insert for specific jewelry shapes
  • Mirror: Full-lid mirror or inset mirror with branded etching
  • LED: Warm white (3000K), cool white (6000K), or RGB color-changing
  • Branding: Gold/silver foil logo stamping, debossing, or metallic plate
  • Closure: Magnetic flap or ribbon-tie with satin ribbon

Production Specifications

  • Base material: 1200–1600 gsm greyboard, wrapped in velvet-flocked paper
  • Mirror: 2 mm high-clarity glass mirror, precisely cut and edge-polished
  • LED system: 3 mm LED strip, CR2032 battery powered, magnetic reed switch activation, 50+ hours battery life
  • Printing: Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset for exterior wrapping, foil stamping for logo
  • Assembly: Manual quality assembly with glue-free magnet housing
  • Quality control: LED function test (100%), mirror scratch inspection, velvet adhesion test, magnet closure force measurement
