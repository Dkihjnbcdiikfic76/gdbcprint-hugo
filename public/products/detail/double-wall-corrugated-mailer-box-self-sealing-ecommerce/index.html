Applications

  • E-commerce product shipping and fulfillment
  • Subscription box packaging for monthly delivery services

  • Apparel, cosmetics, electronics, and food product shipping
  • Amazon FBA, Shopify, and marketplace-ready packaging

Customization Options

  • Double-wall construction (BC flute, EB flute) for maximum crush resistance
  • Self-sealing adhesive strip with tear tape for easy consumer opening
  • Flexographic or offset litho printing for brand graphics
  • Full CMYK + Pantone brand colors on white-top kraft liner
  • Interior printing for unboxing experience branding
  • Custom sizes from 150×100×50mm to 600×400×400mm
  • Die-cut handles or perforated tear-open panels
  • Water-based ink printing for eco-friendly compliance

Production Specifications

  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press for high-speed precision printing
  • Fully automatic corrugated box gluing and folding line
  • Board grades: B flute (3mm), C flute (4mm), E flute (1.5mm), EB flute (4.5mm)
  • Kraft liner: 125gsm–200gsm; White-top liner: 150gsm–200gsm
