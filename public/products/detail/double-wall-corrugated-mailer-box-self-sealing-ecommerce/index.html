Double-Wall Corrugated Mailer Box with Self-Sealing Strip for E-Commerce

June 10, 2026

Double-wall corrugated mailer box with integrated self-sealing tear strip — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). 10,000 sqm factory with KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses. Daily output 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • E-commerce product shipping and fulfillment
  • Subscription box packaging for monthly delivery services
  • Direct-to-consumer (DTC) branded packaging
  • Apparel, cosmetics, electronics, and food product shipping
  • Amazon FBA, Shopify, and marketplace-ready packaging

Customization Options

  • Double-wall construction (BC flute, EB flute) for maximum crush resistance
  • Self-sealing adhesive strip with tear tape for easy consumer opening
  • Flexographic or offset litho printing for brand graphics
  • Full CMYK + Pantone brand colors on white-top kraft liner
  • Interior printing for unboxing experience branding
  • Custom sizes from 150×100×50mm to 600×400×400mm
  • Die-cut handles or perforated tear-open panels
  • Water-based ink printing for eco-friendly compliance

Production Specifications

  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press for high-speed precision printing
  • Fully automatic corrugated box gluing and folding line
  • Board grades: B flute (3mm), C flute (4mm), E flute (1.5mm), EB flute (4.5mm)
  • Kraft liner: 125gsm–200gsm; White-top liner: 150gsm–200gsm
  • ISO 9001:2015 quality management throughout production
  • FSC-certified paper sourcing for sustainable packaging

Quality Details

  • Edge crush test (ECT) rated: 32–44 ECT for double-wall
  • Burst strength tested per ISTA 1A shipping standards
  • Self-sealing adhesive: temperature-stable from -20°C to 60°C
  • Tear strip: consistent tear without jagged edges
  • 100% dimension verification on every production batch
  • Free pre-production samples with 5–7 day turnaround
  • MOQ 1000–3000 units, trial orders and OEM/ODM accepted
