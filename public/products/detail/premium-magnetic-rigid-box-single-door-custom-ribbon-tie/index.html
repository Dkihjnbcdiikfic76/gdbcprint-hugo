Elegant single-door magnetic rigid box with satin ribbon tie closure — a best-selling premium packaging format for luxury gifts, corporate incentives, and high-end retail. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., with 22 years of manufacturing experience, ISO 9001 & FSC certifications, and an 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) running KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses.

Applications

Single-door magnetic boxes with ribbon tie add ceremony to the unboxing experience:

  • Corporate Gifts: Executive welcome kits, milestone awards, VIP client gifts, annual meeting souvenirs
  • Luxury Retail: Designer scarves, premium ties, leather accessories, jewelry collections
  • Wedding & Events: Wedding favor boxes, bridal party gifts, event keepsakes, anniversary commemoratives
  • Wine & Spirits: Single-bottle presentation boxes with custom-fit insert, premium spirit gift sets
  • Collectibles: Limited-edition memorabilia, art prints, signed editions, trading card sets
  • Confectionery: Premium chocolate boxes, artisan confectionery sets, tea collection packaging

Customization Options

  • Door Configuration: Single door (left or right hinge), double door (center-opening), asymmetric doors, curved or shaped door panels
  • Closure System: N52 neodymium magnets (3–6 magnets for secure hold), satin ribbon tie (10+ colors), elastic band closure, metal clasp, custom-branded magnetic snap
  • Outer Wrap: 128–200gsm art paper in matte, gloss, soft-touch velvet, linen-textured, pearlescent, or metallic finish; faux leather-look paper, woodgrain paper, carbon fiber texture
  • Printing & Finishing: CMYK + PMS spot colors, gold/silver/rose gold foil stamping, embossed/debossed logo, spot UV (gloss + matte contrast), full-surface holographic cold foil, UV inkjet variable data printing
  • Interior: Custom die-cut EVA/PU foam insert, velvet flocking, satin lining, padded silk, elastic product straps, ribbon pulls
  • Size: From 120×100×40mm (jewelry/small gift) to 500×400×150mm (multi-bottle / large gift set)
  • Add-Ons: Gold/silver edge gilding, foil-stamped interior base logo, printed insert card with brand story, magnetic clasp with custom engraving, removable tray, nested compartments

Production Specifications

  • Rigid Box Line: Automated rigid box line — greyboard cutting, corner taping, wrap application, magnet insertion, and final assembly — all in continuous production
  • Greyboard: 1200–1800gsm, double-ply laminated for heavy-gauge applications; recycled greyboard option for sustainability programs
  • Magnet Integration: N52 grade neodymium magnets embedded between board layers (invisible from exterior); pull force 0.5–2.0 kgf per magnet pair per application
  • Hinge Construction: Full-width board hinge with reinforced paper or fabric backing; 10,000+ open/close cycle tested for durability
  • Ribbon Tie: 15mm/25mm satin ribbon in 20+ colors with sealed-cut ends (anti-fray); polyester or silk blend; custom dye-to-match available for orders over 5,000 units
  • KBA 1050: 15,000 sheets/hour for outer wrap printing, up to 1050×740mm sheet format
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV: 7 colors + UV coating in a single pass for complex designs
  • Lead Time: 10–15 working days standard; 5–7 days for repeat orders
  • Daily Capacity: 50,000–80,000 magnetic rigid boxes

Quality Details

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management — documented QC checkpoints at material receiving, printing, die-cutting, assembly, and final inspection
  • FSC Chain-of-Custody certified — sustainable paper and board throughout the supply chain
  • Magnet Pull Force: 100% force testing on magnet-embedded boards; ±10% tolerance
  • Hinge Endurance: 10,000-cycle open/close test on every new design; no visible wear, cracking, or loosening
  • Ribbon Abrasion: Martindale rub test on ribbon tie — no color transfer to product or box surface
  • Drop Test: ISTA 1A protocol — 76cm free-fall, 6 faces, 3 edges, 1 corner; box must remain closed, product protected
