White Corrugated E-Commerce Box with Tear Strip Subscription

June 10, 2026

Product Overview

White corrugated e-commerce box with integrated tear strip for subscription and direct-to-consumer shipments — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). Built on B/E/BC flute corrugators with KBA and Heidelberg offset presses for vibrant exterior printing across an 18,000 sqm integrated campus in Foshan, Guangdong. Daily output 1.7 million boxes + 220,000+ rigid gift boxes (post-2025 expansion). ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, and ISTA 1A transit-tested. Designed for e-commerce fulfillment centers, subscription brands, and DTC shippers requiring unboxing excellence with carrier-grade durability.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Flute Types: E-flute (1.2mm, fine print surface), B-flute (2.5mm, crush resistance), BC-flute (6.0mm, heavy-duty), F-flute (0.8mm, micro-flute for luxury)
  • Liners: White-top kraft (bright print surface), coated white-top (gloss CMYK), brown kraft (eco), mottled white (economy)
  • Board Grades: 125–200gsm liners with 105–140gsm fluting medium; single-wall and double-wall constructions
  • Tear Strip: Integrated PET tear tape or perforated tear strip for easy consumer opening — no tools required
  • Closure: Self-sealing latex strip, double-sided tape, or tuck-flap; optional tamper-evident seal
  • Print: CMYK offset on white-top liner (KBA 1050); flexo for single-color brand marks
  • Daily Output: 1.7 million boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, ISTA 1A transit testing
