FSC Certified Kraft Paper Box with Window Food Packaging

June 10, 2026

FSC certified kraft paper box with clear PET window for food packaging — designed for bakeries, confectionery brands, and organic food companies seeking sustainable packaging with product visibility. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003) in a 10,000 sqm ISO 9001 and FSC certified factory. Printed on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses with food-safe, low-migration inks. 22 years of expertise delivering 1.7 million boxes daily to clients in 40+ countries.

Applications

  • Bakery and pastry takeaway boxes (croissants, donuts, cookies, muffins)
  • Organic and natural food product packaging
  • Chocolates and confectionery gift boxes
  • Tea and coffee sample / retail packaging
  • Dried fruit, nuts, and snack packaging
  • Artisan soap and handmade cosmetic boxes
  • Deli and gourmet food counter display packaging
  • Catering and event favor boxes

Customization Options

  • Window Style: Single front window, wrap-around panoramic, die-cut shaped window, multiple small windows
  • Window Material: Clear PET (standard), biodegradable PLA film, anti-fog PET, UV-filtering PET
  • Box Style: Straight tuck end, reverse tuck end, auto-lock bottom, gable-top, sleeve-and-tray
  • Kraft Paper: Natural brown kraft, bleached white kraft, coated one-side kraft for high-quality print
  • Printing: 1–4 color CMYK offset with soy-based or water-based food-safe inks; Pantone spot colors available
  • Finishes: Aqueous coating (food-safe), matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch feel, embossing
  • Closure: Tuck flap, magnetic snap, ribbon tie, cotton string handle, jute tie
  • Size: 100×70×40mm individual pastry to 350×250×80mm bakery tray

Production Specifications

  • Press: KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset (15,000 sheets/hr) + Heidelberg CD 102 7+1 UV
  • Paper: FSC certified kraft 250–400gsm with full chain-of-custody traceability
  • Inks: Soy-based and water-based food-safe inks; low-migration UV inks for indirect food contact
  • Window Patching: Automatic window patching machine — high-clarity PET, precision die-cut apertures
  • Die-Cutting: Rotary and flatbed steel-rule die-cutting with ±0.3mm accuracy
  • Glue: Food-safe hot-melt and cold-set adhesives compliant with FDA/GB standards
  • Daily Capacity: 1.7 million boxes; dedicated kraft eco line for food-grade production
  • Lead Time: 10–15 working days new design; 7–10 days repeat orders

Quality Assurance

  • ISO 9001:2015 — full production traceability from paper roll to finished box
  • FSC Chain-of-Custody — certified sustainable forestry, every batch traceable to source
  • Food Safety: Low-migration inks tested per EU Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004; migration <10 ppb
  • Window Bond Integrity: Accelerated aging (72h at 57°C/95% RH) — zero adhesive failure
  • Grease Resistance: Kraft paper rated Kit 5–8 for oily and greasy food products
  • Odor Testing: VOC compliance per GB/T — zero off-odor transfer to food products
  • Free pre-production samples with food-safe decoration — 5–7 working day turnaround
