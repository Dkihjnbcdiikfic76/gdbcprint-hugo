Heavy-Duty Corrugated Shipping Box with Foam Insert for Fragile Items

June 10, 2026

Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s heavy-duty corrugated shipping box with custom die-cut foam insert provides military-grade protection for fragile, high-value items during transit. Double-wall BC flute corrugated board (6–7mm thickness) plus precision die-cut EVA or PE foam cradles each item individually. ISTA 1A drop-tested, self-locking bottom, optional tear strip for easy recipient opening. Manufactured in our ISO 9001:2015 certified 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 smart factory) — 22 years, KBA 1050 + Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, 1.7M daily output.

Applications

  • Electronics: Smartphones, tablets, laptops, hard drives, circuit boards, sensors
  • Medical Devices: Diagnostic equipment, surgical instruments, implant packaging
  • Optics & Lenses: Camera lenses, microscopes, telescopes, precision optics
  • Glass & Ceramics: Laboratory glassware, artisanal ceramics, wine glasses, bottles
  • Luxury Goods: High-end watches, jewelry, designer accessories in transit
  • Industrial Parts: Precision machined components, aerospace parts, automotive sensors
  • E-Commerce: Direct-to-consumer shipping of fragile high-value products
  • Exhibition & Trade Show: Safe transport of display samples and prototypes

Customization Options

  • Corrugated Board: B-flute (3mm), C-flute (4mm), BC double-wall (6–7mm), triple-wall (10mm+)
  • Box Style: Regular slotted container (RSC), self-locking bottom, die-cut mailer, telescoping
  • Sizes: Custom — 150×100×50mm to 600×400×300mm
  • Foam Insert: EVA (closed-cell, shock absorbent), PE (cross-linked), PU (open-cell), anti-static (ESD safe)
  • Foam Density: 25–80 kg/m³ depending on item weight and fragility
  • Foam Color: Black, white, grey, red, blue, custom
  • Printing: Flexo (1–3 colors), offset litho-laminated, or digital — CMYK + Pantone
  • Finishes: Aqueous coating, matte/gloss varnish, spot color
  • Features: Tear strip, perforated opening, self-locking bottom, handle cutouts, tamper-evident seal
  • Inserts: Corrugated dividers, molded pulp trays, custom die-cut partitions

Quality Assurance

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified — documented QC at every stage
  • Board: edge crush test (ECT), burst strength (Mullen), basis weight verified per batch
  • Foam: density ±5%, compression set test, cell structure uniformity
  • Die-cut precision: ±0.5mm foam tolerance, ±1mm board tolerance
  • Adhesion: glue bond peel test, hot melt temperature verification
  • Drop test: ISTA 1A — 10 drops from 760mm, zero product damage
  • Vibration test: ISTA 3A available for long-distance shipping validation
  • Final inspection: AQL 2.5 Level II, 100% foam-to-box fit check
  • Free pre-production samples with 7–10 day turnaround
