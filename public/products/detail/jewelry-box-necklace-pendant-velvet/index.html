Product Overview

Bincai’s premium necklace and pendant jewelry box features a plush velvet interior with a removable satin display cushion. The hinged lid design opens to a full display angle, making it ideal for retail counter presentation and e-commerce unboxing. Manufactured with KBA 1050 4-color offset printing and precision die-cutting in our 18,000 sqm Foshan factory. 22 years of luxury packaging expertise.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Press: KBA 1050 4-color offset — precise registration for intricate patterns
  • Board: 1.5mm greyboard core, 157gsm art paper wrap
  • Interior: 2mm velvet flocking in 20+ color options
  • Cushion: Satin-covered foam insert, removable, with necklace slot
  • Closure: Hinged lid with magnetic catch (optional)
  • Daily Output: 220,000+ gift boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody
