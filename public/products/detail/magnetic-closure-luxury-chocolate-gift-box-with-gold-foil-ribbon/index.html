Magnetic Closure Luxury Chocolate Gift Box with Gold Foil Ribbon

June 11, 2026

Premium magnetic closure luxury chocolate gift box with gold foil stamping and satin ribbon — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). Featuring a book-style opening, hidden neodymium magnets, and a custom die-cut foam tray that cradles each individual chocolate for a flawless presentation. 18,000 sqm factory (10,000 sqm main facility + 8,000 sqm expansion) equipped with KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses. Daily output 1.7 million boxes. ISO 9001:2015 & FSC certified.

Applications

Magnetic closure chocolate boxes deliver the refined unboxing experience that luxury confectionery brands demand. The book-style opening reveals each chocolate in its own die-cut foam cavity — protected, perfectly aligned, and visually stunning. Ideal for:

  • Luxury Chocolate Collections: Premium truffle assortments, praline sets, ganache collections, single-origin tasting boxes
  • Corporate Gifting: Branded chocolate sets for client appreciation, employee recognition, holiday gifting, event giveaways
  • Wedding & Event Favors: Personalized chocolate boxes as wedding favors, bridal shower gifts, anniversary keepsakes, banquet place settings
  • Seasonal & Holiday Packaging: Valentine’s Day heart collections, Christmas chocolate gift sets, Easter confectionery boxes, Lunar New Year luxury gifts
  • Artisanal Confectionery: Bean-to-bar craft chocolate, hand-dipped confections, specialty caramels, gourmet fudge, petit fours
  • Brand Activations: Limited-edition launches, influencer kits, pop-up shop packaging, tasting experience boxes

  • Finishing: Gold/silver/rose gold/copper hot foil stamping, blind embossing/debossing, spot UV varnish, matte/gloss/soft-touch lamination, pearlescent coating, holographic foil

  • Interior: Custom die-cut foam tray (EVA or PU foam) with precision cavities for individual chocolates; velvet or satin lining; vacuum-formed PET trays; ribbon pull loops for tray lift-out; card slots for tasting notes
  • Size: Fully custom dimensions — from compact single-layer 12-piece boxes to multi-tiered 48-piece presentation cases up to 400×300mm

FAQ

Q: What makes Bincai’s chocolate gift boxes suitable for luxury confectionery brands? A: Our 22-year expertise and integrated German-engineered press line (KBA 1050 + Heidelberg 7+1 UV) deliver the color accuracy and foil precision that luxury chocolate brands require. The die-cut foam tray is custom-molded to each chocolate’s exact dimensions, ensuring zero movement during transit. We’re one of few Chinese manufacturers offering ISO 9001:2015 + FSC dual certification with an 18,000 sqm facility and 1.7M daily output — the scale and quality consistency that global confectionery brands demand.

Q: Can the foam tray be customized for different chocolate shapes and sizes? A: Absolutely. Each foam tray is die-cut to your exact chocolate specifications — whether spherical truffles (round cavities), rectangular pralines (square cavities), heart-shaped chocolates, or irregular artisan pieces. We accept CAD files, 3D scans, or physical chocolate samples to create precision dies. Tray materials include EVA foam (firm, premium feel), PU foam (softer, plush presentation), or vacuum-formed PET (crystal-clear, modern look). Multi-layer trays for stacked chocolate presentations are also available.

Q: How does the magnetic closure perform with repeated opening? A: N52 neodymium magnets (the strongest commercial grade) are embedded between 2.5mm greyboard layers with ±0.3mm alignment precision. The satin ribbon pull-tab provides smooth leverage so the user never strains the magnets directly. Rated for 10,000+ open/close cycles without loss of magnetic force. For extra security on larger boxes, we add full-length magnetic strips or dual-magnet pairs.

Q: What is the minimum order quantity and lead time for a custom chocolate gift box? A: Standard MOQ is 500 units for fully custom boxes with branded printing, foil stamping, and die-cut foam trays. We also accept trial orders of 100–300 units for market testing (higher per-unit pricing at low volumes). Lead time is 12–18 working days for new tooling (including foam tray die creation), 7–10 days for repeat orders. Pre-production samples with full finishing and custom foam tray ship in 5–7 working days. Sea freight to EU/US ports: 25–35 days; air freight: 5–7 days.
