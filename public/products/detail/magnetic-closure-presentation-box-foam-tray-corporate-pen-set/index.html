Custom Magnetic Closure Presentation Box with Foam Tray for Corporate Pen Set

June 11, 2026

Custom magnetic closure presentation box with precision die-cut foam tray for corporate pen sets and executive gifts — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). The magnetic flip-lid design opens like a book to reveal a custom die-cut foam tray holding pens, USB drives, cufflinks, or other executive accessories in individual cavities. The satisfying magnetic snap and premium materials communicate brand quality from the first touch. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across 10,000 sqm factory floor. Daily output 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • Corporate pen and stationery gift sets
  • Executive welcome kit and onboarding gift boxes
  • Luxury USB drive and tech accessory presentation
  • Cufflink, tie clip, and men’s accessory gift sets
  • Award and recognition ceremony presentation boxes

  • Magnetic flip-lid closure with satisfying snap action
  • Precision die-cut foam tray: 10mm–30mm cavity depths for secure item fit

  • Board thickness: 1200gsm–1800gsm greyboard with 128gsm–157gsm art paper wrap
