Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s recyclable kraft folding carton for eco-friendly food packaging delivers sustainable protection with brand-forward printing. FSC certified kraft paperboard, soy-based inks, and water-based adhesives ensure full recyclability and industrial compostability. Manufactured in our ISO 9001:2015 certified 18,000 sqm facility (10,000+8,000 smart factory) with KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses — 22 years of experience, daily output 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • Organic & Natural Foods: Granola, snack bars, dried fruit, nuts, tea, superfoods
  • Bakery & Confectionery: Artisan bread, cookies, chocolates, pastries with food-safe inks
  • Takeaway & Delivery: Eco-conscious restaurant to-go packaging with brand printing
  • Farmers Markets: Honey jars, jam, preserves, handmade goods — rustic kraft aesthetic
  • Coffee & Tea: Specialty coffee bags, loose-leaf tea cartons, gift sets
  • Meal Kit & Subscription: Dry ingredient compartments, recipe card inserts
  • Health & Wellness: Supplement cartons, protein bars, wellness product packaging

Customization Options

  • Styles: Tuck-top (straight/reverse), auto-lock bottom, gable top, sleeve, window carton
  • Sizes: Fully custom — 60×40×20mm to 400×300×120mm
  • Materials: FSC kraft paperboard (200–350gsm), recycled kraft, solid bleached sulfate
  • Printing: Soy-based CMYK offset, Pantone spot colors, food-safe indirect contact certified
  • Finishes: Aqueous matte/gloss coating, blind embossing, registered emboss, no-plastic policy
  • Windows: PLA biodegradable film window, die-cut open window, or no-window
  • Inserts: Die-cut kraft dividers, molded pulp trays, corrugated partitions
  • Closure: Tuck flap, auto-lock bottom, tear strip, paper sticker seal
