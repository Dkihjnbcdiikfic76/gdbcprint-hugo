Heavy-duty custom printed corrugated shipping box engineered for e-commerce, retail distribution, and international freight — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. with 22 years of packaging expertise (founded 2003). Available in B-flute (3mm, crush-resistant), E-flute (1.5mm, fine print surface), and BC double-wall (6mm, heavy cargo) constructions with kraft or white-top liner options. Self-locking bottom eliminates tape during assembly, and optional tear strip enables easy consumer opening. Full-color flexo post-print or litho-laminated offset printing on KBA 1050 4-color press delivers vibrant branding. ISTA 1A drop-test validated for safe courier shipping. Produced across Bincai’s 18,000 sqm dual factory (10,000+8,000 sqm smart factory) in Foshan, Guangdong. ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified. Daily output 1.7 million boxes. MOQ 500 units.

Applications

  • E-commerce direct-to-consumer shipping boxes for apparel, footwear, accessories
  • Subscription box fulfillment — monthly beauty, snack, pet, and lifestyle boxes
  • Retail shelf-ready packaging with tear-away top for instant display
  • Food and beverage shipping (pizza, meal kit, wine, craft beer)
  • Electronics and fragile goods with die-cut interior partitions
  • Industrial parts and automotive components shipping
  • Moving and storage boxes with reinforced handles

Customization Options

  • Flute profile: B-flute (3mm), C-flute (3.5mm), E-flute (1.5mm), BC double-wall (6mm), EB double-wall (5mm)
  • Liner: kraft (natural brown), white-top (bright print surface), coated white (gloss finish), or mottled white
  • Printing: 1-4 color flexo direct print, litho-laminate offset (KBA 1050 4-color), or digital short-run
  • Box style: RSC (regular slotted container), HSC (half-slotted), FOL (full overlap), die-cut custom shape, self-locking auto-bottom
  • Closure: self-locking bottom, tuck-top, tear strip, adhesive strip (peel-and-seal), or tape-reinforced
  • Surface finish: water-based varnish, UV spot coating, or matte/gloss lamination (litho-laminate only)
  • Add-ons: die-cut carry handles, ventilation holes, perforated tear-away panels, QR code printing
  • Interior: die-cut corrugated partitions/dividers, molded pulp cushioning, or foam corner protectors
  • Sustainability: 100% recycled content available, FSC certified, water-based inks, fully recyclable
  • Size range: 100×100×50mm (small mailer) to 1200×800×800mm (pallet box); fully custom dimensions

Production Specifications

  • Corrugator: 2.5m wide high-speed single-facer producing B, C, E, and F flutes
  • Liner grades: kraft 125–300gsm, test liner 125–200gsm, white-top 150–250gsm
  • Burst strength: 200# (standard) to 275# (heavy-duty double-wall); Mullen test certified
  • Edge crush test (ECT): 32–44 lb/in for standard shipping, 55+ lb/in for stacked pallet loads
  • Printing: flexo 1-4 color (direct print) or offset litho-laminate on KBA 1050 4-color press
  • Die-cutting: rotary die-cutter for high-speed RSC production; flatbed Bobst for custom shapes
  • Gluing: inline cold-set PVA adhesive; hot-melt for self-locking bottom and tear strip application
  • Assembly: flat-packed for efficient shipping; self-locking styles assemble in under 3 seconds
  • Daily output: up to 300,000 corrugated boxes per shift
  • Lead time: 8–12 days new die; 5–7 days repeat; 3–5 days rush (plain brown box express)

Quality Details

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management — raw material to finished product traceability
  • FSC Chain-of-Custody certified — sustainable forestry sourcing verified
  • ISTA 1A drop-test certified — survive 10 drops from 760mm with full payload
  • Burst strength (Mullen) and edge crush (ECT) testing per TAPPI standards on every production batch
  • Box compression test (BCT) available for stacked warehouse and container loading validation
  • AQL 2.5 Level II visual inspection per ISO 2859-1
  • 22 years manufacturing expertise — founded 2003, 220+ skilled workers
  • 18,000 sqm factory with dedicated corrugated production line
