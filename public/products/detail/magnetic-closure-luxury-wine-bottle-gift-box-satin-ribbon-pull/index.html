Magnetic Closure Luxury Wine Bottle Gift Box with satin ribbon pull tab by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. — a Diamond Member on Made-in-China.com with 22 years of manufacturing excellence (founded 2003). Designed to transform a standard 750ml wine or spirits bottle into a premium gift presentation, this rigid box features a die-cut interior cavity that cradles the bottle securely, a satin ribbon pull for effortless lifting, and N52 neodymium magnets for a satisfying snap-shut closure. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across our 18,000 sqm facility. Daily output: 1.7 million color boxes + 220,000+ gift boxes.

Applications

  • Winery Gifting: Premium retail packaging for limited-edition and reserve wines
  • Corporate Gifts: Branded wine boxes for client appreciation and holiday gifting
  • Spirits & Champagne: Custom-fit inserts for champagne, whiskey, cognac, and specialty spirits
  • Wedding Favors: Personalized wine boxes for wedding party gifts and table settings
  • E-commerce: Protective shipping packaging for online wine retailers and subscription boxes

Customization Options

  • Exterior: Art paper wrap (157-200gsm) with matte, gloss, or soft-touch lamination
  • Colors: CMYK printing plus Pantone spot colors, metallic inks, and flood coating
  • Logo: Gold foil, silver foil, holographic foil stamping, embossing, or debossing
  • Interior: Velvet, satin, or suede-lined cavity; custom die-cut foam insert for non-standard bottles
  • Ribbon: Satin ribbon pull in custom colors with optional foil-printed branding
  • Closure: Magnetic flip-lid (front), magnetic side-opening, or two-piece telescoping lid
  • Window: Optional die-cut window with PET film to showcase the bottle label

Quality Control

StageCheckpoint
1Material inspection — greyboard thickness, art paper GSM verification
2Printing — color consistency vs. Pantone reference under D50 lighting
3Lamination — adhesion test, no bubbling or peeling
4Die-cutting — greyboard precision ±0.5mm, cavity fit check with actual bottle
5Foil stamping — alignment ±0.3mm, consistent coverage, no halo
6Magnet mounting — polarity check, flush embedment, pull-force test
7Ribbon attachment — pull strength test, secure adhesive bond
8Assembly — lid alignment, cavity fit, magnetic closure snap test
9Final inspection — 100% visual under controlled lighting
10Packing — individual polybag + corner protectors + master carton
