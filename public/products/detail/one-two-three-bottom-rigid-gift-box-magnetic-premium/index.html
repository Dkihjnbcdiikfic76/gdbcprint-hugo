Product Overview

Bincai’s 1-2-3 bottom rigid gift box combines the premium feel of a rigid box with the flat-shipping convenience of a folding carton. The patented 1-2-3 collapsible base pops up in seconds — no gluing, no assembly tools. Hidden N42 neodymium magnets in the lid provide a satisfying snap closure. Ideal for corporate gifts, premium apparel, candles, and luxury consumer goods. Ships flat to save 60%+ on freight costs versus pre-assembled rigid boxes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does the 1-2-3 bottom mechanism work, and is it durable for repeated use? A: The 1-2-3 bottom uses precisely-scored crease lines at three positions on each side panel. Step 1: fold the two side flaps inward. Step 2: fold the front panel up. Step 3: fold the rear panel down — the interlocking tabs snap into place under tension, creating a rigid base without any adhesive. The greyboard crease lines are engineered for 50+ assembly/disassembly cycles without tearing, validated through our in-house cycle testing. Each box ships flat and assembles in under 5 seconds by an untrained operator — no tools, no glue, no training required.

Q: What freight savings can I expect from flat-shipping versus pre-assembled rigid boxes? A: A standard 250x200x80mm rigid box ships at approximately 0.004 m³ when flat versus 0.004 m³ when assembled — but the flat configuration allows 4-6x more boxes per pallet due to vertical stacking efficiency. Typical freight savings are 55-65% for sea freight (FCL) and 40-50% for air freight. For a typical 5,000-unit order, this translates to approximately $800-1,200 savings on sea freight and $2,000-3,500 on air freight versus pre-assembled boxes. Our flat-pack design also reduces warehouse storage footprint by 70% at your fulfillment center. We provide palletization diagrams and loading plans with every quote.

Q: Can the magnets be customized — different strengths, sizes, or hidden placement? A: Bincai stocks N35, N42, and N52 grade neodymium magnets in 8 standard sizes (6mm-20mm diameter, 1mm-3mm thickness). N42 is our standard recommendation — it provides 8-12N pull force per pair, giving a satisfying snap that’s strong enough to hold the lid closed during handling but not so strong that it’s difficult to open. For luxury applications, we can double the magnet count (4 corners) or upgrade to N52 for a more premium magnetic feel. All magnets are embedded between the greyboard and wrap layers — they are completely invisible from the exterior and interior, with no risk of detachment or scratching the product inside.

Q: What interior customization options create the best unboxing experience? A: Bincai offers a full range of interior treatments to elevate the unboxing moment. Die-cut EVA foam trays (available in 40+ colors) precisely cradle your product — each cavity is CNC-routed for a friction fit. Velvet flocking (spray-on fiber) in black, navy, or custom Pantone colors creates a jewelry-grade interior for watches and accessories. Satin-lined cardboard platforms with ribbon pull-tabs allow customers to lift the product elegantly. For multi-product gift sets, custom cardboard dividers with thumb-notches organize items while maintaining the premium feel. We recommend a two-layer approach: foam tray for product security + satin dust cover for the reveal moment. Free interior design consultation with 3D renderings included for orders above 2,000 units.
