Magnetic Closure Folding Gift Box Custom Printed CMYK

June 10, 2026

Product Description

Our custom magnetic closure folding gift box with CMYK printing delivers a premium unboxing experience for luxury retail, cosmetics, electronics, jewelry, and corporate gifting. The integrated magnetic flap ensures secure, satisfying closure while maintaining a sleek, seamless exterior — no clasps or ribbons required. Each box is precision-manufactured using a KBA 1050 4-color offset press for vibrant, high-resolution CMYK graphics and finished on a Heidelberg 7+1 UV press for striking spot UV effects, foil stamping, embossing, and matte or gloss lamination.

Features

  • Magnetic Closure System: Hidden neodymium magnets embedded in the flap and box body provide a secure, repeatable snap-shut closure that stays flat during shipping and retail display.
  • CMYK Offset Printing: Full-color artwork printed on our KBA 1050 4-color press at up to 200 LPI for photo-realistic brand reproduction across the entire box surface.
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV Finishing: Inline spot UV, aqueous coating, and specialty ink effects applied in a single pass for fast turnaround without sacrificing premium quality.
  • Custom Structure Options: Available as one-piece folding magnetic boxes, two-piece rigid magnetic boxes, magnetic closure mailers, and drawer-style magnetic boxes with insert trays.
  • Material Choices: 1200–2000 gsm greyboard wrapped in 157–350 gsm art paper, kraft paper, or specialty textured stocks — all FSC certified.
  • Value-Added Finishes: Hot foil stamping (gold, silver, rose gold, holographic), debossing/embossing, soft-touch lamination, and window patching available.

Factory

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing operates a 10,000 sqm production facility in Foshan, China, with 22 years of paper packaging expertise. Our factory runs 5 automated production lines including the KBA 1050 4-color offset press, Heidelberg 7+1 UV press, automatic die-cutting machines, folder-gluers, and magnetic insertion equipment — yielding a daily output of 1.5 million boxes. We are ISO 9001:2015 quality management certified and FSC Chain-of-Custody certified, ensuring consistent quality and responsible material sourcing for every order.
