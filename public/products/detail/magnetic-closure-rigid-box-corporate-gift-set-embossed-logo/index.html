Magnetic Closure Rigid Box for Corporate Gift Sets with Embossed Logo

June 10, 2026

Custom magnetic closure rigid box for corporate gift sets — engineered by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging manufacturer with 22 years of industry expertise (founded 2003). Built for corporate promotions, executive gifts, and premium brand giveaways, this rigid box features a substantial 2.5mm greyboard construction with embossed company branding and magnetic flip-cover closure. All production runs on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across a 10,000 sqm factory floor with 1.7 million box daily output. ISO 9001 and FSC certified.

Applications

  • Corporate executive gift sets (pens, notebooks, tech accessories)
  • Year-end holiday corporate gift packaging
  • Employee recognition and award packaging
  • VIP client appreciation gift boxes
  • Trade show and exhibition hand-out packaging
  • Brand launch event and product announcement kits
  • Custom employee onboarding welcome kit boxes

Customization Options

  • 2mm–3mm greyboard with magnetic flip-closure mechanism
  • Full-surface embossing or debossing for raised/ recessed logo treatment
  • Gold, silver, rose gold, or holographic hot foil stamping
  • Soft-touch matte, high-gloss, or textured lamination finishes
  • CMYK offset + Pantone spot color matching
  • Spot UV highlight on logo or design accents
  • Velvet, satin, or flocked interior lining
  • Custom die-cut foam or cardboard insert trays
  • Ribbon pull tab or finger notch for easy opening
  • Metal corner guards for added premium durability
  • Custom dimensions for any product configuration
