Quality Control & Sustainability

  • Embossing Registration: ±0.3mm to print, 100% first-article verification, in-process sampling every 500 pcs
  • Foil Adhesion: 3M 810 tape test on foil-stamped areas, zero lift-off acceptance
  • Magnet Pull Force: Individual magnet pair testing, 2.5–4.0kg secure closure, smooth finger-open
