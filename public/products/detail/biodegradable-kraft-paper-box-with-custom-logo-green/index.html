Product Overview

Biodegradable kraft paper box with custom logo printing — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). Designed for brands committed to plastic-free, sustainable packaging without compromising on print quality or structural integrity. Built on KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses with soy-based inks across an 18,000 sqm integrated campus in Foshan, Guangdong. Daily output 1.7 million boxes + 220,000+ gift boxes (post-2025 expansion). ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certified. Ideal for cosmetics, organic food, apparel, and lifestyle brands seeking zero-plastic, fully recyclable packaging solutions.

Manufacturing Specifications

  • Material: 250–450gsm unbleached kraft paperboard; 100% recycled post-consumer content options (85–100% PCR)
  • Board Construction: Single-layer kraft (250–350gsm) for flexible cartons; kraft-wrapped greyboard (1.5–2.5mm) for rigid boxes
  • Inks: Soy-based (ASTM D6866 verified bio-content ≥60%); water-based flexo inks for single-color kraft runs
  • Adhesives: Water-based PVA (ASTM D6400 biodegradable); starch-based for kraft-to-kraft bonding
  • Finishes: Aqueous matte coating (water-based, recyclable), natural uncoated kraft, vegetable-based wax
  • Press: KBA 1050 4-color for CMYK on kraft; Heidelberg 7+1 for precise PMS color matching
  • Print Quality: 1–4 color process; foil-free debossing for natural logo marking
  • Daily Output: 1.7 million boxes (post-2025 expansion)
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, EN 13432 (compostability on request), ASTM D6400
