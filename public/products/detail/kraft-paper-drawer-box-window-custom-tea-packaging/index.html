Kraft Paper Drawer Box with Window Custom Tea Packaging

June 10, 2026

Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing’s kraft paper drawer box with die-cut PET window delivers a premium unboxing experience for tea and gourmet products while maintaining full eco-credentials. The slide-out drawer design — complete with ribbon pull tab — reveals product through a crystal-clear PET window, combining shelf appeal with sustainable packaging. Every box is manufactured in our ISO 9001:2015 certified 18,000 sqm facility (10,000+8,000 smart factory) using FSC certified kraft paperboard and biodegradable soy-based inks.

Applications

  • Loose Leaf Tea: 50g, 100g, 250g formats — airtight drawer keeps teas fresh
  • Tea Bag Sets: Multi-compartment drawer inserts for assorted tea bag collections
  • Herbal Blends & Tisanes: Natural kraft aesthetic complements organic/herbal branding
  • Gourmet Food Gifting: Dried fruits, nuts, cookies, chocolate truffles, spice sets
  • Corporate Gifts: Custom branded drawer boxes for VIP client appreciation
  • Wedding Favors: Personalized with couple names, wedding date foil stamping
  • Subscription Boxes: Monthly tea clubs, curated snack boxes with reusable drawer format

Customization Options

  • Sizes: From 80×80×30mm (sample size) to 300×200×80mm (family size) — fully custom dimensions
  • Materials: 157–350gsm kraft paper laminated to 1.0–2.5mm greyboard; PET window thickness 0.3–0.5mm
  • Printing: Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset — CMYK + Pantone spot colors + UV coating in single pass
  • Finishes: Blind embossing / debossing, hot foil stamping (gold, silver, rose gold, copper, holographic), spot UV, matte/gloss aqueous coating, soft-touch velvet lamination
  • Inserts: Custom die-cut foam or cardboard inserts with individual tea compartments; magnetic closure drawer variant available
  • Ribbon Color: Pull-tab ribbon in satin, grosgrain, or cotton — color matched to brand palette
  • Window Shape: Round, square, rectangle, heart, leaf, or custom die-cut PET window shape

Quality Assurance

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system — full batch traceability
  • Incoming material inspection: basis weight verification, moisture content testing, FSC chain-of-custody validation
  • In-process QC: print registration check every 500 sheets, color density monitoring via spectrophotometer, drawer fit tolerance ±0.5mm
  • Final inspection: AQL 2.5 Level II sampling, 100% functional test (drawer slide action, ribbon pull strength, window clarity)
  • Pre-shipment: drop test (ISTA 1A), compression test, humidity chamber test for tea packaging
  • Third-party testing available: SGS, Intertek, Bureau Veritas upon request

Sustainability

  • FSC Mix Credit certified — supports responsible forest management
  • 100% biodegradable kraft paperboard and soy inks
  • PET window is recyclable (R-PET options available on request)
  • Greyboard core uses minimum 85% post-consumer recycled content
  • Water-based adhesives — no solvent emissions
  • Carbon footprint report available for bulk orders (>10,000 units)
