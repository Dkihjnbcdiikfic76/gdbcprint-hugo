Custom Tuck Top Folding Carton CMYK Offset Printing

June 10, 2026

Premium custom tuck top folding carton with full CMYK offset printing — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). Our 10,000 sqm Foshan factory operates KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, delivering 1.7 million boxes daily. ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified. The classic tuck-top design offers secure closure with effortless opening — ideal for retail shelf display where end caps and side panels double as brand real estate.

Applications

  • Retail shelf packaging for cosmetics, skincare, and personal care products
  • Food and confectionery cartons (chocolate, tea, cookies, supplement packaging)
  • Consumer electronics accessories (earbuds, cables, phone cases)
  • Apparel and fashion accessories (ties, scarves, socks, belts)
  • Pharmaceutical OTC and health supplement folding cartons
  • Promotional gift-with-purchase and deluxe sample kit packaging
  • Trade show giveaway and brand activation kit boxes

Customization Options

  • Box Style: Straight tuck end (STE), reverse tuck end (RTE), tuck-top auto-lock bottom, tuck-top with thumb notch, tuck-top with dust flaps
  • Paper: 128–200gsm C1S (coated one side) art paper, 250–400gsm SBS (solid bleached sulfate), kraft paper, or black-core premium board
  • Printing: CMYK offset (KBA 1050 at 15,000 sheets/hour), Pantone spot colors (up to 6 PMS + CMYK), metallic Pantone inks
  • Foil Stamping: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper, holographic — flat stamp, multi-level emboss/deboss combination, registered stamp on die-cut window edge
  • Finishes: Matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch velvet coating, spot UV (gloss or matte), aqueous varnish, textured embossing, blind deboss
  • Window: Die-cut open window, PET window film, PLA biodegradable film window
  • Inserts: Custom die-cut paper dividers, E-flute corrugated platform, vacuum-formed PET tray
  • Size: 40×40×20mm to 350×250×100mm — any custom dimensions
  • Closure: Friction tuck (standard), tuck with slit-lock, tuck with magnetic coin closure, tuck with Velcro dot

Production Specifications

  • Press: KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset at 15,000 sheets/hour + Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV for premium runs
  • Paper: Greyboard 300–450gsm lined with 128–200gsm art paper; SBS 250–400gsm solid board
  • Die-Cutting: Bobst SP 102 E autoplaten die-cutter with ±0.2mm precision; custom steel rule dies
  • Foil Stamping: Bobst SP 102 BMA hot foil stamper — multi-zone temperature control, ±0.15mm registration
  • Gluing: Automatic folding-gluing line (Bobst Mistral 110) at 100,000 cartons/hour for straight-line; manual for complex lock-bottom
  • Daily Capacity: 220,000+ folding cartons
  • Lead Time: 7–10 working days new tooling; 5–7 days repeat orders; 3–5 days rush service available

Quality Assurance

  • Print Registration: ±0.1mm color-to-color registration on KBA 1050; ±0.15mm on Heidelberg UV
  • Foil Adhesion: Sutherland rub test — 50 double rubs with 4-lb weight, zero wear
  • Glue Bond: Pull-apart test — fiber tear on ≥95% of bonded area (ASTM D903)
  • Tuck Flap Fit: 100% inline inspection — tuck flaps close flush, no bowing or gap
  • Window Film Clarity: Light transmission ≥90% (ASTM D1003), haze ≤3%
  • Dimensional Tolerance: ±0.5mm on all dimensions; ±0.3mm on critical fit edges
  • Scuff Resistance: Sutherland rub test — ≤5% ink transfer after 100 double rubs
  • ISO 9001:2015 documented quality gates at prepress, printing, die-cutting, gluing, and final inspection
  • FSC Chain-of-Custody certified — full traceability from certified forest to finished carton
  • Free pre-production samples in 5–7 working days (freight collect)
