Luxury Ring Box with Magnetic Closure Velvet Flocking Interior

June 10, 2026

Elegant luxury ring box with magnetic closure and premium velvet flocking interior — designed and manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. With 22 years of jewelry packaging expertise, ISO 9001 & FSC certifications, and precision craftsmanship, we create ring boxes that transform the unboxing moment into a brand experience. Every box is produced on our KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses in a 18,000 sqm facility (10,000+8,000 smart factory).

Applications

Our magnetic-closure velvet ring boxes serve diverse jewelry retail and gifting scenarios:

  • Engagement Rings: Classic hinged-lid ring box for surprise proposals; premium presentation for diamond and gemstone rings
  • Wedding Bands: Dual-ring boxes for wedding band sets; single-band presentation for ceremony exchange
  • Fashion Rings: Everyday jewelry retail packaging for boutiques, department stores, and online jewelry brands
  • Heirloom & Vintage: Archival-quality ring storage with acid-free lining for antique and estate jewelry
  • Luxury Brand Retail: Countertop display-ready ring packaging with foil-stamped brand logo
  • Corporate & Promotional: Employee recognition rings, milestone anniversary gifts, promotional jewelry sets
  • Custom Jewelry Designers: Independent jewelers and artisan ring makers needing branded packaging at MOQ 500

Customization Options

  • Box Structure: Classic hinged lid, magnetic flip-top, drawer-slide, book-style magnetic flap, two-piece lid-and-base, or slim-profile pocket box
  • Exterior Wrapping: 128–200gsm art paper in matte, gloss, soft-touch velvet, linen-textured, pearlescent, or metallic finishes; genuine leatherette (PU) wrapping for ultra-premium feel
  • Interior Lining: Velvet flocking in 12+ standard colors (black, navy, burgundy, cream, grey, champagne, white, royal blue, emerald, blush pink, chocolate, charcoal); custom Pantone-matched flocking available
  • Ring Slot: Precision die-cut single slot, dual-slot, or angled display slot; foam or flocked EVA insert with ±0.2mm cavity tolerance for secure ring fit
  • Foil Stamping: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper, or matte gold hot foil stamping on lid exterior, interior lid, or both; blind emboss/deboss for subtle branding
  • Magnet Type: Neodymium N35-N52 grade magnet disc (4–8mm diameter); single or dual magnet placement; hidden magnet for seamless aesthetic
  • Closure Details: Flush-close magnetic alignment (±0.3mm); satin ribbon pull-tab; spring-hinge for snap-open action
  • Edge Finishing: Gold/silver edge gilding; colored edge painting to match brand palette
  • Add-Ons: LED light module (battery-powered, activates on open); printed insert card with care instructions; outer sleeve/obi band; custom tissue paper
  • Size Range: Compact single ring (45×45×30mm) to multi-ring presentation (200×150×50mm)

Production Specifications

  • Rigid Box Assembly: Fully automatic line — greyboard cutting (800–1500gsm), corner taping, wrapping paper lamination, interior flocking application, magnet insertion, hinge assembly
  • Greyboard Density: 800gsm for slim boxes, 1200gsm for standard, 1500gsm for premium heavyweight construction; double-ply lamination for extra structural integrity
  • Velvet Flocking: Electrostatic flocking process with 0.8–1.5mm polyester fiber pile height; adhesive curing at controlled 55–65°C for maximum bond strength
  • Magnet Insertion: Machine-pressed neodymium magnets with ±0.2mm depth tolerance; polarity verification sensor on-line; pull-force tested to 0.8–2.5 kgF per magnet
  • Printing: KBA Rapida 105 4-color at 15,000 sheets/hour + Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV for specialty coatings and instant curing
  • Foil Stamping: Bobst SP 102 BMA with ±0.15mm registration and multi-zone temperature control
  • Lead Time: 10–15 working days for new tooling; 7–10 days for repeat orders
  • Daily Capacity: 30,000–50,000 jewelry boxes per day

Quality Assurance

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified — documented quality gates at greyboard inspection, wrapping lamination, flocking adhesion, magnet alignment, and final assembly
  • FSC Chain-of-Custody — certified sustainable paper and board throughout the supply chain
  • Magnet Alignment Test: 100% inspection — lid must close flush with ≤0.3mm gap on all four sides
  • Velvet Adhesion: Accelerated aging (72h at 57°C / 95% RH) + rub test (500 cycles) — zero peeling or fiber shedding
  • Flock Integrity: Tape adhesion test (3M #810) — no visible fiber transfer; colorfastness to light (ISO 105-B02) — minimum grade 4
  • Hinge Cycle Test: 5,000 open/close cycles — no hinge looseness, cracking, or delamination
  • Foil Durability: Sutherland rub test — 50 double rubs with 4-lb weight, no visible wear
  • Odor Testing: VOC emission test per GB/T — safe for enclosed display cases and direct consumer handling

FAQ

Q: What is the MOQ for custom luxury ring boxes with velvet flocking? A: Standard MOQ is 500-1000 units per design. We also accept trial orders of 100-300 units for market testing with adjusted unit pricing. Free pre-production samples with full finishing are provided in 5-7 working days so you can evaluate quality before committing to volume.

Q: How strong are the magnetic closures, and will they stay closed during shipping? A: We use neodymium N35-N52 grade magnets (4-8mm diameter) with pull-force testing to 0.8-2.5 kgF per magnet. Every box undergoes 100% magnet alignment inspection — lid must close flush with ≤0.3mm gap on all four sides. Boxes are drop-tested per ISTA 1A standards to ensure magnets hold during transit.

Q: What interior lining options are available beyond velvet flocking? A: Our standard is electrostatic velvet flocking with 0.8-1.5mm polyester fiber pile in 12+ colors (black, navy, burgundy, cream, grey, champagne, white, royal blue, emerald, blush pink, chocolate, charcoal). We also offer satin lining, genuine suede, leatherette, and custom Pantone-matched flocking. For eco-conscious brands, recycled PET flocking is available.

Q: Can the ring slot be custom-sized for my specific ring dimensions? A: Yes. We create precision die-cut foam or flocked EVA inserts with ±0.2mm cavity tolerance to securely fit your ring. Send us your ring dimensions (band width, stone height, overall diameter) and we’ll include a custom-fit slot in your free pre-production sample. Multi-slot configurations (dual-ring, ring+earring) are also available.
