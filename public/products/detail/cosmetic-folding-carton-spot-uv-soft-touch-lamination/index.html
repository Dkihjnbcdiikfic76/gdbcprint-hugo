Cosmetic Folding Carton with Spot UV and Soft-Touch Matte Lamination

Cosmetic folding carton with spot UV varnish and soft-touch matte lamination — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). 10,000 sqm factory with KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses. Daily output 1.7 million boxes.

Applications

  • Luxury skincare and cosmetic cream packaging
  • Premium lipstick, mascara, and makeup product boxes
  • Fragrance and perfume secondary packaging
  • Beauty subscription box outer cartons
  • High-end personal care and grooming product boxes

Customization Options

  • Spot UV varnish over key design elements for tactile contrast
  • Soft-touch matte lamination for velvety premium feel
  • Hot gold, silver, or holographic foil stamping
  • Embossed or debossed brand logo and decorative patterns
  • Reverse tuck end, straight tuck, or auto-lock bottom structure
  • Die-cut window with PET film for product visibility

  • Custom sizes and insert trays for multi-product sets

Production Specifications

  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press with inline aqueous coating
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV press for instant-cure spot UV and specialty coatings
  • Bobst die-cutting and folder-gluer for precision finishing
  • SBS board: 250gsm–450gsm for structural strength
  • Lamination: BOPP matte, soft-touch, or gloss film
  • Window patching with PET, PVC, or biodegradable film
