Premium custom jewelry display box with integrated LED lighting and velvet flocked interior — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). The lid opens to activate a warm white LED ribbon that illuminates the necklace or pendant, creating a luxury retail presentation that elevates perceived value. Rigid box construction with 1200–1600gsm greyboard core wrapped in premium art paper, featuring a plush velvet flocked cavity for secure product display. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 sqm smart factory).

Applications

  • Fine jewelry retail counter displays — rings, necklaces, pendants, bracelets
  • Bridal and engagement ring presentation boxes
  • Luxury watch and timepiece packaging with illuminated display
  • Premium corporate awards and recognition presentation
  • High-end promotional gift sets for VIP clients
  • Trade show and exhibition product showcasing

Customization Options

  • LED color temperature: warm white (2700K), cool white (4000K), or RGB multi-color
  • LED activation: magnetic reed switch (lid open → light on), manual slide switch, or touch sensor
  • Power source: replaceable CR2032 coin battery (200hrs life), AAA battery pack, or USB-C rechargeable module
  • Interior cavity: die-cut velvet flocked tray for single piece, or multi-cavity for sets
  • Interior lining: velvet flock (black, navy, burgundy, cream), satin, suede, or leatherette
  • Exterior finish: matte lamination, soft-touch velvet coating, hot foil stamping, embossing
  • Closure options: magnetic flap, satin ribbon tie, hidden magnet, push-lock clasp
  • Board thickness: 1200gsm–1600gsm greyboard core for rigid luxury feel
  • Foil stamping in gold, silver, rose gold, copper, or custom Pantone metallic

Production Specifications

  • KBA 1050 4-color offset press for high-speed precision color printing
  • Heidelberg 7+1 UV press for specialty finishes and instant curing
  • Rigid box assembly line with automated corner taping and wrapping
  • Velvet flocking: electrostatic flocking machine for uniform, durable fiber application
  • LED module: pre-tested ribbon with 50,000-hour rated lifetime, CE certified
  • Automated die-cutting for precision cavity shapes matching product dimensions
