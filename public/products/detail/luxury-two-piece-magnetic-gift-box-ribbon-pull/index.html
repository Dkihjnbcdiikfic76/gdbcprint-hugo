Luxury Two-Piece Magnetic Gift Box with Ribbon Pull

June 11, 2026

Product Overview

This luxury two-piece magnetic closure gift box combines premium materials with elegant functionality. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing — with 22 years of experience since 2003 — the rigid structure uses 2.0mm greyboard wrapped in 157gsm art paper for a smooth, durable finish. A satin ribbon pull under the lid ensures effortless opening, while the N35-grade neodymium magnets deliver a satisfying snap closure. Inside, soft velvet lining protects delicate items like jewelry, watches, or premium cosmetics. With a daily output capacity of 1.7 million color boxes and 220,000+ gift boxes from our 18,000 sqm integrated factory, Bincai delivers at scale without compromising quality.

Features

  • Premium Construction: 2.0mm greyboard core with 157gsm art paper wrap, matte or gloss lamination
  • Magnetic Closure: 4 × N35 neodymium magnets embedded symmetrically for balanced closure force
  • Ribbon Pull: 25mm satin ribbon with heat-sealed ends, color-matched to box exterior
  • Interior Lining: Velvet flocking in black, white, or custom Pantone colors
  • Print Quality: CMYK offset on Heidelberg 7+1 UV press — sharp text, vibrant solids
  • Finishing Options: Soft-touch lamination, spot UV, gold/silver/rose gold foil stamping
  • Size Range: Custom from 120×120×40mm to 380×280×100mm
  • Certifications: ISO 9001:2015 quality management, FSC Chain-of-Custody certified materials
  • Sample Service: 3-5 day pre-production samples with digital proofs

Factory

Bincai operates from an 18,000 sqm facility in Shunde, Foshan, Guangdong — the heart of the Pearl River Delta manufacturing hub. Our production floor features a KBA 1050 4-color press, Heidelberg 7+1 UV press, Bobst flatbed die-cutters, and fully automatic rigid box assembly lines. An 80+ person skilled workforce, supported by 15 quality testing instruments, maintains rigorous QC at every stage: material inspection (G4-1682 spectrophotometer), die-cutting accuracy (±0.15mm), magnet pull-force testing, and final AQL statistical sampling per ISO 2859-1.
