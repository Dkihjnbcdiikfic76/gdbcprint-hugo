Product Overview

This luxury two-piece magnetic closure gift box combines premium materials with elegant functionality. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing — with 22 years of experience since 2003 — the rigid structure uses 2.0mm greyboard wrapped in 157gsm art paper for a smooth, durable finish. A satin ribbon pull under the lid ensures effortless opening, while the N35-grade neodymium magnets deliver a satisfying snap closure. Inside, soft velvet lining protects delicate items like jewelry, watches, or premium cosmetics. With a daily output capacity of 1.7 million color boxes and 220,000+ gift boxes from our 18,000 sqm integrated factory, Bincai delivers at scale without compromising quality. The two-piece construction — separate lid and base — is the gold standard for luxury unboxing, offering a dramatic reveal that elevates brand perception. Each box undergoes individual QC inspection for magnet alignment, ribbon tension, and velvet adhesion before leaving our Shunde facility.
