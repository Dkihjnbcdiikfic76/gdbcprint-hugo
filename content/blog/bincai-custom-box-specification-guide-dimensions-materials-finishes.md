## Step 1: Measuring Your Product for Box Dimensions

The foundation of every successful box project is accurate dimensional specification. Industry convention uses Length × Width × Depth (L × W × D), where:

- **Length (L)**: The longest horizontal dimension of the opening
- **Width (W)**: The shorter horizontal dimension
- **Depth (D)**: The vertical dimension (box height)

### The Golden Rule: Product + Clearance + Insert

Your box interior dimensions must account for three layers:

| Layer | What It Is | Typical Addition |
|---|---|---|
| **Product** | Your item's exact outer dimensions | Baseline measurement |
| **Clearance** | Gap between product and box wall | 1–3 mm per side (snug) or 3–5 mm (easy-fit) |
| **Insert/Interior** | Foam, flocked tray, satin lining, cardboard divider | 2–15 mm depending on insert type and material thickness |

**Formula**: Box Interior Dimension = Product Dimension + (Clearance × 2) + (Insert Thickness × 2)

For example, a watch case measuring 80 × 80 × 60 mm with a snug fit (1.5 mm clearance on each side) and a 5 mm suede-lined foam insert on each wall requires: 80 + (1.5 × 2) + (5 × 2) = 93 mm interior length. Adding the box wall thickness (typically 1.5–2.5 mm for rigid boxes, 0.35–0.6 mm for folding cartons) gives you the exterior dimensions.

### Common Measurement Mistakes

- **Measuring the product inside its existing packaging** — always measure the bare product
- **Forgetting irregular shapes** — a wine bottle neck is narrower than the body; measure the widest point
- **Ignoring closure mechanisms** — magnetic flap boxes require extra internal clearance for the magnet housing
- **One-dimensional thinking** — a 200 × 150 mm box that is 10 mm deep needs very different structural engineering than one that is 100 mm deep

### Flat-Ship Boxes: A Special Case

Bincai designs many boxes for flat-ship delivery — shipped knocked-down and assembled by the buyer or at a fulfillment center. These save 60–75% on freight but require precise structural design for the knockdown ratio and crease alignment. If flat-shipping is part of your logistics plan, mention it at the specification stage — it affects substrate selection, joint design, and scoring specifications.

## Step 2: Choosing the Right Substrate (Material)

The substrate determines structural integrity, print quality, tactile feel, and cost. Here are the primary options we manufacture:

| Substrate | Typical Thickness | Best For | Print Quality | Cost Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SBS (Solid Bleached Sulfate)** | 0.3–0.6 mm (10–24 pt) | Luxury folding cartons, cosmetics, high-end retail | Excellent — bright white, smooth coating | Mid-High |
| **FBB (Folding Box Board)** | 0.35–0.7 mm (14–28 pt) | General folding cartons, food, pharma | Very good — mechanical pulp middle for stiffness | Mid |
| **CCNB (Clay-Coated News Back)** | 0.4–1.0 mm (16–40 pt) | Economy folding cartons, backing boards, rigid box wrap | Good — grey back, one-side coated | Low-Mid |
| **Kraft Paperboard** | 0.3–0.6 mm (12–24 pt) | Eco/natural packaging, food, soap, candles | Moderate — uncoated, natural brown | Low-Mid |
| **Duplex/Grey Board** | 1.0–3.5 mm | Rigid box base, structural support | N/A — usually wrapped/covered | Low |
| **Corrugated (Single/Double/Triple Wall)** | 1.5–8.0 mm | Shipping boxes, mailers, heavy-duty export | Moderate — direct print or litho-laminate | Low-High |

### Material Selection Decision Matrix

| Your Priority | Best Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Premium unboxing** | SBS + rigid box construction | Bright white surface, flawless print, luxury feel |
| **Eco-first brand** | FSC-certified Kraft or 100% PCR board | Natural aesthetic, biodegradable, recyclable |
| **Heavy product protection** | Double-wall corrugated (B/C flute) | BHS 2.5m corrugator, ISTA 3A certified |
| **Cost optimization** | CCNB for folding cartons; litho-laminated corrugated for rigid | 30–50% savings vs SBS with acceptable print quality |
| **Food safety compliance** | FBB or SBS with FDA-compliant barrier coating | Meets 21 CFR 176.170/180, EU 1935/2004 |
| **Flat-ship folding cartons** | SBS or FBB with pre-scored creases | Clean fold lines, automatic bottom lock, no glue required |

## Step 3: Selecting Surface Finishes

Surface finishing transforms a functional box into a brand statement. Bincai offers the full spectrum on our KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses:

| Finish | Effect | Best Application | Production Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Matte Lamination** | Velvet, non-reflective, fingerprint-resistant | Luxury rigid boxes, electronics, spirits | 25 µm BOPP film, heat-applied |
| **Gloss Lamination** | High-shine, vibrant colors, water-resistant | Retail display boxes, toy packaging | 25 µm BOPP film, enhances color saturation |
| **Soft-Touch (Soft-feel)** | Suede-like tactile experience | Premium cosmetics, jewelry, limited editions | Water-based coating, 3–5 µm, Heidelberg UV inline |
| **Spot UV** | Selective high-gloss highlight on matte background | Logo emphasis, pattern accents, QR codes | Heidelberg 7+1 = 7 color + 1 UV coating in a single pass |
| **Hot Foil Stamping** | Metallic gold/silver/copper/rose gold/holographic | Logo, brand name, decorative borders | Bobst SP 102-E autoplaten, ±0.1 mm registration, up to 250 tons pressure |
| **Embossing / Debossing** | Raised or recessed 3D relief | Brand mark, pattern texture, braille | Requires custom die (3–5 days), same Bobst SP 102 platform |
| **Glitter UV** | Sparkling reflective particles in clear coating | Holiday collections, beauty, celebration | Special effect coating, inline on Heidelberg UV |
| **Aqueous Coating** | Protective clear coat, satin or gloss | High-volume folding cartons, food | Fast-drying, scuff-resistant, most economical |
| **Window Patching** | Die-cut window with PET/PVC/PLA film | Product visibility — chocolates, candles, toys | Automated line, 400+ cartons per hour |

### Finish Compatibility

Not every finish works on every substrate. Key rules:

- **Hot foil on uncoated kraft** → foil adheres poorly; use a coated kraft or apply a foil-stampable coating first
- **Soft-touch on porous surfaces** → absorbs unevenly; best on SBS or FBB with pre-coat
- **Spot UV registration** → requires ±0.1 mm precision; Bincai's Heidelberg 7+1 UV inline system achieves this
- **Embossing depth** → limited on thin folding carton board (<0.4 mm); best on 1.5 mm+ rigid box board

**Pro tip**: Request a finish combination sample. Bincai provides free pre-production samples in 5–7 business days so you can feel the texture, see the registration, and verify color matching before mass production begins.

## Step 4: The Specification Checklist — What to Include in Your RFQ

When you email your RFQ to a manufacturer, include these 12 data points for the fastest, most accurate quote:

1. **Product Dimensions** (L × W × D in mm, including product + clearance + insert)
2. **Box Style** (rigid set-up, folding carton, magnetic closure, corrugated mailer, shoulder-neck, drawer/slide, pillow, hexagon, etc.)
3. **Substrate** (SBS, FBB, CCNB, kraft, corrugated — with preferred thickness in mm or pt)
4. **Printing** (colors — 4C/CMYK, PMS spot, or both; single-side or double-side)
5. **Surface Finish** (matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch, spot UV, foil, embossing, aqueous — list all)
6. **Interior** (none, EVA foam insert, flocked tray, satin lining, cardboard divider, die-cut insert, ribbon pull)
7. **Closure** (none/tuck-flap, magnetic, ribbon tie, elastic band, Velcro, snap button)
8. **Quantity** (sample run, pilot 500–1,000, mass production 10,000+)
9. **Packaging** (individual polybag, shrink-wrap, assembled vs flat-ship)
10. **Certifications Required** (FSC, food-grade, EN 71 toy safety, REACH/RoHS, Prop 65)
11. **Reference Images** (photos of competitor packaging, sketches, CAD drawings, or brand guidelines)
12. **Target Price / Budget Range** (optional but helpful — allows us to recommend the most cost-effective substrate and finish combination)

## FAQ

**Q: What is the minimum quantity for a custom paper box order?**
A: Bincai's standard MOQ is 500–1,000 units for folding cartons and 300–500 for rigid boxes, depending on complexity. For brands needing smaller quantities for market testing or limited editions, we offer flexible pilot runs. Our smallest run is typically 200 units — contact us for specifics based on your specifications.

**Q: How do I know which substrate is right for my product?**
A: Ask three questions: (1) What is the weight of my product? (2) Is the box for retail display or e-commerce shipping? (3) What is my target price point? A 50g tube of lipstick needs a very different box than a 5kg electronics kit. Bincai's engineering team provides substrate recommendations as part of our free specification review.

**Q: Can you match a specific Pantone color?**
A: Yes. Bincai's Heidelberg 7+1 UV press with X-Rite IntelliTrax closed-loop color management achieves ΔE < 2.0 (industry standard is ΔE < 3.0). We maintain a library of over 2,000 ink formulations and can match any PMS color. For the most demanding requirements, we provide drawdown samples for approval before production.

**Q: What if I do not have a CAD file or dieline?**
A: No problem. Send us your product dimensions, reference images, and a sketch of what you have in mind. Bincai's in-house structural design team creates the CAD dieline (typically 2–3 days) and sends it for your approval before die making begins.

**Q: How do you ensure color consistency across multiple production runs?**
A: We use X-Rite IntelliTrax spectrophotometry for every print run, maintaining digital records of density, dot gain, and Lab values. For brands with strict color standards, we archive physical reference samples and match to those in addition to digital targets. Our Heidelberg operators average 15+ years of experience — they know color intuitively as well as scientifically.
