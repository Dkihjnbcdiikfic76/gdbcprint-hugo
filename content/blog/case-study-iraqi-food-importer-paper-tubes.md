## Industry

**Food & Dry Goods Import/Distribution** — a Baghdad-based importer serving Iraq's retail food sector with packaged coffee, tea, nuts, dried fruit, protein powders, and premium wine gift sets. Iraq's import-dependent food market relies on ocean freight through the port of Umm Qasr, where summer temperatures exceed 50°C and humidity from the Persian Gulf route challenges every packaging decision. The distributor supplies supermarkets, specialty stores, and food-service channels across Baghdad, Basra, and Erbil — making packaging durability during transport a make-or-break factor.

## The Problem

Before working with Bincai, this Iraqi food importer managed packaging procurement across four separate suppliers:

1. **Fragmented supply chain across categories.** Coffee cans came from one factory, nut containers from another, protein powder tubes from a third, and wine gift boxes from a fourth. Each supplier had different lead times, minimum orders, quality standards, and shipping coordination — creating constant logistics friction. A single delayed container disrupted the entire product rollout.

2. **Moisture failures in transit.** Iraq's Gulf-region ocean freight route exposes cargo to extreme humidity for 25–35 days. Early shipments of plain kraft tubes without barrier lining arrived with warped bodies and compromised seals. Retailers in Basra — where summer humidity regularly hits 90% — reported 8–10% shelf-quality issues on packaged nuts and dried fruits within the first month on display.

3. **Metal and plastic alternatives were economically unviable.** Imported aluminum tins cost 2–3× more than paper tube equivalents and added container weight that inflated freight charges. Plastic containers — while cheaper — faced Iraq's strict import regulations on non-biodegradable packaging and lacked the premium shelf presence the brand needed to compete with imported European products.

## The Bincai Solution

Bincai proposed consolidating all packaging formats into **multi-size, food-grade aluminum-lined paper tubes** from a single factory — same material family, same quality standard, one supplier relationship.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Cylinder paper tubes with aluminum foil inner liner |
| **Sizes** | 250g, 400g, 500g for dry goods; custom for wine bottles |
| **Material** | Food-grade kraft paper + airtight aluminum foil barrier |
| **Closure** | Peel-off aluminum lid or metal plug (category-dependent) |
| **Printing** | Custom CMYK offset printing with brand logo |
| **MOQ** | 1,000–3,500 units per SKU |
| **Certification** | ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody (C147399) |

**Key manufacturing advantages:**

- **Single SKU family across product lines.** Coffee, tea, nuts, and protein powder all use the same tube construction — only size and print artwork vary. This simplified inventory, reduced per-unit cost through volume aggregation, and eliminated cross-supplier quality variation.
- **Airtight aluminum foil liner as standard.** Every tube ships with a peel-off aluminum lid heat-sealed to the body. This creates an oxygen and moisture barrier that withstood Iraq's summer transit conditions — no more warped tubes or clumped powder.
- **No tooling fees for custom sizes.** The paper tube manufacturing process uses spiral winding, meaning diameter and height are software-defined rather than mold-dependent. The importer could trial new SKUs (wine bottle tubes, apparel packaging) without new tooling investment — lowering the barrier to category expansion.
- **Sampling-first approach.** Bincai shipped free samples with the client's logo pre-printed, allowing the Iraqi team to verify shelf appeal and moisture performance locally before committing to production volumes.

## The Results

The importer placed 37 orders over 2.5 years (October 2023 – January 2026), expanding from an initial trial of coffee packaging into five product categories:

| Metric | Before Bincai | After Bincai |
|---|---|---|
| **Packaging suppliers** | 4 separate factories | 1 factory (Bincai) |
| **Shelf-quality issues (month 1)** | 8–10% rejects | <3% |
| **Product categories covered** | 3 (coffee, nuts, wine) | 5 (+ protein powder, apparel) |
| **Largest single order** | 5,000 units | 15,000 units |
| **Supplier relationship** | Transactional, per-order | Ongoing, multi-year partnership |
| **Lead time consistency** | Varies by supplier (25–55 days) | Consistent 20–30 days |

The client's category expansion tells the story: what began as a coffee packaging trial in late 2023 grew to include nut containers, protein powder tubes, wine gift boxes, and even apparel retail packaging by early 2026. Each new category reused the same tube platform — the printing changed, the engineering didn't. The importer now carries Bincai paper tubes across five retail product lines with a single reorder workflow.
