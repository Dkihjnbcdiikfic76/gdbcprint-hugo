## What FSC Certification Means for Packaging

- **Traceability**: Every FSC-certified box can be traced back through the supply chain to responsibly managed forests. Our chain-of-custody system tracks materials from forest to finished box.
- **Environmental Protection**: FSC-certified forests preserve biodiversity, protect water resources, and respect indigenous land rights. Choosing FSC-certified packaging directly supports responsible forestry.
- **Consumer Trust**: 73% of global consumers prefer sustainably packaged products (Nielsen 2023). The FSC logo on your packaging signals environmental commitment that drives purchasing decisions.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) and US Lacey Act increasingly require proof of sustainable sourcing. FSC certification provides the documentation needed for market access.

## Bincai's FSC Commitment

## FAQ

**Q: Does FSC certification add significant cost to my packaging?**
A: FSC-certified paperboard typically adds 5–12% to raw material cost. For a standard rigid gift box, this translates to roughly $0.05–$0.15 per unit. Many brands find this offset by the marketing value and consumer preference benefits.

**Q: How is the FSC logo applied to the box?**
A: The FSC logo can be printed directly on your box during production along with your brand artwork. We'll provide a mockup showing the correct FSC logo placement and size per FSC branding guidelines. The logo requires a minimum size of 10mm and must include the certification code.

**Q: Can existing packaging designs be converted to FSC?**
A: Yes — we can produce your existing packaging designs using FSC-certified materials without changing the visual appearance. The only addition is the FSC logo. Material performance (rigidity, print quality, folding endurance) is identical to conventional paperboard.
