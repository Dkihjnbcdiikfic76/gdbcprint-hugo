## Shipping Modes: Sea Freight vs Air Freight

| Factor | Sea Freight (FCL) | Sea Freight (LCL) | Air Freight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cost per kg** | $0.80–$1.50 | $1.50–$3.00 | $4.50–$8.00 |
| **Minimum volume** | 20ft container (28 CBM) | 1–15 CBM | No minimum |
| **Transit to US West Coast** | 18–25 days | 22–30 days | 5–7 days |
| **Transit to EU (Rotterdam)** | 28–35 days | 32–40 days | 6–8 days |
| **Transit to Middle East** | 18–25 days | 20–28 days | 4–6 days |
| **Best for** | Orders > 15 CBM | Orders 2–15 CBM | Urgent / < 2 CBM |

**Real example:** A US cosmetics brand orders 50,000 folding cartons (approx. 8 CBM). LCL sea freight to Los Angeles costs ~$2,400 and takes 25 days. Air freight would cost ~$12,000 but arrive in 7 days. The brand chooses LCL sea freight because the 18-day difference isn't worth $9,600.

## Container Loading: How Many Boxes Fit?

Paper boxes are lightweight but bulky — you almost always **"cube out" before you weigh out.** Here's what fits in standard containers:

### 20ft Container (28 CBM payload)

| Box Type | Folded Dimensions (mm) | Boxes per Carton | Cartons per Pallet | Pallets per 20ft | **Total Boxes** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Folding Cartons (flat) | 400 × 300 × 2 | 500 | 24 | 10 | **120,000** |
| Corrugated Mailers (flat) | 500 × 400 × 3 | 200 | 20 | 10 | **40,000** |
| Rigid Gift Boxes (assembled) | 250 × 200 × 80 | 50 | 12 | 10 | **6,000** |
| Jewelry Boxes (assembled) | 120 × 80 × 50 | 200 | 16 | 10 | **32,000** |

### 40ft High Cube (68 CBM payload)

| Box Type | Folded Dimensions (mm) | Boxes per Carton | Cartons per Pallet | Pallets per 40HQ | **Total Boxes** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Folding Cartons (flat) | 400 × 300 × 2 | 500 | 24 | 20 | **240,000** |
| Corrugated Mailers (flat) | 500 × 400 × 3 | 200 | 20 | 20 | **80,000** |
| Rigid Gift Boxes (assembled) | 250 × 200 × 80 | 50 | 12 | 20 | **12,000** |
| Magnetic Closure Boxes (assembled) | 300 × 250 × 60 | 40 | 10 | 20 | **8,000** |

**Pro tip:** Folding cartons and corrugated mailers ship flat — that's why the quantities are dramatically higher. If your rigid box design can be flat-packed for assembly at destination, you can increase container capacity 5–10× and slash per-unit freight cost by 60–80%.

## Incoterms: Which One Should You Use?

| Incoterm | What Bincai Handles | What You Handle | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EXW (Ex Works)** | Production only | Everything from factory gate onward | Experienced importers with own forwarder |
| **FOB (Free On Board)** | Production + trucking to port + export clearance | Ocean freight + insurance + destination | Most common — we recommend this |
| **CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight)** | All of FOB + ocean freight + insurance | Destination customs + duties + trucking | Buyers who want freight included in invoice |
| **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** | Everything door-to-door (including duties & taxes) | Nothing — we handle it all | First-time importers, Amazon FBA sellers |

**Bincai's recommendation:** Start with FOB Shenzhen. You control the freight forwarder relationship (critical for competitive rates), and we handle the factory-to-vessel leg including Chinese export customs. Once you've established a relationship with a forwarder, transition to CIF for simplicity. DDP is available for established clients with predictable duty rates.

## Customs Documentation: What You Need

Every Bincai export shipment includes a complete documentation package:

| Document | Purpose | Issued By |
|---|---|---|
| **Commercial Invoice** | Declares value, HS code, country of origin | Bincai |
| **Packing List** | Carton count, dimensions, weight per carton | Bincai |
| **Bill of Lading (B/L)** | Contract of carriage with shipping line | Carrier / Forwarder |
| **Certificate of Origin (Form A / Form F)** | Qualifies for preferential duties under GSP / China-ASEAN FTA | CCPIT / Customs |
| **FSC Chain-of-Custody Certificate** | Proves paperboard from FSC-certified forests | Bincai (FSC-C156391) |
| **ISO 9001:2015 Certificate** | Factory quality management system | SGS / Bureau Veritas |
| **Fumigation Certificate** | Required for solid wood pallets (ISPM 15) | Inspection agency |

**HS Code for paper boxes:** 4819.10 / 4819.20 — "Cartons, boxes and cases, of corrugated paper or paperboard" and "Folding cartons, boxes and cases, of non-corrugated paper or paperboard." US duty rate: **0–1.1%** under normal trade relations. EU: **0%** for most paper packaging categories.

## Common Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

**Mistake 1: Not budgeting for destination charges.**
Ocean freight is only part of the cost. Budget for: THC (terminal handling charge) at destination ($150–$300), customs brokerage ($100–$250), duty (0–6% depending on country), trucking ($300–$800), and warehouse receiving. Total destination costs for a 20ft container to the US typically run **$800–$1,500** beyond the freight itself.

**Mistake 2: Underestimating transit time during peak season.**
August through October (back-to-school through holiday season) adds 7–14 days to ocean transit. Container space becomes scarce and rates rise 30–50%. **Plan Q4 orders by June.**

**Mistake 3: Forgetting to request FSC Chain-of-Custody documentation.**
If your brand markets FSC-certified packaging, you need the CoC certificate at customs. Request it when you place the order — retroactive documentation is expensive and slow. Bincai's FSC license: **FSC-C156391.**

**Mistake 4: Using solid wood pallets without ISPM 15 treatment.**
ISPM 15 requires heat treatment or methyl bromide fumigation for all solid wood packaging. Bincai uses ISPM 15-compliant plywood pallets by default — ask for the fumigation certificate if your destination country requires it.

**Mistake 5: Not insuring your cargo.**
Marine cargo insurance costs ~0.3–0.5% of declared value. On a $30,000 shipment, that's $90–$150. Bincai automatically insures all shipments under our CIF terms. For FOB terms, arrange coverage through your forwarder.
