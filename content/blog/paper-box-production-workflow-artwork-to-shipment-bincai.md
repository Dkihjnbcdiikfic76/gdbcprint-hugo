## What Happens After You Place an Order?

For many international buyers, the gap between "we approved the artwork" and "boxes arrived at the warehouse" is a black box. This post opens it up — walking through each production stage inside Guangdong Bincai Color Printing's 18,000-square-meter facility so you know exactly what happens to your order, how long each step takes, and what quality controls protect your brand at every stage.

---

## Stage 1: Pre-Press & Artwork Engineering (1–2 Days)

### File Reception & Validation

Every order starts with your artwork files — typically Adobe Illustrator (.ai), PDF, or EPS. Our pre-press team checks:

| Check | Tool | What We Verify |
|---|---|---|
| **Resolution** | Adobe Acrobat Pro | 300+ DPI at print size |
| **Bleed** | PitStop Pro | 3 mm bleed on all sides |
| **Color mode** | Enfocus Switch | CMYK (not RGB) |
| **Font embedding** | Preflight | All fonts outlined or embedded |
| **Overprint/Trapping** | Acrobat Output Preview | Correct overprint settings for white ink, varnish |
| **Die-line accuracy** | CAD overlay | Die line matches structural design |
| **Barcode scannability** | Axicon verifier | UPC/EAN reads at target magnification |

### Digital Proofing

Before plates are made, you receive a **digital color proof** (PDF via email) showing exact trim lines, fold positions, and color separations. For premium orders — rigid gift boxes, luxury packaging — we offer a **hard-copy Epson proof** calibrated to ISO 12647-2 standards, shipped by express courier.

### CTP Plate Making

Once the proof is approved, our **Kodak Magnus Q800 CTP** system images aluminum plates at 2,400 DPI. Plates are processed, inspected under a 10× loupe, and queued for press within 4 hours.

## Stage 2: Offset Printing (1–3 Days)

### Press Selection

Your order is routed to the optimal press based on volume, color requirements, and substrate:

| Press | When We Use It | Max Speed | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **KBA Rapida 1050** | 4-color (CMYK), high-volume | 15,000 sph | Folding cartons, corrugated mailers, kraft boxes |
| **Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV** | CMYK + spot colors + UV coating in one pass | 15,000 sph | Premium rigid boxes, magnetic closure, jewelry boxes |
| **Heidelberg Speedmaster SM 52** | Short runs, small format | 12,000 sph | Samples, prototypes, low-MOQ orders |

### Inline Color Management

Our **X-Rite IntelliTrax** automated spectrophotometry system scans color bars on every sheet, comparing L*a*b* values to ISO 12647-2 targets. Deviations beyond ΔE 2.0 trigger an automatic press stop. Production sheets are pulled every 500 impressions for manual densitometer verification.

### Substrate Range

We print on paperboard from 200 gsm (lightweight folding cartons) to 1,200 gsm (rigid box wraps), including:

- **SBS (Solid Bleached Sulfate)** — premium white, ideal for cosmetics and luxury
- **Coated Duplex Board** — grey back, economical for consumer goods
- **Kraft Board** — natural brown, FSC-certified for eco brands
- **Specialty Boards** — metallized, holographic, black core, textured

## Stage 3: Surface Finishing (1–2 Days)

After printing, sheets go through one or more finishing processes:

| Process | Machine | Effect | Typical Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lamination** | Wen Chyuan auto laminator | Gloss or matte film, 25–30 micron | Protects print, adds durability |
| **Spot UV** | Heidelberg cylinder with UV curing | Glossy raised varnish on selected areas | Logo highlight, pattern effects |
| **Foil Stamping** | Bobst SP 102 BMA hot foil stamper | Gold, silver, copper, rose gold, holographic | Luxury branding, metallic accents |
| **Embossing/Debossing** | Bobst SP 102-E with embossing die | Raised or recessed 3D design | Tactile logos, premium feel |
| **Soft-Touch Coating** | Roller coater | Velvet-matte finish | High-end cosmetics, electronics |
| **Aqueous Varnish** | Heidelberg inline coating unit | Gloss, matte, or satin protective coat | General protection, scuff resistance |

Each finishing station has its own QC checkpoint. Foil registration is verified to ±0.2 mm tolerance using overlay gauges; spot UV alignment is checked under UV blacklight.

## Stage 4: Die-Cutting & Creasing (1–2 Days)

### Die Preparation

For every new order, our tooling department produces a custom **steel rule cutting die** mounted on 18 mm birch plywood. The die includes:

- **Cutting rules** — hardened steel blades that slice through paperboard
- **Creasing rules** — rounded rules that compress fibers for clean folding
- **Perforating rules** — for tear-off panels and coupons
- **Rubber ejection** — strips that push cut blanks out of the die

Die accuracy is verified against the CAD structural design on an optical comparator.

### Bobst SP 102-E Automatic Die-Cutter

The heart of our die-cutting operation is the Swiss-engineered **Bobst SP 102-E**, which processes 7,500 sheets per hour at ±0.1 mm registration accuracy. Its centerline system ensures every sheet registers to the same reference point regardless of sheet-to-sheet variation.

For large-format rigid boxes (up to 600×450 mm), our **Bobst 102 BMA** handles the heavier 1,200+ gsm board that smaller cutters can't manage.

### Waste Stripping & Blank Separation

The Bobst's integrated stripping station removes internal waste (windows, slots) and the nicking tool separates individual blanks from the sheet grid. 100% of blanks pass through a conveyor inspection where operators check for:

- Clean cuts without burring or tearing
- Crease depth that folds without cracking
- No residual waste or hangers
- Print-to-cut registration within ±0.3 mm

## Stage 5: Folding & Gluing (1–2 Days)

### Automated Folder-Gluers

Three automated folder-gluers run in parallel, each configured for specific box styles:

| Machine | Speed | Box Styles |
|---|---|---|
| **Bobst Mistral 110 A2** | 100,000 boxes/hour | Straight-line, crash-lock, 4-corner, 6-corner |
| **Bobst Ambition 106 A1** | 80,000 boxes/hour | Auto-lock bottom, tuck-top, tuck-end |
| **Brandtjen & Kluge EHF** | 60,000 boxes/hour | Rigid box wrapping, magnetic closure assembly |

### Adhesive Systems

We use three adhesive technologies depending on the box's end use:

- **Hot-melt (EVA-based)** — instant bond for high-speed folding cartons; Nordson application systems with ±0.01 g precision
- **Cold emulsion (PVAc)** — for paper-to-paper bonds; longer open time for complex folds
- **PUR (polyurethane reactive)** — waterproof, food-safe; used for freezer packaging and high-humidity export shipments

All adhesives are **FDA 21 CFR 175.105 compliant** for indirect food contact and stored in climate-controlled rooms at 20±2°C.

### Inline Quality Checks

Each gluer has:
- **Glue detection sensors** — infrared cameras verify bead presence and continuity on every box
- **Barcode scanners** — confirm the correct blank is being processed (prevents mix-ups)
- **Coded eject gates** — defective boxes are automatically diverted without stopping the line
- **Batch counters** — tied to the ERP system for real-time production tracking

## Stage 6: Manual Assembly & Embellishment (1–3 Days — Rigid Boxes Only)

Rigid gift boxes, magnetic closure boxes, and jewelry boxes require skilled manual assembly:

| Step | Description | Skill Level | Pieces/Hour |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chipboard wrapping** | Hand-wrapping printed sheet around 1.5–2.5 mm greyboard | Experienced | 60–80 |
| **Magnet insertion** | Embedding N35/N52 neodymium magnets in lid and base | Specialist | 100–120 |
| **Ribbon/elastic fitting** | Securing satin ribbon pulls, elastic bands | General | 80–100 |
| **Interior lining** | Flocking, foam inserts, satin, velvet, or suede lining | Specialist | 40–60 |
| **Window patching** | Die-cut PET or PVC window film application | Experienced | 80–100 |
| **Final assembly** | Lid-to-base marriage, hinge alignment | QC operator | 50–70 |

Each station has a laminated QC card showing acceptable tolerances. Our 80+ skilled workers — many with 15+ years of box-making experience — perform these operations with consistency that machines alone cannot replicate.

## Stage 7: Quality Control — 5-Stage Inspection (Continuous)

Our quality system, certified to **ISO 9001:2015**, operates at five checkpoints:

| Stage | Inspection Point | Method | AQL Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Incoming** | Paperboard, adhesives, magnets, foil | Visual + lab test | AQL 1.5 (normal) |
| **2 — In-Process** | Press sheets, die-cut blanks | X-Rite IntelliTrax + visual | AQL 1.0 (tightened) |
| **3 — Post-Production** | Finished boxes before packing | Dimensional + functional | AQL 0.65 (tightened) |
| **4 — Pre-Shipment** | Packed cartons, random sampling | Full FRI (Final Random Inspection) | AQL 0.4 (tightened) |
| **5 — Outgoing** | Pallet condition, labeling, documentation | Visual + document audit | 100% verification |

### Quality Testing Laboratory

Our in-house lab performs:

- **Burst strength** (Mullen tester) — measures paperboard resistance to rupture
- **Edge crush test (ECT)** — critical for corrugated mailers
- **Cobb test** — water absorption rate of paperboard
- **Rub resistance** (Sutherland) — verifies print and coating durability
- **Adhesion testing** — peel strength of laminates and glued joints
- **Climate aging** — 48-hour accelerated aging at 40°C/75% RH

### Packing Specifications

| Box Type | Inner Pack | Outer Pack | Pcs/Carton |
|---|---|---|---|
| Folding cartons (flat) | PE bag, 50 pcs | 5-ply export corrugated | 500–1,000 |
| Rigid boxes (assembled) | Individual PE bag + tissue | 5-ply with corner protectors | 12–48 |
| Corrugated mailers (flat) | PE bag, 25 pcs | 5-ply export corrugated | 200–500 |
| Magnetic closure (assembled) | Individual PE bag + foam separator | 5-ply with dividers | 24–72 |

### Palletization & Container Loading

All pallets are heat-treated (ISPM 15), stretch-wrapped, and labeled with:
- Purchase order number
- Product code and description
- Carton quantity and net/gross weight
- Country of origin (Made in China)
- Handling instructions

Our logistics team coordinates FOB Foshan, CIF, and DDP shipments. A typical 20-foot container holds 180,000–250,000 flat folding cartons or 15,000–25,000 assembled rigid boxes. We provide **loading supervision photos** and **container seal numbers** by email before departure.
