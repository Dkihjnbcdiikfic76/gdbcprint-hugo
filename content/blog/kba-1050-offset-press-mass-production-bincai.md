Behind every paper box shipped from Guangdong Bincai Color Printing is a manufacturing engine that runs with German precision. At the heart of our 10,000 sqm factory in Foshan's Pearl River Delta manufacturing hub sits the **KBA 1050 4-color offset press** — a workhorse that anchors our daily output of **1.7 million boxes**.

## Why KBA 1050?

The Koenig & Bauer (KBA) Rapida 105 is a large-format sheetfed offset press built for high-volume commercial printing. At Bincai, this press handles:

- **Format size up to 740 × 1,050 mm** — accommodating large folding carton layouts and rigid box wraps with minimal waste
- **4-color CMYK in a single pass** — consistent, sharp color registration at speeds exceeding 15,000 sheets per hour
- **Heavy stock capability** — handles paperboard from 200 gsm lightweight cartons to 600 gsm rigid box covers without compromise
- **Quick-change plate system** — enables fast job turnover between production batches, keeping lead times short

## The Two-Press Strategy: KBA + Heidelberg

Bincai operates a dual-press production line that no single-technology shop can match:

| Capability | KBA 1050 (4-Color) | Heidelberg 7+1 UV |
|---|---|---|
| **Role** | Mass production backbone | Premium & specialty finishing |
| **Colors** | CMYK (4) | CMYK + 3 spot + inline UV coating |
| **Best for** | High-volume folding cartons, corrugated mailers, rigid box wraps | Luxury packaging, metallic inks, spot UV, soft-touch effects |
| **Speed** | 15,000+ sph | 15,000+ sph |

This combination means a single order can run through the KBA for base printing, then through the Heidelberg for premium spot UV or foil-ready coating — all under one roof, with no outsourcing delays.
