## The Challenge

A premium tea brand in Switzerland was sourcing from two separate suppliers — one for retail gift boxes and another for bulk tea powder canisters. This created inconsistent branding, double the logistics overhead, and mismatched lead times. The gift box supplier had a 45-day minimum lead time, while the canister supplier could not match the client's eco-friendly FSC-certified material requirements. With EU Single-Use Plastics Directive compliance approaching, the brand needed a single partner who could deliver both formats with sustainable materials and unified quality control.

## The Bincai Solution

Bincai proposed a dual-format packaging program: custom folding gift boxes with satin ribbon closure for retail gift sets, plus food-grade kraft paper tube cans with aluminum foil inner liner for bulk tea powder. Both formats shared the same CMYK offset printing, matte lamination, and brand color profile — ensuring visual consistency across channels.

We shipped 3 free sample variations within 7 days. The client tested each format with their Swiss retail partners and approved within 2 weeks.

**Specifications:**
| Format | Retail Gift Box | Bulk Tube Can |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| Material | FSC-certified art paper + greyboard | FSC-certified kraft paper + aluminum foil liner |
| Printing | CMYK offset + matte lamination | CMYK offset + matte finish |
| Closure | Satin ribbon | Aluminum easy-peel lid |
| MOQ | 5,000 units | 5,000 units |

## The Results

| Metric | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Suppliers | 2 (gift box + canister) | 1 (Bincai unified) |
| Unit cost (gift box) | $0.38/pcs | $0.24/pcs |
| Unit cost (tube can) | $1.22/pcs | $1.08/pcs |
| Sample turnaround | 21 days (gift box supplier) | 7 days |
| Material certification | Supplier-dependent | FSC-certified, both formats |
| Brand consistency | Two suppliers, two QC standards | Single QC, unified print profile |
| EU SUPD compliance | Partial | Full (paper-based, no plastics) |

The client scaled from 12,000 gift boxes in their first order to a repeat order of 10,000 tube cans within 6 months. Procurement costs dropped 15% by consolidating two suppliers into one, and brand consistency improved across all Swiss retail channels.
