Choosing between rigid, folding, and corrugated paper boxes? Here is the complete guide from Guangdong Bincai Color Printing — 22 years of manufacturing experience.

## Rigid Gift Boxes

Rigid boxes (also called set-up boxes) are made from 2-3mm greyboard wrapped in premium paper. They are non-collapsible, giving them unmatched structural integrity and a luxury feel.

**Best for:** Cosmetics, jewelry, electronics, luxury gifts, perfume, wine, watches
**MOQ:** 500-1000 units
**Price:** $0.50-$3.00/unit
**Features:** Magnetic closure, ribbon pull, velvet insert, gold foil stamping, embossing

Our KBA 1050 4-color offset press and Heidelberg 7+1 UV press ensure vibrant, precise printing for every rigid box.

## Folding Cartons

Folding cartons are made from 200-400gsm paperboard, die-cut and creased for flat shipping. They are the most cost-effective option for retail packaging.

**Best for:** Retail products, cosmetics, food, electronics, pharmaceuticals
**MOQ:** 1000-3000 units
**Price:** $0.10-$0.80/unit
**Features:** Custom die-cut shapes, window cutouts, hang tabs, auto-lock bottom

With 1.7 million boxes daily output, we handle any volume.

## Corrugated Mailers

Corrugated boxes use B, E, or F flute construction for strength and protection during shipping.

**Best for:** E-commerce shipping, subscription boxes, product mailers
**MOQ:** 500-1000 units  
**Price:** $0.25-$1.50/unit
**Features:** Self-locking bottom, tear strip, custom print, high burst strength

All our boxes can be produced with FSC certified materials and soy-based inks for truly sustainable packaging.
