## Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.

Founded in 2003, we are a full-chain printing and packaging enterprise integrating design, development, raw material processing, printing, laminating, die-cutting, mounting, gluing, and logistics — all under one roof.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Founded | 2003 (22 years) |
| Factory Area | 10,000 sqm (+ 8,000 sqm smart factory) |
| Employees | 80+ skilled workers |
| Daily Output | 1.5M color boxes + 200K gift boxes |
| Clients | 200+ global brands |
| Exported To | 50+ countries |
