## Product Categories

### [Rigid Gift Boxes](/categories/rigid-gift-boxes/)
Luxury rigid boxes with 1200–1800gsm greyboard core, wrapped in art paper, PU leatherette, or fabric. Magnetic closure, ribbon pull, hot foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, soft-touch lamination. Ideal for luxury goods, corporate gifts, electronics, and premium retail packaging.

### [Folding Cartons](/categories/folding-cartons/)
Auto-lock bottom, tuck-top, reverse-tuck, and sleeve folding cartons in 250–450gsm FBB/SBS/CCNB. CMYK offset + Pantone spot colors, aqueous/UV coating, die-cut windows, food-grade inks. For food, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, electronics, and retail.

### [Corrugated Mailers](/categories/corrugated-mailers/)
B/C/E/F-flute corrugated shipping boxes and e-commerce mailers. Single-wall, double-wall, full-overlap (FOL), regular slotted (RSC), die-cut custom shapes. Tear strips, perforations, honeycomb inserts. Heavy-duty industrial and subscription box packaging.

### [Magnetic Closure Boxes](/categories/magnetic-closure-boxes/)
Premium magnetic flip-lid boxes with N52 neodymium magnets embedded in greyboard. Rigid construction with book-style opening, satin/velvet lining, ribbon pull tabs, hot foil debossing. For luxury gift sets, electronics, corporate presentation kits, and jewelry.

### [Kraft & Eco Boxes](/categories/kraft-eco-boxes/)
FSC-certified kraft paper boxes with soy-based inks, water-based adhesives, and biodegradable lamination. Bamboo fiber handles, seed-embedded hang tags, PLA windows. 100% recyclable and compostable options. For sustainable brands and eco-conscious packaging.

### [Jewelry Boxes](/categories/jewelry-boxes/)
Velvet flocking, PU leatherette, satin-lined jewelry boxes with ring rolls, necklace hooks, earring strips, and bracelet compartments. Magnetic flip lids, drawer slides, lock-and-key hardware. For fine jewelry retail, bridal, and luxury watch presentation.
