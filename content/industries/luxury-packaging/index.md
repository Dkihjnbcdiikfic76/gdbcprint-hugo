## Luxury Packaging Solutions

### Rigid Gift Boxes
Double-layer greyboard construction (1200–2000gsm) with premium art paper wrap (128–250gsm). Available with magnetic closure (N52 neodymium magnets, polarity sensor verified), ribbon tie with reinforced anchor points, or precision hinged lid. Book-style, clamshell, two-piece telescopic, drawer-slide, and custom configurations. Edge gilding (gold/silver/colored) and mitered corner wrapping for seamless luxury appearance.

### Jewelry & Watch Boxes
Velvet flocking (18 colors, rayon fibers electrostatically bonded), satin (190T woven polyester), suede (microfiber, 0.8mm), or premium leatherette (PU, 0.6mm) interior linings. LED mirror lids (3000K warm white), necklace hooks (velvet-wrapped), ring slots (die-cut foam or flocked groove), watch pillows (satin-covered foam), bracelet/bangle grooves, and fully custom compartment layouts. Multi-tier designs with up to 5 drawers.

### Premium Magnetic Closure
N42-N52 grade neodymium magnets (1.17–1.48 Tesla remanence) with triple-layer nickel-copper-nickel corrosion-resistant plating. Single-door, double-door, book-style, and perimeter-strip configurations. Magnets CNC-routed to ±0.3mm tolerance in greyboard cavities. Pull force ≥2.5 kgF per pair. 100% go/no-go flush-closure inspection on every box. 10,000-cycle hinge durability tested.
