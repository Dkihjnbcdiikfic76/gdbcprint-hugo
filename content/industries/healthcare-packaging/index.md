Guangdong Bincai Color Printing produces pharmaceutical and healthcare packaging at our 18,000 sqm ISO 9001:2015 and FSC-certified facility in Foshan. With KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses producing 1.7 million boxes daily, we serve pharmaceutical companies, medical device manufacturers, and healthcare distributors worldwide.

## Healthcare Packaging Solutions

### Folding Cartons
Pharmaceutical folding cartons with tamper-evident features, braille embossing, and serialization-ready print. CMYK offset + Pantone spot colors on FSC-certified board. ISO 9001 quality control with batch traceability.

### Rigid Gift Boxes
Premium rigid boxes for medical device kits, surgical instrument sets, and healthcare gift packs. Custom foam/EVA inserts protect delicate instruments. Magnetic closure with velvet flocking interior.

### Corrugated Mailers
Temperature-controlled shipping boxes for pharmaceutical logistics. B/E-flute corrugated with insulated liners. ISTA 1A drop-test certified. Flat-pack design for warehouse efficiency.
