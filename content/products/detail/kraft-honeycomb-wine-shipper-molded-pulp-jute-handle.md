The kraft paper honeycomb wine shipper represents a breakthrough in sustainable wine logistics — combining FSC-certified kraft outer construction with a hexagonal honeycomb structural core that delivers crush resistance rivaling double-wall corrugated at 40% less material weight. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. — a 22-year Foshan packaging specialist with 18,000 sqm of dual-factory production — this shipper uses 100% biodegradable molded pulp bottle cradles and jute rope carry handles for a fully home-compostable packaging system.

Bincai operates KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset and Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV presses for winery logo reproduction using soy-based inks. The honeycomb core is produced in-house using water-based PVA adhesive, and molded pulp inserts are manufactured from post-industrial recycled paper pulp. ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, and OK Compost HOME (EN 13432) certified.

## Customization Options

- **Honeycomb Core:** Hexagonal cell sizes of 12mm, 15mm, or 21mm depending on required crush strength. Larger cells for lighter payloads, smaller cells for heavy champagne/magnum bottles
- **Bottle Configuration:** 1-bottle, 2-bottle, 3-bottle, and 6-bottle molded pulp inserts. Custom configurations for mixed bottle sizes (750ml + 375ml) or magnum (1.5L)
- **Board Outer:** Natural kraft (unbleached) or bleached white kraft. Optional clay-coated kraft for higher print fidelity
- **Printing:** Single or dual-color soy-based ink printing on kraft surface. Hot foil stamping for winery logo in gold, silver, or copper
- **Handle:** Natural jute rope (6mm diameter) with reinforced knot anchors. Optional cotton webbing handle for heavier loads
- **Closure:** Integrated tuck-top flaps with die-cut locking tabs. No tape or adhesive required for assembly
- **Branding:** Custom-printed wine information panel, tasting notes card slot, vintage and varietal labeling area
- **Inserts:** Biodegradable molded pulp with contoured bottle cradles. Optional divider wings for glass-to-glass separation in multi-bottle configurations
- **Add-ons:** Seed paper thank-you card, compostable cellulose window for bottle visibility, hemp twine additional securing
