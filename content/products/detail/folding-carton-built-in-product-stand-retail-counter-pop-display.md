Innovative dual-function folding carton that transforms from shipping mailer to countertop display stand — manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.**, a Diamond Member on Made-in-China.com since 2015, Audited Supplier with 22 years of experience. The patented crease-and-fold structure ships flat as a standard carton for product protection during transit, then unfolds at retail into an angled display stand that elevates the product for maximum visibility. Die-cut locking tabs hold the display position without glue, tape, or tools — pure structural engineering from Bincai's in-house design team. Eliminates the need for separate retail display fixtures, reducing packaging waste and store labor. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across 18,000 sqm factory (10,000+8,000 sqm smart factory).

## Customization Options
- Board: SBS C1S 300gsm–450gsm for premium look; kraft back for eco-conscious brands
- Display angle: 15°, 30°, or 45° tilt for optimal product visibility at counter height
- Window: die-cut product display window with clear PET or anti-glare PET
- Product retention: die-cut cradle tabs, elastic band slots, or foam adhesive pads
- Printing: CMYK offset with up to 6 PMS spot colors; full interior and exterior printing
- Surface: gloss/matte aqueous coating, soft-touch lamination, or spot UV on branding
- Foil: hot foil stamping on logo area; gold, silver, rose gold, or copper
- Tear-away: perforated panel reveals stand structure; tamper-evident retail transformation
- Size: die-cut to any dimensions; standard counter footprint 80×80mm to 200×150mm
- Multi-pack: cascading multi-tier display for product ranges (3–6 SKU presentation)
