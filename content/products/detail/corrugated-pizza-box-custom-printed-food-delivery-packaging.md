## Production Workflow

1. **Corrugating** — in-house single-face laminating line bonds liner to fluting medium under heat and pressure
2. **Board Cutting** — corrugated sheets cut to blank size on rotary shear with automatic stacker
3. **Printing** — flexographic (volume runs) or KBA 1050/Heidelberg offset (premium) with inline spectrophotometer color monitoring
4. **Die-Cutting** — flatbed die-cutter shapes box with integrated vent holes, fold lines, and locking tabs
5. **Slotting & Scoring** — precision creasing ensures clean, square folds for professional box appearance
6. **Gluing** — high-speed folder-gluer applies food-grade adhesive to manufacturer's joint
7. **QC Inspection** — burst strength test (Mullen), edge crush test (ECT), grease penetration test, print registration check
8. **Packing & Palletizing** — flat-packed boxes counted, banded, and palletized with moisture barrier wrap

## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between E-flute and B-flute for pizza boxes?**
A: E-flute (1.2mm thickness) provides a smoother printing surface for premium offset litho branding and a more compact profile — ideal for retail frozen pizza packaging and high-end pizzerias. B-flute (2.5mm thickness) offers 25% higher crush resistance (ECT 40 vs. ECT 32) and better heat insulation — preferred for delivery operations where boxes are stacked in hot bags. We can produce sample runs of both for side-by-side testing.

**Q: Are the inks and coatings food-safe?**
A: Yes. All printing inks and coatings used on food-contact surfaces comply with FDA 21 CFR (USA) and EU Regulation 1935/2004. We use water-based flexographic inks and soy-based offset inks — both are low-migration formulations tested by accredited third-party laboratories. Full migration test reports are available for your regulatory documentation.

**Q: How do the steam vents prevent sogginess?**
A: The die-cut or laser-cut steam vents (typically 4–8 vents of 8mm diameter) allow water vapor to escape from the hot pizza rather than condensing on the box ceiling and dripping back onto the crust. Vents can be arranged in a custom pattern — the Bincai standard layout places 2 vents on each side panel plus 2 center-roof vents for cross-ventilation. For extra-crispy requirements, we can increase vent count or diameter.

**Q: Can I print different designs on different box sizes in one order?**
A: Yes. You can mix up to 3 box sizes (e.g., 10\", 14\", 18\") with different artwork per size within a single production run, provided the same corrugated flute and liner specification is used. Each size requires its own printing plate, and there's a plate charge of approximately $80–150 per design depending on complexity. Minimum combined order quantity is 1,000 units (flexo) or 2,000 units (offset).

**Q: What's the lead time for a new pizza box design?**
A: New custom designs: 10–15 working days (includes artwork proofing, plate making, die fabrication, and production). Repeat orders: 5–7 working days. Rush service (7 days for new designs) is available with a 15% surcharge. Free pre-production samples in 5–7 days for approval before mass production.

**Q: Can these boxes be stored flat and assembled on demand?**
A: Yes. Bincai pizza boxes are shipped flat-packed and can be assembled in under 5 seconds by restaurant staff. The fold-and-lock design requires no glue or tape. For high-volume operations, we can also supply pre-glued knock-down-flat (KDF) boxes that pop open with one motion — MOQ 5,000 units. Both formats stack efficiently, with flat-packed saving approximately 60% warehouse space vs. assembled boxes.

**Q: What certifications do the corrugated boxes carry?**
A: ISO 9001:2015 (quality management), FSC Chain-of-Custody (sustainable forestry — license code available for client labeling), FDA 21 CFR (US food contact), EU 1935/2004 (EU food contact). Additional certifications available on request: ISPM 15 (heat-treated export pallets), BRCGS Packaging, and Sedex SMETA social compliance audit.
