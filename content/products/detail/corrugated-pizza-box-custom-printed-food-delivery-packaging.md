Custom printed corrugated pizza box for food delivery and takeout packaging — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). Engineered for heat retention, grease resistance, and structural integrity during delivery transport, these corrugated pizza boxes keep pizzas hot, crisp, and presentable from oven to doorstep. Available in standard square formats (8" to 18") plus custom sizes, with single-wall E-flute or B-flute corrugated construction. KBA 1050 offset and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses print vibrant branding directly onto kraft or white-top liner across our 10,000 sqm ISO 9001 & FSC certified factory. Daily output capacity of 1.5 million boxes meets the demands of chain pizzerias and ghost kitchens.

## Customization Options
- Corrugated flute: E-flute (thin, crisp printing) or B-flute (thicker, stronger) single-wall construction
- Liner options: natural kraft, white-top kraft, coated white for photo-quality printing
- Flexographic printing: up to 4 colors, cost-effective for large-volume runs
- Offset litho printing: KBA 1050 CMYK with Pantone matching for premium brand graphics
- Custom structural designs: standard tuck-top, side-lock, auto-bottom, or unique die-cut shapes
- Ventilation holes: laser-cut or die-cut steam vents to maintain pizza crispness
- Perforated tear strip for easy serving — box folds flat into serving plates
- Food-grade inks and coatings: water-based, soy-based, and low-migration options
- Grease-resistant interior coating or greaseproof liner sheet for heavy-topping pizzas
- Custom brand printing: full-color logo, menu, social media QR codes, and nutritional info
- Sizes from 8" personal pizza to 18" family/party size with custom dimensions available

## Production Specifications
- Presses: KBA 1050 4-color offset for premium printing + Heidelberg 7+1 UV for specialty finishes
- Corrugator: in-house single-face laminating line for E-flute and B-flute production
- Board: food-grade kraft or white-top liner, single-wall corrugated, 100% recyclable
- Die-cutting: flatbed die-cutter with ±1mm tolerance for fold-and-lock structural precision
- Finishing: flexo printing (large volumes), offset litho (premium), aqueous coating, UV varnish
- Assembly: pre-glued or flat-packed depending on logistics requirements and storage efficiency
- Quality control: ISO 9001 process with burst strength, edge crush, and grease resistance testing
- Lead time: 10-15 days for new custom corrugated boxes; 5-7 days for reprints
- MOQ: 500 units; pre-production samples with 5-day turnaround
