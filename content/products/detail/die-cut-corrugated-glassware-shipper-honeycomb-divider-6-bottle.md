## Product Overview

This die-cut corrugated glassware shipper incorporates an integrated honeycomb kraft divider system that isolates each of six wine glasses or stemware pieces in individual protective cells — eliminating glass-to-glass contact during transit. The honeycomb grid absorbs lateral shock while the outer corrugated shell (B-flute single-wall or BC-flute double-wall for heavier crystal) provides structural crush resistance. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing on a BHS 2.5m Corrugator line with high-speed flexo printing and Bobst die-cutting, every shipper undergoes ISTA 3A drop-test protocol validation. With 22 years of corrugated packaging expertise, ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certification, and 180,000+ daily corrugated box output across an 18,000 sqm integrated facility, Bincai delivers glassware shipping solutions that combine protection with export-ready compliance.
