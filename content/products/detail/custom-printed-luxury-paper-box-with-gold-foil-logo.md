## Product Overview

This custom printed luxury rigid box elevates your brand with precision gold foil stamping on premium greyboard construction. The two-piece setup box design delivers the weight, rigidity, and tactile experience that luxury consumers expect. Produced on our Heidelberg 7+1 UV press with hot foil stamping and embossing stations in our 18,000m² ISO 9001 certified factory.

- **Gold Foil Stamp**: Hot foil application in gold, silver, rose gold, copper, or holographic — precise registration on textured surfaces

- **Rigid Construction**: 1200-1600gsm greyboard wrapped in premium art paper (157-250gsm)
