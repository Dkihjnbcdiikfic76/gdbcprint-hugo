## Product Overview

The Bincai Kraft Paper Gift Box with Cotton Handle and Soy Ink Printing offers a fully sustainable packaging solution for eco-conscious brands. Constructed from FSC-certified kraft paperboard printed with soy-based inks, each box features a natural cotton rope carry handle and is fully recyclable and biodegradable. Manufactured at our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility with 22 years of paper box expertise, Bincai produces 1.7 million boxes daily across KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV press lines while maintaining ISO 9001 and FSC chain-of-custody certifications.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Material**: 300gsm-400gsm FSC-certified kraft paperboard, unbleached natural finish
- **Handle**: 6mm braided cotton rope (undyed) threaded through reinforced die-cut slots
- **Ink**: Soy-based ink printing (low-VOC, food-safe), CMYK on KBA 1050 offset
- **Construction**: Auto-lock bottom with reinforced side panels, no adhesives required for assembly
- **Finishing**: Natural matte surface, optional aqueous coating for moisture resistance
- **Sustainability**: 100% recyclable, biodegradable, FSC Chain-of-Custody certified
- **Recycled Content**: 70-100% post-consumer recycled kraft available on request
