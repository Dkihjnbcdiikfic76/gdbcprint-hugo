Guangdong Bincai Color Printing's custom rigid box with die-cut window display for perfume packaging merges shelf impact with luxury protection. A precision die-cut window showcases the bottle through optional PET or PLA film, while the rigid board construction, magnetic closure, and velvet/foam interior cradle the product securely. ## Customization Options

- **Box Style**: Two-piece rigid with magnetic flap, book-style opening, drawer with window, hinged lid
- **Window Shape**: Rectangle, circle, oval, heart, custom die-cut silhouette matching bottle shape
- **Window Material**: Clear PET (standard), biodegradable PLA film, or open cutout
- **Sizes**: Custom-fit to any bottle dimensions — 50×50×80mm to 250×250×350mm
- **Materials**: 157gsm art paper wrap over 1.5–3.0mm greyboard, specialty papers (linen, leatherette, pearl)
- **Interior**: Velvet flocking (black, white, red, navy), satin lining, die-cut foam insert, molded pulp
- **Printing**: CMYK offset + Pantone spot on Heidelberg 7+1 UV, full coverage or pattern
- **Finishes**: Gold/silver/rose gold foil stamping, embossing/debossing, spot UV, soft-touch matte lamination
- **Closure**: Hidden magnet, ribbon tie, magnetic flap, or friction fit
