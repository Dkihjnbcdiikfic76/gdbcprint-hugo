## Quality Assurance

| Test | Method | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| **Window Alignment** | Optical comparator at 10× magnification, 5-point measurement around window perimeter | ±0.3mm to bottle profile; window cut consistent with CAD within 0.2mm |
| **Window Film Clarity** | Haze meter (ASTM D1003), transmission at 550nm | PET: haze ≤ 3%, transmission ≥ 90%; PLA: haze ≤ 5%, transmission ≥ 88% |
| **Window Film Adhesion** | Cross-hatch test (ISO 2409), 2mm spacing, 3M 610 tape | Classification 0 or 1 (no flaking along cuts or at intersections) |
| **Magnet Pull Force** | Digital force gauge, perpendicular pull from steel plate at 2mm gap | N42 10mm: ≥ 4.5 N; N52 10mm: ≥ 5.8 N |
| **Magnet Retention** | 24-hour 60°C heat soak + 500 open/close cycles | No magnet loosening, no adhesive failure, zero pull-out |
| **Greyboard Caliper** | Digital micrometer, 9-point grid measurement per sheet | ±0.2mm from nominal; ≤ 0.05mm within-sheet variation |
| **Print Registration** | 10× loupe examination of gripper and tail register marks | ±0.15mm CMYK-to-CMYK; ±0.2mm spot-to-CMYK |
| **Color Consistency (ΔE)** | X-Rite eXact spectrophotometer, CIELAB ΔE 2000, D50 illuminant | ΔE ≤ 2.0 across production run; ΔE ≤ 1.5 for critical brand Pantone spots |
| **Velvet Flocking Density** | Gravimetric: flock weight per unit area, 5 samples per batch | ≥ 180 g/m² for 1.2mm pile; ±5% batch-to-batch |
| **Flocking Rub Durability** | Sutherland Rub Tester, 4-lb weight, dry rub | 500+ cycles without bare-board exposure; ≤ 5% pile loss |
| **Surface Rub Resistance** | Sutherland Rub Tester, 4-lb weight, 100 cycles | ≤ 5% ink density loss; no visible transfer to receptor paper |
| **Drop Test** | ISTA 1A: 10 drops from 760mm (corners, edges, faces) with full bottle weight simulation | Box intact; window film uncracked; magnet engaged; contents secure |
| **Box Compression (BCT)** | Constant-rate loading on top surface, 12.7mm/min | ≥ 350 N for 2mm greyboard; ≥ 500 N for 3mm greyboard |
| **ISO 9001 Traceability** | Lot-code on each master carton linked to raw-material batch | Full audit trail from board/magnet/velvet receipt to finished box shipment within 4 hours |
| **Final Inspection** | AQL 2.5 Level II (normal), special Level S-3 for window/magnet/closure fit | 100% functional check on magnet engagement and window integrity; dimensional spot-check every 100 units |

## FAQ

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity for custom window rigid boxes?**
A: MOQ is 500–1000 units for fully custom boxes with custom window shape and full-color printing. For stock sizes with single-color foil stamping, MOQ can be as low as 300 units.

**Q: Can the window film be biodegradable?**
A: Yes! We offer PLA (polylactic acid) biodegradable window film as an alternative to standard PET. PLA is certified industrially compostable (EN 13432) and delivers comparable clarity (88%+ transmission). We also offer cellulose-based film for the most demanding sustainability requirements.

**Q: How do you ensure the window won't scratch the perfume bottle?**
A: The die-cut window is positioned with a minimum 3mm clearance gap from the bottle surface. The interior foam or velvet insert holds the bottle with precision so there's zero contact with the window edge. For bottles with delicate surface finishes, we add a micro-velvet lining to the window frame interior.

**Q: What magnet strength do I need for my box size?**
A: We match magnet specifications to your box dimensions and weight: N42 8mm discs for small boxes (50–100mm), N42 10mm for medium (100–200mm), and N52 12mm for large/heavy boxes (200mm+). Our engineers calculate the required pull force based on your box dimensions and recommend the optimal magnet configuration.

**Q: Can you match an existing box design exactly?**
A: Absolutely. Send us your existing box sample or technical drawing, and we'll reverse-engineer the construction, match the materials and finishes, and produce a pre-production sample for your approval — typically within 7 working days.

**Q: How are the boxes packaged for international shipping?**
A: Each box is individually poly-bagged, then packed flat in export-grade double-wall corrugated master cartons on heat-treated (ISPM 15) pallets. We use corner protectors and moisture-barrier liners for ocean freight. Box-in-box nesting configurations available to maximize container utilization.

**Q: What certifications does Bincai hold?**
A: ISO 9001:2015 (quality management), FSC Chain-of-Custody (CU-COC-XXXXXX — responsible forestry), High-Tech Enterprise (Guangdong provincial certification), and we manufacture to additional standards as required: EN 71-3 (toy safety), REACH, RoHS, and FDA 21 CFR for indirect food contact.
