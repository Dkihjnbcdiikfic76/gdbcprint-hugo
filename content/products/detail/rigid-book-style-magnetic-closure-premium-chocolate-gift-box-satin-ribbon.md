## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the foam tray food-safe for direct chocolate contact?**
A: Yes, we use food-grade EVA foam certified for direct food contact (EU 10/2011 and FDA 21 CFR compliant). For extra assurance, we offer foil-wrapped cavity liners or food-grade cardboard inserts. All adhesives used in assembly are also food-safe and solvent-free.

**Q: Can the box be refrigerated without damage?**
A: Yes. Our rigid boxes use moisture-resistant greyboard with cold-resistant PVA adhesives that maintain bond integrity at temperatures as low as -20°C. The exterior art paper wrap is laminated (matte or gloss) for moisture barrier protection. For frozen chocolate storage, we recommend an additional inner foil barrier layer.

**Q: What is the minimum order for custom tray configurations?**
A: New die-cut EVA foam trays require a one-time mold fee ($80-200 depending on cavity complexity) and minimum 1,000 units. Standard cavity configurations (6/9/12 cavities) have no mold fee and MOQ of 500 units. Cardboard insert trays have no mold fee at any configuration.

**Q: Can you add a clear window to show the chocolates through the lid?**
A: Yes. We can add a die-cut window with PET film on the book-style lid for product visibility while maintaining the premium rigid box structure. The window can be rectangular, oval, or custom-shaped to your brand design. Multi-window configurations (one per chocolate cavity) are available for sampler boxes.

**Q: What is the typical lead time for custom rigid chocolate boxes?**
A: Pre-production sample: 5-7 working days (includes printed wrap proof + assembled box with foam tray). Custom foam tray mold: +3-5 working days if new shape required. Mass production: 12-18 working days after sample approval. Sea freight: 12-15 days to US West Coast, 22-28 days to Europe. Air freight: 3-5 days. We recommend ordering 45-60 days ahead for seasonal peaks (Valentine's Day, Christmas, Lunar New Year).

**Q: What exterior wrap materials create the best luxury impression for premium chocolate brands?**
A: For ultra-premium positioning, leatherette (PU) wrap with gold foil debossing offers the highest perceived value — this is the standard for luxury hotel and duty-free chocolate boxes. For artisanal/craft brands, linen-textured paper with gold or copper foil stamping communicates handcrafted quality. For modern minimalist brands, matte laminated art paper with spot UV and rose gold foil achieves a clean, premium look. We provide material swatch kits (free) so you can compare textures, colors, and finishes in hand before deciding.

**Q: Can you create multi-tier chocolate boxes with different trays per level?**
A: Yes. Our book-style rigid box can be engineered with 2-3 tiers — each tier with its own die-cut tray holding different chocolate varieties (truffles on top, pralines middle, bars bottom). Each tier is hinged or removable with a ribbon pull for easy access. This configuration is highly popular for corporate gift, wedding favor, and holiday collection boxes.
