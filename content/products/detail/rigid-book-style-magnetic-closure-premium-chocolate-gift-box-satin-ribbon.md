Rigid book-style magnetic closure gift box designed for premium chocolates, truffles, and fine confectionery — manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** This elegant book-style rigid box opens like a hardcover volume to reveal a custom die-cut tray cradling individual chocolates or pralines. The hinged lid features dual magnetic snap closures for secure, satisfying opening and closing, complemented by a satin ribbon pull for easy access. The exterior can be wrapped in art paper, leatherette, or linen with gold foil hot stamping for a luxury brand impression. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across our 18,000 sqm factory.

## FAQ
**Q: Is the foam tray food-safe for direct chocolate contact?**
A: Yes, we use food-grade EVA foam certified for direct food contact. Alternatively, we offer foil-wrapped cavity liners or cardboard inserts.

**Q: Can the box be refrigerated without damage?**
A: Our rigid boxes use moisture-resistant greyboard and cold-resistant adhesives suitable for refrigeration and freezer storage.

**Q: What is the minimum order for custom tray configurations?**
A: New die-cut foam trays require a one-time mold fee ($80-200) and minimum 1,000 units. Standard cavity configurations have no mold fee.

**Q: Can you add a clear window to show the chocolates?**
A: Yes. We can add a die-cut window with PET film on the lid for product visibility while maintaining the premium rigid box feel.
