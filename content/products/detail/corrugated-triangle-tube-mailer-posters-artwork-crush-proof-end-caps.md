Corrugated triangle tube mailer for posters, artwork, and large-format prints — designed and manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience. The triangular prism design provides superior crush resistance compared to traditional round tubes — three reinforced edges distribute impact forces, and the rigid end caps lock into position to prevent telescoping during transit. Constructed from 200lb test ECT-32 B-flute corrugated board with kraft or white-top liner, each mailer features a self-sealing adhesive strip (peel-and-seal) and a perforated tear strip for frustration-free recipient opening. Ideal for e-commerce poster sellers, art galleries, photographers, architects, and print-on-demand businesses. Produced on KBA Rapida 105 4-color and Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV presses at our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility under ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certification.

## Customization Options

Each triangular tube mailer is configured precisely for your products:

- **Dimensions**: Length 12"–48" (305–1220mm), internal cross-section adjustable for 1-10 rolled items. Standard sizes for 18"×24", 24"×36", and A1/A2 formats
- **Board Grade**: B-flute (3mm, ECT-32, 200lb test) for standard protection, C-flute (4mm, ECT-40) for heavier artwork, or double-wall BC-flute for gallery-grade shipments. White-top kraft liner for premium brand presentation
- **Printing**: Full CMYK offset on white-top liner via KBA Rapida 105 (15,000 sph). High-coverage logos, branding messages, handling instructions ("Do Not Bend", "Fragile", "This Side Up"), QR codes, and social media handles. Pantone spot colors available for brand consistency
- **End Caps**: Standard crush-proof interlocking design, reinforced double-layer caps, or silicone-grip press-fit caps for reusable mailers. Optional tamper-evident seal strip
- **Closure**: Self-sealing peel-and-stick adhesive strip (no tape needed), full-length adhesive with tear strip, or traditional tuck-flap + tape closure
- **Interior**: Optional kraft tissue interleaf (prevents ink transfer between prints), glassine paper liner (archival-grade for fine art), or poly sleeve (moisture barrier for international shipping)
- **Surface**: Aqueous coating for scuff resistance and water repellency, or matte varnish for premium unboxing feel
