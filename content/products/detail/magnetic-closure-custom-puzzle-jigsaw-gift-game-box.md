## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity for custom puzzle boxes?**
A: Standard MOQ is 500–1,000 units for full custom boxes. Trial orders of 300 units accepted for single-color or foil-only designs on stock sizes. Volume pricing tiers at 1,000, 3,000, 5,000, and 10,000+ units.

**Q: Can you match my game's box artwork and brand colors precisely?**
A: Yes. We use inline spectrophotometers on Heidelberg presses maintaining ΔE ≤ 2.0 color consistency across the entire production run. We can match any Pantone Solid Coated or Uncoated color, and provide color drawdowns for approval before production.

**Q: What foam density should I choose for my puzzle pieces?**
A: EVA 30–35 Shore A (soft) for lightweight cardboard puzzle pieces; 40–45 Shore A (medium) for wooden or thick puzzle pieces; 50–55 Shore A (firm) for heavy game components. Our engineers recommend density based on your piece weight and count.

**Q: How durable is the magnetic closure over time?**
A: Our magnetic closure boxes are tested to 5,000+ open-close cycles without hinge separation or magnet weakening. The N42–N52 neodymium magnets maintain ≥95% hold force after 10 years of normal use. We can upgrade to reinforced hinge construction for frequently-opened game boxes.

**Q: Can you add a display window so customers see the puzzle image without opening?**
A: Yes. We offer die-cut windows with 0.3–0.5mm PET film or PLA biodegradable film inserts. The window can show the full puzzle image, a branded teaser, or a sample of included components.

**Q: What's the typical development timeline from concept to delivery?**
A: Structural design: 2–3 days. 3D render: 24 hours. Pre-production sample: 5–7 days. Production: 10–15 days for new artwork, 7–10 days for repeats. Rush service available at 5–7 days with surcharge.

**Q: Are your materials safe for children's products?**
A: Yes. All inks are soy-based and compliant with EN 71-3 (toy safety, migration of elements), CONEG (heavy metal limits), and FDA 21 CFR for indirect food contact. Water-based adhesives are solvent-free. FSC certification ensures responsible paper sourcing. We can provide full compliance documentation.
