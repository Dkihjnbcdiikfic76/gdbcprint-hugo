## FAQ

**Q: How does the grease-resistant coating work, and will it prevent oil stains on the outside of the box?**
A: Our grease-resistant aqueous coating is a water-based fluorochemical treatment applied to the interior surface of the paperboard during the coating process. It creates a molecular barrier that repels oil and grease while remaining fully recyclable and compostable (unlike PE or wax coatings). In standardized Kit testing (TAPPI T559), our coating achieves Kit values of 8–12, meaning it withstands aggressive oils like butter, lard, and vegetable shortening without any bleed-through to the exterior. For extra-heavy applications like deep-fried foods, we can upgrade to a double-coating with Kit 12+ performance.

**Q: What's the difference between crash-lock bottom and auto-bottom — or are they the same thing?**
A: They are the same mechanism — both terms describe a folding carton bottom that uses interlocking die-cut tabs and precision-scored crease lines that automatically lock into position when the carton is squared up. "Crash-lock" is the traditional industry term (referring to the speed of assembly), while "auto-bottom" is the more modern, descriptive term. The mechanism is identical: no adhesive, no tape, no manual folding — the bottom locks itself under tension in under a second. These cartons ship flat (knocked down) for maximum transport efficiency.

**Q: Are these cartons certified for direct food contact?**
A: Yes, our food-grade configuration uses water-based adhesives, low-migration offset inks, and FDA-compliant aqueous coatings suitable for direct food contact with dry and fatty foodstuffs. We provide migration testing certificates showing compliance with FDA 21 CFR 176.170 (paper and paperboard in contact with aqueous and fatty foods) and EU Framework Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004. For unwrapped baked goods, we recommend the grease-resistant coating plus an optional PE or PLA barrier film for extended shelf-life protection.
