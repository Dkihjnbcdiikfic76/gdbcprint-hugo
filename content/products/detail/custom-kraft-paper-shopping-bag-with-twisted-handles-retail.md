## Product Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing produces premium custom kraft paper shopping bags with twisted paper handles, engineered for retail, boutique, and promotional applications. Our bags combine the natural aesthetic of kraft paper with reinforced twisted handles that offer comfortable carrying and reliable load-bearing up to 5 kg. Every bag is manufactured in our 18,000 sqm factory complex in Foshan, Guangdong, using FSC-certified kraft paper sourced from responsibly managed forests.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Paper substrate**: Virgin kraft (80-200 GSM), recycled kraft (90-180 GSM), or FSC-certified kraft in natural brown, white, or black
- **Handle types**: Twisted paper handles (standard, 3-5 ply), flat paper handles, die-cut handles, cotton rope handles
- **Printing**: KBA 1050 4-color offset for solids and gradients; Heidelberg 7+1 UV for PMS spot colors, metallic inks, and high-gloss varnish effects
- **Lamination**: Gloss or matte lamination (1.5-3 mil), aqueous coating, soft-touch velvet finish
- **Reinforcement**: Cardboard bottom insert (1-2 mm greyboard), reinforced gusset options for heavy loads
- **Bottom style**: Square bottom (flat), pinch bottom, or block bottom with expandable gusset
- **Eco options**: PLA biodegradable lamination, water-based inks, soy-based inks, 100% recycled kraft
- **Production capacity**: Up to 300,000 bags per day across dedicated bag-making lines

## FAQ

**What is the minimum order quantity for custom kraft shopping bags?**

Our standard MOQ is 500 pieces per design. For single-color printing on stock kraft paper, we can accommodate trial orders as low as 300 pieces. Multi-color or PMS-matched designs typically require 1,000 pieces minimum.

**Can the twisted handles be color-matched to my brand?**

Yes. Twisted paper handles can be produced in natural kraft, white, black, or any PMS spot color. We twist handles from colored kraft paper rather than painting them, ensuring the color holds through the entire handle thickness and resists wear during use.

**Are these bags food-safe?**

We offer food-grade inner PE or PLA coatings that create a moisture barrier, making the bags suitable for direct contact with dry food items such as baked goods, coffee beans, and confectionery. For hot or greasy foods, we recommend an aluminum foil inner lining.

**What is the typical lead time from order to delivery?**

Pre-production samples ship within 5-7 business days after artwork approval. Bulk production takes 15-20 business days, with express shipping (DHL/FedEx/UPS) adding 3-7 days or sea freight adding 15-30 days depending on destination.
