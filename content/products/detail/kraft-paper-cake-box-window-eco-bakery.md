## Product Overview

Bincai's kraft paper cake box combines eco-credentials with bakery-grade functionality. The FSC certified kraft board provides natural rustic charm, while the die-cut PET window showcases your creations — cakes, cupcakes, pastries, or cookies. The auto-lock bottom assembles in under 3 seconds with no tape or glue. Printed with soy-based inks for full compostability. An optional compostable PLA window film makes the entire box biodegradable. MOQ starts at 1,000 units, produced in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility. 23 years of sustainable packaging expertise.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset press for full CMYK branding — soy-based inks throughout
- **Board**: 350gsm FSC certified kraft board — natural brown or bleached white; alternative 400gsm for larger sizes
- **Window**: Die-cut with 0.2mm PET film or compostable PLA film (ASTM D6400 certified for industrial composting)
- **Printing**: Soy-based flexo (1-2 color) for economy runs or full CMYK offset for photo-quality branding
- **Ink**: Vegetable-derived soy inks — compliant with EN 71-3 (heavy metals), FDA 21 CFR for indirect food contact
- **Bottom**: Auto-lock crash-lock mechanism — assembles in under 3 seconds with zero tape or adhesive; also available as tuck-bottom or 1-2-3 collapsible base
- **Adhesives**: Water-based emulsion adhesives — VOC-free, food-safe formulation
- **Food Safety**: Full migration testing available; soy inks certified for indirect food contact per EU Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004
- **Eco Features**: FSC chain-of-custody, fully recyclable kraft board, compostable PLA window option, soy-based inks, water-based adhesives
- **Daily Output**: 220,000+ cartons (post-2025 smart factory expansion to 18,000 sqm)
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, Soy Ink Certification
