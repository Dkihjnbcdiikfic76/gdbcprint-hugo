## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the advantage of flat-pack folding cartons for apparel brands?**
A: Flat-shipped folding cartons reduce freight costs by up to 90% versus pre-assembled rigid boxes — you can ship 10× more units per pallet or container. The full-flap lid and die-cut insert tray assemble in seconds without glue or tools. This is ideal for apparel brands that do their own fulfillment and want to store packaging flat until needed. Bincai's Bobst die-cutters ensure all crease lines are precision-scored for clean, crack-free folding every time.

**Q: Can the soft-touch lamination be combined with foil stamping?**
A: Yes — soft-touch matte lamination with selective spot UV gloss accents and hot foil stamping is our most popular finish combination for luxury apparel packaging. The soft-touch creates a premium tactile feel, spot UV makes logo elements pop with glossy contrast, and foil stamping (gold, silver, rose gold, copper, or holographic) adds a metallic luxury cue. All three finishes are applied in-line on our Heidelberg 7+1 UV press for perfect registration.

**Q: What insert tray options are available for apparel presentation?**
A: Custom die-cut insert trays are the core of this product. Options include: single-garment tray with ribbon pull for easy lifting, multi-compartment tray for shirt + tie + cufflink sets, satin-lined tray for premium lingerie presentation, flocked tray for jewelry accessories, and windowed tray that shows the garment through a PET or PLA film. All insert trays are die-cut from the same CCNB board as the outer carton for consistent structural integrity and recyclability.

**Q: What is the minimum order quantity and lead time?**
A: Standard MOQ is 1,000 units for existing tooling. Custom die-cut insert trays requiring new tooling have a 2,000-unit MOQ. Lead time is 15-25 days depending on finishing complexity (spot UV + foil adds 3-5 days vs standard CMYK). Pre-production samples ship in 3-5 days. Bincai runs 1.7M color boxes daily on KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, so production capacity is always available.
