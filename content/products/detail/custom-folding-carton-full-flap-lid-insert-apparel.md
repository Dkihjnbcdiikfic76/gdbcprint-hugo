## Product Overview

Bincai's full-flap folding carton delivers a premium unboxing experience for luxury apparel brands. The oversized hinged lid opens flat for full product presentation, while the die-cut insert tray cradles folded garments, scarves, or accessories. Soft-touch lamination with spot UV logo accents creates a tactile, high-end feel. Flat-shipped for maximum freight efficiency — ships 10x more units per pallet than rigid boxes. 22 years of folding carton expertise on KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: KBA 1050 4-color offset + Heidelberg 7+1 UV — full CMYK plus spot colors
- **Board**: 350gsm CCNB (Clay-Coated News Back) structural board
- **Wrap**: 157gsm art paper with custom CMYK printing
- **Lamination**: Soft-touch matte with selective spot UV gloss accents
- **Closure**: Full-flap hinged lid with friction-fit tuck
- **Die-Cut**: Bobst automatic die-cutting — ±0.3mm precision
