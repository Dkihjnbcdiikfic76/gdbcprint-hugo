## FAQ

**Q: How does the soft-touch lamination hold up during shipping and handling?**
A: Soft-touch matte lamination is highly durable — Sutherland Rub Test validated at 50+ cycles with zero degradation. Unlike aqueous coatings that can scuff, the 3-5μm soft-touch film provides lasting tactile quality from factory floor to customer doorstep. For extra protection, we offer an anti-scuff over-lacquer that preserves the soft-touch feel through aggressive fulfillment center handling.

**Q: Can the insert tray be designed for multiple garment types in the same box?**
A: Yes. We can die-cut a single insert tray with multiple compartments — for example, a shirt cavity (280×200mm), a tie channel (80×30mm), and cufflink slots (20×20mm each). The maximum recommended tray complexity is 6 compartments within 400×300mm. For more complex configurations, we suggest our two-tier or drawer-style boxes.

**Q: What's the minimum order for a fully custom die-cut insert tray?**
A: MOQ is 1,000 units for standard sizes with an existing insert design. Custom die-cut insert trays require a one-time die tooling fee ($150-300 depending on complexity) with a minimum production run of 2,000 units. The die is stored at our facility for future reorders at no additional tooling cost for 24 months.

**Q: Can I get FSC certification on these boxes for my brand's sustainability claims?**
A: Yes. Our FSC Chain-of-Custody certification (FSC Mix Credit) allows us to provide FSC-certified cartons with the FSC logo printed on the box. We can also provide soy-based inks and PLA (compostable) window film as eco-friendly upgrades. The FSC certification documentation is included with each shipment.

**Q: What's the lead time from order to delivery?**

Ocean freight adds 18-30 days to major ports; air freight 5-7 days for urgent orders.
