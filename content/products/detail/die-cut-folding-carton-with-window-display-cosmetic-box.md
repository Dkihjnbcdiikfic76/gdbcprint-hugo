Custom die-cut folding carton with transparent window display for cosmetics, skincare, and beauty product packaging — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). The precision die-cut window with crystal-clear PET or PVC film lets consumers see the product inside before purchase, driving shelf appeal and retail conversion. Research shows window-packaged cosmetics achieve 24–37% higher in-store purchase intent compared to fully opaque cartons. KBA 1050 4-color offset press and Heidelberg 7+1 UV press deliver vibrant, batch-consistent printing across our 18,000 sqm (10,000+8,000 smart factory) ISO 9001 & FSC certified factory. The folding carton structure is engineered for flat shipping (reducing freight cost by up to 60%) and easy assembly on automated or manual packaging lines. Daily output capacity of 1.7 million boxes with fully automatic folder-gluer lines and integrated window-patching systems.

## Quality Assurance
| Inspection Point | Method | Standard |
|:---|---|:---|
| Print registration | Loupe magnification + overlay template | ±0.15mm for 4-color process |
| Color consistency | X-Rite spectrophotometer — sampled every 500 sheets | ΔE ≤2.0 from master standard |
| Window film clarity | Visual haze inspection under D50 lighting | Haze ≤3% (ASTM D1003) |
| Window-to-print alignment | Go/no-go overlay gauge — 100% automated camera check | ±0.5mm |
| Window adhesion | Peel test at corner (sampled 2% per batch) | ≥5N/cm bond strength |
| Glue-line integrity | Visual + pull test on glued side seam | No gap, full fiber tear on board |
| Carton squareness | Fold-and-measure — 90° corner jig | ±1° from 90° |
| Surface finish | Visual under 5000K lighting — 100% | No scratches, scuffs, fisheyes, or orange peel |
| Crease/fold accuracy | Digital micrometer on crease depth | ±0.05mm from spec |
| Packed compression | Box compression test (BCT) on master carton | ≥specified stacking strength |
| Food-grade compliance | Migration testing (if required) | EU 1935/2004, FDA 21 CFR |
| FSC chain of custody | Per-batch CoC documentation | FSC-STD-40-004 |
