## FAQ

**Q: How does the adjustable depth work?**
A: The mailer has multiple pre-scored fold lines along the sides. Choose the score line matching your book's thickness, fold at that line, and the mailer wraps snugly around the contents. No void fill needed — the corrugated walls provide direct crush protection.

**Q: Is the self-seal adhesive strong enough for international shipping?**
A: Yes. Our hot-melt adhesive strip is tested to maintain seal integrity from -20°C to 60°C and survives ISTA 1A drop testing. For high-value international shipments we recommend adding clear packing tape over the seal as insurance.

**Q: What's the difference between E-flute and B-flute for book mailers?**
A: E-flute (1.2mm) is thinner and more compact — ideal for lightweight books and lower postal dimensional weight charges. B-flute (2.5mm) offers superior crush resistance for heavier books, photo albums, and framed items. We'll recommend based on your product weight and shipping method.

**Q: Can you print my logo on the mailer exterior?**
A: Yes. Flexo printing (1-3 colors) directly on kraft is the most economical option for branding. For full-color exterior printing we offer litho-laminated kraft (offset-printed paper sheet laminated onto corrugated), which delivers photo-quality brand presentation.
