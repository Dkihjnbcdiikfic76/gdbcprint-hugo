## Product Overview

A premium rigid magnetic closure gift box with plush velvet flocked interior, designed for high-end jewelry, watches, and luxury accessories. The soft-touch exterior with hidden neodymium magnets creates a clean, elegant unboxing experience with no visible hardware. Manufactured in our 18,000m² ISO 9001 factory with Heidelberg 7+1 UV press, Bobst hot foil stamping, and CNC die-cut insert precision. Available in custom dimensions and finishes for brand-specific presentation requirements.

## Features

- **Magnetic Closure**: N42-N52 neodymium disc magnets embedded in front flap — clean invisible closure with 5.0 N/cm retention
- **Velvet Interior**: Plush flocked velvet (0.8–1.5mm pile) in custom colors — black, navy, burgundy, cream, charcoal most popular
- **Rigid Construction**: 1.5–2.5mm greyboard (1000–1600gsm) core with 157gsm art paper wrap — superior crush resistance
- **Soft-Touch Finish**: Aqueous soft-touch coating over matte lamination — fingerprint-resistant, luxurious tactile feel
- **Foil Stamping**: Gold, silver, rose gold, holographic foil via Bobst flatbed press with embossing option
- **Insert Options**: CNC die-cut EVA foam, velvet-covered tray, satin pillow, cardboard partition — sized to your product

- **Press**: Heidelberg Speedmaster 7+1 UV + KBA 1050 4-color — ±0.15mm print registration

- **Daily Output**: 220,000+ gift boxes across all rigid categories
