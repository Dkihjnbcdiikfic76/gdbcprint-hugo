## FAQ

**Q: Is the velvet material used in this jewelry travel pouch box eco-friendly?**
A: Bincai offers both conventional velvet-flocking and eco-conscious alternatives for our jewelry pouch boxes. Our standard velvet finish uses a high-quality flocking process applied to a rigid paperboard base, and we can source recycled polyester flocking for brands with sustainability targets. The paperboard core is FSC-certified and fully recyclable, while the overall box design minimizes material waste compared to oversized rigid jewelry cases. For clients seeking the most eco-friendly option, we also offer a kraft-paper-exterior version of this travel pouch box with the same compact zipper-closure structure.

**Q: How durable is the zipper closure on this travel pouch box — can it withstand daily use?**
A: The zipper closure on our velvet jewelry travel pouch box is engineered for repeated daily use, with reinforced stitching at stress points and a smooth nylon coil zipper that resists snagging and separation. Bincai's quality control team tests zipper durability to 5,000+ open-close cycles on every production batch, far exceeding what a typical end consumer would use over the product's lifetime. The zipper track is precision-mounted into the rigid paperboard frame, ensuring alignment stays true even with frequent travel. We use YKK-grade zipper components sourced from ISO 9001:2015 certified suppliers.

**Q: Can I customize the interior layout to fit different jewelry types — rings, necklaces, bracelets, or earrings?**
A: Yes, the interior of this pouch box is fully customizable with die-cut foam inserts, velvet-covered dividers, elastic straps, or satin-lined compartments to secure any jewelry type. Whether you need a single ring slot, a multi-bracelet layout, necklace hooks, or a combination of all three, Bincai's in-house structural design team creates a tailored insert solution for your specific product range. Our factory's 10,000 sqm facility plus the new 8,000 sqm smart factory (2025) handles custom tooling and die-making in-house, keeping lead times short and costs competitive even for fully bespoke interiors.
