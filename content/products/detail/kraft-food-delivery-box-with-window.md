## Product Overview

The Bincai Kraft Food Delivery Box with Window combines environmental responsibility with practical food presentation. Made from FSC-certified recycled kraft board (350gsm) with a clear PET window, this box lets customers see the food inside while maintaining structural integrity during delivery. Printed with soy-based inks on KBA 1050 4-color presses.

## Features

- **Eco Materials**: 350gsm FSC-certified recycled kraft board; fully biodegradable (excludes PET window)
- **Window Options**: Clear PET (standard) or PLA compostable film (upgrade); sizes from 60×40mm to full-lid
- **Structural Types**: Tuck-top, auto-lock bottom, or gable-top; all fold flat for efficient shipping
- **Food-Safe**: Soy-based inks; no harmful coatings; meets FDA 21 CFR indirect food contact
- **Custom Sizes**: 150×100×60mm standard; range from 100×80×50mm to 300×200×80mm
- **Print**: Single-color flexo or full-color offset (KBA 1050) on kraft surface
- **Ventilation**: Optional die-cut vent holes for hot food steam release
