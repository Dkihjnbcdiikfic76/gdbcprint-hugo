## Product Overview

The Bincai Kraft Food Delivery Box with Window combines environmental responsibility with practical food presentation. Made from FSC-certified recycled kraft board (300-400gsm) with a clear window, this box lets customers see the food inside while maintaining structural integrity during delivery. Printed with soy-based inks on KBA 1050 4-color presses at Bincai's 18,000 sqm integrated facility in Foshan, Guangdong. Ideal for bakeries, meal-prep services, salad bars, and sustainable food brands seeking compostable or recyclable takeaway packaging.

## Features

- **Eco Materials**: 350gsm FSC-certified recycled kraft board; fully biodegradable box body. Available with PET window (standard) or PLA compostable film (certified to EN 13432)
- **Window Options**: Clear PET (recyclable) or PLA compostable film for full biodegradability; sizes from 60x40mm to full-lid transparent panel
- **Structural Types**: Tuck-top, auto-lock bottom, or gable-top handle; all fold flat for efficient warehouse storage and freight savings
- **Food-Safe**: Soy-based inks throughout; no mineral oil hydrocarbons (MOSH/MOAH compliant); meets FDA 21 CFR indirect food contact and EU 1935/2004
- **Custom Sizes**: Standard 150x100x60mm; range from 100x80x50mm to 350x250x100mm for family meal kits
- **Print**: Single-color flexo (economy) or full-color CMYK offset (KBA 1050) directly on kraft surface; Pantone spot colors available
- **Ventilation**: Optional die-cut vent holes for hot food steam release; pattern-customizable (circular, slot, logo-shaped)
- **Closure**: Tuck-top with locking tab or self-locking auto-bottom for heavier meals (tested to 1.5kg)
