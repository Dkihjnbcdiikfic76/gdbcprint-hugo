## Corrugated Tablet iPad Mailer with Die-Cut Foam Cradle — Electronics Shipping Solution

Protect valuable electronics during e-commerce fulfillment with Bincai's precision-engineered corrugated tablet mailer. Designed for the rigorous demands of direct-to-consumer electronics shipping, this mailer combines structural integrity with a custom die-cut foam cradle that suspends the tablet away from all six box walls.

### Why This Mailer is Essential for Electronics E-Commerce

Tablets and iPads are high-value, impact-sensitive products. Standard brown boxes with void fill lead to damage claims, returns, and disappointed customers. Bincai's tablet mailer eliminates these risks through engineered protection.

| Challenge | Bincai Solution |
|---|---|
| Impact during transit drops | 20mm foam cradle suspends tablet, absorbing shock energy before it reaches the device |
| Screen damage from pressure | Rigid corrugated walls prevent crushing; foam distributes pressure evenly |
| Accessories scratching device | Separate accessories compartment isolates charger, cable, and manual |
| Returns due to damaged packaging | Double-wall corrugated option for heavier tablets and multiple devices |
| Brand presentation | Full-color KBA 1050 offset printing on kraft exterior — branded box from doorstep arrival |

### ISTA 1A Drop Testing

All Bincai tablet mailer designs undergo ISTA 1A (International Safe Transit Association) drop testing:

| Test | Procedure | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Edge Drop | 10 drops from 760mm on all edges | No device damage; foam cradle intact |
| Corner Drop | 10 drops from 760mm on all corners | Corrugated corners may crush slightly; tablet protected |
| Face Drop | 10 drops from 760mm on all 6 faces | Zero tablet damage across all tested configurations |
| Compression | Top-load to 200 lbs | Less than 5mm deflection with double-wall construction |
| Vibration | 1-hour random vibration (truck simulation) | No foam degradation; tablet position maintained |
