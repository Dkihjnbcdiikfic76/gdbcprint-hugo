## FAQ

**Q: Can I order different compartment configurations — for example, 3 compartments instead of 2?**
A: Yes. The compartment layout is fully customizable. Common configurations include: 2 compartments (cufflinks + tie clip), 3 compartments (cufflinks + tie clip + lapel pin), and 4 compartments (cufflinks + tie clip + collar stays + pocket square). Each cavity is CNC-milled to your exact product dimensions with velvet or satin lining. You provide specifications or a physical sample, and our tooling team delivers a production-ready insert within 7 working days.

**Q: What is the difference between velvet paper and real fabric velvet for the interior?**
A: We offer both options. Velvet paper (also called flocked paper) is a paper substrate with electrostatically applied nylon fibers — it provides the look and soft-touch feel of velvet at a lower cost and is our standard option. Real fabric velvet (polyester or rayon velvet bonded to paperboard) offers a deeper, richer texture and is preferred for ultra-luxury brands. Fabric velvet adds approximately $0.30–$0.80 per unit depending on box size. Both options are available in our full color range.

**Q: Can these boxes be personalized with individual names — for groomsman gifts, for example?**
A: Yes, we offer individual personalization through digital foil stamping and UV inkjet printing. For orders of 100+ units, we can apply unique names, initials, or dates to each box without the cost of traditional hot foil dies. Our digital foil system prints variable data at production speed, making it cost-effective for personalized wedding party gifts, corporate recognition awards, and limited-edition releases. Setup cost is approximately $60 for the variable data program.

## Sustainability

Bincai is committed to environmentally responsible packaging. Our rigid boxes support your brand's sustainability goals without compromising on quality or shelf impact:

- **FSC Certified Paper** — All paperboard and wrapping paper used in our rigid boxes is sourced from FSC-certified forests and controlled sources. Forest Stewardship Council certification ensures that the fiber in your packaging comes from responsibly managed forests.

- **Recyclable Core Materials** — The greyboard core of our rigid boxes is made from 100% recycled post-consumer and post-industrial fiber. The paper wrapping can be separated from the board core in standard recycling streams, maximizing end-of-life material recovery.

- **Soy-Based Inks** — We offer soy-based and vegetable-based offset inks as an alternative to conventional petroleum-based inks. Soy inks produce vibrant color reproduction while emitting fewer VOCs and making the printed surface easier to de-ink during recycling.

- **Plastic-Free Construction** — The entire box — from greyboard core to paper wrap to magnetic closure — is plastic-free. There is no PET laminate, no PVC window, and no plastic insert. This makes the box fully compatible with paper recycling streams and aligned with the EU Single-Use Plastics Directive.

- **Flat-Ship Efficiency** — Our rigid display boxes can be designed with flat-ship (knock-down) construction, where the box ships as a flat sheet with pre-creased fold lines and self-assembly features. This reduces shipping volume by up to 80% versus pre-assembled boxes, cutting both freight cost and carbon footprint.
