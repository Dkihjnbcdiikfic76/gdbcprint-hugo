## Product Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. — founded in 2003 and recognized among China's leading eco-packaging manufacturers — presents its certified compostable kraft paper food box with a crystal-clear PLA (polylactic acid) window. Featured in global packaging trade publications for advancing sustainable food-service solutions, our factory operates at a daily output of 1.7 million color boxes plus 220,000+ rigid gift boxes from our 18,000 sqm facility. ## Quality Assurance

| Test | Method | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Board Thickness Tolerance | Digital micrometer, 5-point measurement per sheet | ±0.05mm from nominal caliper |
| PLA Window Clarity | Haze meter (ASTM D1003) | Haze value ≤ 8% for 50μm film |
| Print Registration | 10x loupe inspection at gripper and tail edges | ±0.1mm CMYK-to-CMYK registration |
| Color Consistency (ΔE) | X-Rite eXact spectrophotometer, CIELAB ΔE 2000 | ΔE ≤ 2.0 across production run; ΔE ≤ 1.5 for brand-critical Pantone spot colors |
| Glue Bond Strength | T-peel test on bonded flaps at 23°C/50% RH | ≥ 5 N/cm peel force; no fiber tear below 3 N/cm |
| Window Adhesion | 90° peel test on PLA-to-kraft bond | ≥ 4 N/cm; no window detachment after 48h at 40°C/75% RH |
| Surface Finish Visual | D65 illuminant viewing booth, 0°/45° geometry | No visible scratches, orange peel, pinholing, or coating voids at 600mm viewing distance |
| Abrasion Resistance | Sutherland Rub Tester, 4-lb weight, 100 cycles | No ink transfer or coating breakthrough; ≤ 5% gloss reduction |
| Moisture Resistance (Internal) | Cobb test (TAPPI T441), 2-minute water exposure | ≤ 35 g/m² water absorption for aqueous-coated kraft |
| FSC Chain-of-Custody | Transaction certificate per shipment batch | 100% traceability from certified forest to finished box; FSC-STD-40-004 compliant |
| ISO 9001 Traceability | Lot-number coding on every carton | Full raw-material-to-shipment traceability within 4 hours |
| Drop Test | ISTA 1A: 10-drop sequence from 760mm height | No structural failure; window intact; contents undamaged |
| Compostability Verification | EN 13432 / ASTM D6400 independent lab testing | ≥ 90% disintegration within 12 weeks; ≥ 90% biodegradation within 180 days; no ecotoxicity |
| Heavy Metals (Ink) | ICP-MS digestion, EN 71-3 migration limits | Pb < 90 ppm, Cd < 75 ppm, Hg < 60 ppm, Cr(VI) < 60 ppm, Sb < 60 ppm, As < 25 ppm, Ba < 1000 ppm, Se < 500 ppm |
| Food Contact Safety | Overall migration testing per EU 10/2011 | ≤ 10 mg/dm² total migration for all food simulants |
