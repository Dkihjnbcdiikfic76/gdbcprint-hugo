## Product Overview

The Bincai Custom Tuck-Top Folding Carton delivers premium cosmetic packaging with hot foil stamping accents that elevate brand perception at retail. Manufactured at our 18,000 sqm integrated facility in Foshan, Guangdong, each carton is produced on KBA 1050 4-color offset presses with Heidelberg 7+1 UV finishing. With 22 years of folding carton expertise and 1.7 million boxes produced daily, Bincai serves cosmetic brands across 30+ countries.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Structure**: Tuck-top closure with friction-lock dust flaps for secure product retention
- **Board Grades**: 250gsm-400gsm art paper, 300gsm-500gsm SBS, 1.0mm-1.5mm greyboard
- **Print**: KBA 1050 4-color offset (CMYK), Pantone spot colors on Heidelberg 7+1 UV
- **Hot Foil**: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper, holographic — precision-registered at ±0.15mm
- **Finishing**: Matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch coating, spot UV, aqueous varnish
- **Die-Cutting**: Bobst flatbed die-cutter, ±0.5mm tolerance
