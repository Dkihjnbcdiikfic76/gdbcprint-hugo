## Product Overview

Bincai's rigid telescopic round hat box combines the structural integrity of cylindrical forming with luxury gift-box finishing. The 2mm greyboard body is machine-wrapped in 157gsm art paper with full CMYK offset printing, accented by a metallic gold foil band and a reinforced satin ribbon carry handle. The telescopic lid fits with precision-tooled tolerance, and the flat-top design allows for stacked display in retail environments. Manufactured on Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses with custom cylindrical forming dies in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility. 22 years of luxury packaging expertise.

- **Press**: Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset — 360° seamless wrap printing

## Seven-Stage Quality Control Gates

| Gate | Stage | Inspection | Defect Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | Raw Material Incoming | Greyboard caliper 2.0±0.1mm, art paper gsm verification, FSC cert validation, ribbon tensile test | Board <1.8mm → batch reject; non-FSC fiber → trace & quarantine |
| **G2** | Pre-Press & Plate Making | Color proof ΔE ≤2.0, plate registration marks verified, foil stamping die alignment test | ΔE >2.5 → ink reformulation; die offset >0.2mm → remount |
| **G3** | Printing (Heidelberg 7+1 UV) | 360° cylindrical wrap registration ±0.15mm, ink adhesion, spot color consistency | Registration drift >0.3mm → cylinder timing adjustment |
| **G4** | Die-Cutting & Cylindrical Forming | Roundness ±1.0mm diameter, scoring depth 50% of board, seam alignment ±0.5mm | Roundness >1.5mm → die adjustment; seam gap >0.8mm → forming station recalibration |
| **G5** | Surface & Foil Application | Bobst SP 102 BMA foil adhesion via ASTM D3359 cross-hatch (3B minimum), 110–130°C dwell, 2.5 MPa pressure, gloss consistency | Adhesion <3B → temp/pressure recheck; tarnish → foil batch reject |
| **G6** | Assembly & Ribbon Attachment | Lid fit 0.3–0.7mm clearance, ribbon grommet pull ≥15kg, velvet pad alignment ±1mm, hinge smoothness | Lid bind → sizing recheck; ribbon pull <12kg → grommet reinforcement |
| **G7** | Final QC & Packing | ISTA 1A drop test, visual cosmetic per ISO 2859-1 AQL 2.5 Level II, barcode scan, stack load test ≥25kg | Drop failure → batch quarantine; cosmetic defect ≥AQL → 100% re-inspect |
