## Product Overview

The Folding Carton Sleeve & Tray Combo is purpose-built for the booming premium candle and artisan soap market — a packaging format that combines retail shelf impact with practical product protection. Manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** (23 years of paper box expertise, 18,000 sqm ISO 9001 & FSC certified facility), this two-piece system pairs a rigid-feel printed sleeve with a precisely scored fold-up tray that cradles candles, soap bars, bath bombs, or skincare sets without additional inserts.

The outer sleeve is printed on our **KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset press** with optional **Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 7+1 UV** for premium finishes, then die-cut with an elegant window — round, oval, arched, or custom shape — that reveals the product within. A transparent PET or compostable PLA window film is bonded behind the aperture. The inner tray features scored fold lines, auto-lock corner tabs, and optional partition walls to separate multiple items without shifting during transit.

### Why Sleeve-and-Tray Wins on Retail Shelves
- **360° branding**: Unlike tuck-top cartons where branding is confined to faces, the sleeve offers continuous wrapping surface
- **Product visibility**: Die-cut windows drive impulse purchases — consumers want to see wax color, soap swirls, and botanicals before buying
- **Tactile engagement**: Soft-touch lamination on the sleeve invites handling — customers pick up and examine the product
- **Gift-ready**: The format feels premium without requiring additional gift wrapping — slide the sleeve off like unwrapping a present

### Sleeve-Tray vs. Tuck-Top vs. Magnetic: Which Format Fits Your Product?

## Quality Control & Testing

| QC Stage | Inspection Method | Acceptance Standard |
|----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Board Inbound** | Grammage, caliper, brightness, moisture | Grammage ±3%; brightness ≥85% ISO (SBS); moisture 5–7% |
| **Color Consistency** | Heidelberg Prinect Inpress closed-loop spectrophotometry — inline + offline verification | ΔE ≤ 2.0 across entire production run |
| **Window Alignment** | Go/no-go gauge per 500 units | ±0.3mm positioning; zero film wrinkles or bubbles |
| **Film Clarity** | Haze meter — light transmission test | ≥90% visible light transmission; no yellowing or clouding |
| **Sleeve-Tray Fit** | Dimensional gauge — inner tray slides into sleeve with controlled friction | ±0.5mm clearance; tray stays in sleeve when inverted at 45° angle |
| **Glue Seam** | Peel test at 23°C and thermal cycle (-20°C to 50°C) | ≥8 N/25mm bond strength; no failure after thermal cycling |
| **Scuff Resistance** | Sutherland Rub Tester — 100 cycles at 2 PSI | No visible ink transfer or surface damage |
| **Visual Final** | 100% inspection under D65 standard lighting (1000 lux) | No print defects, oil stains, glue squeeze-out, or score cracking |
| **Drop Test** | ISTA 1A: 10 drops from 760mm | No structural failure; product retains position in tray |
