## Product Overview

The Folding Carton Sleeve & Tray Combo is purpose-built for the booming premium candle and artisan soap market — a packaging format that combines retail shelf impact with practical product protection. Manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** (22 years of paper box expertise, 18,000 sqm ISO 9001 & FSC certified facility), this two-piece system pairs a rigid-feel printed sleeve with a precisely scored fold-up tray that cradles candles, soap bars, bath bombs, or skincare sets without additional inserts.

The outer sleeve is printed on our **KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset press** with optional **Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 7+1 UV** for premium finishes, then die-cut with an elegant window — round, oval, arched, or custom shape — that reveals the product within. A transparent PET or compostable PLA window film is bonded behind the aperture. The inner tray features scored fold lines, auto-lock corner tabs, and optional partition walls to separate multiple items without shifting during transit.

**Key Specs at a Glance:** ISO 9001:2015 & FSC certified | 1.7M boxes daily output | KBA 1050 + Heidelberg 7+1 UV | 350-400gsm board | Die-cut window + film | Soft-touch / gold foil / spot UV options | MOQ 500 | 8–12 day lead time

## Applications
The sleeve-and-tray format excels when the product needs to be seen and touched — perfect for retail shelves where visual appeal drives purchase decisions:
- **Scented Candles**: Single-wick, triple-wick, and container candles — the tray holds the glass jar securely while the sleeve provides branding surface area
- **Artisan Soap Bars**: Handmade cold-process, glycerin, or milled soaps — window reveals color marbling, botanical inclusions, and texture
- **Bath Bomb & Gift Sets**: Multi-compartment trays for bath bomb + soap + candle combos with clear PET viewing
- **Skincare & Body Care**: Facial oil sets, body butter trios, lip balm collections with custom-sized compartments
- **Wedding Favors & Corporate Gifts**: Personalized sleeve printing with couple names, event dates, or company branding

## Customization Options
- **Printing**: Full CMYK offset litho with 200 LPI screen ruling, Pantone spot colors, metallic inks, cold foil, hot foil stamping (gold/silver/rose gold/holographic)
- **Surface Finishing**: Soft-touch matte lamination (velvet feel), gloss UV spot varnish (high-contrast), textured embossing, debossing, anti-scuff aqueous coating
- **Window Shape**: Round, oval, square, rectangular, arched, heart, star, or fully custom die-cut — PET or compostable PLA window film options
- **Sleeve Style**: Straight tuck end (STE), reverse tuck end (RTE), or open-ended sleeve with friction fit
- **Tray Configuration**: Single cavity, dual cavity, triple cavity, with partition walls, removable insert cards, or thumb-notches for easy product access
- **Bonus Features**: Tear-away panel for promotional messaging, QR code printing for AR unboxing experiences, scratch-off reveal areas

## Production Specifications
- **Board**: 350gsm C1S SBS (solid bleached sulfate) or 400gsm coated duplex board — both with high brightness and smooth surface for premium print reproduction
- **Printing**: KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset (max sheet 720×1050mm) or Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 7+1 UV (750×1050mm)
- **Die-Cutting**: Flatbed die-cutting with ±0.3mm window positioning tolerance, auto-stripping for clean edges
- **Window Patching**: Hot-melt or water-based adhesive film application at 12,000 units/hour on automatic window patching machine
- **Assembly**: Machine-folded tray with hot-melt adhesive corner locks, sleeve formed on automatic folder-gluer
- **Quality Control**: AQL 2.5 (major), AQL 1.5 (critical) — window alignment, film clarity, sleeve-tray fit tolerance (±0.5mm), color consistency under D65 lighting
