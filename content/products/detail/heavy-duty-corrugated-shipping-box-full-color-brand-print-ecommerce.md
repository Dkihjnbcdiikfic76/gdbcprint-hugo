Heavy-duty corrugated shipping box with full-color brand printing designed for e-commerce fulfillment by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003). Available in double-wall or triple-wall configurations with B-flute, BC-flute, or BA-flute corrugation for maximum product protection during shipping. The self-sealing adhesive strip and integrated tear strip provide quick packing efficiency and a clean consumer unboxing experience. Produced on our BHS 2.5m corrugator line with flexographic printing. Daily output: 1.7 million boxes.

## Applications
- E-commerce direct-to-consumer branded shipping boxes
- Subscription box packaging with custom print
- Heavy product shipping (electronics, books, home goods)
- Apparel and footwear e-commerce fulfillment
- Cosmetic and skincare product shipping
- Industrial parts and component packaging

## Customization Options
- Full-color flexographic printing (up to 4 colors) on kraft or white liner
- Double-wall (BC-flute) or triple-wall (BA-flute) construction
- Self-sealing latex adhesive strip for fast packing
- Integrated tear strip for easy consumer opening without tools
- Die-cut handles for heavy item portability
- Custom size from 150×150×50mm to 800×600×400mm
- Kraft brown or bleached white exterior finish
- Inside printing for branded unboxing experience
- Perforated return strip for e-commerce returns

## Production Specifications
- BHS 2.5m corrugator line for single, double, and triple wall board
- Automatic flexographic printer-slotter-die-cutter inline production
- Automatic folder-gluer for high-speed box assembly
- KBA 1050 4-color offset for litho-laminated premium finishes
- 18,000 sqm manufacturing facility (10,000+8,000 sqm, 2025 expansion)
- ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management
- FSC Chain of Custody certified for responsible fiber sourcing
- 22 years of packaging manufacturing excellence
- Daily output capacity: 1.7 million boxes

## Quality Assurance
- Edge crush test (ECT) and burst strength (Mullen) testing
- Box compression test (BCT) per ISTA standards
- Adhesive strip peel strength and seal integrity testing
- Drop test and vibration test simulation for transit conditions
- Pre-production sample with actual flute and print for approval
- Third-party inspection available (SGS, Bureau Veritas, TÜV)
