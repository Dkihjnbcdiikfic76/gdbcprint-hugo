## Product Overview

The Bincai Kraft Paper Pyramid Gift Box redefines sustainable packaging with a design that's beautiful, functional, and 100% home compostable. The distinctive pyramid shape is formed from 350gsm FSC-certified kraft paperboard printed with soy-based inks on our **Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 7+1 UV** and **KBA Rapida 105 4-color** offset press lines. A compostable PLA (polylactic acid) window allows the contents — premium loose-leaf tea, dried lavender, chamomile flowers, or artisan botanicals — to be fully visible while maintaining the box's structural integrity. A natural jute drawstring at the peak doubles as closure and handle. Every component breaks down in a home compost environment within 180 days.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the PLA window truly home compostable?**
A: Yes. The PLA (polylactic acid) film we use is certified to EN 13432 for home composting conditions (ambient temperature, 180 days). Unlike industrial-compostable PLA, our grade is specifically formulated for backyard compost piles.

**Q: Can you print full-color CMYK on kraft paper with soy ink?**
A: Yes. Our KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset press achieves excellent color reproduction on kraft substrates using soy-based inks. We use a white under-base layer when vibrant colors are required on dark kraft.

**Q: What is the minimum order quantity for custom pyramid boxes?**
A: MOQ is 500 units per design. Samples available at 50-unit quantities. Digital proof turnaround: 2 business days. Physical sample: 7 business days.

**Q: How long does production take?**
A: Standard lead time is 10–14 working days after sample approval. Rush service (7–10 days) available at a 15% surcharge.

**Q: Are the boxes shipped flat or assembled?**
