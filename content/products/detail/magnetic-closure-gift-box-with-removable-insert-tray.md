## Product Overview

A premium magnetic closure gift box featuring a removable insert tray — the ultimate packaging solution for multi-product gift sets, sampler collections, and curated brand experiences. The removable tray allows customers to lift out the entire product array in one elegant motion, revealing often a second layer beneath. Manufactured at **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** using Heidelberg 7+1 UV press printing and precision hand assembly, each box delivers the kind of unboxing experience that drives social media sharing and repeat purchases.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Box construction**: 2.5mm greyboard core, 157gsm art paper wrap, magnetic closure with hidden neodymium magnets
- **Removable tray**: Die-cut 2.5mm greyboard with velvet flocking, satin lining, or custom-printed paper wrap
- **Tray cavities**: Precision die-cut — square, rectangular, circular, or custom contour; depth from 10mm to 80mm
- **Printing**: Heidelberg 7+1 UV press — CMYK + spot color + UV coating in single pass
- **Magnets**: 4 hidden neodymium magnets (10mm × 1.5mm), 1.5kg total hold force
- **Daily output**: 220,000+ rigid boxes

## Customization Options

- **Size**: Box from 150mm × 150mm × 50mm to 450mm × 350mm × 120mm; tray fits inside with 2mm clearance
- **Tray layers**: Single tray, double stacked trays, tray + base compartment
- **Cavities**: 1-12 cavities per tray; customizable shape for each product type
- **Tray lining**: Velvet flocking (black/white/custom), satin fabric, suede, or printed paper
- **Box finish**: Matte lamination + spot UV, soft-touch coating, linen texture, leatherette paper
- **Foil stamping**: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper — on lid exterior and/or tray surface
- **Ribbon**: Optional satin ribbon pull-tab for easy tray removal
