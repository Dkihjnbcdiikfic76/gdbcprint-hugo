## Product Overview

Our custom folding cartons deliver premium shelf presence with vibrant CMYK offset printing on high-quality paperboard. Designed for retail, e-commerce, and brand packaging, these flat-shipped cartons fold into sturdy, precision-fit boxes. Manufactured in our 18,000m² ISO 9001 certified factory on KBA and Heidelberg presses with closed-loop color management.

## Design Features

- **Auto-Bottom or Tuck-End**: Choose self-erecting auto-bottom for fast assembly or classic tuck-end for secure closure
- **Full CMYK + Spot**: 4-color process plus up to 2 spot colors on Heidelberg 7+1 UV press
- **Soft-Touch Finish**: Velvet matte lamination option for luxury tactile experience
- **Die-Cut Precision**: Steel-rule die-cutting ensures consistent folding and clean edges
- **Flat Shipping**: Cartons ship flat to minimize freight costs, assemble in seconds
