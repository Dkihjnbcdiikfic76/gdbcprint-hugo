## Quality Assurance

| Checkpoint | Standard | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Foam Insert Fit | ±0.5mm cavity tolerance | CNC program verification + physical gauge check |
| Magnet Pull Force | 8-12N (target 10N ± 2N) | Digital force gauge, 100% inline testing |
| Hot Foil Registration | ±0.3mm positional accuracy | Optical comparator, 1st/last article + random sampling |
| Lamination Adhesion | ≥4N/15mm peel strength | Cross-hatch tape test (ASTM D3359, 3B minimum) |
| Color Consistency | ΔE ≤ 2.0 vs. approved color standard | X-Rite spectrophotometer, every 500 sheets |
| Structural Integrity | No delamination after 48h at 40°C/90% RH | Tropical climate simulation; pass: zero corner lift >0.5mm |
| Drop Test | 1.2m free-fall, 6 faces, 1 drop each | ISTA 1A protocol; pass: no foam compression >2mm, no product contact |
| Vibration Test | 60 min at 4Hz, 1" amplitude | ISTA 1A; pass: no foam shift, no magnet dislodgement |
| Odor Test | No detectable odor after 24h sealed | Sensory panel; critical for tea packaging (odor transfer risk) |
| Barcode Scanning | 100% first-pass scan rate | Symbol/Zebra verifier, grade B or higher |
| Foam Colorfastness | No transfer to product after 72h contact | Rub test under 500g weight, 23°C/50% RH |
| FSC Chain-of-Custody | Full traceability from forest to box | FSC-STD-40-004 v3-0; certificate available on request |
