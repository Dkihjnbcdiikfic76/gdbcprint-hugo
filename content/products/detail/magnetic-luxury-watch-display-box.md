## Product Overview

The Bincai Magnetic Luxury Watch Display Box delivers premium presentation for high-end timepieces. Manufactured in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility (10,000+8,000 smart factory) with German-engineered Heidelberg 7+1 UV and KBA 1050 4-color presses, each box combines structural integrity with refined aesthetics. The magnetic closure provides a satisfying snap-lock that keeps watches secure during display and transport.

- **Magnetic Closure**: Precision-aligned neodymium magnets embedded in 2.0mm greyboard, tested to 5,000+ open/close cycles without strength degradation

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What magnetic closure strength do you recommend for watch boxes?**
A: For standard single-watch boxes (120×80×60mm), Bincai uses N35 neodymium magnets delivering ≥4.5N pull force per pair — strong enough to prevent accidental opening during transport but easy to open with one hand. For larger or heavier multi-watch boxes, we upgrade to N42 or N52 magnets delivering 6–10N per pair. The closure feel is calibrated during sampling: you tell us your preferred "snap" strength and we dial it in. Each magnet is individually pull-force tested after assembly.

**Q: Can you produce watch boxes for smartwatches with charging cable storage?**
A: Yes. Our team designs custom interior layouts that accommodate smartwatch bodies, charging pucks, cables, and extra bands in organized compartments. We can integrate cable management channels, elastic band retainers, and USB cable cutouts through the box wall. The velvet or suede interior is die-cut to match your exact smartwatch dimensions including crown and sensor protrusions. We've produced smartwatch packaging for brands shipping Apple Watch, Garmin, and Samsung Galaxy Watch accessories.

**Q: What is your lead time for custom luxury watch boxes?**
A: Standard lead time is 15–20 working days from artwork approval. Pre-production samples ship in 5–7 days. For repeat orders with no design changes, lead time drops to 7–10 working days. Rush service (10 days) is available for urgent launches at a 15–20% surcharge. Our 1.7M daily output capacity means even 50,000-unit holiday season orders ship on time without delays.

**Q: Do you offer sustainable and eco-friendly materials for watch packaging?**
A: Absolutely. Our standard greyboard already uses 100% recycled post-consumer fiber core. For brands with aggressive sustainability goals, we offer: FSC-certified exterior wrap paper with soy-based ink printing, water-based lamination instead of solvent-based, recycled PET velvet (rPET) interior flocking, biodegradable PLA window film, and fully recyclable construction (no mixed materials that impede recycling). We can provide a sustainability certificate documenting the recycled content percentage and carbon footprint reduction for your ESG reporting.

**Q: What is the minimum order quantity for custom foil-stamped watch boxes?**

For brands testing a new market, we can produce 300 units with a modest per-unit surcharge. Repeat orders with no changes start at 250 units. Volume discounts begin at 5,000 units with progressive tier reductions at 10,000, 25,000, and 50,000+ units.
