## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the weight capacity of your BC-flute double-wall mailers?**
A: BC-flute double-wall mailers (6mm) handle 15–35 kg depending on box dimensions. For loads above 35 kg, we recommend triple-wall BCA-flute construction (9mm) rated to 50 kg. Every design is tested to your specific weight and dimensions.

**Q: Can Bincai supply VCI (anti-corrosion) treated mailers for bare metal parts?**
A: Yes. We apply VCI (Volatile Corrosion Inhibitor) treatment to the kraft liner during production. VCI molecules vaporize inside the sealed box, forming a protective monolayer on metal surfaces. Protects bare steel, iron, and aluminum for up to 90 days in standard sea freight conditions.

**Q: Do you provide structural design assistance for custom part shapes?**
A: Absolutely. Send us your part dimensions, weight, and fragility level. Our packaging engineers create a 3D structural design with internal die-cut partitions, foam cushions, or corrugated spacers. We deliver a physical prototype for your fit-test within 5-7 days.

**Q: What testing do your mailers undergo before shipment?**
A: Every design undergoes ISTA 3A parcel delivery simulation: drop test (10 drops from 76 cm), random vibration (60 min), and compression. We provide a test report with your production batch. On request: ASTM D4169, ISTA 6-FedEx, or Amazon SIOC certification.

**Q: Are your corrugated mailers recyclable and environmentally compliant?**
A: Yes. 100% recyclable single-material kraft construction using water-based, repulpable adhesives (no hot-melt contamination). FSC Mix Credit certified. Heavy-metal-free flexo inks. Full compliance documentation for EU Packaging Directive 94/62/EC and US EPA guidelines.
