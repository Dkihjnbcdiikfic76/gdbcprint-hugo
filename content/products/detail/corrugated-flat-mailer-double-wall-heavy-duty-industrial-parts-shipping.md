## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between BC-flute double-wall and single-wall corrugated?**
A: BC-flute double-wall combines B-flute (3mm, finer, better print surface) with C-flute (4mm, coarser, better cushioning) for 6mm total thickness. This delivers 200# burst strength vs 125–150# for single-wall C-flute. Double-wall is preferred for heavy industrial parts (5–25kg), while single-wall suits lighter products (0.5–5kg). We also offer triple-wall for extreme-duty applications.

**Q: Do you offer anti-corrosion treatment for metal parts packaging?**
A: Yes. We can incorporate VCI (Volatile Corrosion Inhibitor) kraft liners or VCI poly film into our corrugated mailers. VCI molecules vaporize and form a protective molecular layer on metal surfaces, preventing rust and corrosion during shipping and storage — ideal for automotive parts, machined components, and steel fabrications.

**Q: What's your maximum weight capacity for double-wall mailers?**
A: Our BC-flute double-wall mailers handle 5–25kg depending on box dimensions and product distribution. For heavier loads (25–50kg), we recommend triple-wall construction. Our engineering team performs edge crush testing (ECT) and can provide load capacity certification for your specific product and box dimensions.

**Q: Can you print our brand logo and handling instructions directly on corrugated?**
A: Yes. Flexographic post-print provides 1–3 color branding directly on the corrugated surface — ideal for logos, handling instructions ("This Side Up", "Fragile"), and recycling symbols. For high-resolution graphics, we apply KBA 1050 4-color offset-printed label panels on the mailer exterior.
