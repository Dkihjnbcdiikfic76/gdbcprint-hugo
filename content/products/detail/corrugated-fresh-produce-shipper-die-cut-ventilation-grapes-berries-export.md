## Product Overview

Bincai's corrugated fresh produce shipper is engineered for the demanding cold-chain export market — where ventilation, humidity control, and stack strength determine whether a shipment of premium table grapes arrives in retail-ready condition or as a total loss. B-flute single-wall corrugated construction with strategic die-cut ventilation holes on all four side panels plus top and bottom enables natural convection airflow around the produce. A moisture-resistant coating prevents box degradation in high-humidity cold storage (85–95% RH), while interlocking top tabs provide stack stability without tape or strapping. Manufactured on KBA 1050 4-color offset presses with precision die-cutting in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility. 22 years of industrial packaging expertise.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: KBA 1050 4-color offset — moisture-resistant ink formulation
- **Board**: B-flute single-wall corrugated, 3mm caliper, 125gsm kraft liner / 120gsm medium
- **Ventilation**: Die-cut circular vents, 15mm diameter, 8 per side panel (32 total), 6 top/bottom
- **Coating**: Water-based moisture-resistant varnish — withstands 95% RH for 72 hours
- **Bottom Style**: Automatic lock-bottom — flat-packed, rapid hand erection
- **Closure**: Interlocking tab top — no tape needed, stack-ready
- **Daily Output**: 1.7 million color boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, ISPM-15 heat treatment available
