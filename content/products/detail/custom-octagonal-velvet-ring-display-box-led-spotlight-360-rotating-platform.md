## Product Overview

Our octagonal velvet ring display box with LED spotlight and 360° rotating platform represents the pinnacle of jewelry presentation engineering. The hinged octagonal lid opens to activate a warm-white LED spotlight that illuminates the ring from above, while a whisper-quiet motorized platform rotates the ring continuously at 0.5 RPM — ensuring every facet catches the light. The interior is fully flocked in premium black velvet with a precision die-cut ring slot accommodating sizes 4–13. Constructed from 2.0mm rigid greyboard wrapped in matching velvet, the octagonal shape adds architectural sophistication that sets it apart from conventional square boxes. A built-in 2000mAh rechargeable lithium battery with USB-C charging provides 8+ hours of continuous display — ideal for retail counters, exhibition showcases, and proposal moments. Manufactured at Bincai's 18,000 sqm integrated factory with 22 years of jewelry packaging expertise. Every box undergoes 7-stage QC from LED module testing to rotation smoothness verification.

## Design Features

- **Octagonal Hinged Construction**: Eight-sided architectural profile creates a distinctive display presence — precision-cut 2.0mm rigid greyboard panels wrapped in premium velvet flocking
- **Integrated LED Spotlight**: Warm-white (3000K) LED module in the lid interior, auto-activated when the lid opens via magnetic reed switch — illuminates the ring with 80 lumens at 30° beam angle
- **360° Motorized Rotating Platform**: Micro DC gear motor (0.5 RPM, ≤25dB noise level) with precision ball-bearing turntable — showcases the ring from every angle without user touch
- **Magnetic Snap Closure + Satin Ribbon Pull**: N35 neodymium magnets provide secure closure during transport; 12mm color-matched satin ribbon for elegant lid opening
- **Die-Cut Velvet Ring Slot**: Precision-cut velvet flocked slot with 18mm × 22mm oval shape — accommodates ring sizes 4–13 (US), solitaire through 3-stone settings
- **USB-C Rechargeable Battery**: Built-in 2000mAh lithium polymer battery charges via USB-C (cable included) in 2.5 hours, providing 8+ hours continuous display or 200+ open/close cycles

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the battery last in real-world use?**
A: In continuous display mode (lid open, LED on, platform rotating): 8+ hours. In retail counter mode (intermittent open/close): 200+ cycles. The auto-on/off magnetic switch ensures the battery only drains when the box is open.

**Q: Can the rotation speed be adjusted?**
A: The standard speed is 0.5 RPM (one full rotation every 2 minutes). Custom speeds (0.25–2.0 RPM) are available for orders of 2,000+ units.

**Q: Is the LED replaceable?**
A: The LED module is rated for 50,000+ hours (MTBF) — effectively the lifetime of the box. We can provide replacement modules for warranty service if needed.

**Q: What ring styles fit in the slot?**
A: The standard oval slot (18mm × 22mm × 8mm deep) accommodates solitaire, halo, three-stone, cluster, and signet rings up to US size 13. For unusually tall settings (>12mm), we can increase the slot depth.

**Q: Can I customize the LED color temperature?**
A: Absolutely! Warm-white 3000K is standard. Cool-white 5000K (better for diamonds), neutral 4000K, and RGB color-changing (Bluetooth app-controlled) are all available.

**Q: Does the box come with a charger?**
A: Each box includes a 1-meter USB-A to USB-C charging cable. USB wall adapters are available as an optional add-on for bulk orders.
