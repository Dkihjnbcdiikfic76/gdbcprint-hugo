## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What velvet colors do you offer?**
A: Standard velvet colors include black, navy, royal blue, burgundy, emerald green, ivory, blush pink, charcoal grey, and chocolate brown. Custom Pantone-matched velvet is available for orders of 3,000+ units with a dye-matching fee and 5-7 extra working days.

**Q: Can you create custom compartment layouts for my specific jewelry pieces?**
A: Yes. We design custom die-cut inserts based on your jewelry dimensions — necklace chain lengths, pendant sizes, clasp types. Provide your jewelry samples or CAD drawings, and we'll create a custom insert layout. The die charge is included in orders of 1,000+ units.

**Q: How is the velvet applied — will it peel or shed?**
A: Our velvet flocking is electrostatically applied to a pre-glued paper wrap, then heat-cured. This creates a durable bond that passes 500+ open/close cycles without peeling. Unlike spray-on flocking, our process produces minimal shedding — we test with adhesive tape to ensure no loose fibers transfer.

**Q: What are the MOQ and lead times?**
A: Standard MOQ is 300-500 units. Lead time is 15-20 working days for standard configurations, 20-25 working days with custom inserts. Rush orders available at 10-12 working days with surcharge.

**Q: Can you include LED lighting inside the box?**
A: Yes. We offer battery-powered LED strip lighting that auto-illuminates when the box is opened. The LED module is embedded in the lid or base with a magnetic reed switch. Power is from CR2032 coin cells (replaceable) with 30+ hours of illumination. LED adds $1.50-3.00 per unit and 3-5 working days.

**Q: Is the box gift-ready?**
A: Yes. Every box comes individually poly-bagged and can be optionally packaged in a kraft outer sleeve or gift bag. We also offer custom tissue paper, ribbon, and hang tags as add-on services.

**Q: Can you hot foil stamp my logo on velvet?**
A: Yes. Hot foil stamping on velvet produces a luxurious, permanent imprint. We use heated brass dies (120-140°C) with metallic foil that bonds into the velvet fibers. Available in gold, silver, rose gold, copper, and holographic. We recommend die sizes up to 80×80mm for best results on velvet.
