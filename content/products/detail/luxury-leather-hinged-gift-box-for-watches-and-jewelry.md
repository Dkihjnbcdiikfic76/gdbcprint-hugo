## Product Overview

Designed for premium watch, jewelry, and luxury accessory brands, this leather-finish hinged gift box combines old-world craftsmanship with modern manufacturing precision. The leatherette wrap over a rigid greyboard core delivers the look and feel of genuine leather at a competitive price point. Manufactured in our 18,000m² ISO 9001 certified factory on Heidelberg and KBA presses with precision die-cutting, foil stamping, and hand-finished assembly. Ideal for high-end retail presentation, corporate gifting, and e-commerce luxury unboxing.

## Features

- **Leather Finish**: Premium PU leatherette or genuine leather wrap options in matte, pebbled, or croc-embossed textures
- **Hinged Construction**: Piano-style metal hinge or continuous leatherette hinge — both rated 10,000+ open/close cycles
- **Interior**: Velvet flocking (0.8–1.5mm pile), satin lining, or suede-finish microfiber in custom colors
- **Insert**: CNC die-cut EVA or PU foam insert with watch cushion, ring slots, or necklace hooks
- **LED Lighting**: Optional battery-powered LED strip (CR2032, 80+ hours) activated by lid opening — motion-sensor module available
- **Magnetic Closure**: N38-N52 neodymium disc magnets hidden in front flap — 5.0 N/cm minimum retention force
- **Foil Options**: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper, holographic, or matte black foil via Bobst flatbed hot stamping press

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: Heidelberg Speedmaster 7+1 UV offset + KBA 1050 4-color — ±0.15mm print registration tolerance
- **Board**: 1.5mm–2.5mm rigid greyboard (1000–1400gsm) wrapped in 157gsm art paper with leatherette overlay
- **Hinge Type**: Piano-style zinc alloy hinge (60mm standard) or leatherette continuous hinge
- **Foiling**: Bobst flatbed hot stamping, 6 foil roll capacity, multi-level embossing + foil in single pass
- **Interior Assembly**: Hand-applied velvet/satin lining, CNC die-cut EVA foam inserts (30–60 Shore A hardness)
- **LED Module**: CR2032-powered, 3× warm white LED strip, magnetic reed switch triggering, 80+ hour battery life
- **Daily Output**: 220,000+ gift boxes across all rigid categories
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, SGS material testing, REACH-compliant materials
