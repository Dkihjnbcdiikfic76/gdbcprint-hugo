## Product Overview

Folding carton sleeve and tray combo designed for premium confectionery retail display — artisan chocolates, macarons, luxury biscuits, nougats, dragées, and fine patisserie products. The classic European-style construction features an outer printed sleeve with a precision die-cut window that slides over a matching inner tray, creating a sophisticated reveal experience that showcases product quality before purchase. Manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** (founded 2003) in our 18,000 sqm Foshan dual-factory complex (10,000+8,000 sqm smart factory). Printed on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses using food-safe, low-migration inks with optional PET or PLA window patching. Daily output of 1.7 million boxes under ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certification.
