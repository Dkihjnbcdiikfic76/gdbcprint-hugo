Recyclable brown kraft paper rigid gift box with magnetic closure — designed by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. for brands that want luxurious presentation without compromising on sustainability. Made from 100% recycled greyboard and wrapped in natural kraft paper, the entire box is fully recyclable in standard paper waste streams. Printed with soy-based inks that are low-VOC and biodegradable, while the magnetic closure delivers the premium unboxing experience customers expect. The natural kraft aesthetic with subtle branding creates an authentic, artisanal feel that resonates with eco-conscious consumers. Produced across 10,000 sqm of factory space on Heidelberg 7+1 UV and KBA 1050 presses. Daily output capacity of 1.7 million boxes.

## Customization Options
- Kraft shade: Natural brown, light kraft, dark kraft, or white kraft
- Printing: Single-color soy ink, multi-color CMYK, or branded stamp
- Finishing: Matte lamination (water-based), embossing/debossing (no foil)
- Interior: Recycled cardboard insert, shredded kraft filler, or unbleached cotton lining
- Closure: Single or dual neodymium magnets (N35-N52)
- Add-ons: Jute twine wrap, seed paper tag, or branded kraft belly band

## Production Specifications
- **Base material:** 100% recycled 1200–1600 gsm greyboard, FSC certified
- **Wrapping:** 128–200 gsm natural kraft paper (unbleached)
- **Ink:** Soy-based offset ink — low VOC, biodegradable, food-safe option available
- **Printing:** Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset with extended gamut for natural substrates
- **Adhesive:** Water-based eco-adhesive, solvent-free
- **Assembly:** Automatic rigid box line with clean magnet housing
- **Quality control:** Recyclability verification (PTS method), magnet strength testing, kraft paper color consistency, soy ink adhesion test
