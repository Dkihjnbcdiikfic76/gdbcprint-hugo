## FAQ

**Q: Is the printed toy gift box safe for children's products? What safety standards do you meet?**
A: Yes. Our toy boxes are manufactured with child safety as a priority. All materials comply with **EN 71-3** (heavy metals migration limits for toy packaging) and **ASTM F963** toy safety standards. Corners are die-cut with a minimum 3mm radius to eliminate sharp edges, and we use non-toxic, low-VOC inks and adhesives. FSC certification ensures paperboard comes from responsibly managed forests — a growing requirement for toy brands selling into EU and North American markets. Third-party lab testing certificates (SGS, Intertek, TÜV) are available on request.

**Q: Can the insert be designed to hold multiple toy pieces or accessories?**
A: Absolutely. Our in-house structural design team creates **multi-cavity die-cut inserts** that organize action figure accessories, game tokens, puzzle pieces, or STEM kit components into dedicated compartments. We can design friction-fit slots, fold-up dividers, or nested tray levels within a single insert. For action figures, we commonly design the insert to lock the figure at the waist and ankles while holding accessories (weapons, alternate hands, stands) in adjacent cutouts. CAD drawings and 3D renderings are provided for approval before tooling.

**Q: What is your MOQ for toy boxes, and can I start with a small test order?**
A: MOQ starts at **500 units** for standard tuck-top or STE cartons with a single-color insert. For boxes with window film, hot foil stamping, or complex multi-cavity inserts, MOQ is **1,000 units** to cover tooling costs. We welcome trial orders — free pre-production samples (freight collect) let you evaluate fit, finish, and print quality before committing to a full production run. Many toy brands start with a 500–1000 piece trial for a Kickstarter campaign or trade show launch, then scale to mass production.

**Q: Are your printing inks and materials safe if the toy box is handled by young children?**
A: Yes. We use **soy-based or low-VOC offset inks** that meet EN 71-3 and CONEG (heavy metals) regulations. Our aqueous and BOPP lamination films are phthalate-free and non-toxic. For brands with the strictest requirements — such as infant/toddler toys (0–3 years) — we can produce with food-grade inks and water-based coatings only, eliminating solvent-based components entirely. Full material safety data sheets (MSDS) are provided with every order, and we recommend third-party testing for your specific market's regulatory requirements.
