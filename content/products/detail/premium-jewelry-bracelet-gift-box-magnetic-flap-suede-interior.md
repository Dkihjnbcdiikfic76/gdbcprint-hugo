## FAQ

**Q: What suede colors are available for the interior lining?**
A: We stock suede in black, navy blue, burgundy, dark grey, ivory, champagne, and chocolate brown. Custom Pantone-matched suede is available for orders above 3,000 units with a 2–3 week lead time for fabric procurement. All suede is OEKO-TEX certified and colorfast-tested to 4+ rating.

**Q: Can the bracelet groove be made to fit a specific bracelet width or shape?**
A: Yes. Provide your bracelet dimensions (outer diameter, width, thickness) and we will create a custom die-cut groove that fits perfectly. For bangles with irregular shapes or decorative elements, we offer multi-point foam or EVA inserts instead of a single groove. Free fitting samples are included with pre-production.

**Q: How does the magnetic flap compare to a traditional lift-off lid box?**
A: The magnetic flap design offers a more elegant unboxing experience — the flap opens like a book cover to reveal the bracelet, creating a dramatic presentation moment. It also eliminates the risk of the lid being lost or separated during use. The flap can be printed on both sides for additional branding space.

**Q: Can these boxes be used for men's bracelets and cufflinks too?**
A: Absolutely. The design is gender-neutral. For men's jewelry, we recommend darker suede colors (black, charcoal, navy) and bolder foil colors (silver, gunmetal, matte black). We can also adjust the groove dimensions to accommodate wider men's bracelet styles and add a second compartment for cufflinks or tie clips.
