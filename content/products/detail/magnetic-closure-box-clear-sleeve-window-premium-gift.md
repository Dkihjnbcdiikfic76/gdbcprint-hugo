Premium magnetic closure rigid box with a transparent PET sleeve window — crafted by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of manufacturing excellence. - **Base material:** 1200–1800 gsm greyboard, wrapped in 157 gsm art paper

- **Finishing:** Laminating machine, foil stamping (gold/silver/holographic), embossing/debossing
- **Assembly:** Automatic rigid box forming line with magnet insertion
- **Quality control:** 100% visual inspection, magnet strength testing, sleeve fit verification
