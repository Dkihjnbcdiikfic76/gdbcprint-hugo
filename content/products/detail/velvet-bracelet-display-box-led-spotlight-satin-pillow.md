## FAQ

**Q: What is the MOQ for LED bracelet display boxes?**
A: MOQ is 300 units for standard configurations with warm white LED. Custom LED colors (RGB, cool white) or unique cavity designs require 500+ units. We offer free 3D renderings and pre-production samples within 7–15 working days.

**Q: How does the LED spotlight work?**
A: The LED module is activated by a magnetic reed switch — when the lid opens, the magnet moves away from the reed switch, completing the circuit and illuminating the bracelet. The light gently fades on (soft-start) for an elegant reveal effect. Battery life is 200+ hours on a single CR2032 cell.

**Q: Can the LED be rechargeable instead of battery-powered?**
A: Yes. We offer a USB-C rechargeable option with a 200mAh lithium-polymer battery that provides 500+ charge cycles and approximately 8–12 hours of continuous illumination per charge. The USB-C port is discreetly integrated into the base.

**Q: What velvet colors are available for the interior?**
A: Standard colors include black, navy blue, charcoal grey, burgundy, cream, and chocolate brown. We can also match any Pantone color for orders of 1,000+ units. The electrostatic flocking process produces a uniform, plush surface with 0.8mm–1.5mm pile height.

**Q: Can I customize the satin pillow to fit my exact bracelet design?**
A: Absolutely. The satin pillow's bracelet channel can be customized from 8mm to 25mm width, with depth adjustable to securely cradle your specific bracelet profile. Simply provide your bracelet dimensions or sample for precision pillow fabrication.

**Q: What exterior customization options maximize brand impact?**
A: We recommend hot foil stamping (gold or rose gold are most popular for jewelry) combined with embossing for a dimensional logo effect. Add a mirror insert under the lid for an enhanced try-on experience. Our Bobst BMA foil stampers deliver gallery-quality metallic branding.

**Q: Are these boxes suitable for international e-commerce shipping?**
A: Yes. Each box passes ISTA 1A drop testing (10 drops from 760mm). We also offer custom corrugated mailer outer boxes with foam cushioning for direct-to-consumer shipping. Our jewelry-grade protective packaging has been tested across 30+ export countries.
