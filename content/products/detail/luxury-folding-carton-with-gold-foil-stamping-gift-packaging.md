- **Premium Confectionery & Gourmet Food**: Chocolate boxes, tea tins, specialty coffee, artisanal sweets for duty-free and boutique retail
- **High-End Fashion Accessories**: Tie boxes, scarf packaging, belt boxes, small leather goods presentation
- **Corporate Gifts & Awards**: Year-end gifts, executive gift sets, award ceremony packaging with company logo gold stamping
- **Jewelry & Watches**: Outer carton sleeves for rigid jewelry boxes, watch presentation cartons with window cutouts
- **Wedding & Event Favors**: Personalized wedding favor boxes, event souvenir packaging, celebration gift cartons
- **Electronics Accessories**: Premium earphone boxes, smart watch packaging, luxury phone case cartons

## Customization Options
- **Foil Stamping**: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper, holographic, and matte gold foil in Pantone-matched colors; large-format stamping up to 750×1050mm on our Bobst foil stamping machines
- **Structure**: Reverse tuck end (RTE), straight tuck end (STE), auto-lock bottom, crash-lock bottom, double-wall tuck, sleeve-and-tray, book-style with magnetic flap
- **Printing**: Full CMYK offset (KBA 1050 4-color) with up to 7 PMS spot colors + UV coating (Heidelberg 7+1)
- **Surface Finishes**: Matte lamination, gloss lamination, soft-touch velvet lamination, spot UV (gloss/matte), glitter UV, textured UV
- **Embossing & Debossing**: Blind emboss, registered emboss (aligned to print), multi-level sculptured emboss, micro-emboss texture
- **Special Effects**: Holographic cold foil, pearlescent coating, thermochromic ink, raised spot UV for braille-like texture
- **Interior**: Printed inside, custom insert trays, die-cut foam inserts, satin ribbon pull tabs, magnetic closure
- **Size**: Fully custom — from compact 60×60×25mm to 400×300×120mm

## Production Specifications
- **Press #1**: KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset press — 15,000 sheets/hour, max sheet 1050×740mm, automated plate changing
- **Press #2**: Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 with UV — 7 colors + coating in one pass, instant UV curing for specialty substrates
- **Foil Stamping**: Bobst SP 102 BMA Autoplaten — 7,500 sheets/hour, programmable foil advance for 40%+ foil savings, multi-zone temperature control (±2°C)
- **Die-Cutting**: Bobst SP 102-SE Autoplaten with stripping station, ±0.1mm registration
- **Folder-Gluer**: Bobst Ambition 106 A2 — automatic crash-lock and 4&6-corner capability, barcode verification
- **Prepress**: CTP (computer-to-plate) with stochastic (FM) screening for photo-realistic reproduction; GMG color management with ISO 12647-2 compliance
- **Lead Time**: 7–10 working days for standard; 10–15 days for complex multi-process
- **Daily Capacity**: 800,000 folding cartons per day across production lines
