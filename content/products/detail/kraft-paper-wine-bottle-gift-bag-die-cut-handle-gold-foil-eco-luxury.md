## Product Overview

Our kraft paper wine bottle gift bag marries eco-conscious materials with luxury presentation through the striking combination of natural kraft texture and gold foil stamping. Constructed from 250gsm FSC-certified kraft paper, the bag features a reinforced die-cut handle, square-bottom gusset, and is precision-sized for standard 750ml wine bottles. ## FAQ

**Q: Will the gold foil adhere well to uncoated kraft paper?**
A: Yes! We use a specialized foil formulation for uncoated kraft that bites into the paper fibers rather than sitting on the surface. The result is excellent adhesion (≥95% in tape test) with a beautiful organic-metallic contrast.

**Q: Can the wine bag support a full bottle by the handle alone?**
A: Absolutely. Our die-cut handle with internal reinforcement patch is tested to ≥15kg sustained load — a full 750ml wine bottle weighs approximately 1.2–1.5kg, well within the safety margin.

**Q: Are the soy-based inks truly biodegradable?**
A: Yes! Our soy inks are USDA BioPreferred certified, VOC-free, and fully biodegradable. The entire bag — kraft paper, soy ink, and reinforcement patch — biodegrades in industrial composting conditions.

**Q: What bottle shapes does this bag fit?**
A: The standard size fits Bordeaux (straight-sided), Burgundy (sloped shoulder), and Champagne bottles. For unusual shapes (Alsace flute, Rhône), we can adjust the gusset dimensions accordingly.

**Q: Can you print on both sides of the bag?**
A: Yes! We can print on both exterior faces and even the gusset sides. This is popular for wineries that want brand messaging visible from multiple angles on retail shelves.

**Q: What's the difference between die-cut handles and rope handles?**
A: Die-cut handles are integrated into the bag body (no separate component) — more eco-friendly and cost-effective. Rope handles offer a more premium tactile feel. Both are available, with die-cut being the most popular for wine bags.
