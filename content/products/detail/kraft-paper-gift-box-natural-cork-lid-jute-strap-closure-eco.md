## Design Philosophy

The cork-kraft-jute combination creates a multi-sensory unboxing experience: the warm, irregular texture of natural cork against the earthy grain of kraft paperboard, tied with the rough-spun character of jute twine. This is packaging that customers keep, reuse, and photograph — turning the box into a brand asset rather than disposable waste. The design speaks directly to the values of the LOHAS (Lifestyles of Health and Sustainability) consumer, communicating environmental commitment through materials rather than words.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the cork lid really sustainable?**
Absolutely. Cork is one of the most sustainable materials available — it's harvested by stripping the bark from cork oak trees every 9 years without cutting down the tree. Cork oak forests (Montados in Portugal) are biodiversity hotspots and carbon sinks — each harvested tree absorbs 3-5× more CO₂ than an unharvested tree. Our cork composite is made from the byproducts of wine cork production, so nothing goes to waste. The material is naturally antimicrobial, water-resistant, and fully biodegradable.

**Q: Can the entire box go in my home compost?**
Yes — every component is OK Compost HOME certified by TÜV Austria. The kraft board, soy ink, water-based adhesive, cork lid, and jute twine will all break down in a home compost pile within 12 months. Remove any foil stamping before composting (though the foil quantity is negligible for industrial composting). We recommend printing composting instructions inside the box to guide end consumers.

**Q: How does the cork lid stay on without magnets or plastic?**
The cork lid is precision waterjet-cut to create a friction fit with the kraft box opening — like a wine cork in a bottle neck. The engagement depth is 2-4mm depending on box size. The cork's natural elasticity provides a secure fit that won't come loose during handling but is easy for the customer to open. For extra security, the jute strap wraps over the lid and fastens with a loop-and-button or toggle.

**Q: Can I print my full-color brand design on kraft paper?**
We can print up to 2 PMS spot colors on kraft by offset (soy-based inks) for a rustic, artisanal look — this is the most popular choice. For full CMYK photography, we recommend laminating 157gsm art paper to the kraft body, which gives you vibrant full-color printing while maintaining the kraft aesthetic on the interior. Alternatively, foil stamping creates a striking metallic contrast on natural kraft.

**Q: What happens to the box when it gets wet or humid?**
Kraft paperboard has inherent moisture resistance and the optional water-based varnish adds further protection. The cork lid is naturally water-resistant (that's why wine bottles use cork). For high-humidity environments or refrigerated display, we recommend the 400-500gsm kraft with aqueous varnish coating. We test all cork lids for 72 hours at 30°C/85% RH to ensure no warping or delamination.

**Q: Is there a minimum order for laser-engraved cork lids?**
MOQ for laser-engraved cork lids is 1,000 units. The engraving is ink-free, permanent, and creates a beautiful organic textural effect — the engraved area darkens slightly against the natural cork. Single-color ink stamp printing on cork is also available from 500 units. We provide a laser engraving proof within 24 hours for approval before production.
