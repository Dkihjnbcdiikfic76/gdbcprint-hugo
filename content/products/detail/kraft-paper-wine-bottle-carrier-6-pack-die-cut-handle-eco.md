## Product Overview

Eco-friendly kraft paper wine bottle carrier designed for 6-bottle wine gift sets and premium retail display. The self-locking die-cut design with integrated carry handle assembles without glue, while internal divider tabs keep bottles separated and protected during transport. Printed with soy-based inks on FSC-certified kraft board for a fully sustainable, 100% recyclable packaging solution. The natural kraft aesthetic with custom logo printing communicates eco-conscious brand values. Manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** at 1.7M boxes daily output under ISO 9001 and FSC certification.
