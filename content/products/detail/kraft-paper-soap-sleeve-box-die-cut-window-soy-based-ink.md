Our kraft paper soap sleeve box combines minimalist eco-design with product visibility — the die-cut window lets customers see and smell your artisanal soap, bath bomb, or natural skincare product before purchase while the sleeve format keeps packaging material to an absolute minimum. Printed with soy-based offset inks for vibrant yet fully biodegradable graphics, each sleeve is manufactured from FSC-certified kraft board using water-based adhesives.
