## FAQ

**Q: What does 'acid-free' mean, and why is it important for art prints?**
A: Acid-free paper has a pH of 7.0 or higher and is manufactured without acidic chemicals that cause paper to yellow, become brittle, and degrade over time. Standard paperboard contains lignin and acidic compounds that off-gas and can transfer to stored contents — this is why old newspapers turn yellow and fragile. Our portfolio boxes use ISO 9706-compliant acid-free lining paper (pH 7.5–8.5) and pH-neutral adhesives that will not cause image degradation, color shift, or embrittlement of your art prints even after decades of storage.

**Q: Can the box accommodate different print quantities — say, from 5 to 50 prints?**
A: Yes. We offer three interior configurations: (1) Flat tray — holds 5–15 prints in a single stack, ideal for gallery sets; (2) Expansion gusset — accordion-fold side walls that expand to hold 20–50 prints with interleaving tissue; (3) Multi-tray — 2–4 removable trays each holding 10–15 prints, ideal for series or collections. Each configuration is custom-built to your specifications with the same acid-free materials throughout.

**Q: Is the box sturdy enough for shipping art prints to customers?**
A: Yes. The 1,600gsm greyboard construction with reinforced corners and magnetic closure is designed to withstand commercial shipping. We test every portfolio box design through ISTA 3A simulated transit testing — including 10-drop test, compression test (200 lb top-load), and vibration test. For prints valued above $200, we recommend adding corner reinforcements and a corrugated outer shipping carton (available from Bincai as a matched set).
