## Product Overview

The book-style magnetic flap rigid box elevates packaging to bibliophilic art — it opens like a treasured hardcover volume, with a wraparound flap secured by concealed magnets and a decorative spine that begs to be displayed on a shelf. Manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** — ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified, 22 years of luxury packaging — each box combines the structural integrity of 1600gsm greyboard with the elegance of book-cloth spine wrapping and gold foil spine titling. Our Heidelberg 7+1 UV press prints the cover art, and our artisan finishing team hand-applies spine reinforcement, magnetic flaps, and interior lining.

## Design Features

- **Book-Style Opening**: The lid opens from the right like a hardcover book — a magnetic flap wraps from the lid over the fore-edge, securing to the base with two concealed N35 neodymium magnets.
- **Decorative Spine**: 40-60mm spine panel wrapped in book cloth, leatherette, or printed art paper — gold/silver foil stamped with product name, brand logo, and decorative bands. Designed to be displayed spine-out on shelves.
- **Magnetic Wraparound Flap**: A full-height flap extends from the lid's front edge, folding over the box body and magnetically snapping to the base — creates a clean, uninterrupted front panel.
- **Interior Platform**: 1600gsm greyboard platform with die-cut cavity precisely fitted to the product — wrapped in velvet flocking, satin, or linen-textured paper.
- **Ribbon Pull**: Satin ribbon loop under the platform for easy product lift-out without tilting the box.

## Applications

- **Premium Books & Journals**: Limited-edition hardcovers, leather-bound journals, photo albums — the book-shaped box is a natural extension of the content.
- **Wine & Spirits**: Single-bottle presentation for prestige cuvées, limited-release whiskies, and collector's cognacs — spine bears the vintage and château.
- **Collector Editions**: Vinyl records, art prints, commemorative coins, and trading card sets — numbered edition plates on the spine.
- **Corporate Gifting**: Executive welcome kits, boardroom gifts, and milestone anniversary presentations.
- **Watch & Jewelry**: Timepiece presentation where the book metaphor suggests a story waiting to be told.

## Production Specifications

- **Die-Cutting**: Bobst Novacut 106-E — 2-piece construction (cover + tray), ±0.1mm tolerance
- **Greyboard**: 1200gsm spine panel, 1600gsm cover and base for structural integrity
- **Spine Assembly**: Book cloth glued and case-wrapped on semi-automatic casing-in machine, spine scored with ±0.2mm hinge gap
- **Magnetic Flap**: CNC-routed magnet cavity in flap edge, N35 10mm×3mm magnets aligned with steel strike plates in base wall
- **QC Protocol**: Flap-to-base alignment (±0.5mm), magnet pull force (>1.2 kg), spine hinge flex (10,000+ cycles), cover flatness (<2mm warp)
- **Daily Output**: 220,000+ rigid boxes across all production lines
- **Lead Time**: 8-10 days sample, 18-22 days mass production
