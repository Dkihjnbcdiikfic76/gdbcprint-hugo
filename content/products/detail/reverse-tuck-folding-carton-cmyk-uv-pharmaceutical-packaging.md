## FAQ

**Q: Are your pharmaceutical packaging cartons GMP-compliant?**
A: Yes. Bincai holds ISO 15378:2017 certification — the GMP standard specific to primary packaging materials for medicinal products. Our production environment, documentation, traceability, and quality control meet pharmaceutical industry requirements for packaging that comes in direct contact with medicinal products.

**Q: Can you incorporate Braille on pharmaceutical cartons?**
A: Yes. We offer Braille embossing compliant with EU Directive 2001/83/EC, which requires Braille labeling on pharmaceutical packaging outer cartons. Our embossing achieves consistent dot height and spacing for tactile readability.

**Q: What tamper-evident features can you include?**
A: We offer multiple tamper-evident solutions: perforated tear strips, security seals, glue-lock closures, and shrink-wrap compatibility. Features are validated per ASTM D3475 standard classification for tamper-evident packaging.

**Q: Do you support serialization and track-and-trace for pharmaceutical cartons?**
A: Yes. Our production lines support variable data printing including GS1 DataMatrix barcodes, serial numbers, lot numbers, expiry dates, and 2D codes required for pharmaceutical track-and-trace compliance (DSCSA, EU FMD). Barcode verification systems ensure 100% readability.
