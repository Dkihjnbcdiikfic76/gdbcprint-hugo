### 7-Stage QC Gate

1. **Incoming Board Inspection** — grammage, caliper, brightness, moisture content per TAPPI T 410
2. **In-Process Printing** — spectrophotometer check every 500 sheets, ΔE ≤2.0 target, inline Prinect
3. **Die-Cutting & Creasing** — ±0.15mm tolerance, 100% structural integrity verification
4. **Gluing & Folding** — inline peel test, adhesive coverage verification, flap alignment
5. **Serialization & Barcode** — ISO/IEC 15416 grade verification, variable data accuracy audit
6. **Finished Goods Audit** — AQL 1.5 (critical), AQL 2.5 (major) per ANSI/ASQ Z1.4, tamper-evident validation
7. **Pre-Shipment Inspection** — random sample pull, batch record review, GMP documentation package

## FAQ

**Q: Is your facility compliant with pharmaceutical GMP standards?**
A: Yes. Bincai operates under ISO 15378:2017 — the GMP standard specific to primary packaging materials for medicinal products. Our facility maintains traceable batch records, controlled production environment, and documented quality management systems audited annually.

**Q: Can you provide Braille embossing for EU pharmaceutical compliance?**
A: Yes. We offer Braille embossing per EU Directive 2001/83/EC with dot height ≥0.3mm and ±0.05mm tolerance. This is applied inline during production on KBA/Heidelberg presses for accurate placement on pharmaceutical cartons.

**Q: What serialization and track-and-trace capabilities do you offer?**
A: We provide variable data printing — lot numbers, expiry dates, 2D data matrix codes, and serialization — with barcode verification to ISO/IEC 15416 Grade B or higher. Full batch traceability from raw material to finished carton.

**Q: What are the MOQ and lead times for pharmaceutical cartons?**
A: MOQ starts at 2,000 units for standard configurations. Pre-production samples ship within 5-7 days. Production lead time is 12-16 days for standard orders, 18-25 days for complex builds with Braille embossing, serialization, and custom insert trays.

**Q: Do you support child-resistant packaging designs?**
A: Yes. We offer child-resistant (CR) folding carton designs tested per ISO 8317 and US 16 CFR § 1700.20 protocols. Push-and-turn, squeeze-and-slide, and peel-push mechanisms are available for pharmaceutical products requiring CR compliance.
