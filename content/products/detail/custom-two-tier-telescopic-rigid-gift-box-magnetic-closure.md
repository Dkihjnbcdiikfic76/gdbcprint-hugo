## Product Overview

Our custom two-tier telescopic rigid gift box delivers an elevated unboxing experience through its dual-level presentation. The telescopic lid lifts off to reveal two stacked trays — each foam-lined and wrapped in luxury art paper — creating a dramatic reveal for premium products. Constructed from 2.5mm rigid greyboard on our Heidelberg 7+1 UV press with precision die-cutting, this box combines structural integrity with visual sophistication. The soft-touch matte lamination adds a velvet-like tactility while the hidden magnetic closure ensures the lid seats perfectly every time. Perfect for gourmet chocolate collections, corporate gift sets, premium cosmetics duo packs, and luxury confectionery assortments.

## Design Features

- **Telescopic Two-Tier Design**: Upper and lower trays slide apart to present products in a curated, gallery-style layout
- **Hidden Magnetic Closure**: Neodymium magnets embedded in greyboard provide a satisfying snap-close action without visible hardware
- **Soft-Touch Matte Lamination**: Luxurious velvety surface finish that resists fingerprints and scratches
- **Satin Ribbon Pull**: Color-matched satin ribbon for effortless lid removal
- **Foam-Lined Inserts**: Custom die-cut EVA foam inserts cradle each product securely
- **Full CMYK + Spot UV**: Offset printing on Heidelberg 7+1 UV press with closed-loop color management for brand-accurate reproduction
- **Embossing/Debossing**: Custom logo, monogram, or pattern embossing available on lid surface and tray interiors
