## Seven-Stage QC Gates

1. **Incoming Material Inspection** — kraft paper grammage (±5%), rope handle tensile strength (≥ 80N), lamination film quality, cardboard insert caliper
2. **In-Process Printing** — spectrophotometer check every 500 sheets (ΔE ≤ 2.5 on kraft), Heidelberg Prinect registration monitoring
3. **Foil Stamping** — registration ±0.15mm, foil adhesion tape test, stamping temperature and pressure verification per batch
4. **Lamination** — bond strength peel test, visual inspection for bubbles/wrinkles/delamination, gloss/matte level verification
5. **Bag Forming & Handle Attachment** — automatic bag-making machine with inline seam inspection, handle pull test (sampling every 500 units), gusset fold integrity
6. **Finished Goods Audit** — AQL 2.5 (major), AQL 1.5 (critical) per ANSI/ASQ Z1.4, load test (2× rated weight, 24h), visual inspection under D65
7. **Pre-Shipment Inspection** — random sample pull Level II, flat-pack bundle integrity, barcode/label verification, moisture content check (≤ 8%)

## FAQ

**Q:** What is the minimum order quantity for custom printed kraft bags?
A: Standard MOQ is 1,000–2,000 units per design depending on size. Smaller sizes (small/medium) start at 1,000 units. Large and extra-large bags start at 2,000 units. We can accommodate 500 units for repeat customers with existing printing plates.

**Q:** Can you match our brand's specific Pantone color on kraft paper?
A: Yes — we use Pantone spot color matching with X-Rite spectrophotometer verification. Due to the natural brown kraft base tone, the ΔE tolerance is adjusted to ≤ 2.5 (vs. ≤ 2.0 on white stock). We recommend providing a physical Pantone chip and reviewing a pre-production sample to confirm color appearance on kraft.

**Q:** How much weight can the bags hold?
A: Load capacity depends on paper weight and bag size: 150g paper holds up to 3 kg, 180g paper up to 4 kg, 200g paper up to 5 kg. All bags include a reinforced cardboard base insert. For heavy or sharp-edged items, we recommend 200g paper with reinforced side seams. Load testing at 2× rated weight for 24 hours is standard QC protocol.

**Q:** Are the bags available without plastic lamination?
A: Yes — we offer a 100% plastic-free option: uncoated kraft paper with embossed/debossed logos and paper rope handles. This option has a more natural, textured feel and is fully biodegradable. Note that uncoated kraft shows more surface texture variation and has slightly lower print vibrancy than laminated versions.

**Q:** What are the lead times and shipping options?
A: Sample turnaround: 5–7 days with free pre-production samples. Production: 10–14 days for standard bags. Bags ship flat-packed in bundles of 25–50 units for maximum freight efficiency. Sea freight: 15–30 days depending on destination port. Air freight: 3–7 days for urgent orders.
