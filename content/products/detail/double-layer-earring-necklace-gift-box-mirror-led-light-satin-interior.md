Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. manufactures this double-layer earring and necklace gift box with integrated LED-lit mirror — a premium packaging solution for jewelry brands selling coordinated sets. The dual-tier design separates earrings in the upper flocked tray from necklaces in the lower satin-lined compartment, preventing tangling during transit. A built-in LED mirror activates on lid opening, letting customers try on jewelry immediately upon unboxing. Produced on KBA Rapida 105 and Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV presses in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility, each box undergoes Seven-Stage QC inspection with ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certification. Daily output capacity of 1.7 million boxes supports volume orders from 300 to 300,000 units.

## Customization Options

- **Board & Construction**: 157gsm art paper over 2.5mm greyboard body; 2.0mm for lightweight designs
- **Printing**: KBA 1050 4-color offset (CMYK + Pantone spot) + Heidelberg 7+1 UV with scuff-resistant coating
- **Surface Finishes**: Soft-touch velvet lamination, gloss lamination, linen-texture paper wrap, pearlescent coating
- **Foil Stamping**: Gold, silver, rose gold, or holographic foil — ±0.15mm registration on Bobst hot foil press
- **Embossing/Debossing**: Blind deboss for subtle logo, registered emboss with foil overlay for premium branding
- **Interior Configuration**: Upper tray: 4-6 cavity flocked insert for earrings/rings; Lower compartment: satin-lined necklace channel with elastic retention bands
- **LED Mirror Module**: 3× AAA battery powered, auto-on with lid-open switch, 5,000-hour LED lifespan; mirror dimensions 80×60mm
- **Closure**: N52 neodymium magnetic closure with hidden-magnet construction
- **Sizing**: Custom dimensions from 200×150×60mm to 280×200×80mm
