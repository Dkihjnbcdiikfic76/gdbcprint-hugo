The magnetic closure luxury watch display box elevates timepiece presentation with museum-quality lighting and refined materials. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. — a 22-year Foshan paper box specialist with 18,000 sqm across two production facilities — this box wraps 2mm rigid greyboard in premium vegan leather (PU) with a soft-touch matte finish, creating a tactile experience worthy of haute horlogerie. The hidden magnetic closure eliminates visible hardware for a seamless, minimalist exterior, while the LED-illuminated interior — activated automatically upon opening — spotlights the timepiece like a gallery showcase.

Bincai operates KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset and Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV presses for precision logo and branding reproduction, with Bobst SP 102-E die-cutters for flawless dimensional accuracy. Every unit undergoes seven-stage QC inspection and is ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certified.

## Customization Options

- **Exterior Wrap:** Premium vegan leather (PU) in 40+ color options — matte black, cognac brown, midnight navy, burgundy, ivory, forest green. Optional genuine Nappa leather upgrade for ultra-luxury positioning
- **Printing:** Gold or silver hot foil stamping for brand logo. Debossing for understated luxury. Full-color UV digital print for complex brand graphics
- **LED System:** Warm white (3000K) or cool white (5000K) LED strip with magnetic reed switch activation. Battery-powered (2x CR2032, 200+ hours) or USB-C rechargeable module. Optional dimmer control
- **Interior Lining:** Ultra-suede (microfiber) in color-matched or contrasting tones. Optional genuine Alcantara® for automotive-grade luxury
- **Watch Pillow:** Removable suede-covered pillow with elastic retention strap. Customizable firmness — soft for leather straps, firm for metal bracelets
- **Magnetic Closure:** Neodymium magnets embedded within board layers — invisible from exterior. Closure force calibrated to 12-18N for secure hold with effortless one-hand opening
- **Branding:** Embossed or foil-stamped logo on lid exterior and/or interior. Custom-printed warranty card slot and microfiber cleaning cloth pocket
- **Accessories:** Optional drawer compartment for additional straps, spring bar tool, and brand booklet
