## FAQ
**Q: Can the pen slots be customized for different pen diameters?**
A: Yes. We can die-cut grooves to match any pen diameter (8-16mm typical). Elastic bands offer a universal-fit alternative for unknown dimensions.

**Q: What is the minimum order for custom sizes and configurations?**
A: MOQ is 500-1,000 units. Tooling fee for custom die-cut trays is $80-200 (one-time).

**Q: Can you include a matching gift bag or sleeve?**
A: Yes. We offer matching paper sleeves, gift bags with ribbon handles, and outer cardboard mailers as a complete packaging solution.

**Q: Are the magnets strong enough to keep the box closed during shipping?**
A: Our dual N35-N52 neodymium magnets provide 500g+ holding force, tested for drop (1.2m) and vibration (ISTA 1A) to ensure secure closure during transit.

**Q: What exterior materials give the most premium look for executive gifts?**
A: PU leatherette with gold foil debossing is our most popular premium choice — it delivers a genuine leather aesthetic at a paper-box price point. Textured linen with blind embossing offers a sophisticated minimal look favored by European luxury brands. Carbon-fiber pattern wrap gives a modern, technical feel ideal for high-end engineering and tech company corporate gifts.

**Q: Can the box be designed to hold a pen set (fountain pen + rollerball + cartridge)?**
A: Yes. We can design multi-slot trays for 2, 3, or 4 writing instruments plus accessories. Common configurations include: fountain pen + rollerball, pen + mechanical pencil set, or pen + ink cartridge + converter. Each slot is individually die-cut to the specific instrument dimensions.

**Q: What lead time should I expect for a custom corporate pen box order?**
A: Standard lead time is 15-20 working days from artwork approval. Sample production is 5-7 days. For large corporate orders (5,000+ units) during Q4 gifting season (Oct-Dec), we recommend ordering 30-45 days in advance to secure production capacity.

## Seven-Stage QC Gates for Luxury Pen Display Boxes

Every pen display box undergoes seven precision quality checkpoints — **luxury presentation quality at scale**:

| Gate | Stage | Inspection Focus | Equipment / Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| **QC1** | Raw Material Inbound | Greyboard caliper ±0.1mm, art paper GSM ±3%, satin color lot consistency, magnet grade certification (N35-N52), FSC CoC docs | Vernier caliper, spectrophotometer, gauss meter |
| **QC2** | Offset Print & Lamination | Registration ±0.15mm, ΔE ≤ 2.0, lamination adhesion (cross-hatch test), matte/gloss consistency | X-Rite eXact, cross-hatch tape test, gloss meter |
| **QC3** | Hot Foil Stamping | Foil adhesion (tape test), edge sharpness, temperature 80-120°C consistency, no halo or ghosting | Bobst SP 102 BMA, visual under 5000K + 10x loupe |
| **QC4** | Die-Cutting & Wrapping | Cut precision ±0.3mm, paper wrap seam ≤0.2mm, no air bubbles, corner fold sharpness, satin lining flat (zero wrinkles) | Bobst SP 102-E, visual inspection station |
| **QC5** | Magnet Installation | Magnet recess depth ±0.1mm, polarity alignment (attract, not repel), holding force ≥500g, no magnet rattle | Gauss meter, force gauge, polarity checker |
| **QC6** | Pen Slot Fit Test | Groove width ±0.2mm, elastic tension consistent, pen insertion/removal force 200-400g, satin lining adhesion | Physical fit test with reference pen, force gauge |
| **QC7** | Final Audit & Packaging | Full open/close cycle (20x), ISTA 1A drop test, barcode scan (grade B+), logo alignment, AQL 2.5 Level II | Drop tester, barcode verifier, certificate of conformance |

## Sustainability & Materials Responsibility

Bincai luxury pen display boxes combine premium presentation with responsible manufacturing:

- **FSC Certified Greyboard**: 1000-1400gsm greyboard core sourced from FSC Chain-of-Custody certified mills — full documentation provided for corporate ESG reporting
- **Water-Based Adhesives**: All rigid box assembly uses PVA water-based adhesives (zero VOC emissions) — safe for workers and environment
