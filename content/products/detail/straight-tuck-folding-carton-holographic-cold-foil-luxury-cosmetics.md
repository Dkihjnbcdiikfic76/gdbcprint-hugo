## Product Overview

The Straight-Tuck Folding Carton with Holographic Cold Foil represents the cutting edge of folding carton aesthetics — where precision offset printing meets the iridescent shimmer of in-line cold foil transfer. Designed for luxury cosmetics, niche skincare, premium fragrance, and beauty brands that demand packaging as exceptional as their formulations, this carton delivers a dynamic visual presence on retail shelves and in unboxing social media content.

The holographic cold foil effect is achieved in-line on our Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV press using Kurz Luxor holographic foil — the foil is transferred during the printing pass with ±0.15mm registration against CMYK artwork. The iridescent rainbow shifts from silver through blue, pink, and gold depending on viewing angle and lighting conditions, creating a living, responsive surface that no static print can replicate. Because the cold foil process is in-line (not a separate offline stamping step), production cost is typically 25–40% lower than traditional hot foil stamping while delivering comparable visual impact.

The straight-tuck end (STE) construction provides clean, secure closure with friction-fit dust flaps that present a finished, premium look on opening. All production runs through our Bobst Visioncut 106 LER die-cutter (±0.1mm accuracy) and Bobst Expertfold 110 A2 folder-gluer, ensuring precise folds, clean glue lines, and consistent assembly across production runs of 50,000+ units. Board stock is 350–400gsm CCNB (FSC-certified recycled) or SBS (virgin fiber, 90+ brightness) printed with full CMYK + Pantone spot colors.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How does in-line cold foil compare to traditional hot foil stamping in terms of visual impact and durability?**
A: In-line cold foil offers comparable reflectivity and brilliance to hot foil at 25–40% lower cost because it's applied during the printing pass rather than a separate offline operation. The holographic rainbow effect is actually a cold foil specialty — it cannot be achieved with hot foil stamps. Durability is excellent: the foil is sealed under a UV-cured overcoat or aqueous coating that protects against scuffing. For applications requiring deep tactile emboss + foil (where the foil must stretch into a 3D relief), hot foil on our Bobst SP 102 BMA/E is the better choice because the heated die physically stretches the foil into recessed areas. We offer both technologies and can recommend based on your design.

**Q: Can the holographic effect be applied only to specific areas (spot foil) rather than full coverage?**
A: Yes, spot cold foil application is a standard capability on our Heidelberg CD 102 7+1 UV. The holographic foil can be applied to specific design elements — logo only, brand name, decorative borders, or geometric patterns — while the remaining surface shows CMYK artwork on the board itself. Registration accuracy is ±0.15mm against the printed artwork, so even fine typography and thin rule lines can be foil-highlighted. The contrast between matte CMYK and shimmering holographic foil on the same surface creates dramatic visual depth.

**Q: What board weight should I choose for a 30ml serum bottle box vs. a multi-product gift set?**
A: For a single 30ml serum bottle, 350gsm CCNB or SBS provides adequate structural support. For a 3–5 piece gift set (multiple bottles or compacts totaling 200–400g), upgrade to 400gsm for added rigidity and crush resistance during transit. For luxury unboxing, 400gsm SBS with soft-touch lamination gives the most premium hand-feel — the heavier board has a more substantial presence, and the suede-like soft-touch finish signals quality before the box is opened. We can produce samples at multiple board weights for your evaluation.

**Q: What is the MOQ for holographic cold foil cartons, and what factors affect pricing?**
A: MOQ is 1,000 units, which makes this technology accessible for indie beauty brands and limited-edition runs. Key price drivers: (1) board weight (350gsm → 400gsm adds ~8%), (2) foil coverage (spot → full coverage adds ~15%), (3) surface finish complexity (aqueous → soft-touch lamination + spot UV adds ~20%), and (4) structural complexity (standard STE → auto-lock bottom with inserts adds ~12%). At 10,000+ units, unit cost drops approximately 30–40% from MOQ pricing due to make-ready amortization.

**Q: Is the holographic cold foil carton suitable for products stored in humid bathroom environments?**
A: The carton's structural integrity in humid environments depends on surface finish choice. Gloss lamination (BOPP film) provides a complete moisture barrier and is recommended for bathroom-stored products. Soft-touch lamination also offers good moisture resistance. Aqueous coating alone provides less moisture protection — for bathroom storage, pair it with SBS board (which has better wet strength than CCNB recycled board). We recommend gloss lamination + SBS 400gsm for products expected to sit on bathroom counters. For extreme humidity (tropical bathrooms without ventilation), consider adding a UV-cured overcoat on top of the lamination.
