## Product Overview

The custom folding paper box with PVC window display and food-safe construction is engineered for bakeries, confectioners, tea brands, and ready-to-eat food products. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing (founded 2003, Foshan, ISO 9001 & FSC certified), this folding carton uses food-grade inks on 250-400gsm coated board, with a crystal-clear PVC window that lets customers see the product. Our 10,000 sqm factory + 8,000 sqm smart factory expansion (2025) delivers 1.7 million boxes daily, with full in-house printing, die-cutting, window-patching, and gluing lines.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: KBA 1050 4-color offset — precision CMYK with Pantone spot color capability
- **Board Grades**: 250gsm/300gsm/350gsm/400gsm C1S or C2S art paper; SBS (solid bleached sulfate) for premium food
- **Inks**: Soy-based, low-migration, EN 71-3 & EU 1935/2004 compliant for food contact
- **Coatings**: Water-based aqueous (food-safe), UV-cured (non-contact side only)
- **Window Film**: 0.2-0.4mm clear PVC, anti-fog PET, or compostable PLA bio-film
- **Window Patching**: Hot-melt or cold-glue machine-applied, ±0.5mm placement precision
- **Daily Output**: 1.7M color boxes + 220,000+ gift boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
