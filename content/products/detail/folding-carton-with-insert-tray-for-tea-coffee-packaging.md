## Product Description

Our custom folding carton with insert tray is purpose-built for tea and coffee packaging — keeping sachets, loose-leaf pouches, tea bags, coffee capsules, and drip bags neatly organized and protected. The precision die-cut insert tray holds individual items securely in place, preventing movement during shipping while creating an elegant retail presentation. Every carton is printed on our KBA 1050 4-color offset press for rich, food-safe CMYK graphics and finished on the Heidelberg 7+1 UV line for optional spot UV, foil accents, and protective coatings.

## Features

- **Custom Insert Tray:** Die-cut cardboard tray engineered to your product dimensions — single-level, multi-level, and compartmentalized layouts for tea bags, coffee sachets, capsules, or loose-leaf pouches.
- **Flat-Shipping Folding Design:** Cartons ship flat and assemble quickly with tuck-top, auto-lock bottom, or glue-flap closures — reducing freight volume by up to 70% versus rigid boxes.
- **CMYK Offset Printing:** KBA 1050 4-color press delivers sharp, food-appealing graphics at up to 200 LPI with consistent color across production runs.
- **Heidelberg 7+1 UV Finishing:** Inline spot UV gloss, matte aqueous coating, or soft-touch lamination applied in a single pass for premium shelf presence.
- **Food-Safe Materials:** FSC certified paperboard, soy-based inks, and water-based coatings available for direct food contact applications.
- **Window Patching Options:** Die-cut windows with PET or PLA film let customers see the product inside while keeping contents protected.
- **Value-Added Finishes:** Hot foil stamping (gold, silver, copper), embossing/debossing, and custom insert printing for branded unboxing experiences.
