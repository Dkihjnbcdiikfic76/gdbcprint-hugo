Custom corrugated mailer box with integrated bubble lining for glassware and fragile item shipping — manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). The design combines double-wall B-flute corrugated board with an integrated bubble-film liner for dual-layer impact protection, eliminating the need for separate bubble wrap. Ideal for direct-to-consumer wine glass, ceramic mug, candle jar, and fragile decor shipping. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color presses across 10,000 sqm factory floor. Daily output 1.7 million boxes.

## Applications
- Wine glass and stemware e-commerce shipping
- Ceramic mug and pottery direct-to-consumer packaging
- Candle jar and glass container shipping
- Fragile home decor and figurine mailers
- Laboratory glassware and scientific instrument shipping

## Customization Options
- Integrated bubble lining: 5mm–10mm bubble film bonded to inner walls
- Double-wall B-flute or E-flute corrugated for crush resistance
- Self-locking bottom for quick assembly without tape
- Tear-strip opening for recipient-friendly unboxing
- CMYK offset printing up to 4 colors on kraft or white outer liner
- Custom size from 100×100×100mm single-glass to 400×300×300mm gift-set
- Perforated return strip for easy returns
- Edge crush test (ECT) rating: 32–44 depending on board grade
