## Quality Assurance

1. **Material Inspection** — Vegan leather tensile strength (ASTM D412), abrasion resistance (ASTM D3884), colorfastness to light (ISO 105-B02)
2. **Board Lamination** — Bond strength test, 100% visual inspection for bubbling/delamination
3. **Magnet Verification** — Pull force testing (target: 8-15N per magnet pair), polarity check, pocket depth gauge
4. **Foil/Hot Stamp QC** — ±0.15mm registration measured, adhesion tape test (ASTM D3359), 100% visual under D65 lighting
5. **Hinge Cycle Testing** — 10,000 open/close cycles on sample batch, torque measurement at 5,000 and 10,000 cycles
6. **Assembly QC** — Velvet flock adhesion, pillow insert fit, magnetic closure alignment, lid-to-base gap ≤ 0.5mm
7. **Pre-Shipment** — AQL 2.5 (major), AQL 1.0 (minor) per ISO 2859-1 Level II
