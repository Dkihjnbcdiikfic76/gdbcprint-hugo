Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. produces this tuck-top auto-bottom folding carton specifically engineered for cosmetic cream jars — 30ml, 50ml, and 100ml formats. ## Customization Options

- **Board Grade**: 350gsm CCNB (standard, good print surface); 350gsm SBS (premium, bright white); 400gsm for heavier jars
- **Printing**: KBA 1050 4-color offset (CMYK + Pantone) + Heidelberg 7+1 UV with inline aqueous coating
- **Hot Foil Stamping**: Gold, silver, rose gold, copper foil on Bobst hot foil press — ±0.15mm registration on brand logo, product name, and decorative accents
- **Spot UV**: Selective high-gloss varnish on key design elements — contrasts with matte background for premium tactile effect
- **Embossing/Debossing**: Blind deboss for subtle brand texture; registered emboss with foil overlay
- **Base Style**: Auto-lock bottom (standard, fastest assembly); crash-lock bottom (stronger, for heavier jars); straight tuck with glue (economy)
- **Window Options**: Die-cut window with PET film for product visibility; full-panel window
- **Sizing**: Custom dimensions for 15ml to 100ml jar formats

Bincai's folding carton expertise comes from 23 years of supplying high-speed cosmetic filling lines across Asia, Europe, and North America. For cosmetic brands, this means faster time-to-market with cartons that run flawlessly on automated filling equipment.
