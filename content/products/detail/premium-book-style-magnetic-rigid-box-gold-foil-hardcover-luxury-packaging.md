## Product Overview

Bincai's book-style magnetic rigid box replicates the tactile elegance of a fine hardcover book — complete with spine debossing, gold foil accents, and a satisfying magnetic closure that snaps shut like closing a treasured volume. The dual-panel magnetic flap opens flat to 180°, revealing a velvet-lined interior tray. Hand-assembled with precision book-binding techniques in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility. 22 years of luxury packaging expertise.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: KBA 1050 4-color offset — vibrant CMYK on book-cover wrap
- **UV Enhancement**: Heidelberg 7+1 UV — selective spot UV on title/logo elements
- **Board**: 2.0mm greyboard core (book-board weight), 157gsm art paper wrap
- **Spine**: 15-30mm width with custom debossed title, ribbon headband detail
- **Interior**: Velvet-lined tray (2mm electrostatic flocking), 16 color options
- **Closure**: Dual concealed N35 neodymium magnets in front flap corners
- **Finishing**: Gold foil hot stamping on spine + cover, matte lamination
- **Daily Output**: 220,000+ rigid boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody
