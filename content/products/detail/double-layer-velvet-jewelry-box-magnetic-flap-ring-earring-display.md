## Product Overview

## Quality Assurance

| Test | Method | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| **Ring Slot Fit** | Calibrated ring mandrel (US sizes 4–13, EU 46–72), insertion/extraction test per slot | Smooth insertion with gentle friction hold; ring does not fall out when tray inverted at 45° |
| **Velvet Pile Density** | Gravimetric: flock weight per unit area, 5 samples per batch | ≥ 180 g/m² for 1.2mm pile; ±5% batch-to-batch |
| **Flocking Abrasion** | Martindale Abrasion Tester, 12 kPa pressure, dry | 10,000+ cycles without bare-board exposure; ≤ 10% pile loss at 20,000 cycles |
| **Flocking Adhesion** | Cross-hatch test (ISO 2409), 2mm spacing, 3M 610 tape | Classification 0 or 1 — no flaking along cuts or at intersections |
| **Magnet Pull Force** | Digital force gauge, perpendicular pull at 2mm gap | N42 10×5mm: ≥ 4.0 N per magnet pair |
| **Magnetic Flap Alignment** | Digital caliper measurement of flap-to-body gap (closed) | ±1.0mm uniform gap; flap seats flush without rocking |
| **Tray Fit** | Insertion/extraction cycling, manual + automated jig | Tray slides in/out without binding; clearance 0.5–1.0mm on all sides |
| **Velvet Color Consistency** | Spectrophotometer, CIELAB ΔE 2000, D65 illuminant | ΔE ≤ 1.5 batch-to-batch for standard colors; ΔE ≤ 2.0 for custom Pantone |
| **Print Registration** | 10× loupe, gripper and tail marks | ±0.15mm CMYK-to-CMYK; ±0.2mm foil-to-print |
| **Foil Adhesion** | 3M 610 tape test on stamped foil | ≤ 5% foil lift; no edge peeling visible at 3× magnification |
| **Drop Test** | ISTA 1A: 10 drops from 760mm (corners, edges, faces) | Box intact; magnet engaged; tray seated; velvet uncracked; contents secure |
| **Tarnish Prevention** | Silver coupon test (ASTM B809): 24 hours at 50°C / 85% RH inside closed box | No visible tarnish on silver coupon (when anti-tarnish liner specified) |
| **Final Inspection** | AQL 2.5 Level II (normal), special Level S-3 for magnet/fit/closure | 100% functional check on magnetic closure, tray fit, and velvet coverage; dimensional spot-check every 100 units |

## FAQ

**Q: What ring sizes do the standard slots accommodate?**
A: Our standard 16mm ring slots accommodate US sizes 4–9 (EU 46–60) comfortably. We also offer 20mm wide slots for larger rings (US 10–13, EU 62–72). Custom slot widths and shapes are available — send us your ring size distribution and we'll engineer the perfect fit.

**Q: Will the velvet shed fibers onto the jewelry?**
A: No — our electrostatic nylon flocking process uses a high-strength water-based acrylic binder (formaldehyde-free) that achieves a pile pull-out resistance exceeding 1.5 N per fiber tuft. The velvet is tested for 10,000+ insertion cycles in our Martindale abrasion tester without bare-board exposure.

**Q: Can you do custom colors beyond the standard options?**
A: Yes! We Pantone-match velvet flocking to any color in the formula guide. Custom color MOQ is 1000 units, with a one-time $150 color-matching fee (waived on orders over 5000 units). The lead time for a custom color sample is 10–12 working days.

**Q: Does the magnetic closure keep the box securely shut during shipping?**
A: Absolutely. The N42 neodymium magnets (4.0+ N pull force per pair) hold the flap firmly closed. We ISTA 1A drop-test every design from 760mm on all corners, edges, and faces. For e-commerce shipping where boxes may experience rough handling, we offer N52 upgraded magnets with 5.8+ N pull force.

**Q: Can the lower compartment be customized for specific jewelry pieces?**
A: Yes! We CNC-machine custom EVA foam inserts for the lower compartment to hold specific necklace chains, bracelet forms, watch cushions, or brooch pins. We can also add certificate pockets, care-card slots, or a separate hinged compartment for cleaning cloths.

**Q: What's the difference between your velvet and cheaper alternatives?**
A: Many factories use spray-on flocking (flocking fibers + adhesive sprayed onto board), which results in uneven density, rapid shedding, and a harsh feel. We use electrostatic flocking — nylon fibers are charged and oriented vertically into the adhesive by an electric field, producing dense, uniform coverage with a soft, plush hand-feel that lasts for years, not months.

**Q: How are these boxes packaged for international shipping?**
A: Each box is individually poly-bagged, then nested in export-grade double-wall corrugated master cartons. Trays are packed separately to prevent compression of ring slots during transit. All pallets are heat-treated ISPM 15 compliant. We can also provide flat-packed boxes (customer assembles trays) for maximum container efficiency on large orders.

**Q: What certifications do you hold?**
A: ISO 9001:2015 (quality management since 2018), FSC Chain-of-Custody (responsible forestry), High-Tech Enterprise (Guangdong provincial), and we comply with REACH, RoHS, and EN 71-3 (toy safety — relevant for children's jewelry packaging).
