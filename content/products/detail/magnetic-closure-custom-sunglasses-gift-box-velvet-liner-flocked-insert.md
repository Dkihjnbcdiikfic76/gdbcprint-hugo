## Customization Options

- **Board & Construction**: 157gsm C1S art paper over 2.5mm greyboard (standard); upgrade to 3.0mm greyboard for heavier frames, or 1.5mm for slim-profile designs
- **Printing**: KBA 1050 4-color offset (CMYK + spot colors); Heidelberg 7+1 UV with instant-cure aqueous coating for scuff resistance
- **Surface Finishes**: Matte lamination, gloss lamination, soft-touch velvet lamination, spot UV varnish
- **Foil Stamping**: Hot foil in gold, silver, rose gold, copper, or holographic — ±0.15mm registration via Bobst hot foil press
- **Embossing/Debossing**: Blind emboss, registered emboss, or multi-level sculpted emboss with foil overlay
- **Magnet Configuration**: N52 neodymium magnets, 8mm or 10mm diameter, 1 or 2 pairs; hidden-magnet construction with seamless exterior
- **Interior Options**: Flocked PET insert tray (standard), molded EVA foam, die-cut velvet-covered foam, satin-ribbon pull tab
- **Sizing**: Custom dimensions from 160×60×40mm (standard sunglasses) to oversized 200×90×60mm (aviator/oversized frames)
