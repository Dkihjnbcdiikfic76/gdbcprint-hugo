## Product Overview

Bincai's corrugated book wrap mailer eliminates the two biggest pain points of e-commerce fulfillment: taping boxes and customers struggling to open them. The pressure-sensitive hot-melt self-sealing adhesive strip bonds instantly on contact — press the flap down and it's sealed, no water-activated tape, no packing tape dispenser, no labor. At the receiving end, the integrated tear-tape opening delivers a clean, frustration-free unboxing in one pull — your customer never reaches for scissors or a box cutter.

The adjustable-depth design (patented crease system with 5mm depth increments) allows one SKU to accommodate varying product thicknesses — from a single paperback (15mm) to a hardcover trilogy set (60mm). B-flute corrugated construction (2.5mm thickness, 125gsm virgin kraft liners) provides 32+ lb/in ECT crush resistance — tested and validated to ISTA 1A standards. FBA-compliant, Amazon-ready.

Manufactured on our fully automatic corrugated line (corrugator → flexo printer → slotter → die-cutter → auto-gluer) in our 10,000 sqm primary factory (+8,000 sqm smart factory) in Foshan. Full-color offset labels are produced in-house on our KBA 1050 4-color offset press and Heidelberg 7+1 UV press — eliminating outsourced printing, reducing lead times, and delivering premium CMYK graphics directly on your mailer. 22 years of corrugated engineering — Bincai has produced over 1.2 billion corrugated mailers since 2003.

## FAQ

**Q: How does the self-sealing adhesive perform in extreme temperatures?**
A: Our hot-melt pressure-sensitive adhesive is validated from −20°C to +60°C through 3-cycle freeze-thaw testing. In cold climates, apply firm hand pressure for 3 seconds to ensure full bond activation. In hot/humid climates (>40°C, >80% RH), the adhesive maintains bond strength with zero transfer or oozing. For extreme conditions, we offer a double-strip or full-coverage adhesive configuration for additional security.

**Q: Can the tear strip be customized with my brand's color?**
A: Yes. Standard tear strip is clear polypropylene. Custom colors (red, blue, green, gold, black, brand Pantone) are available at 5,000+ unit minimum with a one-time color matching fee. Custom-printed tear strips ("OPEN HERE" or brand message) available at 10,000+ units.

**Q: What's the maximum product weight this mailer supports?**
A: B-flute (standard): up to 2.5 kg. E-flute: up to 1.5 kg. BC double-wall: up to 8 kg. For heavier products, we recommend adding EVA foam corner blocks or switching to a full corrugated shipping box with foam insert. All weight ratings assume the product is centered and snug within the adjustable depth — loose products require additional void-fill.
