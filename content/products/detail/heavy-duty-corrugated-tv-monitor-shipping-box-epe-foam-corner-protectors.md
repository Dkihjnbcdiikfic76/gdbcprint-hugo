## Product Overview

Bincai's heavy-duty corrugated TV/monitor shipping box is engineered for the most demanding electronics logistics. Double-wall BC-flute construction delivers exceptional crush resistance (ECT ≥7.5 kN/m), while custom die-cut EPE foam corner blocks cradle the display at its four most vulnerable points. Top and bottom cushion pads absorb vertical impact forces during transit. The self-locking bottom eliminates the need for additional assembly labor, and reinforced H-tape closure ensures the box stays sealed through multi-leg distribution. ISTA 3A certified for parcel delivery and LTL freight environments.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Flute Construction**: Double-wall BC-flute (B-flute inner 2.5mm + C-flute outer 3.6mm), combined caliper ~6mm
- **Board Grade**: Kraft liner 200# / 200# / 200# (42 ECT rated)
- **Edge Crush Test**: ≥7.5 kN/m (44 lbs/in) — suitable for stacking in warehouse rack systems
- **Burst Strength**: ≥1,600 kPa (Mullen) — puncture-resistant for mixed-load shipping
- **Foam Protection**: Die-cut EPE (expanded polyethylene) foam corner blocks, density 28 kg/m³, custom contoured to TV bezel profile
- **Closure**: Self-locking bottom flaps + reinforced 72mm H-tape top seal
- **Printing**: 1-2 color flexo (standard) or full-color litho-laminate with UV coating
- **Daily Output**: 1.5M+ corrugated boxes
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, ISTA 3A drop-tested
