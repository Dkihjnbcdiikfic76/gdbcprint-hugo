The telescopic rigid gift box with gold foil monogram window is designed for luxury apparel brands — from premium shirts and silk scarves to cashmere sweaters and designer accessories. - **Press**: Heidelberg CD 102 7+1 UV for exterior wrap with soft-touch coating; KBA Rapida 105 for volume

- **Board**: 2.5mm greyboard for body, 3.0mm for larger sizes
- **Wrap**: 157gsm art paper with soft-touch aqueous or UV coating
- **Gold Foil**: Hot foil stamping, temperature-controlled for consistent transfer
- **Window Bonding**: Ultrasonic or solvent-free adhesive PET film lamination
