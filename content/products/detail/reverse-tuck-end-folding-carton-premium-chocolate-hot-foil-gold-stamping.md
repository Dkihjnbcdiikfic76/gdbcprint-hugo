## Product Overview

Bincai's reverse tuck end (RTE) folding carton delivers premium chocolate presentation at volume-friendly economics. The RTE structure — with top and bottom flaps tucking from opposite ends — provides secure closure while enabling flat-packed shipping for efficient logistics. Double-sided CMYK printing on 350gsm C2S (coated two sides) art board, enhanced with matte lamination and hot gold foil stamping. Manufactured on KBA 1050 4-color offset presses in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility. 22 years of folding carton expertise.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: KBA 1050 4-color offset — high-registration double-sided CMYK printing
- **UV Enhancement**: Heidelberg 7+1 UV — selective spot UV on brand logo area
- **Board**: 350gsm C2S (coated two sides) solid bleached sulphate (SBS) board
- **Lamination**: Full-coverage matte film, 18-micron BOPP
- **Foil**: Hot gold foil stamping on front panel — product name + logo
- **Structure**: Reverse tuck end (RTE) — top flap tucks from rear, bottom from front
- **Optional**: Auto-lock bottom conversion for heavier products
- **Daily Output**: 1.7 million color boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody
