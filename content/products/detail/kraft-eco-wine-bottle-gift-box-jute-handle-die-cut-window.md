## Seven-Stage Quality Control Gates

| Gate | Stage | Inspection | Defect Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | Raw Material Incoming | Kraft board caliper (±5%), moisture content (6-8%), FSC cert documentation, jute tensile strength ≥15kg | Board caliper out-of-spec → batch reject; FSC cert expired → supplier hold |
| **G2** | Pre-Press & Plate Making | Plate inspection at 10× magnification, dot gain calibration, color target ΔE ≤2.0 under D65 | Plate defect >25µm → re-make; ΔE >2.5 → ink formula adjustment |
| **G3** | Printing (KBA 1050 + Heidelberg 7+1 UV) | Color bar every 500 sheets, registration ±0.1mm, ink density within ±0.05, UV coating thickness gauge | Registration shift >0.2mm → press stop; ink density drift → fountain adjustment |
| **G4** | Die-Cutting | Blade sharpness check every 5,000 impressions, window alignment ±1mm, handle slot clean-cut (no fiber tear), scoring depth 50% of board thickness | Blade dulling → change die; window misalignment >1.5mm → setup correction |
| **G5** | Gluing (Water-Based PVA) | Bond strength ≥4N/15mm, open time 30-60s, no squeeze-out, auto-bottom locking force test | Bond <3N/15mm → glue temperature/pressure check; squeeze-out → nozzle calibration |
| **G6** | Jute Handle Assembly | Handle insertion (manual+fixture guided), pull test 15kg (every 200th unit), knot security check, handle slot reinforcement tab integrity | Pull failure <12kg → assembly line stop; knot slip → re-training |
| **G7** | Final QC & Packing | ISTA 1A drop test (sampling), visual AQL 2.5 Level II, window film clarity, barcode scan, count accuracy, flat-fold dimension check | Drop failure → full batch quarantine; visual defect rate >4% → 100% inspection |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the kraft board truly eco-friendly?**
A: Yes. We use FSC certified kraft board with 30-80% post-consumer recycled content. Our standard formulation is 80% recycled fiber with virgin kraft top liner for print quality. Soy-based inks and water-based adhesives ensure the entire box is recyclable in standard paper streams. For fully compostable requirements, we offer PLA window film certified to EN 13432.

**Q: Can the jute handle support a full wine bottle?**
A: Yes. Each jute twine handle is pull-tested to 15kg — well above the 1.5kg weight of a standard 750ml wine bottle. The handle passes through reinforced die-cut slots with folded kraft reinforcement tabs that distribute weight across the box structure, not just the die-cut hole edges.

**Q: What are the MOQ and lead times?**
A: Standard MOQ is 500-1,000 units depending on complexity. Lead time is 12-18 working days for standard configurations, 15-22 working days for custom sizes or premium window films. Rush orders available at 7-10 working days with surcharge.

**Q: Can you print inside the box as well?**
A: Yes. We offer 1-color or 2-color inside printing for brand messaging, vineyard story, tasting notes, or care instructions. Inside printing adds 2-3 working days and a plate charge.

**Q: Does the window film come in compostable options?**
A: Yes. We offer PLA (polylactic acid) window film certified compostable to EN 13432 standards — degrades in industrial composting facilities within 90 days. Also available: rPET (recycled PET) for brand recycling programs, or open die-cut window with no film for maximum sustainability.

**Q: What bottle sizes does this box accommodate?**
A: Standard 750ml wine bottle (Ø75-80mm × 290-330mm tall). We also manufacture for 375ml half-bottles, 1.5L magnums, champagne/sparkling wine bottles (wider diameter), and custom spirit bottle shapes. Each size requires its own custom die, included in the setup cost.

**Q: Can you match my vineyard's specific kraft shade?**
A: Yes. We can dye-match kraft board to your brand's shade — natural light kraft, medium brown kraft, dark kraft, or even black kraft. Dye-matching adds 3-5 working days for sampling and a small color-matching fee.
