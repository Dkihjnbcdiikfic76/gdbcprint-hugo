## Quality Control Gates
1. **Paper Roll Inspection** — Burst strength (≥200 PSI), edge crush test (ECT ≥32 lbs/in), moisture content
2. **Corrugator Monitoring** — Flute height, bond strength, caliper inline on BHS digital control
3. **Print Registration** — Flexo registration within ±1mm, color density to Pantone standards
4. **Die-Cutting** — Score depth and slot accuracy, ±0.5mm tolerance
5. **Insulation Assembly** — Liner adhesion, gel pack fit, tear-strip perforation test
6. **Cold Chain Validation** — ISTA 7E thermal profiling, 24–48 hour temperature hold test
7. **Final QC** — AQL 2.5 Level II, drop test (1.2m, 6 faces), compression test
