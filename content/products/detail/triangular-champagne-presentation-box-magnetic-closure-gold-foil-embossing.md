## Product Overview

Bincai's triangular champagne presentation box transforms the classic bottle gift into a sculptural statement. The three-sided rigid construction with magnetic side-seal closure creates an elegant unboxing ritual, while gold foil embossing on the matte-laminated exterior catches light from every angle. A custom die-cut EVA foam cradle securely holds standard 750ml champagne or sparkling wine bottles. Flat-pack shippable design minimizes freight costs without compromising the premium presentation. Built with 23 years of rigid box manufacturing expertise in our 18,000 sqm ISO 9001 & FSC certified facility.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: KBA 1050 4-color offset — precise color registration on all panels
- **UV Enhancement**: Heidelberg 7+1 UV press — selective spot UV on brand elements
- **Board**: 2mm greyboard core, 157gsm art paper wrap with matte lamination
- **Interior**: Die-cut high-density EVA foam cradle with black velvet flocking overlay
- **Closure**: Magnetic side-seal — invisible magnets embedded in board edges
- **Exterior Finish**: Matte lamination base + gold foil embossed logo/pattern on lid
- **Shape**: Triangular (3-sided), flat-pack shippable pre-assembly
- **Daily Output**: 220,000+ rigid boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody
