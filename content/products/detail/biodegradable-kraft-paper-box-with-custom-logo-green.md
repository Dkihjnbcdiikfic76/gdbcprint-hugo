## Seven-Stage Quality Control Gates

| Gate | Stage | Inspection | Defect Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | Raw Material Incoming | Caliper, moisture content, FSC CoC documentation, kraft fiber grade | Moisture >8% or COA missing → batch quarantine |
| **G2** | Pre-Press & Plate Making | Plate registration, ink viscosity (soy-based), color proof against PMS target | ΔE >3.0 on proof → plate recalibration |
| **G3** | Printing (KBA 1050) | Print registration ±0.15mm, ink adhesion (tape test), color consistency ΔE ≤3.0 | Registration drift >0.2mm → press stop & recalibrate |
| **G4** | Die-Cutting & Creasing | Dimensional accuracy ±0.5mm, crease depth 50–60% of board thickness, edge cleanliness | Cut-through or >0.5mm deviation → die replacement |
| **G5** | Gluing (Water-Based PVA) | Bond strength ≥4N/15mm, open time 30-60s, no squeeze-out, seam alignment | Bond <3N → glue temperature/adjustment check |
| **G6** | Assembly & Finishing | Handle attachment pull test ≥8kg, window film clarity, closure function (tuck/magnetic), surface inspection | Handle failure <5kg or window haze → line stop |
| **G7** | Final QC & Packing | ISTA 1A drop test (sampling), visual cosmetic AQL 2.5 Level II, barcode scan, count accuracy, biodegradability cert spot-check | Drop failure → full batch quarantine & root cause |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the kraft paper box actually biodegradable?**
A: Yes. Our standard kraft boxes use unbleached kraft paperboard, soy-based inks, and water-based PVA adhesives. The complete box achieves ≥90% biodegradation within 180 days per EN 13432 and ASTM D6400 standards when processed in industrial composting facilities. We can provide third-party test certificates on request.

**Q: Can I get 100% recycled kraft board?**
A: Absolutely. We offer 85–100% post-consumer recycled (PCR) kraft board options. The 100% PCR board has a slightly more textured surface than virgin kraft but prints beautifully with soy-based inks. FSC Recycled certification is available for full recycled-content chain-of-custody.

**Q: What if I need a plastic window for product visibility?**
A: We offer PLA (polylactic acid) film windows — certified compostable per ASTM D6400 — as a plastic-free alternative to traditional PET/PVC windows. PLA windows add approximately $0.03–0.06/unit and have excellent clarity comparable to PET. For brands that can accept a PET window, we use recycled PET (rPET) options.

**Q: How does the kraft box hold up in shipping?**
A: Kraft paperboard at 350–450gsm provides excellent structural integrity for e-commerce shipping. For heavier products or international freight, we recommend kraft-wrapped greyboard (1.5–2.5mm rigid construction) which offers the eco aesthetic with the crush resistance of a rigid box. All designs are ISTA 1A drop-tested before production approval.

**Q: What is the minimum order for custom-printed kraft boxes?**
A: MOQ is 500 units for standard 1–2 color soy-ink printing. Complex multi-color designs (3–4 color CMYK on kraft) typically require 1,000+ units to absorb plate-making costs. We accept trial orders as low as 100 units for unprinted kraft samples.

**Q: Can the kraft box be used for food packaging?**
A: Yes. Our food-grade kraft boxes use FDA 21 CFR 176 compliant paperboard (indirect food contact) and soy-based inks. For direct food contact, we offer additional barrier coatings and liners. Full documentation including FDA and EU 1935/2004 compliance statements is provided with every food-grade order.
