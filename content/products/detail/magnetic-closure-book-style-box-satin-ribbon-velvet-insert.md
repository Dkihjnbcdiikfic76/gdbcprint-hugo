## Product Overview

The Bincai Magnetic Closure Book-Style Box combines the elegance of a hardcover book with the security of embedded neodymium magnets and the soft luxury of a satin ribbon tie. Designed for premium corporate gifts, stationery sets, and luxury accessory presentation, each box is built on 2.0mm-3.0mm greyboard wrapped in 157gsm art paper with a velvet-flocked insert tray. Produced at our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility running KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, Bincai delivers over 220,000+ gift boxes daily with ISO 9001 and FSC certifications.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Construction**: 2.0-3.0mm greyboard book-style shell with spine hinge, wrapped in 157gsm art paper
- **Magnet System**: 2× N42 neodymium disc magnets (10mm ∅) embedded in front and back panels
- **Closure Force**: 3.0-4.5 N/cm² — secure snap without over-closure
- **Ribbon**: 25mm satin ribbon tie (color-matched to box), sewn into spine
- **Interior**: Die-cut velvet-flocked insert tray with product-specific cavities
- **Customization**: Gold/silver foil stamping on cover and spine, embossing/debossing
- **QC**: 15-point checklist covering magnet alignment (±1mm), ribbon tension, lid gap (±1mm), and velvet adhesion
