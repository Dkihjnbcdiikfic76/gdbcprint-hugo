Folding carton for electronics accessories with precision die-cut insert and full-color custom printing by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003). Designed for earphones, USB chargers, data cables, power banks, phone cases, screen protectors, and small consumer electronics. The flat-packed folding design minimizes shipping volume and warehouse space while the die-cut insert secures products during transit and retail display. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses. Daily output: 1.7 million boxes.

## Applications
- Earphone and earbud retail packaging with insert compartments
- USB charger and cable packaging for retail shelf display
- Power bank and portable battery packaging
- Phone case and screen protector retail boxes
- Smartwatch band and wearable accessory packaging
- Small electronics and gadget OEM packaging

## Customization Options
- Custom die-cut insert for precise product fit and protection
- Window patching with PET or PVC film for product visibility
- Full-color CMYK offset printing with gloss or matte lamination
- Aqueous coating, UV spot varnish, or soft-touch finish
- Gold or silver foil stamping for brand logo
- Tuck-end, auto-lock bottom, or crash-lock bottom structure
- Hang tag or Euro slot for retail pegboard display
- QR code and barcode printing for retail scanning
- Custom size from 50×50×20mm to 400×300×100mm

## Production Specifications
- KBA Rapida 1050 4-color offset press with inline aqueous coating
- Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 7+1 UV press for premium effects
- Automatic die-cutting on Bobst SP 102 BMA for precision inserts
- Folder-gluer line for high-speed carton assembly
- 18,000 sqm manufacturing facility (10,000+8,000 sqm, 2025 expansion)
- ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management
- FSC Chain of Custody certified paper sourcing
- 22 years of folding carton manufacturing experience
- Daily output capacity: 1.7 million color boxes
