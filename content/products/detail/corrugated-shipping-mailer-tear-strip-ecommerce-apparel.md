## Product Overview

The Corrugated E-Commerce Shipping Mailer with tear strip is engineered for apparel brands, textile retailers, and soft goods fulfillment operations that demand both product protection and an elevated unboxing experience. Manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** — 22 years of packaging expertise across an 18,000 sqm ISO 9001 & FSC certified facility — this mailer eliminates the frustration of tape-sealed boxes with a clean, one-pull tear strip that opens smoothly without ripping the branding surface.

The self-locking bottom design requires no tape, glue, or staples — the die-cut tabs interlock with a firm press, saving 3–5 seconds per unit in packing line labor and eliminating tape costs entirely. Full-color branding is achieved through flexographic printing (up to 4 colors) or KBA offset litho-lamination for photographic-quality graphics on the outer liner. B-flute corrugated walls provide ECT 32–40 crush resistance while remaining lightweight enough to minimize dimensional weight shipping charges — the sweet spot between protection and postage cost.

## Applications
This mailer format bridges the gap between basic poly mailers and full corrugated cartons — offering branded presentation with engineered protection:
- **Apparel & Fashion**: T-shirts, hoodies, jeans, dresses, activewear — the wide footprint prevents folding creases while the tear strip delivers a premium unboxing moment
- **Home Textiles**: Bed sheets, pillowcases, towels, blankets, throws — flat format preserves folded presentation
- **Subscription Boxes**: Monthly apparel subscriptions, curated gift boxes, beauty sample kits — full-color branding drives social media sharing
- **Soft Goods Fulfillment**: Plush toys, fabric accessories, scarves, hats — crush protection without oversized carton waste
- **E-Commerce Returns**: Pre-scored return flap on reverse side for easy customer returns — scan the QR code, reseal, drop off

## Customization Options
- **Printing**: 1–4 color flexographic printing (water-based inks) for bold branding, or full CMYK litho-laminate for photographic reproduction with UV varnish
- **Tear Strip**: Single center strip, dual side strips, or full-perimeter tear-open — can be branded with repeating logo pattern or solid company color
- **Closure**: Self-locking bottom + tear strip top, or peel-and-seal adhesive strip with silicone release liner for cold-chain or humidity-resistant applications
- **Surface**: Standard kraft (natural brown), white-top kraft (bright retail appearance), clay-coated white for litho-laminate, or custom PMS tinted liner
- **Interior**: Optional anti-static coating for electronics, food-grade barrier for direct food contact, or water-resistant treatment for cold-chain
- **Size Range**: Custom dimensions from 200×150mm (small apparel) to 600×450mm (bulk textiles) — adjustable depth via variable score lines

## Production Specifications
- **Board**: B-flute single-wall corrugated (2.5mm caliper), kraft or white-top liner, 125–200gsm outer / 115–150gsm inner
- **Fluting**: B-flute (47–53 flutes per linear meter, standard ECT 32–40) — option for E-flute (1.2mm) for lighter items
- **Printing**: Flexographic (water-based inks, up to 4 colors) or KBA 1050 offset litho-laminate (full CMYK + spot)
- **Die-Cutting**: Rotary or flatbed die-cutting with ±1.0mm tolerance, tear strip perforation at 4 teeth per cm
- **Assembly**: Automatic folder-gluer with hot-melt adhesive for side seam, self-locking bottom pre-scored on corrugator
- **Quality Control**: AQL 2.5 (major), AQL 1.5 (critical) — tear strip function tested on every batch (100% functional test), ECT tested per production run, print registration, box flatness, score line integrity at 90° fold
