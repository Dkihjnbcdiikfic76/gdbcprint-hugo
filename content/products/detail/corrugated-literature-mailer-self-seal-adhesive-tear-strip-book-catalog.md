## Product Overview

The Corrugated Literature Mailer with Self-Seal Adhesive solves the two biggest pain points in book and catalog fulfillment: taping labor and frustrated recipients struggling to open packages. The integrated self-seal adhesive strip eliminates manual tape application — simply peel the release liner, fold, and press for a permanent bond that exceeds ISTA 3A shipping standards. On the receiving end, dual tear strip perforations allow the recipient to open the mailer cleanly in under 3 seconds without scissors or box cutters, preserving the contents in pristine condition. Manufactured on Bincai's BHS 2.5m corrugator line with B-flute (3mm caliper, ECT 32), this mailer provides the ideal balance of crush protection and lightweight shipping economics for books, catalogs, magazines, and document sets.

## FAQ

**Q: Will the self-seal adhesive hold during summer shipping to hot climates?**
A: Yes — our hot-melt adhesive is formulated for a service temperature range of -20°C to +65°C. The adhesive is applied at 160°C and cures to a thermoset bond that does not re-melt in transit. We've validated performance through ISTA 7E summer profile testing (72 hours at 50°C/90% RH).

**Q: Can I get smaller quantities for testing?**
A: Yes — we offer sample packs of 10–20 units in your chosen size and print configuration for $30–60 including DHL/FedEx shipping. Lead time for samples is 5–7 business days.

**Q: Does the tear strip work with heavy contents?**
A: Yes — the dual tear strip is designed to function reliably with contents up to 5kg. For heavier items (5–15kg), we recommend upgrading to double-wall EB-flute construction with reinforced tear strip. The tear force scales proportionally with board grade.

**Q: Can the mailer be used for Amazon FBA?**
A: Absolutely — this mailer meets Amazon Frustration-Free Packaging (FFP) Tier 1 (Certified Frustration-Free) requirements: no scissors or box cutters needed, recyclable, and right-sized to the product. We can provide ISTA 6-Amazon.com-SIOC test reports for your specific SKU.
