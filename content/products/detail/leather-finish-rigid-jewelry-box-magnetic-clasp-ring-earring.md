|-------|------------------|-------------------|

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity for custom jewelry boxes?**
A: MOQ is 500 units for standard configurations (magnetic clasp, one die-cut slot layout). For complex multi-piece designs with embossing + foil stamping, MOQ starts at 1,000 units. Trial orders accepted for market testing.

**Q: Can Bincai match a specific leather texture or Pantone color?**
A: Yes. We source PU leatherette in smooth, pebbled, crocodile, saffiano, and custom-embossed textures. Color matching to any Pantone code — we maintain a library of 200+ standard colors and can custom-dye for orders above 3,000 units.

**Q: How are the die-cut slots sized for my specific jewelry pieces?**
A: You provide ring/earring dimensions (or ship us samples), and our CAD team creates a CNC die-cut template. Slot tolerances are ±0.3mm ensuring a snug, secure fit without scratching. We provide a digital proof for approval before cutting.

**Q: What certifications do your jewelry boxes carry for EU/US retail?**
A: Full compliance package: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody (FSC-C147358), REACH (SVHC-tested), California Prop 65, and Sedex SMETA 4-Pillar audited. We provide on-request lab test reports for phthalates, heavy metals, and formaldehyde.

**Q: How long from order to delivery?**
A: Standard lead time is 15–20 days from sample approval to production completion. Complex orders (foil stamping + embossing + multi-slot inserts) require 20–30 days. Sea freight to US/EU ports adds 25–35 days; air freight available for urgent orders at 5–7 days.
