## Customization Options
- Corrugated grade: single-wall C flute (4mm), double-wall BC flute (6mm), triple-wall ACA (10mm), or custom
- Wall construction: regular slotted container (RSC), half-slotted (HSC), full-overlap (FOL), die-cut custom
- Foam partitions: EPE (expanded polyethylene), PU (polyurethane), EVA, or cross-linked PE foam
- Cavity layout: custom CNC-routed cavities for each part geometry with ±1mm tolerance
- Pallet integration: integrated pallet skids, bolt-on pallet base, or slip-sheet compatible
- Coating: moisture-resistant (aqua stop), oil-resistant, anti-static ESD, or VCI (volatile corrosion inhibitor)
- Printing: 1-2 color flexo, full-color litho-laminated label, or plain kraft with shipping marks
- Strapping: PP (polypropylene) or PET (polyester) strapping, 2-4 strap channels
- Accessories: corner protectors (paper, plastic, or steel), edge guards, desiccant pouches

## Seven-Stage Quality Control Gates

| Gate | Stage | Inspection | Defect Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | Raw Material Incoming | Corrugated board caliper ±0.1mm, moisture 6.5–8.5%, FSC cert verification, linerboard basis weight | Moisture >9.0% → batch reject; non-FSC fiber → trace & quarantine |
| **G2** | Corrugator Production | Flute profile geometry, bond strength, warp control (<1.5mm across 800mm width) | Warp >2.0mm → corrugator speed/steam adjustment |
| **G3** | Printing (Flexo / Litho-Lam) | Color ΔE ≤2.0, print registration ±0.5mm, ink adhesion tape test | ΔE >2.5 → ink formulation re-check; registration >1mm → plate remount |
| **G4** | Die-Cutting | Dimensional accuracy ±1.0mm, scoring depth consistent (50% of board), edge quality — no crush | Deviation >1.5mm → die adjustment; edge crush → stripper rubber replacement |
| **G5** | Foam Insert CNC | Cavity fitment ±0.5mm, foam density 80–100 kg/m³ EPE / 45–60 Shore A PU, ESD resistivity for electronics | Fitment >1mm → CNC recalibration; density out of spec → material batch reject |
| **G6** | Assembly & Strapping | Glue bond ≥4N/15mm, stitch spacing 50–65mm, PP/PET strap tension 350–500N, corner protector placement ±5mm | Bond <3N → glue temp check; strap tension <300N → tensioner calibration |
| **G7** | Final QC & Packing | ISTA 1A drop test (10-drop sequence, 760mm), visual cosmetic per ISO 2859-1 AQL 2.5 Level II, barcode scan, count accuracy, pallet squareness | Drop failure → full batch quarantine & root cause analysis; barcode mismatch → line stop |
