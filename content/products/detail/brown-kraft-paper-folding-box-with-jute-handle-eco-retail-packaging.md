## FAQ

**Q: How much weight can the jute handle support, and is it safe for carrying glass bottles or heavy gift sets?**

A: Our standard 5mm natural jute twine handle has a laboratory-tested tensile break strength of ≥ 80 N (approximately 8 kg or 17.6 lbs), and our quality protocol requires every handle attachment to support three times the rated box weight in static load testing. For heavier applications such as wine-bottle gift sets, ceramic dinnerware collections, or multi-book bundles, we upgrade to 8mm jute twine (≥ 150 N break strength) with metal-eyelet reinforced attachment points that distribute load across a larger board area. As a practical guideline, a standard 250×180×100mm box with 5mm jute handle comfortably carries up to 3 kg, while upgraded configurations handle up to 6 kg. We always recommend specifying your target payload during the quotation stage so our engineers can select the appropriate handle gauge, attachment method, and board grammage to ensure safe, confident carrying for your customers.

**Q: Are these boxes with jute handles suitable for food products, and do they meet food-safety regulations?**

A: Yes — our brown kraft boxes with jute handles are manufactured with food-contact compliance in mind. The FSC-certified kraft board is available with an aqueous (water-based) moisture-barrier coating that is FDA 21 CFR compliant for indirect food contact, and all soy-based inks meet EN 71-3 and CONEG heavy-metal limits. The natural jute twine is untreated and free from chemical dyes or finishes in its standard form, making it suitable for dry food packaging such as bakery items, whole fruits, packaged dry goods, and sealed confectionery. For direct food contact (unwrapped baked goods, fresh produce), we recommend adding a food-grade inner liner or greaseproof paper insert. Our quality team can provide migration-test certificates and statements of composition upon request. Note that for hot or moist foods, the aqueous-coated interior option is strongly recommended to prevent oil and moisture from compromising the structural integrity of the kraft board during the retail-to-home journey.

**Q: Can I order different jute handle colors to match my brand palette, and what is the minimum quantity for custom-dyed handles?**

A: We offer jute handles in natural golden-brown as standard, plus bleached white jute and a range of stock dyed colors including black, navy, red, and forest green at no additional color-matching charge for orders of 1000 units or more. For custom Pantone-matched jute dye colors, the minimum order is 3000 units to cover the dye-lot preparation at our jute processing partner, with a one-time dye-matching fee. The dye process uses low-impact, azo-free reactive dyes that maintain the jute fiber's natural biodegradability while achieving rich, saturated color. For smaller quantities (500–3000 units) where custom dye isn't feasible, we recommend pairing natural jute with a colored cotton ribbon threaded through the same handle slots — cotton ribbon is available in any Pantone color from MOQ 500 units. Our sales team can send you a jute color swatch card showing all stock options with corresponding Pantone approximations within 3 working days.

**Q: How do these boxes ship, and what is the assembly process at my fulfillment center or retail store?**

A: The boxes are shipped flat-packed with the jute handles bundled separately inside each master carton to minimize freight volume — this typically reduces shipping cubic volume by 60–70% compared to pre-assembled boxes, significantly lowering your landed cost per unit. Assembly at your facility takes approximately 15–20 seconds per box: (1) pop the flat box body open into its 3D shape and lock the auto-lock bottom panel; (2) thread each end of the jute twine through the pre-punched die-cut slots in the top flaps; (3) tie a simple overhand knot on the interior side of each slot to secure the handle. No glue, tape, or tools are required. Our packaging engineers provide a one-page illustrated assembly guide and a video tutorial link with every shipment. If you prefer fully pre-assembled delivery, we can accommodate this for an additional assembly labor charge and higher freight cost — please specify during quotation. For large-scale fulfillment operations, we also offer handle pre-threading with unknotted ends so your team only needs to tie the final knot, reducing assembly time to under 10 seconds per box.
