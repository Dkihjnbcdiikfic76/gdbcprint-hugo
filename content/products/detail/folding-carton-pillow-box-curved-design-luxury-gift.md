## Product Overview

The pillow-shaped folding carton with its signature curved silhouette brings sculptural elegance to gift packaging — far beyond the conventional rectangular box. Manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** — ISO 9001:2015 and FSC certified — this box is produced on our Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset press for razor-sharp CMYK registration with simultaneous gold foil hot stamping. The C1S (coated one side) 350gsm art board provides a luxurious weight and rigidity, while the soft-touch matte lamination delivers that sought-after velvet-like tactile experience that premium brands demand. Each pillow box ships flat for space-efficient logistics and assembles in seconds with a simple tuck-and-lock mechanism.

## Design Features

- **Signature Curved Profile**: The pillow shape creates an organic, sculptural silhouette unlike standard rectangular cartons — ideal for luxury, boutique, and artisan brands.
- **Dual-Side Hot Stamping**: Gold or silver foil can be applied to front and back in a single pass on the Heidelberg 7+1 — no secondary offline process.
- **Soft-Touch Matte Lamination**: Micro-textured BOPP film creates a velvety surface that resists fingerprints and scratches — tested to 5,000+ rub cycles (Sutherland Rub Test).
- **Snap-Lock Assembly**: Pre-scored tuck flaps with die-cut locking tabs — assembles in under 3 seconds without adhesive. Ships flat for 80% space savings in transport.
- **Optional Window Patching**: PET or PVC window patching for product visibility — ideal for candles, soaps, and confectionery.

## Applications

- **Premium Candles & Home Fragrance**: Display candle jars, reed diffusers, and wax melts with the product visible through an optional die-cut window.
