## Product Overview

Our custom heart-shaped rigid gift box combines romantic design with premium craftsmanship. The distinctive heart silhouette is constructed from 2.5mm rigid greyboard, wrapped in luxurious art paper with a plush velvet-flocked interior. A discreet magnetic closure keeps contents secure while the satin ribbon adds an elegant opening experience. Manufactured on our Heidelberg 7+1 UV press with precision die-cutting for consistent heart contours — ideal for Valentine's Day, wedding proposals, anniversary gifts, and luxury jewelry presentation.

## Design Features

- **Heart Silhouette**: Precision die-cut heart shape with smooth, consistent contours across every unit
- **Magnetic Snap Closure**: Hidden neodymium magnets provide secure closure without visible hardware
- **Velvet Interior**: 1.5mm premium velvet flocking in choice of red, black, white, or custom PMS colors
- **Satin Ribbon Pull**: Coordinated satin ribbon for elegant opening and premium unboxing experience
- **Full CMYK + Spot**: Vibrant offset printing on Heidelberg 7+1 UV press with closed-loop color management
- **Embossing/Debossing**: Custom logo or pattern embossing available on lid surface
