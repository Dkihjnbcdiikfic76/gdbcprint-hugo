## Product Overview

Our premium white kraft paper gift bag combines eco-friendly materials with luxury finishing techniques. Crafted from 200gsm white kraft paper with reinforced side and bottom gussets, this bag supports up to 5kg of merchandise while maintaining a crisp, high-end appearance. The die-cut handle eliminates metal or plastic hardware for a clean, sustainable design. Full CMYK offset printing with spot UV gloss highlights brand logos and design elements, creating contrast between matte kraft and glossy accents. Manufactured in our ISO 9001 certified factory on KBA 1050 and Heidelberg presses.

## Quality Assurance

| Test | Method / Standard | Acceptance Criteria |
|---|---|---|

| Spot UV Registration | Visual alignment check, 100% inline | ±0.3mm position tolerance |
| Color Consistency | X-Rite eXact — ΔE vs. master proof | ΔE ≤2.0 |
| Print Registration | Heidelberg Prinect inline | ±0.15mm color-to-color |
| Lamination Adhesion | Cross-hatch tape test (ISO 2409) | Grade 0-1, zero lifting |
| Load Test | Static load — 6kg for 24 hours | No handle failure, no base tear |
| AQL Sampling | ISO 2859-1 Level II | AQL 2.5 (major), AQL 1.0 (critical) |

### 7-Stage QC Gate

1. **Incoming Material Inspection** — kraft paper grammage, moisture content, and whiteness verification
2. **In-Process Printing** — spectrophotometer color check every 500 sheets, ΔE ≤2.0 target
3. **Die-Cutting Verification** — handle cut precision, gusset fold accuracy (±0.5mm)
4. **Spot UV Application** — screen-applied UV varnish with registered alignment verification
5. **Gluing & Assembly** — reinforced gusset bonding, handle integrity, bottom board insertion
6. **Finished Goods Audit** — AQL 2.5 (major), AQL 1.5 (critical) per ANSI/ASQ Z1.4
7. **Pre-Shipment Inspection** — random sample pull, 6kg load test on 2% of shipment

## FAQ

**Q: What is the minimum order quantity for custom paper bags?**
A: Standard MOQ is 1,000 units per design. Custom size tooling requires 5,000 units. For trial orders with standard sizes and existing dies, we accept quantities as low as 500 units with a small-run surcharge.

**Q: Can the die-cut handle shape be customized?**
A: Yes — we manufacture custom steel-rule dies for unique handle shapes beyond the standard oval and rectangular cutouts. Custom handle dies add 3-5 days to tooling time and require a 5,000-unit minimum order.

**Q: How strong are these bags?**
A: The 200gsm white kraft with reinforced 80mm gussets supports up to 5kg of merchandise. For heavier items (up to 8kg), we recommend upgrading to 250gsm kraft with a reinforced bottom board. Every bag passes a 6kg static load test for 24 hours before shipment.

**Q: Can I get samples before placing a full order?**
A: Free pre-production samples ship within 5-7 business days of artwork approval. Samples include your custom printing, selected finish (spot UV, lamination), and handle style. Expedited 3-day sampling is available.

**Q: Are these bags suitable for food packaging?**
A: Yes — our water-based PVA adhesive and soy-based offset inks are food-safe for indirect contact. For direct food contact, we offer FDA-compliant barrier coatings applied inline on the Heidelberg 7+1 UV press.
