## Customization Options
- Flute: double-wall BC (6mm) for medium weight, EB (8mm) for heavy, or triple-wall (12-15mm) for premium furniture
- Partitions: custom die-cut corrugated dividers with labeled compartments; foam-lined slots for delicate surfaces
- Hardware pack pouch: integrated kraft paper envelope or PE zip bag for screws, dowels, and tools
- Printing: 1-4 color flexo on kraft exterior; full CMYK litho label or preprint liner option
- Branding: registered print with PMS color matching, UV varnish, or hot foil stamping on label area
- Interior: die-cut foam corner protectors, corrugated edge guards, or molded pulp cushioning
- Closure: self-locking crash-lock bottom, full-overlap flaps, or gummed paper tape
- Tear strip: integrated tear-open strip for easy consumer access without tools
- Instruction sleeve: die-cut slot on lid interior to hold assembly manual booklet
- Size: custom from 300×200×80mm (small shelf kit) to 1200×600×150mm (desk kit)
