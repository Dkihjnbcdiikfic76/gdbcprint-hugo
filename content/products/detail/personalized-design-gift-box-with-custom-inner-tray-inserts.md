## Product Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing's personalized design gift box combines premium rigid construction with fully customized inner tray configurations. Built in our 18,000 sqm integrated factory in Foshan, each box features a precision-fit insert tray that holds products securely while presenting them with elegance. From cosmetics and jewelry to electronics and gourmet confectionery, the custom tray insert transforms a beautiful box into a complete packaging solution — all produced on German-engineered KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses with 22 years of expertise.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: KBA 1050 4-color + Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset — ΔE ≤ 2.0 color consistency across runs up to 100,000 units
- **Board**: 1200–1600gsm greyboard core with 157–250gsm art paper wrap
- **Inner Tray**: Die-cut greyboard insert with choice of velvet flocking, satin, EVA foam, or cardboard partitions
- **Surface Finishes**: Matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch velvet matte, spot UV, hot foil stamping (gold/silver/rose gold/copper/holographic)
- **3D Effects**: Embossing, debossing, textured paper wrap, blind emboss
- **Daily Rigid Box Output**: 220,000+ units (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, SGS

## Applications

- **Cosmetics & Skincare**: Serum gift sets, makeup palettes, luxury cream jars — velvet-flocked cavities protect glass bottles
- **Electronics**: Earbuds, smartwatches, USB drives — precision EVA foam inserts prevent movement during shipping
- **Confectionery**: Premium chocolates, macarons, artisan cookies — food-safe tray partitions with grease-resistant coating
- **Corporate Gifts**: Branded welcome kits, executive gifts, award packaging — custom logo embossing + foil branding
- **Jewelry & Watches**: Cufflink sets, bracelet displays, pocket watch presentation — satin-lined cavities with elastic retention straps
- **Wine & Spirits**: Single-bottle gift sets with foam cradle insert and magnet closure
