## Seven-Stage QC Gates

Every heavy-duty corrugated shipping box with foam insert passes through seven documented quality control gates at Bincai's ISO 9001:2015 certified facility:

| Stage | Gate | Standard | Verification |
|-------|------|----------|-------------|
| **1. Raw Material** | Incoming board & foam inspection | FSC CoC, ECT ≥44 lb/in, foam density ±5% | Batch certificate + lab sampling |
| **2. Prepress** | Color proofing & registration | ISO 12647-2, ΔE ≤2.0 under D65 | Heidelberg Prinect Inpress inline spectrophotometry |
| **3. Corrugating** | Flute formation & lamination | BC double-wall caliper 6–7mm, bond strength ≥70 N/m | Pin adhesion test per TAPPI T821 |
| **4. Die-Cutting** | Board & foam precision | Board ±1mm, foam ±0.5mm, clean edges | 100% first-off + periodic sampling |
| **5. Foam Bonding** | Insert-to-box adhesion | Peel strength ≥2.5 N/15mm, zero foam shift | 72-hour bonded assembly test |
| **6. Assembly** | Box formation & fit check | AQL 2.5 Level II per ISO 2859-1, gap tolerance ±1mm | 100% foam-to-box fit verification |
| **7. Pre-Shipment** | Drop & vibration testing | ISTA 1A (10 drops, 760mm) or ISTA 3A | Zero product damage, zero foam detachment |
