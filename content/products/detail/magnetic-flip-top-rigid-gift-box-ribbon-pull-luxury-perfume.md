## Product Overview

Bincai's magnetic flip-top rigid gift box is engineered for luxury perfume brands demanding packaging as exquisite as the fragrance inside. The 2mm rigid greyboard construction wrapped in 157gsm art paper with soft-touch matte lamination delivers a velvety tactile finish that signals premium quality at first touch. A die-cut EVA foam insert — custom-molded to your exact bottle profile — cradles the perfume securely, while the magnetic flip-top lid with integrated satin ribbon pull creates an effortless, elegant opening ritual. Hot foil stamping with embossing elevates your brand logo into a tactile dimension. Produced on KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses at our 18,000 sqm ISO 9001 & FSC certified factory with 22 years of luxury packaging expertise.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Box Construction**: 2mm rigid greyboard core, 157gsm C1S art paper wrap, seamless 4-corner wrapping
- **Surface Finish**: Soft-touch matte lamination (velvet-like tactile), matte/gloss lamination options
- **Lid Design**: Flip-top with hidden magnetic closure (≥3.5N holding force), 180° lay-flat hinge
- **Ribbon Pull**: 15mm double-face satin ribbon, color-matched to brand palette, integrated into lid edge
- **Foam Insert**: 25-40mm die-cut EVA foam, custom-molded to bottle/atomizer profile, flocked or fabric-wrapped surface

- **Press**: KBA 1050 4-color offset + Heidelberg 7+1 UV
- **Factory**: 18,000 sqm integrated facility, Foshan, Guangdong
- **Daily Output**: 220,000+ rigid boxes
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody

## FAQ

**Q: What is the MOQ?**
A: Standard minimum order quantity is 500-1,000 units. Sample orders and small-batch production available.

**Q: Can you match my exact bottle shape in the foam insert?**
A: Absolutely. Send us your bottle sample or 3D CAD file, and we create a custom die-cut EVA foam mold with ±0.5mm tolerance.

**Q: What is soft-touch matte lamination?**
A: A specialty film laminate that creates a velvety, fingerprint-resistant surface — the industry standard for luxury cosmetic and fragrance packaging.

**Q: How many foil colors can I use?**
A: Up to 3 foil colors with precise registration. Each additional color adds one production pass but creates stunning multi-tone branding.

**Q: Do you offer sustainable material options?**
A: Yes — FSC certified greyboard, recycled art paper, water-based coatings, and biodegradable lamination films available.

**Q: What file formats do you accept for artwork?**
A: AI, CDR, PDF, EPS with outlined fonts and embedded images at 300 DPI minimum. Free pre-press file checking included.

**Q: How long does production take?**
A: Standard 12-18 working days after sample approval. Rush orders available at 7-10 days.
