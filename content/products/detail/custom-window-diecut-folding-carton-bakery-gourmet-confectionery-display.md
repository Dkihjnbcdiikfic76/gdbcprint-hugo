## Product Overview

The Bincai Custom Window Die-Cut Folding Carton puts your baked goods and confections on full display while protecting them through retail handling. Each carton features a precision die-cut window with crystal-clear PET film or compostable PLA film, auto-lock bottom construction for instant pop-open assembly, and full-color CMYK offset printing with food-grade inks and coatings. Manufactured at Bincai's 18,000 sqm integrated facility in Foshan, Guangdong, with 22 years of packaging expertise and ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, and FDA 21 CFR compliance. Daily output exceeds 1.7 million color boxes across KBA 1050 and Heidelberg 7+1 UV press lines.
