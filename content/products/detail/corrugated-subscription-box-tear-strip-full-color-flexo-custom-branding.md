## FAQ
**Q: What is the difference between B-flute and E-flute for subscription boxes?**
A: B-flute (3mm) offers better crush resistance for heavier items (2-10 kg). E-flute (1.5mm) has a smoother surface for higher print quality and is suited for lighter items (up to 2 kg). We recommend B-flute for most subscription box applications.

**Q: Can I print inside the box?**
A: Yes. Interior flexo printing is available on the inside liner for branded unboxing messages, social media handles, or care instructions.

**Q: How does the tear strip work and is it reliable?**
A: The tear strip is a reinforced filament tape applied during gluing. It's tested for 500+ pulls without failure and leaves a clean edge when removed.

**Q: What is the minimum order for custom full-color flexo print?**
A: MOQ is 500-1,000 units. Flexo printing plates cost $80-200 per color (one-time) and are reusable for repeat orders.
