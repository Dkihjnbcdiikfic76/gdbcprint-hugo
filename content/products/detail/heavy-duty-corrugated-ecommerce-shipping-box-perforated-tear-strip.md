## Quality Control

- **Burst Strength**: Mullen burst test per batch, minimum 1,400 kPa (B-flute), 2,100 kPa (double-wall)
- **Edge Crush**: ECT ≥ 5.5 kN/m for B-flute, ≥ 8.0 kN/m for BC double-wall — verified per ISTA 3A
- **Tear Strip**: Pull-force 3–6kg, clean tear without delamination, 100% functional test per batch
- **Glue Bond**: Peel adhesion ≥ 2.5N/cm, drop test (1m, 6 faces) on glued joints
- **Print Registration**: Spectrophotometer verification, ±0.2mm on litho-laminate
- **Moisture**: Board moisture content 8–10% at gluing, verified by pin-type moisture meter
- **Dimensional**: ±1mm on box dimensions, ±0.5mm on internal fitments
- **Sample Protocol**: Free pre-production sample in 5–7 days, production reference sealed for batch matching
