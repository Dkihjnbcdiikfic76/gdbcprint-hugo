## Customization Options
- **Flute**: B-flute (3mm, everyday), C-flute (4mm, heavier items), E-flute (1.5mm, fine print), BC double-wall (maximum protection)
- **Print**: 1–4 color flexo (water-based inks), litho-laminated preprint for photo-quality graphics, inside + outside print
- **Liner**: Natural kraft (brown), white top (vibrant colors), mottled white, coated white (litho-quality)
- **Closure**: Self-locking crash-lock bottom, tear strip opening, perforated return panel, tuck-top with tab lock
- **Features**: Die-cut carry handle, ventilation holes, custom divider inserts, foam cushioning, molded pulp trays
- **Branding**: Full-wrap CMYK printing, spot Pantone, inside-print surprise branding, QR code integration
- **Finishes**: Matte/gloss aqueous coating, soft-touch feel, embossing on litho-laminated surface
- **Size**: 200×150×80mm small subscription to 500×400×300mm multi-product kit

## Production Specifications
- **Corrugator**: In-house single-facer + double-backer for A, B, C, E, F flutes and BC/EB combinations
- **Flexo Printing**: 3-color in-line flexo with automatic stacker; 4-color with water-based inks
- **Litho-Lamination**: Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset on coated white top liner laminated to corrugated medium
- **Die-Cutting**: Rotary die-cutter with ±1mm accuracy; flatbed for complex shapes and tear strips
- **Gluing**: Automatic folder-gluer with self-locking bottom capability; crash-lock mechanism tested to 5kg
- **Tear Strip**: Reinforced tear tape with easy-grip pull tab; perforation line for clean separation
- **Daily Capacity**: 500,000 corrugated boxes from dedicated production line
- **Lead Time**: 10–15 working days custom; 7–10 days repeat with existing tooling

## Quality Assurance
- **ISO 9001:2015** — full traceability from paper roll to finished box
- **FSC Chain-of-Custody** — sustainable sourcing with certificate for every order
- **ECT (Edge Crush)**: Tested per TAPPI T-839 — ECT 32 to ECT 71 based on flute and board grade
- **Burst Strength**: Mullen test per TAPPI T-810 — 200# to 350# test ratings
- **Flat Crush**: FCT per ISO 3035 — verifies structural integrity of flute formation
- **Box Compression**: BCT per ISO 12048 — weight capacity certified before production
- **ISTA 3A Transit Testing**: Drop, vibration, and compression simulation for e-commerce certification
- **Print Registration**: ±1mm flexo, ±0.5mm litho-lam — color density monitored throughout runs
- Free pre-production samples with full decoration — 5–7 working day turnaround
