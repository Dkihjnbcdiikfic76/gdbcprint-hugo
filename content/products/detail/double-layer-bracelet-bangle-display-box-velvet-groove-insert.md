## Product Overview

Professional double-layer bracelet and bangle display box engineered for jewelry retailers, trade show exhibitors, and wholesale distributors. The upper tier features six precision-milled velvet grooves that cradle individual bracelets in separated channels — preventing tangling and scratching during transport and display. A removable lower drawer with eight partitioned compartments stores bangles, cuffs, and expandable bracelets securely. The magnetic flip lid closes with a satisfying snap via N48 neodymium magnets, while the integrated satin ribbon pull tab allows effortless opening. Manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** (founded 2003) in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility — 22 years of dedicated packaging expertise.

## Customization Options

- **Velvet Colors:** Navy (standard), black, burgundy, emerald green, champagne, royal purple — 12 colors available with Pantone matching
- **Groove Configuration:** Standard 6+8 layout; customizable to 4+6, 8+12, or any combination for different bracelet widths (3mm to 30mm)
- **Exterior Wrapping:** Art paper (157gsm, matte/gloss lamination), specialty textured paper (linen, leatherette, carbon fiber), or PU synthetic leather
- **Finishing:** Gold foil, silver foil, holographic foil, blind debossing, embossing, spot UV varnish
- **Logo & Branding:** Hot foil stamping on lid (up to 200×150mm area), interior lid printing, drawer front branding
- **Inserts:** Replaceable velvet tray inserts for seasonal collection changes without reordering full boxes
