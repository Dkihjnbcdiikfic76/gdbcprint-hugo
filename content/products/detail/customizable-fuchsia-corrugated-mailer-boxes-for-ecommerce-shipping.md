## Product Overview

Guangdong Bincai Color Printing manufactures customizable fuchsia corrugated mailer boxes purpose-built for e-commerce shipping, subscription fulfillment, and direct-to-consumer packaging. These mailers combine vibrant full-color offset printing on the outer liner with crush-resistant corrugated fluting that protects contents during transit without adding excessive dimensional weight. Produced in our 18,000 sqm smart factory in Foshan, each mailer is precision die-cut for consistent folding and easy assembly on fast-paced packing lines.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Flute profiles**: B-flute (2.5 mm — best for printing quality), C-flute (3.6 mm — balanced cushioning), E-flute (1.2 mm — retail-grade fine surface), F-flute (0.8 mm — rigid-board substitute), BC double-wall (6.1 mm — heavy-duty), EB double-wall (3.7 mm — premium e-commerce)
- **Board grades**: Kraft liner (brown or white top), test liner, fully bleached white liner for vibrant print reproduction
- **Burst strength**: 200-275 PSI (Mullen) for single-wall; 350-400 PSI for double-wall; ECT ratings from 32 to 48 available
- **Printing**: KBA 1050 4-color offset on coated white-top liner for photographic-quality reproduction; Heidelberg 7+1 UV for metallic fuchsia, PMS spot, and UV varnish effects
- **Closure systems**: Self-sealing adhesive strip with silicone release liner, tear-strip opening, tuck-top with locking tab, or tape-strip closure
- **Coating and finish**: Aqueous coating, gloss UV, matte UV, soft-touch, or high-gloss lamination over printed surface
- **Sustainable options**: 30-100% recycled content, FSC-certified liners and mediums, water-based adhesives, soy-based inks
- **Production capacity**: 1.7 million color boxes daily, with dedicated corrugated converting lines for mailer production

## Quality Assurance

| Test | Method / Standard | Acceptance Criteria |
|------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Burst strength (Mullen) | TAPPI T807 / ASTM D3786 | Single-wall ≥200 PSI; double-wall ≥350 PSI |
| Edge crush test (ECT) | TAPPI T811 / ASTM D2808 | ECT 32–48 lbs/in per flute specification |
| Box compression test (BCT) | TAPPI T804 / ASTM D642 | ≥85% of design-rated top-load capacity |
| Drop test | ISTA 3A (parcel delivery) | No structural failure, product damage, or seal breach after 10-drop sequence |
| Flute caliper | TAPPI T411 / caliper gauge | B: 2.5±0.2 mm; C: 3.6±0.2 mm; E: 1.2±0.1 mm; BC: 6.1±0.3 mm |
| Print registration | Spectrodensitometer + visual under D65 | ≤0.5 mm misregister; ΔE ≤2.5 for PMS spot colors |
| Glue bond strength | TAPPI T809 / ASTM D904 | ≥80% fiber tear on manufacturer's joint; no delamination |
| AQL sampling | ANSI/ASQ Z1.4, Level II, AQL 2.5 (major) | Lot accepted per sampling plan; zero critical defects |
| Dimensional tolerance | Steel rule / digital caliper | Inside dimensions ±1.5 mm for boxes <300 mm; ±2.0 mm for ≥300 mm |
| Stack compression | Static load test, 24-hr dwell | No creep failure; deflection ≤3% of original height |

## Seven-Stage QC Gates

| Stage | Inspection | Standard |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Gate 1 — Incoming raw materials | Paperboard grammage, moisture content, burst factor, ring crush, Cobb value | Supplier certificate of analysis + in-house spot check per GB/T 6544 / ISO 2759 |
| Gate 2 — Pre-press proofing | Digital color proof, plate registration, screen angles, trapping | Color match within ΔE 2.5; plate integrity zero defects |
| Gate 3 — Printing run | Inline color monitoring every 500 sheets; densitometer readings; register marks | Density ±0.05; register ≤0.05 mm drift; no hickeys, scumming, or picking |
| Gate 4 — Lamination / coating | Adhesion test (cross-hatch), visual gloss check, UV cure verification | 100% adhesion; consistent sheen across full sheet; no orange peel or pinholing |
| Gate 5 — Die-cutting & creasing | First-off approval: cut accuracy, crease depth, waste stripping, nick integrity | ±0.3 mm from die board; clean edges; crease cracks limited to 2% of score length |
| Gate 6 — Gluing & assembly | Joint tensile test, fold-crease integrity, closure strip peel adhesion | 100% fiber tear; consistent fold without spring-back; peel force ≥3.5 N/25 mm |
| Gate 7 — Finished goods audit | AQL Level II sampling: dimensional check, print quality, closure function, barcode scannability, ISTA packed shipment audit | AQL 2.5 major / 4.0 minor; zero critical; barcode grade ≥C (ISO 15416) |

## FAQ

**Q: What is the MOQ for custom printed mailer boxes?**
A: Minimum order quantity is 500 pieces per design for single-color printing, and 1,000 pieces for multi-color CMYK or PMS-matched printing. Lower trial quantities are available for sampling and market testing — contact us for pilot-run options.

**Q: Are your mailer boxes FBA sizing compliant?**
A: Yes. We manufacture to common Amazon FBA tier dimensions (small standard, large standard, oversized) and can custom-size mailers to fit your product within FBA dimensional-weight brackets. Our design team optimizes flute grade and board weight to minimize shipping costs while preserving ISTA-verified protection.

**Q: What is the shelf life of the self-seal adhesive strip?**
A: The self-sealing hot-melt adhesive strip has a minimum 12-month shelf life when stored at 10–30°C and 40–60% relative humidity, away from direct sunlight. Accelerated aging tests confirm adhesion retention beyond 18 months under recommended storage conditions.

**Q: Can you use recycled content in the corrugated board?**
A: Absolutely. We offer 30%, 50%, 70%, and 100% recycled content across liners and fluting mediums. FSC-certified virgin and recycled grades are available with full chain-of-custody documentation. Soy-based inks and water-based adhesives further reduce environmental impact.

**Q: How do flat-ship mailer boxes save on freight costs?**
A: Our flat-ship mailers are die-cut and creased to ship flat in bulk, occupying up to 60% less volumetric space than pre-erected boxes. This translates directly to lower ocean-freight or LTL charges. Your fulfillment center folds and assembles them on demand using the integrated self-seal strip — no tape or additional labor required.

**Q: Can I get samples before placing a production order?**
A: Yes. We provide free structural samples (plain brown kraft) for fit testing within 5–7 business days. Custom-printed samples are available within 10–12 business days with a nominal setup charge, fully credited against your production order.

**Q: What is the typical lead time for 5,000 units?**
A: Standard lead time for 5,000 custom-printed mailer boxes is 15–18 business days after artwork approval. Rush production (10–12 business days) is available for a surcharge. Ocean freight adds 18–30 days depending on destination port; air freight options available for urgent orders.
