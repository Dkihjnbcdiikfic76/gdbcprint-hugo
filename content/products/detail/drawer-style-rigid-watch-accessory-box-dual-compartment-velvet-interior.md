## Product Overview

Premium drawer-style rigid watch and accessory gift box with dual compartments — designed and manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.**, a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). This innovative two-tier design combines a top-level watch display platform with a smooth-gliding pull-out drawer for accessories, merging premium presentation with functional storage that watch collectors and luxury brands demand. The top compartment features a custom die-cut EVA or PU foam watch pillow (±0.2mm tolerance) in your choice of velvet, suede, or leatherette lining, while the drawer below accommodates extra straps, bracelet tools, warranty cards, and owner's manuals. The **magnetic slide-lock mechanism** uses N42 neodymium magnets for secure closure with a satisfying glide action — tested for 20,000+ open/close cycles with zero degradation. Produced on KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset (15,000 sheets/hour) and Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV presses (inter-deck UV curing, single-pass CMYK+spot+coating) with Bobst SP 102 BMA hot foil stamping at ±0.15mm registration at our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility. CNC corner wrapping ensures ±0.3mm precision at all four corners. Daily output of 220,000+ rigid boxes under ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody, and Sedex SMETA certification.

## Seven-Stage QC Gates

| Gate | Stage | Inspection | Defect Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | Raw Material | Greyboard density ≥650 g/m², magnet grade N42 cert, velvet/suede color batch match, FSC CoC | Off-spec density → batch rejection |
| **G2** | Prepress & Plate | CTP plate curve, dot gain ≤22%, color proof ΔE ≤2.0, embossing die check ±0.05mm | Dot gain >22% → plate recalibration |
| **G3** | Printing | Color ΔE ≤2.0, registration ±0.2mm, ink adhesion tape test 4B+, coating uniformity | ΔE >3.0 → ink + press recalibration |
| **G4** | Die-Cutting & Corner Wrap | Dimensional ±0.3mm, corner gap ≤0.3mm, crease depth 45-55% board, zero edge burr | Corner gap >0.5mm → CNC recalibration |
| **G5** | Rigid Box Assembly | Magnet insertion depth, board lamination zero bubble, inner liner adhesion, drawer rail alignment | Magnet misalignment → line stop + adjustment |
| **G6** | Foam Insert & Lining | Foam cavity ±0.2mm, lining zero wrinkle/stain, adhesive bond ≥4N/15mm | Wrinkle >2mm → lining replacement |
