Drawer-style rigid watch and accessory gift box with dual compartments — designed and manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd., a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). This innovative two-tier design combines a top-level watch display platform with a smooth-gliding pull-out drawer for accessories, combining premium presentation with functional storage. The top compartment features a custom die-cut EVA or PU foam watch pillow in your choice of velvet, suede, or leatherette lining, while the drawer below accommodates extra straps, bracelet tools, warranty cards, and owner's manuals. ## Customization Options

Every dual-compartment drawer box is engineered to your exact specifications:

- **Drawer Mechanism**: Precision-cut slide rails with N42 neodymium magnetic lock — smooth glide with satisfying magnetic snap at full closure. Tested for 20,000+ open/close cycles with zero degradation
- **Interior Configuration**: Top tray — custom die-cut EVA foam (40-70 shore hardness), PU memory foam, or flocked PET for watch display. Bottom drawer — configurable dividers, elastic strap holders, document sleeve, or tool slots. All linings available in velvet (black, navy, grey, burgundy, cream), suede, or leatherette
- **Surface Finishes**: Soft-touch matte lamination (suede-like tactile feel), glossy lamination (high-shine luxury), textured leatherette wrap (PU or genuine leather), linen-textured paper, or carbon-fiber pattern film. Full CMYK + Pantone spot color offset printing on KBA 1050
- **Foil Stamping & Embossing**: Hot foil stamping in gold, silver, copper, rose gold, or custom Pantone metallics on Bobst SP 102 BMA with ±0.15mm registration. Blind embossing, foil embossing, deep-deboss, or combination foil+emboss for dimensional brand logos
- **Drawer Pull**: Satin ribbon pull (color-matched to interior), metal tab pull (gold, silver, rose gold, matte black), finger-cut crescent, or leather tab
- **Construction**: 2.5mm precision-wrapped greyboard (1200-1600gsm), reinforced corner joints with CNC corner wrapping for 90° precision ±0.3mm. Optional 3.0mm board for extra-large watches or heavy accessories
