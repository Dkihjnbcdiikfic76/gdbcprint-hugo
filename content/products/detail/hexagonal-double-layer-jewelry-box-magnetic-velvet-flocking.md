The Hexagonal Double-Layer Jewelry Box is a sophisticated six-sided rigid packaging solution engineered for multi-piece jewelry presentation. - **Closure**: Four N52 neodymium magnets (8mm dia.) — hidden mounting with fabric cover. Magnetic lid-to-base alignment with satisfying click. Optional satin ribbon hinge for traditional presentation.
- **Interior Tray**: Removable upper tray with die-cut velvet-lined cavities (2–6 compartments). Lower compartment with full velvet lining. Tray removal via satin ribbon pull-tab. Custom cavity shapes: ring slot, earring card, necklace loop, watch pillow.
- **Velvet Flocking**: 0.8mm pile electrostatic flocking — available in black, white, cream, grey, navy, burgundy, emerald, royal blue, and custom Pantone-matched colors. Solvent-free process.

## Seven-Stage QC Gates

1. **Incoming Material Inspection** — greyboard grammage, velvet pile density, magnet grade (N52 gauss meter verification), art paper whiteness
2. **In-Process Printing** — spectrophotometer color check every 500 sheets, ΔE ≤ 2.0, Prinect registration monitoring
3. **Hexagonal Die-Cutting** — precision angle check (120° ±0.1°), dimensional verification ±0.5mm flat-to-flat, crease depth consistency
4. **Foil Stamping & Embossing** — registration ±0.15mm on hexagonal surface, foil adhesion tape test, embossing depth ±0.1mm
5. **Tray & Interior Assembly** — cavity alignment check, velvet bond peel test, tray fit test (10 insert/remove cycles), magnet polarity verification
6. **Finished Goods Audit** — AQL 2.5 (major), AQL 1.5 (critical) per ANSI/ASQ Z1.4, full functional test, color match under D65, lid alignment ≤ 0.5mm
7. **Pre-Shipment Inspection** — random sample pull Level II, packaging integrity, barcode/label verification

## FAQ

**Q:** Can the hexagonal box be produced in different sizes?
A: Yes — we offer 6 standard sizes from 120mm to 250mm flat-to-flat diameter. Custom sizes between 80mm and 350mm are available with a one-time die setup fee. Interior tray cavity layout is fully customizable to your product dimensions.

**Q:** What makes the hexagonal construction different from rectangular boxes?
A: Hexagonal boxes require precision 120° corner angles with ±0.1° tolerance to ensure the lid aligns perfectly with the base on all six sides. Our CNC die-making and dedicated complex-geometry assembly line ensure consistent alignment that rectangular box processes cannot achieve.

**Q:** Can the upper tray be customized for specific jewelry items?
A: Absolutely — we design die-cut tray cavities to your exact product dimensions. Common layouts include: 2 ring slots + 1 earring card, 1 necklace loop + 1 bracelet groove, or 6 ring slots for ring sets. We can also include a watch pillow in the lower compartment.

**Q:** What are the minimum quantity and lead times?
A: MOQ is 500 units per design. Sample turnaround: 5–7 days with free pre-production samples. Production lead time: 12–16 days for standard hexagonal boxes with trays. Rush orders of 300+ units available at 7-day turnaround with surcharge.

**Q:** Is the hexagonal box available without the jewelry tray for other product types?
A: Yes — the hexagonal box can be produced as a single-compartment box without the upper tray for confectionery, candles, tea, cosmetics, or any product. We can also add die-cut foam inserts, satin pillows, or custom dividers for non-jewelry applications.
