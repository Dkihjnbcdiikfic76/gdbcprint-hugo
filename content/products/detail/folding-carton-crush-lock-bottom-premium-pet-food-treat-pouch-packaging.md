## Product Overview

Bincai's crush-lock bottom folding carton is engineered for the premium pet food market — where packaging must perform on shelf, in transit, and through the consumer's kitchen. The 400gsm FSC-certified SBS (Solid Bleached Sulfate) board delivers crisp white printing surface with crush-lock auto-bottom for rapid hand or machine erection. Matte lamination exterior with spot UV branding accents creates a tactile premium feel, while the food-grade aqueous coating on the interior ensures direct food-contact safety for freeze-dried treats, kibble pouches, and supplement packaging. Manufactured on KBA 1050 4-color offset presses with inline aqueous coating in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility. 22 years of packaging expertise.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: KBA 1050 4-color offset — inline aqueous coating station
- **Board**: 400gsm FSC-certified SBS (Solid Bleached Sulfate), bright white
- **Bottom Style**: Crush-lock auto-bottom — flat-packed, pops open for filling
- **Exterior Finish**: Matte lamination (1.5mil) + spot UV gloss on brand elements
- **Interior Coating**: Food-grade aqueous coating, FDA 21 CFR 176.170 compliant
- **Closure**: Tuck-top with friction-lock tab — secure reclose
- **Daily Output**: 1.7 million color boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
