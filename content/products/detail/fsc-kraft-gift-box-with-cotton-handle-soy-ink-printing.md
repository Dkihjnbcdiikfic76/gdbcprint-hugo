## Product Overview

A 100% plastic-free, fully recyclable and compostable gift box made from FSC-certified kraft paper with a natural cotton rope handle and soy-based ink printing. Every component — from the board to the handle to the ink — is biodegradable, making this box the ultimate choice for brands committed to zero-waste packaging. Manufactured at **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** with energy-efficient production processes and water-based adhesives, this box meets the most stringent sustainability requirements while delivering the structural quality expected from a 22-year packaging manufacturer.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Board**: FSC-certified kraft paper, 300-450gsm, unbleached natural brown
- **Printing**: Soy-based offset ink on KBA 1050 4-color press — low-VOC, food-safe
- **Handle**: 6mm natural cotton rope, knotted internally, tensile strength 8kg
- **Adhesive**: Water-based PVA glue — solvent-free, non-toxic
- **Daily output**: 1.7 million boxes total factory capacity
- **Lamination**: None (compostable) or water-based matte coating (industrial compostable)
- **Window option**: PLA (polylactic acid) biodegradable film instead of PET

## Customization Options

- **Size**: From 120mm × 80mm × 60mm to 400mm × 300mm × 200mm
- **Board weight**: 300gsm (light items), 350gsm (standard), 400gsm (medium), 450gsm (heavy duty)
- **Handle style**: Cotton rope (natural white), jute twine (rustic), twisted kraft paper (fully paper)
- **Printing**: 1-4 color soy ink, single-side or double-side, natural kraft brown base
- **Closure**: Tuck-top, lid-and-base, magnetic (paper-wrapped magnets), string-and-button
- **Window**: PLA biodegradable window (home compostable), die-cut open window, or no window
- **Inserts**: Kraft cardboard dividers, molded pulp tray, shredded kraft cushioning
- **Customization**: Debossing/embossing on kraft (no foil required), laser engraving effect
