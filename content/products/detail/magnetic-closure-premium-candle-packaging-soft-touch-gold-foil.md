## Product Overview

Bincai's magnetic closure candle packaging box is designed for premium candle brands that understand packaging is half the product experience — before the customer smells the fragrance, they feel the box. The 2.0mm greyboard core delivers substantial weight and rigidity. A soft-touch matte wrap with gold foil branding creates shelf presence that competes with Diptyque, Jo Malone, and Byredo. Inside, a flocked PET tray cradles the candle vessel securely with ±0.5mm precision. Dual N35 neodymium magnets provide a satisfying, silent close. Manufactured in our Foshan smart factory (18,000 sqm) on Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset, KBA 1050 4-color presses, and Bobst BMA foil stampers. 220+ skilled workers, 23 years of packaging expertise.
