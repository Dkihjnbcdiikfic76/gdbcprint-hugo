## FAQ

**Q: What bottle sizes can your hinged rigid box accommodate?**
A: We engineer custom bottle cradles for all standard formats: 375ml half bottles, 750ml standard, 1.0L, 1.5L magnum, and specialty formats up to 3.0L. Each cradle is precision die-cut to the exact bottle dimensions — we request a bottle sample or CAD file for perfect fit verification.

**Q: Can you match our brand's metallic finish for the exterior board?**
A: Yes. Our Heidelberg 7+1 UV press prints on metallic pearl board substrates with CMYK + spot metallic inks for custom color matching. We offer a wide range of pearl finishes — silver pearl, gold pearl, champagne pearl, and custom Pantone-matched metallic boards. Bobst foil stamping adds premium metallic accents.

**Q: What interior materials do you offer for wine boxes?**
A: Standard options include satin (most popular — elegant sheen, colors available), velvet flocking (luxurious deep pile), and matte paper lining (clean, modern). All interior materials are acid-free and safe for long-term bottle storage. Custom Pantone color matching available for satin and velvet.

**Q: Are these boxes export-ready for international wine and spirits distribution?**
A: Yes. All materials are FSC-certified. Boxes are ISTA-tested for export shipping — we've delivered wine boxes to distilleries, vineyards, and distributors across 50+ countries. Flat-pack shipping available for efficiency. Worldwide delivery via DHL/FedEx/UPS/Sea from our Foshan logistics hub.
