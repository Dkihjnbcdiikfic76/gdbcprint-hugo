## Customization Options

- **Board:** 350-400gsm recycled kraft board with natural brown finish. Optional FSC virgin kraft (bleached white) for a cleaner look or greyboard core with kraft wrap for premium rigidity
- **Printing:** Soy-based offset CMYK on kraft surface with matte aqueous coating. Single-color kraft stamp aesthetic available for an ultra-rustic, handmade feel
- **Window:** Compostable PLA film (30-50 micron) — EN 13432 certified for industrial composting. Crystal-clear transparency with 90%+ light transmission. Optional die-cut open window (no film) for a plastic-free tactile experience
- **Tray:** Die-cut recycled kraft tray with individual soap cavities (3-6 compartments). Contoured cutouts hold round, rectangular, or irregular soap shapes securely. Optional molded pulp tray for added cushioning
- **Closure:** Tuck-top lid with hidden magnetic snap or natural jute twine wrap. Cotton ribbon tie for bridal/gift market appeal
- **Add-ons:** Seed-embedded hang tag (plantable, wildflower mix), jute carry handle, embossed brand logo, hot foil stamping on kraft (gold, silver, copper)
- **Sizing:** Common formats: 180×130×50mm (3-bar set), 240×160×50mm (4-bar set), 280×180×55mm (6-bar set)
