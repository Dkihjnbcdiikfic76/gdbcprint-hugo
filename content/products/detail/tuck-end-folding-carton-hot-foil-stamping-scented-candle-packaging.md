## FAQ

**Q: What paperboard do you use for candle packaging cartons?**
A: We use 350–400gsm C1S (coated one side) solid bleached sulphate board for candle packaging. The coated side provides excellent print reproduction on KBA 1050 4-color offset, while the uncoated back offers structural strength. Alternative substrates include art paper-lined greyboard and FSC-certified kraft board for eco-conscious brands.

**Q: Can you add a die-cut window to show the candle inside?**
A: Yes. We offer precision die-cut windows with crystal-clear PVC or eco-friendly PLA film inserts. Window shapes can be custom — round, square, arched, or brand-logo shaped. The window is sealed with ultrasonic welding or adhesive for a clean finish.

**Q: What foil stamping colors are available?**
A: We stock gold, silver, copper, rose gold, and holographic foils. Custom Pantone-matched metallic foils are available for larger orders (5000+ units). Our Bobst SP 102 BMA foil stamping machine achieves ±0.15mm registration accuracy for fine-detail brand logos and typography.

**Q: Are these cartons export-ready and compliant with international standards?**
A: Yes. All materials are FSC Chain-of-Custody certified. Our factory operates under ISO 9001:2015 quality management. Boxes are ISTA-tested for export shipping and available flat-packed for efficient container loading. Worldwide delivery via DHL/FedEx/UPS/Sea from our Foshan logistics hub.
