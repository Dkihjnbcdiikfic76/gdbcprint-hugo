## Product Overview

Bincai's premium necklace and pendant jewelry box is purpose-built for jewelry retailers, e-commerce brands, and boutique artisans who demand retail-ready presentation at wholesale economics. The hinged lid opens to a full 180° display angle — the box transforms from protective packaging into a countertop showcase in one motion. A plush velvet interior (2mm flocking, 0.8mm pile) cradles the necklace chain in anti-tangle channels, while the removable satin display cushion elevates the pendant for maximum visual impact.

Manufactured in our 18,000 sqm ISO 9001:2015 and FSC-certified facility in Foshan, Guangdong, every box passes through KBA 1050 4-color offset printing, Bobst automatic die-cutting, and hand-applied velvet flocking assembly. ## FAQ

**Q: Can the velvet color be matched to my brand's Pantone?**
A: Yes. Our in-house flocking line can match any Pantone color for the velvet interior. We stock 20+ popular colors (black, navy, burgundy, cream, charcoal, forest green) for immediate sampling. Custom Pantone-matched velvet adds 3-5 days to the sample timeline and requires minimum 2,000 units for the custom flocking batch.

**Q: What's the difference between your flocking velvet and genuine suede?**
A: Flocking velvet is electrostatically applied short fibers (0.8–1.2mm pile) onto adhesive-coated greyboard — it delivers the plush look and feel of velvet at a significantly lower cost. Microfiber suede (available as upgrade) provides superior abrasion resistance and a more premium hand feel. We recommend flocking for retail display and suede for high-end collector or luxury items that will be handled frequently.

**Q: Can I get a physical sample before committing to production?**

Digital color proof within 48 hours. Sample shipped via DHL/FedEx (arrives 3-5 days after dispatch). MOQ 500 applies to production orders only.

**Q: What's the lead time from order to delivery?**
