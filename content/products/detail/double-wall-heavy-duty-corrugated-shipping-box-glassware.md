## Product Overview

Double-wall heavy-duty corrugated shipping box engineered specifically for glassware, ceramics, and fragile items — manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.**, a full-chain packaging enterprise with 22 years of experience (founded 2003). This BC-flute double-wall construction combines a 2.5mm B-flute and 3.6mm C-flute bonded with high-strength starch adhesive, delivering 12-18 kg/cm² Mullen burst strength and 32-44 lb/in Edge Crush Test (ECT) values that exceed ISTA 1A requirements for fragile product protection. Every box is engineered for certified ISTA protocol compliance with in-house drop-test, vibration, and compression testing equipment. Our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility combines a 10,000 sqm main factory with KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses and an 8,000 sqm smart factory (commissioned 2025) featuring dedicated corrugated production lines with Bobst flatbed automatic die-cutters and flexo folder-gluers. Daily output of 1.7 million boxes including 500,000+ medium-to-large corrugated boxes with ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certifications. MOQ 500-1000 with free pre-production samples in 3-7 working days.

## Quality Assurance & Testing

| Test | Standard | Specification |
|---|---|---|
| **Mullen Burst Strength** | TAPPI T810 | Every production batch tested; minimum 12 kg/cm² for glassware-grade boxes |
| **Edge Crush Test (ECT)** | TAPPI T811 | Verifies stacking load capacity; minimum 32 lb/in standard grade |
| **Flat Crush Test** | TAPPI T808 | Ensures flute structure integrity after die-cutting and folding |
| **Drop Test** | ISTA 1A | Single box, 10-drop sequence from 760mm — zero product damage, box maintains integrity |
| **Drop Test** | ISTA 3A | Parcel delivery simulation: compression + random vibration + 10-drop sequence |
| **Vibration Test** | ISTA 3A | Random vibration profile simulating truck/air transit for 60 minutes |
| **Compression Test** | TAPPI T804 | Top-load to failure — verifies stacked pallet warehouse performance |
| **Cobb Test (Water Absorption)** | TAPPI T441 | ≤35 g/m² for kraft liners — critical for humid climate shipping |
| **Pin Adhesion Test** | TAPPI T821 | Verifies flute-to-liner bond strength after folding and vibration |
| **Insert Fit** | Internal | Custom foam/pulp insert ±1.0mm; product zero movement after ISTA 1A drop sequence |
| **Barcode Scan** | ISO/IEC 15416 | Grade B minimum; 100% first-pass scan rate |
| **Visual Inspection** | AQL 2.5 Level II | Surface defects, printing defects, glue application, insert alignment |

## Seven-Stage QC Gates

| Gate | Stage | Inspection | Defect Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | Raw Material | Liner GSM ±5%, medium flute profile, FSC CoC, moisture content 6.5-8.5% | Off-spec GSM or moisture → batch rejection |
| **G2** | Corrugating | Flute formation, bond strength (pin adhesion), combined board caliper ±0.3mm | Delamination or flute crush → corrugator adjustment |
| **G3** | Printing (Flexo or Litho) | Color match ΔE ≤3.0, registration ±1mm (flexo) / ±0.2mm (litho), ink adhesion | ΔE >4.0 or misregistration → press stop |
| **G4** | Die-Cutting | Dimensional ±10mm, crease depth 45-55% of combined board, zero edge tear | Edge tear or mis-score → die inspection |
| **G5** | Gluing & Folding | Bond strength, fold accuracy ±1mm, auto-bottom function test | Bond failure → glue pot check |
| **G6** | Insert Assembly | Foam/pulp insert fit, zero insert movement, cavity alignment to product | Insert misfit → die adjustment |
| **G7** | Final QC & Pre-Shipment | ISTA 1A drop (random sample 1:200), burst re-test, visual AQL 2.5, count accuracy, pallet stack test | Drop failure → batch quarantine + root cause analysis |
