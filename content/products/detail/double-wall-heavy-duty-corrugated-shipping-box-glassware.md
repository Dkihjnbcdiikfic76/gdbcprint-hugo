- Subscription box outer shipping cartons

## Customization Options
- Double-wall BC-flute construction for superior crush resistance
- Single-wall B-flute or E-flute for lighter items
- Kraft brown, white top, or full-color printed exterior
- Die-cut foam inserts with product-specific cavity layouts
- Custom flexographic or offset litho-printed branding
- Self-locking bottom for quick assembly without tape
- Perforated tear strips for easy consumer opening
- Custom sizes from 150×100×80mm to 600×400×400mm

## Production Specifications
- Corrugated board: BC-flute double-wall (6mm–7mm thickness)
- Burst strength: 12–18 kg/cm² (Mullen test certified)
- Edge crush test (ECT): 32–44 lb/in for stacking strength
- Flexographic printing up to 3 colors for cost-effective branding
- Offset litho-printed litho-laminated corrugated for premium graphics
- Automatic corrugated box production line with inline gluing
- Kraft liner: 125gsm–200gsm for surface durability

## Production Quality & Testing
- Mullen burst strength testing per TAPPI T810 standard
- Edge crush test (ECT) per TAPPI T811 for stacking performance
- Flat crush test per TAPPI T808 for flute integrity
- Drop test certification: ISTA 1A and 3A protocols
- Cobb test for moisture resistance of kraft liners

- FSC-certified corrugated medium and linerboard
