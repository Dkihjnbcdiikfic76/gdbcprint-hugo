### 7-Stage QC Gate

1. **Incoming Material Inspection** — greyboard density, velvet pile height, magnet grade N35, satin fabric GSM
2. **In-Process Printing** — spectrophotometer color check every 500 sheets on Heidelberg/KBA press
3. **Die-Cutting & Creasing** — ±0.15mm tolerance, 100% panel alignment verification
4. **Velvet Flocking Application** — electrostatic fiber deposition, pile uniformity, dual-color registration check under D65
5. **Assembly & Cushion QC** — magnet recess depth, hinge range of motion, pillow form fit, elastic retention band tension
6. **Finished Goods Audit** — AQL 2.5 (major), AQL 1.5 (critical) per ANSI/ASQ Z1.4
7. **Pre-Shipment Inspection** — random sample pull, full functional test, cosmetic review under D65 lighting

## FAQ

**Q: What is the minimum order quantity for custom velvet bracelet boxes?**
A: MOQ starts at 500 units for standard configurations with existing cushion profiles. Custom tooling for unique bracelet pillow shapes requires MOQ of 1,000+ units. OEM/ODM trial orders are welcome.

**Q: How many two-tone velvet color combinations are available?**
A: We stock 20+ nylon flock colors (black, navy, grey, burgundy, cream, chocolate, emerald, blush, ivory) — mix any two for interior contrast. Custom Pantone-matched flocking is available for orders of 3,000+ units.

**Q: Can the box fit different bracelet styles and sizes?**
A: Yes. We offer 3 standard bracelet pillow profiles (round, oval, rectangular) with elastic retention bands. Custom pillow tooling supports cuff bracelets, charm bracelets, bangles, and anklets up to 200×150mm internal dimensions.

**Q: How long do samples and production take?**
A: Pre-production samples ship within 5-7 days of artwork approval. Standard production lead time is 10-14 days. Complex dual-tone designs with custom foil dies and embossing may require 15-20 days.

**Q: Are the materials jewelry-safe and anti-tarnish?**
A: Yes. All velvet flocking is applied to anti-tarnish treated greyboard. Adhesives are water-based PVA, VOC-free. We offer silver protection-grade lining with activated carbon interlayer for sterling silver and fine jewelry — prevents tarnish for up to 2 years under normal storage conditions.
