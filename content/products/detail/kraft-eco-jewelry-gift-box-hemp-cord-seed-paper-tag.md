**Q: What types of seeds are in the seed paper?**

**Q: How long does the seed paper remain viable?**
A: Seed paper has a 2-year viability when stored in cool, dry conditions. Each hang tag includes simple planting instructions printed in soy ink.

**Q: Can you print full color on recycled kraft?**
A: We recommend 1-2 color designs for the most authentic eco-aesthetic on uncoated kraft. For full-color CMYK, we offer clay-coated white kraft that maintains the sustainable story while enabling vibrant graphics.

**Q: Is the box food-safe for edible jewelry or treats?**
A: Yes. Our soy-based inks are food-safe and the recycled kraft board meets FDA indirect food contact standards. Specify food-grade requirements when ordering.
