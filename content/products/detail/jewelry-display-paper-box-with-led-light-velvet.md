- **LED Lighting System**: Battery-powered (CR2032/AAA lithium) LED module with 20,000+ hour lifespan; touch-activated (press top or lid) or magnetic reed switch auto-on when opened; warm white (3000K), cool white (6000K), or RGB color options; single-point spotlight, perimeter strip, or full-base illumination
- **Interior Material**: Velvet flocking (black, navy, burgundy, grey, cream), satin-lined cavities with die-cut foam inserts, suede-like microfiber lining, or molded EVA foam with flocked surface; custom-fit cutouts for specific jewelry shapes
- **Exterior Finish**: Full-color CMYK offset printing with Pantone matching; hot foil stamping (gold, silver, rose gold); embossing and debossing for brand logos; matte or gloss lamination; soft-touch velvet coating; wrapped art paper (157–250gsm)
- **Structure**: Two-piece lid-and-base, book-style hinge, magnetic flap closure, or drawer-style; window cutout with clear PET or UV-resistant acrylic for visible product display; custom dimensions from 70×70×30mm (ring box) to 300×200×60mm (necklace set)
