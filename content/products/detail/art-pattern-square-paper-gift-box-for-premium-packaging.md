## FAQ

**Q: Can you match a specific art pattern from my brand guidelines?**
A: Yes — our prepress team works from your AI/PDF/PSD files to create offset plates matching your brand pattern precisely. We use X-Rite spectrophotometer color matching with ΔE ≤2.5 tolerance. Physical proof samples include full pattern print for your approval before mass production.

**Q: What's the difference between matte lamination and soft-touch coating?**
A: Matte lamination is a 25-micron BOPP film applied with heat/pressure — durable, fingerprint-resistant, with a smooth matte finish. Soft-touch velvet coating is a liquid-applied UV-cured finish with a velvety tactile feel — more luxurious but slightly less abrasion-resistant. Both are available; matte lamination is recommended for high-handling retail environments.

**Q: Can I get a different box shape — rectangular, hexagonal, round?**
A: Absolutely. While this product listing focuses on the square format, our Bobst die-cutters handle rectangular, hexagonal, octagonal, and custom die-cut shapes. Tooling for custom shapes typically adds 3-5 days to sample preparation. Contact us with your dimensions for a tailored quote.

**Q: How are the art patterns printed — is it digital or offset?**
A: We use offset lithography (KBA 1050 4-color + Heidelberg 7+1 UV) for art patterns because offset delivers consistent color density across long runs (unlike digital which can drift) and supports Pantone spot colors for exact brand color reproduction. Digital printing is available for ultra-short runs (<100 units) but with a cost premium.

**Q: What interior options do you recommend for jewelry versus cosmetics?**
A: For jewelry, we recommend anti-tarnish velvet flocking (0.8mm pile) with die-cut foam cavities — the velvet prevents scratching while foam holds each piece securely. For cosmetics, satin-lined trays with elastic retention bands keep bottles and compacts from shifting. Both options are fully customizable to your product dimensions.
