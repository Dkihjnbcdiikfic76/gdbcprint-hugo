### 7-Stage QC Gate

1. **Incoming Material Inspection** — greyboard grammage, velvet pile density (1.0mm ±0.1mm), magnet grade verification
2. **In-Process Printing** — spectrophotometer color check every 500 sheets, ΔE ≤2.0 target
3. **Die-Cutting Verification** — ±0.3mm dimensional tolerance on drawer slides and insert slots
4. **Flocking Inspection** — 100% visual + touch inspection for even pile density and zero bald spots
5. **Assembly QC** — drawer alignment, magnetic closure force, mirror alignment, LED circuit test
6. **Finished Goods Audit** — AQL 2.5 (major), AQL 1.5 (critical) per ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 under D65 lighting
7. **Pre-Shipment Inspection** — random sample pull, full functional test including 10-cycle drawer open/close

## FAQ

**Q: Can the drawer configuration be customized?**
A: Yes — choose 1, 2, or 3 drawers and customize each layer: top for necklace hooks (2-4) + ring slots (4-8), middle for bracelet cradles (1-3), bottom for ring slots (8-12) or watches. We create a custom die-cut template for your approval before production.

**Q: What velvet colors are available?**
A: 20+ colors including black, navy, burgundy, ivory, grey, chocolate, emerald, royal blue, and rose pink. Custom PMS-matched velvet is available for orders above 5,000 units. Exterior wrapping can be velvet, leatherette, or art paper with soft-touch lamination.

**Q: How quickly can I get samples?**
A: Free pre-production samples ship within 5-7 business days of artwork approval. Expedited 3-day samples available for time-sensitive projects. Samples include your custom drawer configuration, selected velvet color, and foil branding.

**Q: What is the MOQ for custom orders?**
A: Standard MOQ is 300-1,000 units depending on complexity. For trial orders with standard velvet colors and no custom tooling, we accept orders as low as 100 units. Full custom die-making (drawer inserts) requires 1,000-unit minimum.

**Q: Can the LED mirror lid be customized?**
A: Yes — choose warm white (3000K), cool white (6000K), or RGB. Battery compartment is concealed in the lid lining. LED strip lifespan rated at 10,000+ hours. Mirror size and shape fully customizable.
