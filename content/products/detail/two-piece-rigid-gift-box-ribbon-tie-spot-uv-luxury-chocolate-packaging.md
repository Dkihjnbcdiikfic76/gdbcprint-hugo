## Quality Assurance

Quality control for rigid gift boxes follows a comprehensive 7-stage gate process:

1. **Incoming Material Inspection:** Greyboard grammage and caliper verification; wrapping paper grammage, whiteness, and surface smoothness check; ribbon tensile strength and colorfastness test.
2. **In-Process Printing:** Spectrophotometer color check every 500 sheets; ΔE ≤ 2.0 target; Heidelberg Prinect inline registration monitoring.
3. **Die-Cutting & Creasing:** ±0.3mm dimensional tolerance; crease depth uniformity check to prevent cracking at fold lines.
4. **Lamination QC:** Peel adhesion test per ASTM D903; visual inspection for bubbles, delamination, or orange-peel texture.
5. **Assembly QC:** Lid-to-base fit clearance 0.5–1.0mm for smooth operation; ribbon attachment pull-tested to 5kg minimum; spot UV alignment verified under magnification.
6. **Finished Goods Audit:** AQL 2.5 (major defects), AQL 1.0 (minor defects) per ANSI/ASQ Z1.4; full functional test under D65 standard lighting.
7. **Pre-Shipment Inspection:** Random sample pull; ISTA 1A drop test from 760mm; compression strength verification; final visual under D65 lighting.
