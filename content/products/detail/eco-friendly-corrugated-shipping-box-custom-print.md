## Product Overview

Our eco-friendly corrugated shipping boxes combine robust protection with sustainable materials — FSC certified paperboard, soy-based inks, and recyclable kraft liners. Available in B-Flute, E-Flute, and BC-Flute constructions to match your product weight and shipping requirements. Manufactured in our 18,000m² ISO 9001 certified factory with high-speed corrugated converting lines.

## Design Features

- **Self-Sealing Closure**: Integrated adhesive strip eliminates plastic tape — faster packing, cleaner presentation
- **Tear Strip Opening**: Easy-open perforated strip for frustration-free customer unboxing
- **Double/Triple Wall Option**: BC-Flute and triple-wall for heavy industrial parts up to 30kg
- **Kraft Natural Finish**: Unbleached kraft liner with soy-ink printing for authentic eco-branding
- **Custom Die-Cut Inserts**: Internal dividers and cushioning from the same recyclable material
