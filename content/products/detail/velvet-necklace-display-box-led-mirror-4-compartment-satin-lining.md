## FAQ

**Q: Can the LED color temperature be customized?**

A: Yes. Standard options are warm white (3000K), cool white (6000K), and neutral white (4000K). RGB color-changing LED strips are also available for brands wanting dynamic retail display effects. Minimum 1,000 units for RGB upgrade.

**Q: What is the minimum order quantity?**

A: 500 units per design. For first-time clients, we offer a trial run of 300 units at a small surcharge. Free pre-production samples ship in 5–7 days.

**Q: How long do the LED batteries last?**

A: The dual CR2032 coin cell configuration provides 50+ hours of continuous illumination or approximately 3,000 open/close cycles. Batteries are user-replaceable. For high-volume retail, we offer a USB-rechargeable lithium-polymer option (300mAh, micro-USB charging port).

**Q: Can you match my brand's specific Pantone color?**

A: Yes. Our Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV press with inline spectrophotometer achieves ΔE ≤ 2.0 color matching against Pantone solid coated references. We recommend providing a physical brand swatch for the most accurate match.

**Q: Is the velvet flocking durable for long-term retail display?**

A: Our electrostatic nylon velvet flocking achieves ≥80 fibers/mm² density with a cross-hatch adhesion rating of Classification 0–1 (ISO 2409). It withstands 10,000+ finger-touch cycles without visible wear. For high-traffic retail environments, we recommend the suede-effect microfiber upgrade.
