Premium wine bottle magnetic closure gift box with plush velvet interior and elegant satin ribbon pull tab by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003). Designed for single or dual wine bottles, champagne, premium spirits, and craft beverages. The magnetic snap-shut closure creates a premium unboxing experience while the velvet-lined interior cradles and protects the bottle. Gold foil logo stamping and UV spot varnish accents on Heidelberg 7+1 UV press. Daily output: 1.7 million color boxes + 220,000+ gift boxes.

- Premium wine and champagne gift packaging for corporate gifting
- Spirits and craft beverage presentation boxes
- Dual-bottle wine gift sets for holiday and festival gifting
- Winery and vineyard branded packaging
- Duty-free and travel retail packaging
- Wedding and celebration favor gift boxes

## Customization Options
- Single bottle or dual bottle configurations
- Velvet, satin, or flocked paper interior lining
- Gold foil, silver foil, holographic foil, or deboss logo
- Magnetic snap closure with 2, 4, or 6 N52 neodymium magnets
- Satin or grosgrain ribbon pull tab in custom colors
- Die-cut foam or molded pulp insert for bottle neck and base
- Soft-touch matte lamination, gloss, or UV spot varnish

- Custom size for standard 750ml, magnum, or specialty bottles
