## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: Heidelberg 7+1 UV for spot coatings and PMS matching; KBA 1050 4-color for high-volume CMYK runs
- **Die-Cutting**: Bobst flatbed with ±0.15mm registration; auto-lock, tuck-top, reverse-tuck, and crash-lock structures available
- **Board Grades**: 250–400gsm SBS (solid bleached sulphate), 350gsm CCNB (clay-coated newsback), 18–24pt coated unbleached kraft
- **Finishing**: Hot foil stamping (gold/silver/rose gold/copper/holographic), matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch, spot UV, aqueous coating, embossing/debossing
- **Glue**: High-tack hot melt for structural joints; cold glue for crash-lock auto-bottoms
- **Daily Output**: 1.7 million color boxes + 220,000+ gift boxes (post-2025 expansion)
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015 quality management, FSC Chain-of-Custody (sustainable fiber sourcing)
