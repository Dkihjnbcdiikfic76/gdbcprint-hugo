## Product Overview

This rigid gift box features a precision sliding drawer with satin pull ribbon and fully flocked velvet interior — engineered for premium jewelry presentation where the unboxing ritual matters as much as the product inside. The drawer glides smoothly on reinforced greyboard rails tested to 10,000+ open/close cycles, while N52 neodymium magnets provide a satisfying snap-close action. Manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing on Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset with Bobst SP 102 BMA precision die-cutting, every drawer achieves ±0.2mm fit tolerance. With 22 years of rigid box expertise, ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certification, and 220,000+ daily rigid box output across an 18,000 sqm integrated facility, Bincai delivers sliding drawer packaging that elevates every jewelry reveal.
