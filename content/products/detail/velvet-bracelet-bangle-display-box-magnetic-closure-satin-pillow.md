Velvet exterior available in 40+ colors with optional hot foil logo stamping. Produced on KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses across our 18,000 sqm facility (10,000+8,000 smart factory, 2025 expansion). Daily output: 1.7 million color boxes + 220,000+ gift boxes.

- Jewelry retail display for bracelets, bangles, and cuff bracelets
- Ecommerce jewelry packaging with secure magnetic closure for shipping

- Luxury brand gift packaging for bracelet collections and limited editions
- Jewelry subscription box packaging with reusable display-grade construction

- 40+ velvet exterior colors: black, navy, royal blue, burgundy, emerald, blush pink, charcoal grey, ivory, and custom Pantone matching
- Satin pillow in matching or contrasting color with elastic retention strap
- Hot foil stamping on lid: gold, silver, rose gold, copper, or holographic foil

## Production Specifications
- **Material:** Rigid greyboard 1200–1600gsm core wrapped in premium flocked velvet

- **Pillow:** Satin-covered EVA foam pillow with elastic retention strap, removable
- **Hinge:** Full-length cloth hinge with 180° opening angle
- **Finishing:** Hot foil stamping on KBA 1050 4-color press with foil registration accuracy ±0.3mm
- **Certifications:** ISO 9001:2015 quality management, FSC-C147399 chain of custody
