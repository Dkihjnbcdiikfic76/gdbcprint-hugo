## FAQ

**Q: What is the MOQ for corporate pen set presentation boxes?**
A: MOQ is 500 units per design with standard foam tray configuration. Custom foam dies for unique cavity layouts require 1,000+ units. We provide free pre-production samples within 3–5 working days including foam prototyping.

**Q: Can the foam tray accommodate different pen sizes and accessories?**
A: Absolutely. Our CNC die-cut foam fabrication can create cavities for any combination — fountain pens (different barrel diameters), rollerball pens, USB drives, cufflinks, tie clips, business card holders, and more. Simply provide your product dimensions or sample units.

**Q: What foil stamping options are available for corporate branding?**
A: We offer gold, silver, rose gold, copper, matte black, holographic, and custom PMS-matched metallic foils. Our Bobst BMA Swiss-engineered foil stampers deliver ±0.2mm registration accuracy — ideal for fine corporate logos and small text.

**Q: Can I choose different interior lining materials for the foam tray?**
A: Yes. Options include premium suede (most popular for corporate), velvet, satin, and microsuede in standard colors (black, navy, charcoal, burgundy, cream) or custom Pantone-matched colors. We can also color-coordinate the lining to your brand guidelines.

**Q: How long is the production lead time?**
A: 15–20 working days for new designs including custom foam tooling. Repeat orders ship in 7–10 working days. Rush service available for urgent corporate events or product launches.

**Q: Are your materials certified for sustainable sourcing?**
A: Yes. Bincai holds ISO 9001:2015 (quality management) and FSC Chain-of-Custody (sustainable forestry) certifications. Our greyboard core uses 100% recycled fiber. FSC-certified paper wrap and soy-based ink printing available upon request.
