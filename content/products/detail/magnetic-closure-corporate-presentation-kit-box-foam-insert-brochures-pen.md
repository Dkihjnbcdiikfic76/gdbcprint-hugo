## Customization Options

Every presentation kit box is engineered precisely for your content:

- **Magnetic Closure**: Hidden N52 neodymium magnets (2, 4, or 6 depending on box size) engineered for a satisfying snap-close. N52 grade ensures consistent magnetic force after 20,000+ open/close cycles. Optional magnetic + ribbon pull for larger formats
- **Foam Insert**: Custom die-cut EVA foam (40-70 shore hardness) in black, charcoal, or white with laser-cut precision ±0.2mm. PU memory foam for luxury tactile feel. Flocked PET for scratch-free document presentation. Configurable cavities for brochures (A4, A5, US Letter), business cards (standard 90×54mm), pens (standard diameter + custom), USB drives, product samples, and certificates
- **Surface Finishes**: Soft-touch matte lamination (suede-like feel, fingerprint-resistant), glossy lamination (high-shine), leatherette wrap (PU or genuine leather texture), or linen-textured paper. Full CMYK + Pantone spot color offset printing
- **Foil Stamping & Embossing**: Hot foil stamping in gold, silver, copper, or custom Pantone metallics on Bobst SP 102 BMA with ±0.15mm registration. Blind embossing or foil embossing for dimensional logos. Debossing for subtle, elegant branding
- **Construction**: 2.5mm greyboard (1200-1600gsm), CNC corner wrapping with ±0.3mm 90° precision. Optional wrap-around spine hinge for book-style opening or top-lid lift-off design
