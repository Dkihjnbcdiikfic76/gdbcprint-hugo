The Two-Piece Rigid Gift Box with Embossed Linen-Texture Wrap combines tactile sophistication with practical elegance. The telescopic lid and base system — precision-cut to a 3mm overlap tolerance — ensures a smooth, satisfying fit that speaks quality before the recipient even sees the contents. ## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between embossing and debossing on linen texture?**
A: Embossing raises the design above the surface; debossing presses it into the paper. On our linen-textured wraps, both techniques are available. Blind debossing (no foil) is particularly striking on textured papers — the pressed-in design creates subtle shadow play that changes with lighting angle. Multi-level debossing (up to 3mm depth) allows for intricate sculptural effects.

**Q: Can I mix textures — e.g., linen-wrap lid with leather-grain base?**
A: Yes — our two-piece construction allows independent material selection for lid and base. Popular combinations include linen-texture lid with smooth matte base, or leather-grain lid with silk-weave base. No additional tooling cost for different wrap papers on lid vs. base.

**Q: What are the standard turnaround times?**
A: Standard production: 12–15 business days from artwork approval. Custom embossing plate fabrication adds 5–7 business days. Rush production (7–10 business days) available with a 25% surcharge. Sampling: 5–7 business days for stock textures, 10–12 days for custom.

**Q: Is the ribbon pull removable for a cleaner look?**
A: Yes — the ribbon pull can be omitted entirely (friction-fit lid), replaced with a finger notch cutout, or upgraded to a concealed magnetic assist that pushes the base up when the lid is removed. Each option has different MOQ implications; please discuss with our sales team.
