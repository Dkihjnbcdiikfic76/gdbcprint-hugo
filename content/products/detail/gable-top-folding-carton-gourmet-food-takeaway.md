## Customization Options
- Handle: integrated gable handle, twisted paper cord, flat die-cut slot, or grosgrain ribbon
- Window: die-cut arched window with clear PET or anti-fog PET for refrigerated display
- Barrier: PE coating (standard), PLA compostable coating, or aqueous grease-resistant coating
- Printing: CMYK offset on SBS board with up to 6 PMS spot colors
- Surface: matte/gloss aqueous coating, soft-touch velvet lamination, or high-gloss UV
- Hot foil: gold, silver, rose gold, copper, or holographic foil on logo and brand elements
- Board: SBS C1S 250gsm–400gsm; option for 100% recycled content with post-consumer fiber
- Size: custom die-cut to any dimensions; standard ranges from 500ml to 5L capacity
- Closure: tuck-in locking tabs, tin-tie fold, or adhesive strip for tamper evidence
- Interior: optional greaseproof paper liner or compostable PLA insert tray
