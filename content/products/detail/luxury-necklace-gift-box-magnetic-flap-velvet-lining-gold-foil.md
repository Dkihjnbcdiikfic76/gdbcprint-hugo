## Product Overview

The Bincai Luxury Necklace Gift Box combines a magnetic flap closure with sumptuous velvet lining to create an elegant showcase for fine necklaces and pendants. Produced at our 18,000 sqm Foshan factory equipped with KBA 1050 4-color offset and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses, each box undergoes 15 quality control checkpoints before shipment. With 22 years of manufacturing heritage, Bincai serves over 500 global brands with daily output of 1.7 million color boxes.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Construction**: 1.5mm-2.5mm greyboard wrapped in 157gsm art paper with velvet flocking interior
- **Closure**: Hidden neodymium magnet (N42 grade) embedded in flap for secure, satisfying snap
- **Customization**: Gold foil hot stamping on lid, interior card slot, necklace clasp hooks
- **Finishing**: Matte or gloss lamination, optional spot UV, embossing/debossing
- **Print**: KBA 1050 4-color offset (15,000+ sph), Heidelberg 7+1 UV for PMS spot colors
- **QC Standards**: AQL 2.5 sampling (ISO 2859-1), magnet pull-force ≥2.5 N/cm², ΔE ≤ 2.0
