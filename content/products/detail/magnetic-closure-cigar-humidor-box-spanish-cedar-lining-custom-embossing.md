## Product Overview

Our magnetic closure cigar humidor box combines the craftsmanship of fine rigid box manufacturing with authentic Spanish cedar interior lining — the gold standard for cigar storage. The 3mm rigid greyboard construction with magnetic seal strip maintains a stable internal environment, while the Spanish cedar (Cedrela odorata) lining naturally regulates humidity, repels tobacco beetles, and imparts the classic cedar aroma that enhances cigar flavor. A precision hygrometer slot and humidification element compartment complete the functional design. Custom embossing on the lid surface adds brand prestige.

## Design Features

- **Spanish Cedar Interior**: 5mm genuine Cedrela odorata veneer lining — naturally hygroscopic for humidity regulation
- **Magnetic Seal**: Full-perimeter magnetic strip ensures airtight closure for humidity retention
- **Rigid Construction**: 3mm greyboard with premium art paper wrapping for structural integrity
- **Hygrometer Slot**: Precision-cut front panel opening for analog or digital hygrometer (not included)
- **Humidification Compartment**: Internal slot for humidification element (gel beads, Boveda pack, or foam)
- **Embossed Lid**: Custom deep-embossed logo or pattern with gold/silver foil for luxury presentation
- **Cigar Capacity**: Standard configuration holds 10-25 coronas; custom layouts up to 50 cigars
