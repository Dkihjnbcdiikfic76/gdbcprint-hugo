## Product Overview

Bincai's 360° rotating velvet ring display box brings kinetic presentation to fine jewelry showcasing. A motorized rotating platform delivers a full 360° product reveal under 3-point LED spotlight illumination — ideal for retail counters, trade show exhibits, and premium unboxing experiences. Push-button activation with auto-off after 60 seconds. Manufactured with KBA 1050 4-color offset printing and precision die-cutting in our 18,000 sqm Foshan facility. 22 years of luxury packaging expertise.

## Manufacturing Specifications

- **Press**: KBA 1050 4-color offset — high-precision exterior printing
- **Board**: 1.5mm greyboard core, 157gsm art paper wrap with matte lamination
- **Interior**: Deep black electrostatic velvet (2mm fiber) with anti-tarnish treatment
- **Ring Insert**: Formed satin slot cushion, fits rings US size 4-10
- **Rotation**: Miniature DC motor, 6 RPM, whisper-quiet operation
- **Lighting**: 3-point cool white LED array (100+ hours on 3× LR44 batteries)
- **Closure**: Magnetic flip lid with concealed N35 neodymium magnet
- **Finishing**: Gold foil hot stamping + blind debossing on exterior lid
- **Daily Output**: 220,000+ rigid boxes (post-2025 smart factory expansion)
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015, FSC Chain-of-Custody
