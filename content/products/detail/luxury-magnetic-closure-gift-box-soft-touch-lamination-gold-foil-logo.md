## Product Overview

The Bincai Luxury Magnetic Closure Gift Box combines velvety soft-touch matte lamination with precision hot foil stamping to create an unforgettable unboxing experience. Each box features N52 grade neodymium magnetic snap closure for effortless, whisper-quiet lid operation, a fully lined velvet or satin interior, and ±0.1mm gold foil registration via our Heidelberg 7+1 UV press. With 22 years of packaging manufacturing expertise, Bincai operates from a dual 18,000 sqm facility in Foshan, Guangdong, producing over 1.7 million boxes daily under ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certification.
