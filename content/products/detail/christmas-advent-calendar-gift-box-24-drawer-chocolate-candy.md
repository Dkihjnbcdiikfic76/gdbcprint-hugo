## Product Overview

Our Christmas advent calendar gift box is a premium 24-drawer countdown packaging solution for chocolates, candies, mini toys, cosmetics, and spirits samplers. Each numbered drawer pulls out smoothly to reveal a compartment sized for truffles, pralines, or miniature products. The rigid outer shell maintains structural integrity through the full 24-day countdown, while festive CMYK printing with gold foil accents creates an irresistible holiday display. Produced in our 18,000m² ISO 9001 facility on KBA 1050 and Heidelberg presses.

## Design Features

- **24 Numbered Drawers**: Individual pull-out compartments numbered 1-24 with custom drawer knob options
- **Rigid Outer Frame**: 2.5mm greyboard construction withstands daily use without warping
- **Festive Printing**: Full CMYK offset with Christmas motifs — snowflakes, reindeer, winter village, or custom designs
- **Gold Foil Accents**: Hot foil stamping on numbers, borders, and brand logo for premium holiday feel
- **Custom Drawer Size**: Standard 40×40×30mm compartments, customizable for larger items
- **Flat-Pack Option**: Ships knocked-down for assembly, reducing freight costs by up to 60%
