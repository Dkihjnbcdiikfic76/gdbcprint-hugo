## Product Overview

Our Christmas advent calendar gift box is a premium 24-drawer countdown packaging solution for chocolates, candies, mini toys, cosmetics, and spirits samplers. Each numbered drawer pulls out smoothly to reveal a compartment sized for truffles, pralines, or miniature products. The rigid outer shell — constructed from 2.5mm greyboard wrapped in 157gsm C1S art paper — maintains structural integrity through the full 24-day countdown, while festive CMYK printing with gold foil accents creates an irresistible holiday retail display. Outer graphics are printed on our KBA Rapida 105 4-color offset press for rich, vibrant Pantone-matched colours, and drawer wraps run on the Heidelberg Speedmaster CD 102 7+1 UV line for instant-cure gloss and scratch resistance. All drawer blanks are precision-cut on the Bobst SP 102 BMA flatbed die-cutter with ±0.15mm registration for consistent slide-fit across all 24 positions.

## 7-Stage Quality Control Gates

Every advent calendar passes through seven mandatory QC gates before shipment. Reject triggers at any gate halt the batch for rework or reprint.

| Gate | Stage | Inspection Point | Method | Accept Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **QC-1** | Incoming Material | Greyboard caliper, art paper GSM, ink viscosity | Digital caliper + GSM scale | Greyboard 2.5mm ±0.1mm; art paper 157gsm ±3gsm |
| **QC-2** | Print Proof | Colour density, registration, dot gain | X-Rite spectrophotometer + loupe | ΔE ≤ 1.5 vs approved proof; registration ±0.10mm |
| **QC-3** | Die-Cutting | Blank dimensions, crease depth, nick integrity | Bobst SP 102 BMA inline sensor + manual gauge | Drawer blank ±0.15mm; crease depth 50% board thickness |
| **QC-4** | Foil Stamping | Foil adhesion, coverage, registration to print | Tape-pull adhesion test + visual | 100% foil adhesion; no skip, no bleed beyond 0.2mm |
| **QC-5** | Assembly | Drawer slide-fit, alignment, glue bond | Functional slide test on every unit | Smooth pull-out/push-in; no binding; ≤ 2N pull force |
| **QC-6** | Visual Final | Surface defects, print blemishes, numbering accuracy | AQL 2.5 sampling per ISO 2859-1 | Zero critical defects; ≤ 1.5% minor cosmetic |
| **QC-7** | Packing & Palletising | Flat-pack integrity, carton count, pallet stability | Barcode scan + weight check + stretch-wrap tension | Correct SKU count ±0; pallet weight matches BOM ±1kg |
