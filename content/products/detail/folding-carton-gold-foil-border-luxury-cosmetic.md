Luxury folding carton with a continuous gold foil stamped border — designed and manufactured by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. for high-end cosmetics, skincare, and fragrance brands. The gold foil border frames the entire box, creating an immediate impression of luxury and attention to detail. The soft-touch matte finish on the main surface contrasts with the metallic gold border, producing a tactile and visual experience that elevates brand perception. A reverse-tuck or straight-tuck closure is available depending on the required presentation style. Produced on Heidelberg 7+1 UV and KBA 1050 offset presses with integrated foil stamping across 10,000 sqm of factory space. Daily output capacity of 1.5 million boxes.

## Customization Options
- Foil color: Gold (classic), silver, rose gold, holographic, or matte gold
- Border style: Single line, double line, ornamental filigree, or full panel foil
- Surface: Soft-touch velvet, matte lamination, high-gloss UV, or textured linen
- Structure: Reverse tuck end (RTE), straight tuck end (STE), or auto-lock bottom
- Interior: Printed pattern, solid PMS color, or white
- Add-ons: Spot UV logo highlight, embossed brand mark, die-cut window with PET film

## Production Specifications
- **Board:** 300–400 gsm SBS (Solid Bleached Sulfate) or 350 gsm art card, FSC certified
- **Gold foil:** Metallized polyester, 12 μm thickness, available in gold/silver/rose gold/holographic
- **Foil stamping:** Heidelberg cylinder stamper, brass die, temperature 110–130°C, pressure 60–80 tons
- **Printing:** Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset — CMYK + PMS spot colors + UV coating in one pass
- **Soft-touch coating:** Water-based matte tactile finish, 3–5 μm thickness, fingerprint-resistant
- **Die-cutting:** Steel rule die with ±0.3 mm tolerance, automatic stripping
- **Quality control:** Foil adhesion tape test (3M 610), color consistency under D65 lighting, crease and fold accuracy, glue-line integrity
