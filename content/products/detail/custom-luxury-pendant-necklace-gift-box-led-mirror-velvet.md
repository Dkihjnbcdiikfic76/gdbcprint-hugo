| Gate | Stage | What We Check | Rejection Criteria |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does the LED light last, and can customers replace the battery?**
A: The CE-certified LED module runs on a standard CR2032 coin battery with 50+ hours of continuous runtime (equivalent to thousands of openings). The magnetic reed switch activates the LED only when the lid opens past 25–35°, preventing battery drain during storage. The battery compartment is accessible (with small Phillips screwdriver) for customer replacement, and we include a spare battery in bulk orders. For brands preferring sealed units, we offer a non-replaceable 100+ hour extended-life module.

**Q: Does the velvet flocking shed or transfer color onto jewelry?**
A: No. Our electrostatic velvet flocking uses 0.8–1.2mm nylon fibers bonded with hot-melt adhesive at 160–180°C. We test every batch with a Martindale abrasion test (500 cycles, 9kPa pressure) and colorfastness to rubbing (ISO 105-X12) — no shedding and no color transfer. This is critical for white gold, silver, and pearl jewelry where any fiber contamination would be unacceptable. Many luxury jewelry brands have used our boxes for 5+ years without shedding complaints.

**Q: What pendant/necklace sizes can this box accommodate?**
A: Our standard interior accommodates pendants up to 40mm diameter and chains up to 50cm length (princess). We offer custom sizing for: choker (35–40cm), matinee (50–60cm), opera (70–85cm), and rope/lariat (90–120cm). For multi-chain configurations or necklace+earring sets, we add additional satin-grooved slots and earring card inserts. Each design is prototyped with your actual jewelry to ensure perfect fit.

**Q: Can the LED mirror box be shipped internationally? Are there battery restrictions?**
