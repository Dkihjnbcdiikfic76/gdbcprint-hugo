## Product Overview

The double-door rigid gift box with pull-out drawer is the most theatrical unboxing experience in our rigid box portfolio. Two side-panel doors swing open on concealed piano hinges to reveal a smooth-gliding interior drawer — the recipient pulls a satin ribbon tab and the presentation tray emerges with orchestral precision. Manufactured by **Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd.** — ISO 9001:2015 and FSC Chain-of-Custody certified, with 22 years of luxury packaging expertise — each box requires precision hinge alignment, CNC-routed drawer channels, and hand-applied interior flocking. Our 18,000 sqm Foshan factory produces 220,000+ rigid boxes daily on Heidelberg 7+1 UV and KBA 1050 presses.

## Design Features

- **Double-Door Opening**: Two side panels open outward on concealed piano hinges — secured by N35 neodymium magnets for a satisfying close. The reveal is dramatic: doors swing open 180° to expose the drawer front.
- **Smooth-Glide Drawer**: Interior drawer rides on CNC-routed channels with ±0.1mm clearance — no binding, no wobble. Satin ribbon pull tab for effortless extraction.
- **Concealed Hinge Mechanism**: Nickel-plated mini piano hinges (40mm) embedded between greyboard layers — invisible from the exterior, rated for 20,000+ open/close cycles.
- **Full Interior Flocking**: Velvet flocking in 20+ colors applied to all interior surfaces — drawer cavity, door panels, and lid underside. 1.5mm fiber length for premium hand-feel.
- **Magnetic Door Closure**: Each door features dual N35 magnets (10mm × 3mm) aligned with steel strike plates — doors snap shut with authority.

## Applications

- **Luxury Watches**: Watch sits in die-cut foam within the drawer — doors open to reveal timepiece like a jewelry vault. Custom cushion in brand colors.
