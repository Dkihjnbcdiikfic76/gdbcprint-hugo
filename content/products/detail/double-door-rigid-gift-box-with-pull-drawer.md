## Product Overview

The double-door rigid gift box with pull-out drawer delivers the most theatrical unboxing experience in Bincai's rigid box portfolio — two side-panel doors swing open on concealed piano hinges to reveal a smooth-gliding interior drawer. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What makes the double-door design different from a standard rigid box?**
The double-door architecture creates a theatrical reveal — two side doors swing open to expose the drawer, rather than having a lift-off lid. This makes the unboxing experience more ceremonial and Instagram-worthy. It's ideal for products where the packaging experience is part of the brand story.

**Q: How durable are the concealed hinges?**
Our nickel-plated piano hinges are embedded between greyboard layers and rated for 20,000+ open/close cycles — equivalent to opening the box once daily for 54+ years. The alignment jig ensures perfect door symmetry (±0.3°).

**Q: Can the drawer hold heavy items like wine bottles?**
Yes. The drawer rides on CNC-routed channels with 1600gsm greyboard construction. We reinforce the drawer base for spirits bottles (700-750ml, ~1.5kg loaded weight). For extra-heavy items, we add a second layer of greyboard to the drawer base.

**Q: What interior options work best for jewelry versus watches?**
Jewelry sets use velvet-lined cavity trays with partitioned compartments (ring slot, necklace channel, earring posts). Watches use die-cut EVA foam with a precision watch pillow — the cushion holds the watch securely even when the drawer is tilted.

**Q: Is the flocking colorfast and anti-tarnish?**
Our electrostatic velvet flocking uses colorfast nylon fibers tested to 500+ drawer cycles without shedding. For jewelry applications, we offer anti-tarnish treated flocking that absorbs atmospheric sulfur compounds to protect silver and gold-plated items.

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity for full custom tooling?**
MOQ is 500 units for standard configurations. Multi-process orders (foil stamping + embossing + custom flocking + insert) start at 1,000 units. We offer free 3D structural mockups within 24 hours and pre-production samples in 5-7 working days.
