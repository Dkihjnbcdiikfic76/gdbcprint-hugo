Kraft paper eco-friendly soap packaging box with die-cut window and soy-based ink printing by Guangdong Bincai Color Printing Co., Ltd. (founded 2003). Designed for handmade soap bars, organic skincare products, natural deodorants, bath bombs, and eco-conscious cosmetic brands. Made from FSC certified kraft paper with compostable PLA window film or recyclable PET window. Printed with vegetable-based soy ink for full environmental compatibility. The entire package is curbside recyclable in paper streams. Produced across 18,000 sqm facility. Daily output: 1.7 million boxes.

## Applications
- Handmade artisan soap bar retail packaging
- Organic and natural skincare product boxes
- Bath bomb and bath salt packaging
- Shampoo bar and solid conditioner packaging
- Eco-friendly deodorant and body care packaging
- Zero-waste store and refillery product packaging

## Customization Options
- Natural kraft brown or bleached white kraft exterior
- Window die-cut with PLA (compostable) or PET (recyclable) film
- Soy-based ink printing in 1-4 colors with matte finish
- Embossing or debossing for subtle brand texture
- Hang tag or Euro slot for retail display
- Tuck-end, auto-lock bottom, or sleeve structure
- Custom size from 60×40×20mm to 200×150×80mm
- Inside printing for ingredient list and brand story
- FSC logo printing for certified brand communication

## Production Specifications
- KBA Rapida 1050 4-color offset press running soy-based inks
- Automatic die-cutting with window patching inline
- PLA window film for fully compostable packaging option
- FSC Chain of Custody certification (FSC-CXXXXXX)
- 18,000 sqm manufacturing facility (10,000+8,000 sqm, 2025 expansion)
- ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management
- 22 years of paper packaging manufacturing experience
- Daily output capacity: 1.7 million boxes
