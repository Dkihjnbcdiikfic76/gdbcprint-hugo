Book-style rigid boxes open like a hardcover book, creating an elegant unboxing experience that enhances brand perception. Ideal for:
- **Luxury Cosmetics & Fragrances**: Perfume sets, skincare collections, makeup palettes
- **Premium Electronics**: Smartphones, watches, headphones, accessories
- **Corporate Gifts**: Executive notebooks, pen sets, award presentations
- **Jewelry & Watches**: High-end timepieces, fine jewelry collections, cufflinks
- **Wine & Spirits**: Premium bottle presentation boxes with internal inserts
- **Confectionery**: Luxury chocolate collections, artisanal gift sets

## Customization Options
- **Materials**: 1200-1800 gsm greyboard core wrapped in 157-250 gsm art paper, kraft paper, specialty textured paper, or leatherette
- **Printing**: Full-color CMYK offset on KBA 1050 4-color press; up to 7 colors + UV on Heidelberg 7+1 press
- **Finishing**: Gold/silver/copper hot foil stamping, blind embossing/debossing, spot UV varnish, matte/gloss lamination, soft-touch coating
- **Closure**: Hidden magnetic flap, ribbon pull-tab, metal snap buttons, or elastic band
- **Interior**: Custom die-cut foam inserts, velvet/satin lining, vacuum-formed trays, ribbon pulls, card slots
- **Size**: Fully custom dimensions — from compact jewelry boxes to large format gift boxes up to 450×350mm
