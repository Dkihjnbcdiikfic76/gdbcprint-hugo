## Product Overview

Bincai's magnetic rigid gift box for premium electronics is engineered for brands that demand unboxing excellence. The precision-cut EVA foam insert cradles headphones, smartwatches, earbuds, or accessories with zero movement during transit — every cavity is CNC-machined to ±0.5mm tolerance. A soft-touch matte finish and dual N35 neodymium magnets deliver the tactile premium experience consumers expect from brands like Apple, Bose, and Sony. Manufactured in our Foshan smart factory (18,000 sqm total across two facilities) with Heidelberg 7+1 UV offset and KBA 1050 4-color presses. 220+ skilled workers, 22 years of packaging expertise since 2003.

## Applications

- **Headphone & Earbud Packaging**: Over-ear headphones, true wireless earbuds (TWS), and neckband earphone retail boxes with cable management channels
- **Smartwatch & Wearable Packaging**: Apple Watch-style presentation boxes with watch body + strap compartments, fitness tracker display packaging
- **Smartphone Accessory Kits**: Charging cable, power adapter, and case combo gift sets in tiered foam trays
- **Gaming Peripheral Packaging**: Gaming mice, mechanical keyboards, and controller unboxing experiences
- **Corporate Tech Gifts**: Branded USB drives, power banks, and Bluetooth speakers for executive gifting
- **Medical Device Packaging**: Blood pressure monitors, glucose meters, and wearable health devices requiring precision fit
