## Seven-Stage Quality Control Gates

Every Bincai rigid whiskey tube passes through seven mandatory QC inspection gates before shipment — the same rigor trusted by 200+ global spirit brands across 50+ countries:

| Gate | Stage | What We Check | Rejection Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | Incoming Material | Greyboard density (≥2.0mm ±0.05), art paper GSM consistency, silk OEKO-TEX certification, neodymium magnet N35 grade verification, foil batch colorimetry | Board <1.95mm, magnet pull <3.0kg, foil ΔE >1.5 |
| **G2** | Pre-Press Proofing | Color calibration to G7 Master standard, foil plate alignment, cylinder seam artwork registration, Pantone match to brand specification | ΔE >2.0 from target, foil misregistration >0.1mm |
| **G3** | Printing Run (Inline) | Real-time spectrophotometer monitoring every 50 sheets, foil registration on curved surface, ink density consistency | Color shift >1.5 ΔE, foil bleed >0.2mm, hickies/dry spots |
| **G4** | Cylinder Forming | Ultrasonic weld integrity on seam (50kg lateral crush), wall thickness uniformity (caliper gauge at 8 points), magnetic lid snap force (3.0–4.0kg dynamometer) | Seam gap >0.1mm, crush failure <50kg, snap force <2.8kg |
| **G5** | Lining Assembly | Silk adhesion (no wrinkles/bubbles), thread alignment, interior cleanliness (visual under 500 lux), foam insert fit tolerance | Wrinkle >5mm, misalignment >2mm, adhesive bleed-through |
| **G6** | Final QC (AQL 2.5) | 360° visual inspection under 1000 lux, 10-drop ISTA 1A sequence with bottle, magnetic lid cycle test (50 cycles), foil scratch resistance (Sutherland rub) | Any structural crack, foil rub-off, magnet failure, scratch ≥5mm |
| **G7** | Pre-Shipment Audit | Carton drop test (1.2m corner/edge/face), barcode scan verification, moisture content ≤8%, pallet stability | Carton damage, scan failure, moisture >8% |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?**
A: MOQ is 500 units per design. For new mold/tooling projects, we recommend 1,000 units to amortize setup costs. Rush orders as low as 300 units available with surcharge.

**Q: What bottle sizes does the tube accommodate?**
A: Standard formats include 750ml (US), 700ml (EU), 375ml (half), 1L, and 1.75L. Custom diameters from 85mm to 130mm and heights from 180mm to 380mm cover virtually all spirit bottle shapes including square, oval, and flask bottles.

**Q: Can the foil embossing wrap continuously around the tube — 360 degrees?**
A: Yes. Our Heidelberg 7+1 UV foil press with cylindrical substrate handling achieves seamless 360° foil registration with ±0.1mm tolerance. The curved surface foil application is one of our signature capabilities — unlike flat-sheet-only competitors.

**Q: Is the silk lining safe for direct food/beverage contact?**
A: The silk lining contacts the bottle exterior, not the beverage. Our 210T polyester silk is OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified (Class I — safe for baby products). For distilleries requiring food-grade certification on all packaging materials, we provide full material traceability documentation.

**Q: How does the tube protect the bottle during shipping?**
A: The 2.0mm greyboard cylinder with ultrasonically welded seam withstands 50kg lateral crush force. Combined with the silk-lined interior, the bottle is fully suspended with 5mm minimum clearance from all cylinder walls. ISTA 1A drop-test certification confirms survival from 1m drops on all faces, corners, and edges. For e-commerce direct-to-consumer shipping, we recommend an additional corrugated outer carton.

**Q: What's the lead time and sampling process?**
A: Free pre-production samples ship in 5–7 business days. New tooling production lead time is 12–15 working days. Repeat orders ship in 7–10 working days. Express 5-day production available for urgent brand launches (surcharge applies). We ship samples via DHL/UPS/FedEx express worldwide.

**Q: Can you match our exact brand Pantone color?**
A: Yes. We use G7 Master Color certified workflows on both KBA 1050 (4-color) and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses. Pantone matching with ΔE ≤1.5 is standard. For metallic foil colors (gold, rose gold, copper, etc.), we maintain a library of 50+ foil shades and can custom-match to your brand's PMS metallic specification.
