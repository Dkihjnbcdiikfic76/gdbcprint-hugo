## FAQ

**Q: How quickly does this recycled kraft paper gift box biodegrade?**
A: In a home composting environment, our 100% biodegradable kraft paper boxes typically break down within 3-6 months, compared to conventional plastic-coated boxes that can persist for decades. The soy-based ink used in printing is also biodegradable and does not interfere with the composting process. Bincai's FSC-certified kraft material is unbleached and free of harmful chemical coatings, ensuring the entire box — from paper to print — returns safely to the earth. For industrial composting facilities, degradation is even faster, completing within 6-12 weeks.

**Q: Is soy ink printing safe for food-contact and cosmetic packaging applications?**
A: Yes, soy-based ink is widely recognized as one of the safest printing options for food-contact and cosmetic packaging. Unlike petroleum-based inks that contain volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and heavy metals, soy ink is derived from renewable soybean oil and produces significantly fewer emissions during printing and de-inking. At Bincai, our KBA 1050 4-color and Heidelberg 7+1 UV presses are calibrated for soy ink compatibility, delivering vibrant, smudge-resistant prints without compromising the eco-friendly integrity of the box. We also offer food-safe barrier coatings upon request.

**Q: Can I customize the size, structure, and print design for my brand?**
A: Absolutely. Every dimension, structural feature, and print element is fully customizable to match your brand identity and product requirements. Bincai's in-house design team works with you from concept to completion — whether you need a custom insert for product protection, a specific unboxing experience, or multi-color logo printing with precise PMS color matching. Our factory spans 10,000 sqm plus an 8,000 sqm smart factory (commissioned in 2025), giving us the flexibility to handle both small trial runs (MOQ 500-1000 pcs) and large-scale production of 1.7M+ color boxes daily.

**Q: What does FSC certification mean for my brand's sustainability claims?**
A: FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) certification guarantees that the kraft paper used in your packaging comes from responsibly managed forests that meet strict environmental, social, and economic standards. When you choose Bincai's FSC-certified kraft boxes, you can legally display the FSC logo on your packaging, which builds consumer trust and supports your brand's ESG goals. Bincai has maintained FSC chain-of-custody certification alongside ISO 9001:2015 since our founding in 2003, giving you full traceability from forest to finished box over 22+ years of manufacturing excellence.
