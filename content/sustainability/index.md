## Packaging That Respects the Planet

Sustainability at Guangdong Bincai Color Printing isn't a marketing claim — it's certified, audited, and measurable. From FSC Chain-of-Custody raw materials to biodegradable finished boxes, every stage of our manufacturing process is designed to minimize environmental impact without compromising quality.

| Certification / Practice | Detail |
|---|---|
| **FSC Chain-of-Custody** | License FSC-C156391 — full traceability from certified forests to finished box |
| **ISO 9001:2015** | Quality management system ensuring consistent sustainable production |
| **Recycled Content** | Up to 100% post-consumer recycled (PCR) kraft board available |
| **Soy-Based Inks** | Low-VOC, EN 71-3 compliant, biodegradable — standard on all kraft eco lines |
| **Water-Based Coatings** | Plastic-free alternative to traditional lamination — fully recyclable |
| **Biodegradable** | Full box decomposition within 6-12 months in home/industrial compost |

## Manufacturing Efficiency

| Efficiency Metric | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Flat-Pack Design** | Reduces shipping volume by 60% → lower carbon footprint per unit |
| **Dual-Press Strategy** | KBA 1050 (bulk) + Heidelberg 7+1 UV (premium) → energy-optimal production routing |
| **18,000 sqm Integrated Facility** | All stages under one roof → zero inter-facility transport emissions |
| **Waste Reduction** | Paper trim recycled into corrugated medium — < 3% total waste to landfill |
| **LED Factory Lighting** | Smart factory (2025) uses 100% LED → 40% lighting energy reduction |
